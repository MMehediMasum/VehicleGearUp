## Privacy Policy

When you visit or use VehicleGearUp, we may collect the following types of information:

### a) Personal Information

You may provide personal information when you:

* Subscribe to our newsletter
* Contact us via email or contact forms
* Participate in surveys or promotions

This information may include your:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number (optional)
* Mailing address (if applicable)

### b) Non-Personal Information

We also collect non-personal information automatically through website analytics tools, which may include:

* IP address
* Browser type and version
* Pages visited and time spent on the website
* Referral sources and links clicked

This information helps us **improve website performance, user experience, and content relevance**.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

VehicleGearUp uses the collected information to:

* Provide the services, products, and content you request
* Send newsletters, updates, promotions, and relevant offers
* Improve website functionality and user experience
* Monitor website performance and trends
* Respond to inquiries, questions, or customer support requests

We do **not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Cookies & Tracking

VehicleGearUp uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

* Enhance website functionality
* Personalize content and offers
* Analyze traffic and user behavior

You can **disable cookies** through your browser settings, but some features of the website may not function properly if cookies are turned off.

We are not responsible for the **privacy practices or content** of external websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before interacting with them.
