## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Car Seat Types Suit An 18-month-old Best?



Convertible car seats like Baby Trend Trooper and Graco Extend2Fit are ideal. They offer rear and forward-facing modes for safety and comfort.



### How To Choose A Space-saving Car Seat For Toddlers?



Look for slim designs like Graco SlimFit or Safety 1st Ellaris. These fit well in smaller cars and allow 3-across seating.



### Are 3-in-1 Car Seats Good For Long Journeys?



Yes, models like Graco TriRide and Safety 1st Crosstown offer multiple modes. They provide comfort and adaptability for long trips.



### What Safety Features Matter In Toddler Car Seats?



Adjustable headrests, 5-point harnesses, and side-impact protection are key. Seats like Safety 1st Grow and Go include these essential features.



### Can Booster Seats Be Used For 18-month-olds?



No, boosters like Graco Tranzitions and TurboBooster 2. 0 are for older children. Use convertible seats until your child meets booster requirements.



### How To Maintain A Car Seat For Hygiene And Safety?



Choose seats with machine-washable covers like Safety 1st Ellaris. Regular cleaning keeps the seat safe and comfortable for your toddler.
