## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is A Gps Tracker Without Monthly Fees?



A GPS tracker without monthly fees works without subscription costs. It uses built-in technology or Apple Find My for real-time location. This saves money while providing accurate tracking for cars, pets, kids, or elderly without extra charges.



### How Accurate Are No-subscription Gps Trackers?



No-subscription GPS trackers offer precise real-time tracking. Many use cellular or Bluetooth signals, providing location updates within a few meters. Accuracy depends on device quality, coverage, and environment, but top models deliver reliable data comparable to subscription-based trackers.



### Can I Track Multiple Vehicles With One Device?



Most no-monthly-fee GPS trackers track one vehicle per device. For multiple vehicles, you need multiple trackers. Some devices support family or fleet tracking via apps, but each vehicle requires its own GPS tracker for accurate real-time monitoring.



### Do Hidden Magnetic Gps Trackers Work Well?



Yes, hidden magnetic GPS trackers are effective. They attach securely to vehicles, offering discreet real-time tracking. These devices are weatherproof and have strong magnets, making them ideal for cars, motorcycles, or personal items without attracting attention.



### Are Gps Trackers Compatible With Both Ios And Android?



Many no-subscription GPS trackers support iOS and Android. Some work exclusively with Apple Find My (iOS only). Others offer apps for both platforms, ensuring versatile device compatibility and easy access to real-time location data on your smartphone.



### How Long Does The Battery Last On These Gps Trackers?



Battery life varies by model and usage. Most mini GPS trackers last from a few days up to several weeks on a single charge. Real-time tracking and frequent updates reduce battery life faster than intermittent tracking modes.
