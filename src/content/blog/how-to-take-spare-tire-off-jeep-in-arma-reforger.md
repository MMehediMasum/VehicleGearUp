## Locate The Spare Tire

### Check The Rear Of The Jeep

The spare tire often mounts on the back of the Jeep. Look for a large tire attached to the rear door or tailgate. It is usually secured with bolts or a locking mechanism.

### Inspect Underneath The Vehicle

Sometimes, the spare tire hangs under the Jeep’s body. This spot is common for off-road vehicles. You might see a tire secured by a metal cable or a holder near the rear axle.

### Open The Rear Compartment

Some Jeep models store the spare tire inside the rear cargo area. Open the back door and check the floor or side panels. The tire may sit flat or upright behind other gear.

### Look For A Cover Or Shield

The spare tire may have a protective cover. This cover keeps the tire clean and safe from damage. Remove or lift the cover gently to reveal the tire underneath.

## Remove The Spare Tire

### Loosen The Mounting Bolts

First, locate the mounting bolts holding the spare tire in place. These bolts are usually visible and can be loosened by hand or with a basic tool found in the game’s inventory. You don’t need to remove them completely at this stage; just loosen them enough so the tire can be detached easily.

Don’t rush this step. If the bolts are too tight, you may damage the tire or the mounting bracket. Take a moment to wiggle the bolts gently and ensure they’re ready for removal.

### Detach The Tire From The Bracket

Once the bolts are loose, carefully pull the spare tire away from the bracket. This part requires a steady hand to avoid dropping or damaging the tire. You’ll notice the tire comes off smoothly if the bolts are properly loosened.

Have you ever tried yanking the tire off too quickly and ended up struggling? Taking it slow here saves you time later. Holding the tire firmly, pull it straight off the mount and prepare it for use or storage.
