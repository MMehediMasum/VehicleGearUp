### Full Refunds

Walmart accepts returns of car batteries within the return period for a full refund. The battery must be in good condition and unused. Keep the receipt or proof of purchase to speed up the process. Refunds usually go back to the original payment method.

### Exchanges

Exchanging a car battery is possible if the original one is defective or damaged. Bring the battery and receipt to the store. Walmart will replace it with the same or a comparable battery. This option avoids waiting for a refund and lets you leave with a working battery.

### Warranties

Warranties play a crucial role in the return process. If the battery is under warranty, Walmart may offer an exchange instead of a return. Always keep your purchase receipt. It serves as proof of warranty validity. Understanding the warranty terms helps in making a successful claim.

### Online Purchases

Returning batteries bought online follows different rules. Walmart might require you to return it to a store. Alternatively, they may provide a return label for shipping. Ensure you follow the return instructions in your order confirmation. Double-check the return window for online purchases to avoid issues.
