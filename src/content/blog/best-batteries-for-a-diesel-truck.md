Sizes like BCI Group 94R and 48 fit most diesel trucks, offering the right power and fit. Brands such as Weize, ACDelco, OPTIMA, and MARXON provide options with warranties up to 36 months. ## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Battery Size Is Best For Diesel Trucks?



The best battery size for diesel trucks is typically BCI Group 94R or 48. These sizes provide optimal power and fit most diesel models. They ensure sufficient cold cranking amps (CCA) for reliable starts in cold weather and support diesel engine demands efficiently.



### How Many Cold Cranking Amps (cca) Do Diesel Batteries Need?



Diesel truck batteries usually need 700 to 850 CCA. Higher CCA ensures the battery can start large diesel engines in cold conditions. Batteries like Weize Platinum AGM and OPTIMA RedTop offer 720 to 850 CCA, ideal for diesel trucks’ heavy-duty requirements.



### Why Choose Agm Batteries For Diesel Trucks?



AGM batteries provide better vibration resistance and longer life for diesel trucks. They are maintenance-free, have a lower self-discharge rate, and offer superior cold-weather performance. This makes AGM batteries, such as ACDelco Gold and MARXON Group 94R, perfect for diesel truck use.



### How Long Do Diesel Truck Batteries Typically Last?



Diesel truck batteries usually last 3 to 5 years under normal conditions. AGM batteries often offer longer warranties, like 36 months, indicating durability. Proper maintenance and matching the battery’s specs to the truck ensure maximum lifespan.



### Can I Use Optima Redtop Batteries For Diesel Trucks?



Yes, OPTIMA RedTop batteries are suitable for diesel trucks. They offer high CCA, maintenance-free design, and versatile mounting options. Their sealed AGM construction ensures durability and reliable starting power for diesel engines in various conditions.



### What Warranty Should I Expect On Diesel Truck Batteries?



Most quality diesel truck batteries come with a 36-month warranty. This covers defects and performance issues under normal use. Brands like Weize Platinum and MARXON provide such warranties, reflecting confidence in their battery’s reliability and longevity.
