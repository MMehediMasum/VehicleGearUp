## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Torque Is Ideal For Lug Nut Removal?



A torque of 650 to 1300 Nm (480 to 960 Ft-lbs) is ideal. It ensures easy, quick lug nut removal without damaging bolts or wheels.



### How Long Do Cordless Impact Wrench Batteries Last?



Most 4. 0Ah batteries last for 30 to 60 minutes of continuous use. They recharge fully in about 30 to 60 minutes.



### Are Brushless Motors Better For Impact Wrenches?



Yes, brushless motors offer more power, longer runtime, and less maintenance. They improve efficiency and extend the tool's lifespan.



### Can These Impact Wrenches Fit All Lug Nut Sizes?



Yes, included socket sets typically cover common lug nut sizes. This makes them versatile for cars, trucks, and lawn mowers.



### What Speed Settings Suit Different Tasks?



Variable speed modes allow precision. Lower speeds suit delicate tasks; higher speeds provide maximum torque for tough lug nuts.



### Is A 20v Or 21v Battery Better For Power Tools?



Both 20V and 21V batteries provide similar power. The difference is minimal and depends on brand design and battery quality.
