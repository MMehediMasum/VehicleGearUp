## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Pressure Washer Psi For Car Detailing?



A PSI between 1900 and 3000 is ideal for car detailing. It cleans effectively without damaging paint or surfaces.



### Are Foam Cannons Useful For Car Washing?



Yes, foam cannons create thick soap suds. This helps loosen dirt and grime safely on car surfaces.



### How Important Is Gpm In Pressure Washers For Cars?



GPM affects water flow and cleaning speed. Higher GPM means faster rinsing but moderate flow is best for cars.



### Can Electric Pressure Washers Handle Driveway Cleaning?



Yes, many models like Westinghouse and DECOKTOOL clean driveways efficiently with high PSI and multiple nozzles.



### What Features Should I Look For In A Car Pressure Washer?



Look for adjustable pressure, foam cannon, multiple nozzles, and portability. These features improve cleaning and ease of use.



### Are Electric Pressure Washers Better Than Gas For Car Detailing?



Electric washers are quieter, lighter, and maintenance-free, making them ideal for car detailing and smaller cleaning tasks.
