## Locate The Oil Light

Locating the oil light on your 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan is the first step to understanding when it’s time for an oil reset. This small but important indicator keeps you informed about your vehicle’s oil status. Knowing exactly where to find it can save you time and prevent unnecessary worries while driving.

### Understanding The Dashboard Layout

Your Tiguan’s dashboard displays various icons, but the oil light has a distinct look. It usually appears as an oil can symbol, often in red or amber. Spend a moment familiarizing yourself with the entire dashboard layout so you can quickly spot the oil light when it turns on.

### Checking The Instrument Cluster

The oil light is located within the instrument cluster, right in front of the driver. This cluster houses the speedometer, fuel gauge, and other warning lights. If you’ve never closely inspected this area, take a few minutes to look at it while the car is off and on to identify all the symbols.

### Using The Owner’s Manual For Guidance

If you’re still unsure about the oil light’s exact position, the owner’s manual is your best friend. It provides clear images and descriptions of each dashboard light. Keep the manual handy in your glove compartment for quick reference whenever needed.

### Recognizing When The Oil Light Activates

The oil light may come on during startup or while driving. It signals low oil pressure or the need for an oil change. Pay attention to this light—ignoring it could cause engine damage. Have you noticed how the oil light behaves in your Tiguan? Recognizing its patterns helps you act promptly.

The instrument cluster buttons allow easy reset of the oil light on a 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan. This method uses simple controls found on the dashboard. It does not require special tools or a visit to the mechanic. Follow clear steps to reset the oil light using these buttons.

### Find The Trip Button

Locate the trip button on the instrument cluster. It is usually near the speedometer or tachometer. This button controls the display settings on the dashboard. Press it to cycle through the trip data and vehicle information screens. Hold it down to enter reset options.

Press and hold the trip button until the service menu appears. This menu shows various vehicle maintenance options. Use short presses to scroll through the menu items. Stop when you find the oil change or service reset option. Hold the button again to confirm and reset the oil light.




## Reset The Oil Light

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-light-on-a-2019-volkswagen-tiguan-is-essential-after-changing-the-oil-this-process-clears-the-warning-and-keeps-the-system-accurate&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil light on a 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan is essential after changing the oil. This process clears the warning and keeps the system accurate. The reset is simple and can be done in minutes using the vehicle’s dashboard menu.

### Navigate To Oil Reset Option

Turn the ignition to the "on" position without starting the engine. Use the buttons on the steering wheel to open the main menu on the dashboard screen. Scroll through the options until you find "Settings" or "Vehicle Settings." Look for the "Oil Change" or "Oil Reset" option and select it. This menu controls the maintenance reminders.

### Confirm The Reset

After selecting the oil reset option, the screen will ask for confirmation. Choose "Yes" or "Reset" to clear the oil light. Wait a few seconds for the system to process the reset. Turn off the ignition and then start the engine to check if the oil light is off. The light should now be reset and ready for the next oil change.

After resetting the oil light on your 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan, it is important to verify the reset. This ensures that the system recognizes the new oil status. Verifying prevents confusion or missed maintenance alerts.

### Turn The Ignition On Without Starting The Engine

Switch the ignition to the "On" position. Do not start the engine. Look at the dashboard for the oil light status. The light should be off after reset. If it is still on, repeat the reset steps.

### Drive The Vehicle For A Short Distance

Sometimes, the oil light resets fully after driving. Take your Tiguan for a short drive, about 5 to 10 minutes. After driving, turn off the engine and restart. Check if the oil light remains off.

### Use The Vehicle’s Menu To Check Oil Life

Access the car’s menu using the steering wheel controls. Navigate to the "Oil Life" or "Maintenance" section. The display should show 100% or full oil life after reset. This confirms a successful oil light reset.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual For Specific Indicators

The owner’s manual explains dashboard symbols in detail. It also describes what to expect after resetting the oil light. Follow any additional steps if the light does not turn off as expected.

## Manual Reset Using Vcds Tool

Resetting the oil light on your 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan manually with a VCDS tool gives you full control over the maintenance process. This tool connects directly to your vehicle’s computer system, allowing you to clear the oil service reminder quickly and accurately. It’s a straightforward process once you’re familiar with the interface and steps involved.

### Connecting The Vcds Tool To Your Tiguan

First, locate the OBD-II port under the dashboard on the driver’s side. Plug the VCDS cable into this port and then connect the other end to your laptop or device. Make sure your vehicle’s ignition is turned on but the engine is off to ensure proper communication.

### Accessing The Engine Control Module (ecm)

Open the VCDS software and select “Select Control Module” from the main menu. Choose “01 - Engine” to access the engine control module. This module stores the oil change data and service reminders you need to reset.

### Resetting The Oil Service Interval

Within the engine control module menu, find the “Service Interval” or “Adaptation” section. Here, you’ll see options to reset the oil change interval. Select “Reset” or “Clear” to turn off the oil light and reset the system back to zero.

### Verifying The Reset

After resetting, exit the module and turn off the ignition. Restart your Tiguan and check the dashboard to ensure the oil light is no longer illuminated. If it remains on, repeat the reset process or check for any error codes that might indicate another issue.

Have you ever wondered why some vehicles don’t reset their oil light after a simple oil change? Using the VCDS tool eliminates guesswork and prevents unnecessary visits to the dealer. It puts the power in your hands to manage your Tiguan’s maintenance efficiently.

## Tips To Maintain Oil Light Functionality

The oil light on your 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan is a crucial alert designed to protect your engine. Keeping this indicator working properly can save you from costly repairs and keep your vehicle running smoothly. Here are practical tips to help you maintain the oil light’s functionality and ensure it signals you accurately when attention is needed.

### Regular Oil Changes

Changing your oil at the intervals recommended in your owner’s manual keeps your engine lubricated and your oil sensor accurate. Old or dirty oil can cause the oil light to flicker or stay on unnecessarily. I once ignored this and ended up with a sensor malfunction, which was a hassle to diagnose and fix.

Stick to a consistent oil change schedule, and use the right type of oil for your Tiguan. This prevents sludge buildup and helps the oil pressure sensor perform its job without false alarms.

### Check Oil Levels Frequently

Keeping an eye on your oil level is a simple habit that can prevent unexpected oil light warnings. Make it a routine to check your oil using the dipstick every few weeks or before long trips. Low oil levels can trigger the warning light, so topping up early can avoid engine damage.

Have you ever waited too long to check your oil and then faced a sudden oil light? Regular checks give you peace of mind and keep your Tiguan’s engine healthy.






## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Oil Light On A 2019 Vw Tiguan?

To reset the oil light, turn the ignition on without starting the engine. Press the accelerator fully three times within 10 seconds. The oil light will blink and then turn off, indicating the reset is complete.

### When Should I Reset The Oil Light On My Tiguan?

Reset the oil light after changing your oil or performing maintenance. This action ensures your vehicle accurately tracks oil life and alerts you when the next oil change is due.

### What If The Oil Light Won’t Reset On My Tiguan?

If the oil light won’t reset, double-check the oil change was done properly. Consult your owner’s manual or visit a VW dealership for assistance to avoid engine damage.

### Can I Reset The Oil Light Without Special Tools?

Yes, you can reset the oil light manually on a 2019 VW Tiguan. No special tools or diagnostic equipment are needed; just follow the reset procedure carefully.
