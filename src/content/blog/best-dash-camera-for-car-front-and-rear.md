## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Should I Look For In Front And Rear Dash Cams?



Look for 4K video quality, wide-angle lenses, night vision, GPS, Wi-Fi connectivity, and parking mode for complete coverage. These features ensure clear recordings, easy access, and safety monitoring.



### How Does Parking Mode Benefit Dash Cam Users?



Parking mode records events when your car is parked. It activates on motion or impacts, protecting your vehicle from hit-and-runs and vandalism 24/7, even when you’re away.



### Why Is Wi-fi Important In Car Dash Cameras?



Wi-Fi allows fast video transfer and remote control via smartphone apps. It simplifies reviewing and sharing footage without removing the SD card, enhancing user convenience.



### Are 3-channel Dash Cams Better Than Dual-channel Models?



Yes, 3-channel dash cams record front, rear, and inside views simultaneously. This provides more comprehensive coverage, useful for rideshare drivers or enhanced security.



### How Does A Gps Feature Improve Dash Cam Functionality?



GPS logs your driving routes and speeds. It provides location context to videos, useful for insurance claims or legal evidence after accidents.



### What Is The Benefit Of A Starvis Sensor In Dash Cams?



STARVIS sensors enhance night vision and low-light recording. They produce clearer, more detailed footage in dark conditions, improving safety and evidence quality.
