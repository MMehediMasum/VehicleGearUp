## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls in a Honda Civic is the first step to changing the time. These controls are usually easy to find but can vary by model year. Knowing where to look saves time and avoids frustration.

The clock controls are often integrated into the dashboard or infotainment system. Some models use physical buttons near the radio, while others use touchscreen settings. Identifying the right controls allows quick and precise time adjustment.

### Check The Dashboard Area

The most common place for clock controls is the dashboard. Look near the radio or air conditioning vents. Small buttons labeled “Clock,” “H” (hour), and “M” (minute) usually control the time.

### Explore The Infotainment Screen

Many newer Honda Civics use a touchscreen to set the clock. Access the settings menu on the screen. Find the “Clock” or “Time” option. Use the touchscreen to adjust hours and minutes.

### Refer To The Owner’s Manual

The owner’s manual has clear instructions and images. It shows exact locations of clock controls for your model. This guide helps if the controls are not obvious or hidden.

## Adjusting Time For Different Honda Civic Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-time-on-a-honda-civic-varies-by-model-different-years-and-designs-have-unique-settings&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the time on a Honda Civic varies by model. Different years and designs have unique settings. Knowing your model helps set the clock right. This guide breaks down the process for three main types.

### Older Models

Older Honda Civics usually have manual clock controls. Look for small buttons near the radio or dashboard. One button changes the hour, another changes the minutes.

* Turn the ignition to the "On" position without starting the engine.
* Press and hold the "Clock" or "H" button until the hour blinks.
* Use the "H" button to change the hour.
* Press the "M" button to adjust the minutes.
* Release all buttons to save the time.

### Newer Models With Touchscreen

Touchscreen models use the display menu for clock settings. Access the settings through the home screen or main menu. The process is quick and simple.

* Turn on the vehicle and access the touchscreen.
* Tap the "Settings" icon on the home screen.
* Select "Clock" or "Time & Date" from the menu.
* Adjust hour and minute by tapping the arrows or numbers.
* Confirm changes by tapping "Save" or "OK."

### Models With Digital Displays

Digital display Civics combine buttons and screen controls. You may use steering wheel buttons or dashboard controls. The digital display shows the time while adjusting.

* Turn on the ignition.
* Press the "Menu" button on the dashboard or steering wheel.
* Navigate to "Settings" then "Clock."
* Use arrow buttons to set the correct hour and minutes.
* Press "Enter" or "OK" to save changes.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Troubleshooting common issues when changing the time in a Honda Civic can save you time and frustration. Some problems happen often and are easy to fix. Knowing what to check helps you keep the clock accurate. The following sections cover frequent issues and quick solutions.

### Clock Not Changing

The clock may not change if the buttons are unresponsive. Check if the ignition is turned to the "On" position. Sometimes, the system needs the engine running or the key in accessory mode to accept changes.

Try pressing and holding the clock button for a few seconds. This resets the clock setting mode. If the clock still does not change, the system might be locked or frozen. Turning the car off and restarting can clear minor glitches.

Look for any physical damage to the buttons. Dirt or sticky residue can block the controls. Clean the area gently with a soft cloth and mild cleaner.

### Settings Reset After Turning Off

Settings may reset if the vehicle's battery is disconnected or weak. The clock relies on a constant power source to keep time. A loose battery cable or low battery voltage can cause resets.

Check the battery terminals for corrosion or looseness. Tighten and clean them if needed. If the battery is old, consider replacing it. A healthy battery keeps electronic settings saved.

Some Honda Civic models have a backup fuse for the clock. Inspect the fuse box and replace any blown fuses. This can prevent the clock from losing its settings.

### Syncing Time With Gps

Newer Honda Civics may sync the clock automatically using GPS signals. This feature adjusts the time based on location and daylight savings.

Make sure the GPS system is active and has a clear view of the sky. Tall buildings or tunnels can block the signals. Time syncing will not work indoors or underground.

If the time is incorrect, try resetting the GPS system. Follow the owner's manual for specific instructions. Updating the vehicle’s software can also improve syncing accuracy.

Turn off manual clock adjustments to let GPS control the time. Mixing both settings may cause conflicts and errors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Set The Clock On A Honda Civic?

To set the clock on a Honda Civic, press and hold the "Clock" button. Use the "H" and "M" buttons to adjust hours and minutes. Release the "Clock" button to save the time.

### Can I Change The Time On Honda Civic Without The Manual?

Yes, you can change the time without the manual. Locate the clock settings on your dashboard or infotainment system. Follow on-screen prompts or press the clock button and use hour and minute buttons to adjust the time.

### Why Won't My Honda Civic Clock Update Automatically?

Your Honda Civic clock may not update automatically if GPS or radio signals are weak. Check your settings to enable automatic time updates. Manually set the clock if automatic update fails.

### Does Changing Time Affect Honda Civic's Other Settings?

Changing the time does not affect other vehicle settings. It only updates the clock display. Make sure to save the new time to avoid reset after turning off the vehicle.
