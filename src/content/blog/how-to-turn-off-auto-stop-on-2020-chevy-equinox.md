**If you drive a 2020 Chevy Equinox, you’ve probably noticed the Auto Stop feature kicking in when you least expect it. While it’s designed to save fuel, it can sometimes feel more annoying than helpful.**

You’re not alone if you want to turn this feature off and take full control of your ride. In this post, you’ll learn exactly how to disable Auto Stop on your Chevy Equinox quickly and easily. Keep reading to discover simple steps that put you back in the driver’s seat—literally.




## Auto Stop Feature In Chevy Equinox

The Auto Stop feature in the 2020 Chevy Equinox is designed to improve fuel efficiency and reduce emissions. It automatically shuts off the engine when your vehicle is stationary, such as at traffic lights or in heavy traffic. This technology helps you save fuel without requiring any extra effort on your part.

### Purpose Of Auto Stop

The main goal of Auto Stop is to cut down on unnecessary idling. By turning off the engine during stops, your Equinox reduces fuel consumption and lowers pollution. This not only benefits the environment but also helps you spend less on gas over time.

Many drivers notice a difference in their fuel economy, especially in city driving where stop-and-go traffic is common. Have you ever wondered how much fuel you waste waiting at a red light? Auto Stop tackles this exact problem.

### When Auto Stop Activates

The Auto Stop feature activates under specific conditions to ensure smooth operation. Your Equinox will shut off the engine when:

* The vehicle is completely stopped
* The brake pedal is fully pressed
* The engine is at the right temperature
* The battery has enough charge
* Climate control settings allow it

This system won’t activate if it might interfere with your comfort or vehicle performance. For example, if you’re using the air conditioning heavily, Auto Stop might delay activation to keep the cabin comfortable.

Have you noticed your engine turning off at some stops but not others? Now you know why—it’s your vehicle making smart decisions based on these conditions.

## Reasons To Disable Auto Stop

Disabling the Auto Stop feature on your 2020 Chevy Equinox might improve your driving experience. This system turns off the engine at stops to save fuel. Yet, some drivers find it less convenient or effective. Understanding the reasons to turn it off helps you decide what suits your needs.

### Driving Preferences

Some drivers feel Auto Stop interrupts smooth driving. The engine shutting off and restarting can feel jerky. This affects comfort, especially in heavy traffic or stop-and-go conditions. Turning off Auto Stop keeps the engine running continuously. This offers a more consistent and predictable driving feel.

### Battery Concerns

Auto Stop relies on the vehicle’s battery to restart the engine. Older or weak batteries may struggle with this demand. Frequent engine stops and starts can drain the battery faster. Disabling Auto Stop reduces stress on the battery. This can extend battery life and avoid unexpected breakdowns.

### Climate Control Issues

Auto Stop can affect heating and cooling inside the vehicle. When the engine stops, the air conditioner or heater may weaken. This leads to discomfort, especially in extreme weather. Turning off Auto Stop keeps climate control working steadily. This maintains a stable temperature for passengers at all times.

  


## Steps To Turn Off Auto Stop

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-off-the-auto-stop-feature-on-your-2020-chevy-equinox-can-enhance-your-driving-experience-if-you-find-the-feature-more-of-a-hindrance-than-a-help-while-this-feature-is-designed-to-improve-fuel-efficiency-by-shutting-off-the-engine-when-you-come-to-a-stop-you-might-prefer-to-disable-it-for-a-smoother-drive&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning off the auto stop feature on your 2020 Chevy Equinox can enhance your driving experience if you find the feature more of a hindrance than a help. While this feature is designed to improve fuel efficiency by shutting off the engine when you come to a stop, you might prefer to disable it for a smoother drive. Here are the steps you can take to deactivate the auto stop function, each with its distinct approach.

### Using The Auto Stop Button

In some Chevy Equinox models, you’ll find an auto stop button located on the dashboard. This button provides a straightforward way to disable the feature. Simply press the button before you start driving, and the auto stop system should be temporarily turned off for your current driving session.

Keep in mind that you may need to press the button each time you start your vehicle, as this method often does not permanently disable the feature. It’s a quick fix for those who prefer a traditional driving experience without the frequent engine restarts.

### Adjusting Settings Through Infotainment

Another method to control the auto stop feature is through the vehicle’s infotainment system. While not all models offer this option, some newer versions include settings that allow you to manage various vehicle functions.

* Navigate to the vehicle settings menu on your infotainment screen.
* Look for options related to engine or driving settings.
* If available, select the option to disable the auto stop feature.

This approach may require a bit of exploration within the menu, but it can be a more integrated solution if your model supports it.

### Temporary Vs Permanent Deactivation

It’s important to understand the difference between temporary and permanent deactivation of the auto stop feature. Temporary solutions, like using the auto stop button, require action each time you drive. They offer flexibility if you occasionally want the feature active.

Permanent deactivation may involve consulting a professional or potentially voiding your warranty, depending on the method used. Always weigh the benefits against potential drawbacks, and consider how often you truly want the feature disabled. What’s your preference—convenience or consistency?

By understanding these steps, you can choose the method that best suits your driving habits and preferences. Whether you prefer the simplicity of pressing a button or the control of adjusting settings, the choice is yours to make.




## Troubleshooting Auto Stop

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=auto-stop-is-designed-to-save-fuel-and-reduce-emissions-by-shutting-off-the-engine-when-your-2020-chevy-equinox-is-stopped-but-sometimes-you-might-want-to-turn-it-off-and-find-it-wont-cooperate&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Auto Stop is designed to save fuel and reduce emissions by shutting off the engine when your 2020 Chevy Equinox is stopped. But sometimes, you might want to turn it off and find it won’t cooperate. Troubleshooting this feature helps you understand why Auto Stop stays active and how to manage it effectively.

### Auto Stop Not Turning Off

If your Auto Stop won’t turn off, start by checking a few key factors. The system won’t deactivate if the engine temperature is too low or if the battery charge is insufficient. It also stays on if the cabin temperature hasn’t reached the set level, so your air conditioning or heating works properly.

Another common reason is that your foot might still be on the brake pedal. The engine will only restart automatically once you release the brake. If you’re trying to disable Auto Stop permanently, remember it resets every time you start the vehicle, so you may need to turn it off each trip.

Have you noticed the Auto Stop failing during quick stops but working fine in traffic? This usually means the system is actively monitoring driving conditions to optimize performance and cannot be manually overridden beyond temporary disablement.

### Indicator Light Meanings

The dashboard lights tell you a lot about Auto Stop’s status. A white “A” surrounded by a circular arrow usually means the system is active and ready to engage. When the “A” turns green, the engine is off, and the vehicle is in Auto Stop mode.

If the light flashes or turns amber, it indicates a problem. This could be anything from a sensor issue to low battery voltage. Check your owner’s manual for specific warnings, but often a service visit is required if the indicator light stays on.

Paying attention to these lights can save you time and confusion. It’s like your car is giving you clear instructions about when and why Auto Stop is working or not. What does your dashboard tell you right now?

## Tips For Managing Auto Stop

Managing the Auto Stop feature on your 2020 Chevy Equinox can make a noticeable difference in your driving experience. Knowing when to let it work and when to adjust it gives you control over fuel savings and comfort. These tips will help you get the most out of this system without feeling like it’s interrupting your drive.

### Maximizing Fuel Efficiency

Auto Stop is designed to save fuel by shutting off the engine when you’re stopped. To make the most of this, keep your stops long enough for the system to engage—usually a few seconds at traffic lights or stop signs.

Try to avoid unnecessary idling. If you know you’ll be stopped for a minute or more, Auto Stop can help cut down on wasted fuel. However, if you frequently move in short bursts, the system might turn off more often than needed, which can affect your fuel efficiency negatively.

One tip is to plan your routes around traffic patterns. If you face lots of stop-and-go traffic, your fuel savings might be less than on smoother drives. Have you noticed how your fuel gauge reacts on different routes? Paying attention to this can guide your use of Auto Stop.

### When To Let Auto Stop Work

Auto Stop shines in city driving with frequent stops. Let it do its job during traffic lights or when waiting at a railroad crossing. This reduces fuel use and emissions without impacting your ride quality.

But there are times when turning off Auto Stop makes sense. If you’re on a steep hill or in stop-and-go traffic where quick acceleration is necessary, the system might slow you down. In these cases, disabling it temporarily can make driving smoother and safer.

Also, if you use accessories like the air conditioner or heater heavily, Auto Stop might not engage as often. Do you feel a difference in comfort when the engine shuts off? Adjusting your settings based on weather and traffic can help you find the right balance.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Disable Auto Stop On A 2020 Chevy Equinox?

To disable Auto Stop, press the Auto Stop button on the center console. This temporarily turns off the feature for the current drive.

### Can I Permanently Turn Off Auto Stop On My Equinox?

No, the 2020 Chevy Equinox does not allow permanent disabling. You must press the Auto Stop button each time you start the vehicle.

### Why Would I Want To Turn Off Auto Stop In My Equinox?

Turning off Auto Stop can improve comfort in stop-and-go traffic and prevent wear on the starter in frequent stops.

### Does Turning Off Auto Stop Affect Fuel Efficiency?

Yes, disabling Auto Stop may slightly reduce fuel efficiency since the engine runs continuously instead of shutting off at stops.
