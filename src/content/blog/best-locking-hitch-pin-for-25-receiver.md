### What Size Hitch Pin Fits A 2.5-inch Receiver?



A 5/8-inch diameter pin is the standard size for 2. 5-inch receivers. It ensures a secure fit and compatibility with most hitch locks.



### Are Locking Hitch Pins Weatherproof And Rust-resistant?



Yes, many locking hitch pins like Rhino USA and CURT are weatherproof. They resist rust and corrosion for long-lasting durability.



### How Do Locking Hitch Pins Prevent Theft?



Locking hitch pins use keyed locks or tubular keys to secure the pin. This prevents unauthorized removal of your hitch accessories.



### Can One Locking Pin Fit Multiple Receiver Sizes?



Some locks, like B&W Trailer Hitches, fit 2", 2. 5", and 3" receivers. Check product specs for multi-size compatibility.



### What Extra Features Improve Hitch Pin Security?



Features like anti-rattle rings, debris protection, and double safety locks enhance security and reduce noise and wear.



### Are Keyed Alike Sets Beneficial For Towing?



Yes, keyed alike sets like TowWorks let you use one key for multiple locks. This adds convenience and saves time.
