## Jackstands And Tesla Model 3

Using jackstands with a Tesla Model 3 requires caution and knowledge. This electric car has unique design features that affect how you lift and support it safely. Understanding these details helps prevent damage and keeps you safe during maintenance.

### Compatibility Concerns

Not all jackstands fit well with the Tesla Model 3. The car’s low clearance and battery placement create challenges. Using the wrong jackstands can cause harm to the battery or the frame. Always choose jackstands designed for low-clearance vehicles. Check for weight capacity that matches or exceeds the Model 3’s weight. Avoid placing jackstands under the battery pack or soft areas of the chassis.

### Tesla Model 3 Lift Points

Tesla specifies certain lift points for safe jacking and support. These points are reinforced to handle the car’s weight. The main lift points are located near the pinch welds along the sides of the vehicle. Use the provided lift pads or adapters to avoid damage. Never lift the car from the battery case or suspension arms. Following these guidelines prevents costly repairs and ensures your safety.

### Weight Capacity Requirements

Your Tesla Model 3 weighs around 3,800 pounds (1,725 kg). This means each jackstand must support a significant portion of that weight safely. It’s smart to select jackstands with a weight capacity higher than the vehicle’s weight divided by the number of stands you plan to use.

For example, if you use two jackstands, each should handle at least 2,000 pounds to add a safety margin. Never choose jackstands rated exactly at your car’s weight; always give yourself extra strength. Have you checked the weight rating on your current jackstands?

### Material And Build Quality

Jackstands made from high-quality steel offer the best combination of strength and durability. Avoid plastic or lightweight metal stands—they might fail under the heavy load of the Model 3. Look for stands with a solid base and secure locking mechanisms to prevent slipping or collapse.

Powder-coated finishes resist rust and wear, which is crucial if you work on your car outdoors or in humid environments. Place the jack at the designated lifting points. Tesla provides specific jacking points to avoid damaging the battery or the car's structure. Using a protective pad or puck can help distribute the weight evenly and prevent scratches. Once the car is lifted, position the jackstands under the correct jacking points. Lower the car slowly onto the stands, ensuring they are stable and secure. Double-check that the car is firmly positioned before you begin any work underneath. Does this process seem straightforward? ## Common Risks And How To Avoid Them

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-jackstands-with-a-tesla-model-3-carries-some-risks-knowing-these-risks-helps-keep-your-car-safe-and-damage-free&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using jackstands with a Tesla Model 3 carries some risks. Knowing these risks helps keep your car safe and damage-free. Careful steps reduce chances of accidents and costly repairs.

### Potential Damage To Battery And Frame

The Tesla Model 3 has a large battery pack under the floor. Placing jackstands in the wrong spots can damage this battery. Damage may cause expensive repairs or even safety hazards.

The car’s frame also needs careful handling. Using jackstands on weak points can bend or crack the frame. Always check the owner’s manual for proper lifting points.

### Preventing Vehicle Slippage

Slippage happens if the jackstands do not hold the car securely. A slipping car can fall, causing injury or damage. Make sure the ground is flat and solid before lifting.

Use wheel chocks on the wheels that stay on the ground. This keeps the car from rolling. Double-check that jackstands are locked in place and stable before starting work.

  


## Additional Safety Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=working-safely-with-jackstands-under-your-tesla-model-3-requires-more-than-just-the-right-tools-observing-extra-safety-steps-helps-prevent-accidents-and-damage&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Working safely with jackstands under your Tesla Model 3 requires more than just the right tools. Observing extra safety steps helps prevent accidents and damage. These tips keep you secure and protect your car while it is lifted.

### Using Wheel Chocks

Wheel chocks stop the car from moving unexpectedly. Place them firmly against the wheels that stay on the ground. This blocks any rolling or shifting during work. Use strong, durable chocks made for vehicles. Check they fit snugly before lifting the car with jackstands.

### Working On Level Ground

Always work on a flat, stable surface. Uneven ground can cause jackstands to slip or tip over. Avoid soft or loose soil that may shift under weight. Concrete or asphalt is best for stability. This reduces the risk of the car falling while you work underneath it.
