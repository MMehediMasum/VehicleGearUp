Yes, most holders like SUUSON 3-in-1, Jikasho, and OQTIQ are universal. They fit iPhones, Samsung, Android, and other smartphones. Adjustable arms and strong suction cups ensure secure mounting and steady video recording in various car interiors.

Suction cup mounts provide a firm grip on smooth surfaces like windshields or dashboards. Models such as REOKILY and VANMASS have military-grade suction for strong hold. This reduces vibrations, ensuring stable and clear video recording during car rides.
