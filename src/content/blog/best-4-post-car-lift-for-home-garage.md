Yes, casters increase mobility, allowing you to move the lift easily within your garage. Portable lifts with casters enhance flexibility and space management for home garages.



### What Is The Typical Lifting Height Of These 4 Post Lifts?



Lifting heights usually range from 80 to 85 inches. This height accommodates most vehicles and provides enough clearance for maintenance or storage tasks.



### How Reliable Are Hydraulic Systems In These Car Lifts?



Hydraulic systems in these lifts offer smooth, powerful lifting action. They provide consistent performance and are generally easy to maintain, ideal for home garage use.



### Do These 4 Post Car Lifts Support Extra-long Or Wide Vehicles?

### What Warranty Coverage Is Common For 4 Post Car Lifts?



Warranties vary but often include 1 year full coverage with parts replacement up to 36 months. Check specific models for exact warranty terms.
