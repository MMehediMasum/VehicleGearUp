### Are These Tapes Waterproof And Suitable For Outdoor Use?



Absolutely. These tapes are waterproof and designed for outdoor automotive applications. They prevent moisture damage and keep attachments secure in wet conditions.



### How Long Do These Double-sided Tapes Typically Last On Cars?



They provide a permanent bond lasting several years. Their durability depends on surface prep and environmental exposure but generally exceeds standard automotive tape lifespans.
