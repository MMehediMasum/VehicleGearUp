### Which Tires Offer The Best Highway Performance For Honda Pilot?



Firestone Destination LE2 tires excel on highways. They provide smooth rides, good fuel efficiency, and long tread life for highway driving in a Honda Pilot.

Choosing the right tires for your 2021 Honda Pilot is important for safe and smooth driving. The tires listed here offer a mix of durability, comfort, and good performance. All-season tires like Goodyear Assurance Finesse and Firestone Destination LE2 handle different weather well.
