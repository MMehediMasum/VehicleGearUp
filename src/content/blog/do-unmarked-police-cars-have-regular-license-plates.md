### Legal Requirements

Police vehicles, including unmarked ones, must follow state and local laws regarding vehicle registration. Most jurisdictions require that all cars on the road display valid, legal plates without exceptions. This means unmarked cars cannot use secret or unofficial plates to avoid legal scrutiny.

Using regular license plates ensures compliance with these laws. It protects officers from legal challenges and upholds transparency. You might wonder, could skipping this step create issues? Absolutely—it could lead to ticketing or legal disputes that hinder police work.

### Operational Advantages

Regular plates help unmarked police vehicles operate effectively without drawing attention. When a car looks like any other vehicle on the road, it allows officers to observe suspects naturally. This stealth is crucial for investigations, surveillance, and traffic enforcement.

Moreover, standard plates prevent suspects from identifying police vehicles too early, reducing the chances of evasion.
