## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Bilstein 5100 Shocks Ideal For Lifted Trucks?



Bilstein 5100 shocks offer excellent performance with 2-2. 5” front and 0-1” rear lift compatibility. They provide a smooth ride and improved handling for 2017-2024 F250 and F350 Super Duty 4WD trucks. Their monotube design ensures durability and consistent damping.



### How Do Monroe Max-air Shocks Improve Truck Ride Quality?



Monroe Max-Air shocks feature air-adjustable technology, allowing customized ride firmness. They absorb bumps effectively, enhancing comfort and control. These shocks fit various models like Chevrolet Silverado and Ram 1500, improving load handling and ride stability.



### Are Monroe Gas-magnum Shocks Good For Heavy-duty Trucks?



Yes, Monroe Gas-Magnum shocks are designed for heavy-duty trucks like Chevrolet Silverado 2500 HD. They provide superior shock absorption and durability. Their gas-charged design reduces fade and maintains consistent performance under tough driving conditions.



### What Benefits Do Retrue Rear Air Bags Offer For Towing?



RETRUE rear air bags provide up to 5000 lbs of load leveling capacity. They improve stability and reduce rear sag when towing. Compatible with 2015-2025 Ford F150 trucks, they enhance safety and ride comfort during heavy loads.



### Can Monroe Reflex Shocks Enhance Truck Suspension Performance?



Monroe Reflex shocks improve suspension by reducing body roll and vibrations. They deliver a smooth, controlled ride on rough roads. Suitable for various truck models, they enhance handling and driver confidence without compromising comfort.



### How Do Air Adjustable Shocks Differ From Standard Shocks?



Air adjustable shocks allow users to change air pressure for ride firmness. This customization improves comfort and handling depending on load or terrain. Standard shocks have fixed damping, offering less flexibility for varying driving conditions.
