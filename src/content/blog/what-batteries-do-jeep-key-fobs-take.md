## Common Battery Types For Jeep Key Fobs

Knowing the right battery type for your Jeep key fob is essential to keep it working smoothly. Different Jeep models and key fob designs may require specific batteries. Understanding the common battery types helps you avoid the frustration of a dead key fob at the worst possible moment.

### Cr2032 Batteries

The CR2032 is one of the most common batteries used in Jeep key fobs. It is a 3-volt lithium coin cell battery known for its long life and reliability. Many Jeep owners find that CR2032 batteries last over a year with regular use, making them a dependable choice.

If you’re replacing your battery, you’ll notice the CR2032’s size is 20mm in diameter and 3.2mm thick. This compact size fits snugly inside most Jeep key fobs without adding bulk. Are you prepared to check your key fob's battery to avoid unexpected lockouts?

### Cr2025 Batteries

Another battery type sometimes used in Jeep key fobs is the CR2025. It’s slightly thinner than the CR2032, measuring 2.5mm in thickness but with the same 20mm diameter. This small difference in size can be crucial, so it’s important to check your key fob’s requirements before buying.

CR2025 batteries provide similar voltage and a good balance of power and size. Some Jeep models use this battery to keep the key fob lightweight while maintaining sufficient power. Have you ever noticed subtle differences in your key fob's feel or battery life?

### Other Battery Variants

While CR2032 and CR2025 are the most common, some Jeep key fobs use less common battery types. Variants like CR1616 or CR1632 might appear in older or specialized models. These batteries have different sizes and capacities, so using the wrong one can cause poor performance or damage.

## Step-by-step Battery Replacement Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=replacing-the-battery-in-your-jeep-key-fob-is-simple-and-quick-following-a-clear-process-helps-avoid-damage-and-ensures-your-key-works-perfectly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Replacing the battery in your Jeep key fob is simple and quick. Following a clear process helps avoid damage and ensures your key works perfectly. Each step requires attention but no special skills.

### Tools You’ll Need

* Small flathead screwdriver
* New battery (usually CR2032 or CR2025)
* Soft cloth or towel

### Opening The Key Fob

Hold the key fob firmly in one hand. Use the flathead screwdriver to gently pry open the seam. Insert the screwdriver into the small slot or groove on the side. Twist carefully to separate the two halves without breaking the plastic.

### Removing The Old Battery

Once open, locate the battery inside. Note its position and the side facing up. Use the screwdriver or your fingernail to lift the battery out. Handle the battery gently to avoid bending or breaking it.

### Installing The New Battery

Place the new battery exactly as the old one was positioned. Check the positive (+) side is facing the correct direction. Press the battery down gently until it fits securely in place.

### Reassembling The Key Fob

Align both halves of the key fob carefully. Press them together firmly until you hear a click. Ensure there are no gaps and the case is fully closed. Test the buttons to confirm the key fob works properly.

## Tips For Prolonging Key Fob Battery Life

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-jeep-key-fob-battery-alive-longer-saves-you-the-hassle-of-unexpected-lockouts-and-frequent-replacements-small-habits-can-make-a-big-difference-in-how-long-your-fob-stays-powered&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Jeep key fob battery alive longer saves you the hassle of unexpected lockouts and frequent replacements. Small habits can make a big difference in how long your fob stays powered. Let’s look at some practical ways you can extend your key fob’s battery life and keep it working smoothly.

### Proper Storage

Where you keep your key fob when not in use affects its battery life. Avoid tossing it in pockets or bags with other metal objects like coins or keys, which can cause accidental button presses and drain the battery quickly.

Store your key fob in a soft pouch or dedicated compartment to prevent unnecessary wear. Think about where you leave it at home—placing it near electronics that emit signals can also interfere with its performance.

### Avoiding Extreme Temperatures

Extreme heat or cold can severely reduce your key fob’s battery efficiency. Leaving your fob inside a hot car in summer or out in freezing winter weather can cause the battery to drain faster than usual.

Try to keep your key fob at room temperature whenever possible. Have you noticed your key fob acting up after a cold morning? That’s a sign the battery is struggling to perform in low temperatures.

### Regular Maintenance

Check your key fob regularly for dirt or moisture, which can damage internal components and affect battery life. Wiping it gently with a dry cloth every few weeks keeps it clean and responsive.

Also, test your fob’s buttons occasionally to ensure they aren’t sticking or wearing out. Catching these issues early helps you avoid sudden battery drains and keeps your Jeep’s remote functions reliable.

Have you tried reprogramming your key fob after a battery change? Sometimes, a quick reset is all that’s needed to get it working again. Your Jeep’s manual usually provides specific steps, or you can find guides online tailored to your model.

### Checking For Physical Damage

Inspect your key fob carefully for signs of wear and tear. Cracks, water damage, or loose buttons can prevent it from working correctly. Even small internal damage might interrupt the signal between the fob and your Jeep.

Look inside the fob’s casing after opening it. Check if the battery contacts are clean and secure. Dirt or corrosion can block the electrical connection and cause malfunction.
