## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Tires For Honda Goldwing 1800?



The best tires for Honda Goldwing 1800 include Bridgestone Exedra G709, Dunlop Elite 4, and Pirelli Angel ST. These tires offer excellent grip, durability, and stability for touring and sport riding. They are designed to fit Goldwing models from 2001 to 2016.



### Can I Use Shinko Tires On Honda Goldwing 1800?



Shinko tires like the 733 front and 734 rear are mainly for Honda GL1500 models (1988-2000). They are compatible with Goldwing 1500, but not specifically recommended for the 1800 model. For GL1800, Bridgestone or Dunlop tires are better suited.



### How Do Bridgestone Exedra Tires Perform On Goldwing?



Bridgestone Exedra tires provide excellent touring performance with strong grip and smooth handling. They fit the Goldwing GL1800 front and rear. These tires ensure stability and durability, ideal for long-distance rides and varying road conditions.



### Are Pirelli Angel St Tires Good For Goldwing 1800?



Yes, Pirelli Angel ST tires offer high-performance sport touring benefits. They enhance grip, durability, and stability on both front and rear wheels. These tires fit sport and touring bikes, including Honda Goldwing 1800, making them a solid choice for aggressive touring riders.



### What Tire Size Fits Honda Goldwing Gl1800 Rear Wheel?



The common rear tire size for Honda Goldwing GL1800 is 180/60-16. Tires like Bridgestone Excedra G704R and Dunlop Elite 4 match this size. Always check your bike’s specifications before purchasing to ensure proper fit and performance.



### Are Dunlop Elite 4 Tires Suitable For Goldwing 1800 Touring?



Dunlop Elite 4 tires are excellent for Goldwing 1800 touring. They offer strong traction, comfort, and long wear life. Available in front 130/70-18 and rear 180/60R-16 sizes, these tires deliver reliable handling for extended highway rides.
