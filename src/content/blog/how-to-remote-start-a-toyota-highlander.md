### Compatible Toyota Highlander Models

Not all Highlander models support remote start. Usually, models from 2014 and later include this option. Higher trims have this feature more often than base models. Check your owner’s manual or Toyota website for exact compatibility. Some older models may need aftermarket kits to enable remote start.

### Key Fob Features

Your key fob must have a remote start button. This button often has a circular arrow icon. Key fobs without this button cannot start the car remotely. Some Highlanders come with smart key fobs that support push-button start and remote functions. Ensure your key fob battery is strong to avoid connection problems.

### Additional Equipment Needed

* A factory-installed remote start system or an approved aftermarket kit.
* A functioning key fob with remote start capability.
* Vehicle battery and fuel levels must be adequate.
* Parking in a safe and well-ventilated area.
* Some models require the doors to be locked before remote start activates.

  


## Steps To Remote Start Your Highlander

Remote starting your Toyota Highlander is a convenient feature that enhances your driving experience. Whether you want to cool down your car on a hot day or warm it up during winter, knowing how to remote start your Highlander can make your journey more comfortable. Let's dive into the steps and ensure your vehicle is ready to start remotely.

### Prepare The Vehicle

Before you initiate the remote start, ensure that your Highlander is in good condition. Check that all doors, including the trunk, are securely closed. Also, make sure there is enough fuel in the tank, as a low fuel level may prevent the remote start from functioning.

Additionally, ensure that the gear is in the 'Park' position. Double-check that the hood and windows are fully closed to avoid any interruptions. Once these basic checks are complete, you're ready to proceed.

### Using The Key Fob

Your Highlander's key fob is an easy tool for remote starting. Simply press the 'lock' button twice to ensure the vehicle is secured. Then, press and hold the remote start button, usually marked with a circular arrow, for a few seconds.

You will notice the vehicle's lights flash, indicating that the engine is starting. If you encounter any issues, check the battery level of your key fob. A weak battery can sometimes disrupt the signal.

### Using The Toyota App

If you prefer using your smartphone, the Toyota app offers a seamless remote start experience. Download the app and log in with your Toyota account details. Navigate to the remote start option and tap to start the engine.

The app also provides additional features like locking/unlocking doors and checking the fuel level. Ensure your phone has a stable internet connection for smooth operation. This method is perfect for tech-savvy users who love extra control at their fingertips.

### Safety Precautions

While remote starting your Highlander is handy, safety should always be a priority. Never start the vehicle in an enclosed space like a garage, as this can lead to dangerous carbon monoxide buildup. Always ensure the area around the vehicle is clear of obstacles and people.

Be mindful not to leave the vehicle running unattended for extended periods. This not only wastes fuel but can also pose a security risk. Lastly, always keep your key fob or phone within easy reach to quickly stop the engine if needed.




## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=having-trouble-with-your-toyota-highlanders-remote-start-its-frustrating-when-the-system-doesnt-respond-as-expected-especially-on-a-cold-morning-when-you-just-want-to-get-going-quickly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Having trouble with your Toyota Highlander’s remote start? It’s frustrating when the system doesn’t respond as expected, especially on a cold morning when you just want to get going quickly. Troubleshooting common issues can save you time and help you enjoy the convenience of your remote start feature without a hitch.

### When Remote Start Doesn’t Work

If your remote start isn’t activating, first check if your vehicle is in a safe state for remote start. Is the hood fully closed? Sometimes, even a slightly open hood can prevent the system from starting for safety reasons.

Also, ensure your doors are locked. Many Toyota models won’t allow remote start if the doors are unlocked. This is a security measure, but it’s easy to overlook.

### Battery And Signal Problems

Weak or dead batteries in your key fob are a common culprit. If your remote start suddenly stops working, try replacing the key fob battery. It’s a simple fix that often gets things running again.

Signal interference can also block the remote start command. Are you in an area with tall buildings or heavy radio traffic? These conditions can weaken the signal between your key fob and your Highlander.

* Try standing closer to the vehicle when using the remote start.
* Avoid areas with known signal interference.
* Check if other electronic devices are causing interference.

### Consulting The Owner’s Manual

Your Toyota Highlander’s owner’s manual is a goldmine of information. It details specific steps and safety conditions that must be met for remote start to function.

Reading the manual can reveal features or settings you might not be aware of. For example, some models require the car to be in “Park” and the parking brake to be engaged before remote start will work.

Have you explored your manual recently? Sometimes, a quick glance can solve what seems like a complex problem.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Remote Start A Toyota Highlander?

To remote start a Toyota Highlander, press the lock button twice, then press and hold the remote start button. The engine will start, allowing you to warm or cool the vehicle before entering.

### Is Remote Start Available On All Toyota Highlander Models?

Remote start availability depends on the model year and trim level. Newer Highlander models and higher trims often include remote start as a standard or optional feature.

### Can I Use The Toyota App To Remote Start My Highlander?

Yes, if your Highlander supports Toyota’s Connected Services, you can use the Toyota app to remote start your vehicle remotely with your smartphone.

### Does Remote Starting Affect My Toyota Highlander's Warranty?

Using the factory-installed remote start feature does not void your warranty. However, aftermarket remote start installations might impact warranty coverage.
