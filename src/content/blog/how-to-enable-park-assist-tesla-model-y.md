The Model Y offers automatic parking that can take over steering while you control the pedals. This includes both parallel and perpendicular parking maneuvers. You simply activate the system, and the car scans for available spaces, then guides itself into the spot with precision.

If your Park Assist isn’t kicking in, start by checking if all conditions for activation are met. The system usually requires low speeds and clear sensor visibility to engage.

Dirty or obstructed sensors are a common culprit behind Park Assist failures. Take a moment to inspect all sensor areas around your Model Y and clean off any dirt, ice, or debris.

If sensors appear physically damaged or continue to malfunction after cleaning, a professional diagnostic might be needed. Tesla’s sensors are sensitive, and even minor misalignments can cause problems.

### Software Updates Impact

Software updates can both improve and temporarily disrupt Park Assist functionality. After an update, it’s common to experience minor glitches as the system recalibrates.

Check if your Tesla’s software is up to date and review update notes for any changes to the Park Assist feature. Sometimes, toggling the feature off and on after an update restores normal operation.
