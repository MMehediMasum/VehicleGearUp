## Jump-start The Battery

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=jump-starting-the-battery-is-a-quick-way-to-open-the-ford-escape-trunk-if-the-battery-is-dead-this-method-uses-power-from-another-vehicle-to-bring-your-battery-back-to-life&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Jump-starting the battery is a quick way to open the Ford Escape trunk if the battery is dead. This method uses power from another vehicle to bring your battery back to life. Once the battery has enough charge, the trunk can be opened normally.

Safety is key during this process. Follow the right steps to avoid any damage or injury. The first step is to locate the battery under the hood.

### Locate Battery Under Hood

Open the hood of your Ford Escape by pulling the release lever inside the vehicle. The battery is usually on the driver’s side near the front. It has two terminals: positive (+) and negative (-). Make sure you can easily reach both terminals for the jump-start.

### Connect Jumper Cables Safely

Use jumper cables with good insulation. Connect the red cable clamp to the positive (+) terminal of the dead battery. Attach the other red clamp to the positive terminal of the good battery. Next, connect the black cable clamp to the negative (-) terminal of the good battery. Finally, attach the remaining black clamp to an unpainted metal surface on your Ford Escape, away from the battery. This helps prevent sparks.

Start the engine of the vehicle with the good battery. Let it run for a few minutes to charge your battery. Try opening the trunk once the battery has enough power. Disconnect the cables in reverse order after the jump-start.

### Prepare Portable Jump Starter

First, make sure your portable jump starter is fully charged. Check the device’s indicator lights to confirm it has enough power to jump your vehicle or run its electronics. If you don’t own one, consider buying a model that includes multiple charging options and safety features like reverse polarity protection.

Next, locate the jump starter’s clamps or connectors. Keep them clean and free from dirt or corrosion to ensure a solid connection. You’ll typically find positive (red) and negative (black) clamps, which you will attach to your vehicle’s battery terminals or designated jump points.

### Power The Vehicle Electronics

Attach the jump starter clamps carefully: connect the red clamp to the positive terminal and the black clamp to a grounded metal surface if the battery is inaccessible. Once connected, turn on the portable jump starter to supply power to your Ford Escape’s electrical system.

With power restored, try using your key fob or the trunk release button inside the vehicle to open the trunk. This method often works because it reactivates the vehicle’s electronic locks and systems. If the trunk still won’t open, double-check the connections and power status of your jump starter before trying again.
