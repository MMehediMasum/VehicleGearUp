## Locate The Clock Controls

Finding the clock controls in your Honda Civic is the first step to adjusting the time. The controls are usually easy to spot once you know where to look, but they can vary depending on your model year. Knowing exactly where these controls are saves you time and prevents frustration when you want to quickly update your clock.

### Check The Dashboard Area

The clock controls are often located near the main dashboard display. Look around the central console where the radio and climate controls are placed. Many Honda Civics have clock settings integrated with the audio system buttons or touchscreen interface.

### Look For Dedicated Clock Buttons

Some models have specific buttons labeled “Clock,” “H,” “M,” or “Set.” These buttons are usually small and placed close to the radio or infotainment screen. If you spot buttons like these, you’ve likely found the controls you need.

### Consult The Infotainment Screen

Newer Honda Civics often use a touchscreen to adjust the clock. You can find clock settings within the system’s menu. Try tapping the “Settings” or “Setup” icon and look for a section labeled “Clock” or “Time.”

### Use The Owner’s Manual As A Guide

If the controls aren’t obvious, your owner’s manual is a handy resource. It shows diagrams and detailed instructions tailored to your model year. You might be surprised how quickly a few pages can clear up the mystery.

### Consider The Model Year Differences

Honda Civics from different years may have varied clock control placements. Older models often use physical buttons, while newer ones rely on digital menus. Knowing your car’s year helps narrow down where to look and what kind of controls to expect.

## Adjust Hours And Minutes

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-hours-and-minutes-on-a-honda-civic-clock-is-simple-use-the-dashboard-buttons-to-set-the-correct-time-quickly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the hours and minutes on a Honda Civic clock is simple. Use the dashboard buttons to set the correct time quickly. This keeps your car’s clock accurate and easy to read.

Adjusting the hours and minutes on your Honda Civic's clock might seem like a small task, but it can make a significant difference in your driving experience. Imagine cruising down the highway, only to realize your clock is off by an hour. It throws off your schedule and can add unnecessary stress. Let's walk through the simple steps to ensure your clock is precise, so you never have to second-guess the time again. Whether you have a model with dashboard buttons or a touchscreen interface, you'll find the process straightforward and quick.

### Using The Dashboard Buttons

Changing the time using the dashboard buttons is straightforward. Start by turning on your vehicle, but keep it in park to ensure safety. Locate the 'Clock' or 'Time' button on your dashboard—typically near the radio or climate controls. Press and hold this button until the time display starts flashing. Use the 'H' button to adjust the hours and the 'M' button to tweak the minutes. Once you've set the correct time, press the 'Clock' button again to confirm your settings. It's a simple process, but remember to adjust both AM and PM settings for accuracy.

### Using The Touchscreen Interface

If your Honda Civic is equipped with a touchscreen interface, changing the clock is even easier. Begin by turning on your car and accessing the home screen on your touchscreen. Look for the 'Settings' icon, which is usually represented by a gear symbol. Tap it, and navigate to the 'Clock' or 'Date & Time' section. Here, you'll find options to manually adjust the hours and minutes. Use the on-screen prompts to select and modify the time. Once satisfied, tap 'Save' or 'OK' to lock in your settings. This digital method not only offers precision but also ensures you stay on top of your schedule effortlessly. By keeping your clock accurate, you enhance your driving experience, ensuring you reach your destinations on time.

## Set Am Or Pm

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-am-or-pm-on-your-honda-civic-clock-is-important-for-accurate-time-display-the-clock-uses-a-12-hour-format-so-distinguishing-between-morning-and-afternoon-matters&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting AM or PM on your Honda Civic clock is important for accurate time display. The clock uses a 12-hour format, so distinguishing between morning and afternoon matters. Correctly setting AM or PM helps avoid confusion during your drive.

Adjusting the AM or PM setting is simple and quick. It ensures your clock matches the actual time of day perfectly. Follow these steps to set it right.

### Identify The Clock Display Mode

Look at the clock on your dashboard. Check if it shows AM or PM next to the time. This helps you know the current setting before making changes.

### Use The Clock Adjustment Buttons

Find the buttons labeled "H" (hour) and "M" (minute) near the clock display. Press the hour button repeatedly to cycle through hours. Watch the AM or PM indicator change as you go.

### Confirm The Correct Am Or Pm Setting

Stop pressing the hour button once the correct hour and AM/PM appear. The clock will now display the proper time. Verify the setting before moving on.

### Save The Time Setting

Press the clock or set button to lock in the time. Some models save automatically after a few seconds of no input. Check your owner's manual if unsure.

* AM means before noon (midnight to noon).
* PM means after noon (noon to midnight).

## Save The New Time

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-adjusting-the-time-on-your-honda-civic-saving-the-new-setting-is-crucial-without-this-step-your-efforts-wont-stick-and-youll-have-to-repeat-the-process&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After adjusting the time on your Honda Civic, saving the new setting is crucial. Without this step, your efforts won’t stick, and you’ll have to repeat the process. Saving the time ensures your clock displays the correct hour each time you start your car.

### Confirming Your New Time Setting

Once you’ve set the correct hour and minute, look for a button labeled “Set,” “Clock,” or “Enter” on your dashboard or infotainment screen. Pressing this button locks in the new time. If your model has a touchscreen, simply tapping the save or confirm icon might do the trick.

Have you ever changed the clock but noticed it resets after turning off the car? This usually happens because the save step was missed. Double-checking this can save you time and frustration.

### Using Your Honda Civic’s Infotainment System

In newer models, the clock setting is often part of the infotainment system. After entering the time, navigate to the “Save” or “Apply” option on the screen. Without this final step, the system won’t remember the new time after you turn off the engine.

Touchscreens can be tricky—sometimes the save button is small or hidden in a submenu. Take a moment to explore the display fully to avoid losing your updated setting.

### Verifying The Time After Saving

After saving, turn off your Honda Civic and then restart it. Check the clock to see if it shows the new time. If it resets back, try saving the time again, paying close attention to the confirmation step.

It’s a simple check that can save you from repeated clock adjustments. Wouldn’t you rather spend your time driving than fiddling with the settings?




## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-the-honda-civic-clock-can-save-time-and-avoid-frustration-problems-often-arise-from-simple-causes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with the Honda Civic clock can save time and avoid frustration. Problems often arise from simple causes. Understanding these issues helps fix the clock quickly and keeps your dashboard accurate.

Some problems occur due to electrical glitches or user errors. Others happen because of a drained battery or incorrect settings. Knowing what to check first makes the process smoother and less stressful.

### Clock Not Responding

The clock may not respond to button presses. This often happens if the vehicle’s electrical system has a fault. Check the fuse related to the clock display. Replace it if blown.

Sometimes, the buttons might be stuck or dirty. Clean around the buttons gently with a soft cloth. Avoid using water or harsh cleaners. Also, try pressing the buttons firmly but carefully.

Resetting the car’s system by turning off the engine and restarting can help. If the clock still does not respond, the display unit might need professional inspection.

### Time Resets After Engine Off

The clock resetting after the engine turns off usually points to battery issues. The car battery might be weak or disconnected. Check battery connections for tightness and corrosion. Clean the terminals if needed.

A drained backup battery inside the clock module can cause the time reset. This backup battery stores time when the engine is off. If faulty, it requires replacement by a technician.

Software glitches can also reset the clock. Updating the car’s firmware at a dealership might fix this. Regular maintenance ensures the clock keeps correct time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Clock On A Honda Civic?

To change the clock, press the "Clock" or "Menu" button on your dashboard. Use the tuning knob or arrow buttons to adjust hours and minutes. Confirm settings by pressing the "Clock" or "Set" button again.

### Can I Change The Clock Without The Car Running?

Yes, you can adjust the clock with the ignition in the "Accessory" or "On" position. The engine does not need to be running to change the clock settings.

### Why Is My Honda Civic Clock Not Updating?

If your clock isn’t updating, check for a blown fuse or a dead car battery. Also, ensure you followed the correct clock-setting procedure for your model year.

### Does Resetting The Clock Affect Other Car Settings?

No, changing the clock on a Honda Civic does not reset radio presets or other settings. It only updates the time displayed on the dashboard.
