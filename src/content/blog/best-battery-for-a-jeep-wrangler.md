## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Battery Type For Jeep Wrangler?



AGM batteries are the best choice for Jeep Wranglers. They offer strong starting power, are maintenance-free, and resist vibration. Models like ACDelco Gold 94RAGM and OPTIMA RedTop provide reliable performance and long life, ideal for off-road and daily driving.



### How Many Cold Cranking Amps (cca) Does A Jeep Wrangler Battery Need?



A Jeep Wrangler battery should have at least 700 CCA for reliable starts. Batteries like OPTIMA YellowTop provide 800 CCA, ensuring excellent cold-weather performance. Higher CCA batteries improve engine starting in low temperatures and tough conditions.



### Are Auxiliary Batteries Necessary For Jeep Wranglers?



Auxiliary batteries like Mighty Max MM-AUX14 help power accessories without draining the main battery. They are useful for Jeeps with extra electronics, winches, or camping gear. An auxiliary battery increases overall power capacity and preserves the primary battery’s lifespan.



### How Long Do Jeep Wrangler Batteries Typically Last?



Jeep Wrangler batteries usually last between 3 to 5 years. AGM batteries often come with a 36-month warranty, ensuring durability. Regular maintenance and avoiding deep discharges can extend battery life significantly.



### Can I Replace My Jeep Wrangler Key Fob Battery Myself?



Yes, you can easily replace Jeep Wrangler key fob batteries. The CR2450 battery fits models from 2018-2022. Replacement kits come with step-by-step guides, making it a quick, cost-effective fix to keep your remote working perfectly.
