## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Grease Is Best For Boat Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Marine-grade greases like STAR BRITE and Lucas Oil 10320 are best. They resist water and high temperatures. These greases protect bearings from corrosion and wear, ensuring longer trailer life.



### How Often Should I Grease Boat Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Grease boat trailer bearings every 12 months or 1,000 miles. More frequent greasing is needed if exposed to water regularly. Proper maintenance prevents bearing failure and improves performance.



### Can I Use Automotive Grease On Boat Trailer Bearings?



No, automotive grease lacks water resistance. Use marine-grade grease like Sta-Lube SL3121 or Quicksilver 8M0071838. These greases withstand water and harsh marine conditions better.



### Are Lithium Complex Greases Good For Trailer Bearings?



Yes, lithium complex greases like the Wheel Bearing Grease Tube offer high temperature tolerance and water resistance. They provide excellent protection for marine and trailer bearings.



### What Grease Consistency Is Best For Boat Trailer Bearings?



NLGI 2 consistency grease is ideal. It offers the right thickness for good lubrication and stays in place under pressure. Most marine greases listed meet this standard.



### Is Waterproof Grease Necessary For Boat Trailer Bearings?



Absolutely. Waterproof greases like Better Boat Marine Grease prevent water intrusion. This protects bearings from rust, corrosion, and premature failure during marine use.
