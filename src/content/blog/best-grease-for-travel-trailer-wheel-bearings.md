Different greases suit various axle sizes and load capacities, so picking the right product matters. Products like STAR BRITE Marine Grade Grease and AtomLube Ultra Heavy Duty Red Grease offer strong protection against water and high temperatures.



Using compatible seals and plugs, such as SUNCARACCL rubber grease plugs or RigsNParts grease seals, ensures grease stays in place. Regular maintenance with the correct grease helps avoid wheel failure and keeps your trailer safe on every trip.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Grease Is Best For Travel Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Marine-grade lithium grease like STAR BRITE is ideal. It resists water, protects from corrosion, and handles high heat well. This ensures long-lasting lubrication and prevents bearing wear during travel.



### How Often Should I Grease Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Grease your wheel bearings at least once per season or every 12 months. Frequent checks during long trips help avoid bearing failure and extend their lifespan.



### Can I Use Automotive Grease For Trailer Bearings?



Yes, but choose heavy-duty, high-temp lithium grease like AtomLube or RED & TACKY brands. They offer water resistance and durability needed for trailer wheel bearings.



### What Is The Purpose Of Trailer Hub Grease Seals?



Grease seals like those from Transcom prevent dirt, water, and debris from entering bearings. They maintain lubrication inside the hub and extend bearing life.



### Do I Need Grease Caps Or Plugs On Trailer Hubs?



Yes, grease caps or plugs such as SUNCARACCL protect the grease inside the hub. They keep contaminants out and maintain proper lubrication for wheel bearings.



### How Do Bearing Protectors Improve Trailer Maintenance?



Bearing protectors like REESE Towpower reduce bearing wear by preventing water and dirt intrusion. They simplify greasing and enhance bearing protection during travel.
