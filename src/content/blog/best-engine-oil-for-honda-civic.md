For synthetic oils like Mobil 1 Extended Performance 0W-20, change every 7,500 to 10,000 miles. Regular changes maintain engine health and improve longevity. Always follow your Honda Civic owner's manual for specific intervals.



### Are Oem Oil Filters Better For Honda Civic?



Yes, Genuine Honda 15400-PLM-A02 oil filters ensure perfect fit and filtration. OEM filters protect your engine better by trapping contaminants effectively. They are designed specifically for Honda engines, ensuring optimal performance.



### Can I Use 5w-20 Oil In My Honda Civic?



Yes, 5W-20 oils like Valvoline Full Synthetic High Mileage are recommended for most Honda Civics. This viscosity supports cold starts and protects engine parts at high temperatures. Always check your owner's manual to confirm the correct grade.



### What Oil Filter Suits Synthetic Oil For Honda Civic?



FRAM Ultra Synthetic XG7317 is excellent for synthetic oil changes. It offers long-lasting filtration up to 20,000 miles, protecting your engine efficiently. This filter fits well with synthetic oils like Mobil 1 and Valvoline.
