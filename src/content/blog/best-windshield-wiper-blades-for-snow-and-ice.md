## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Wiper Blades Work Best For Snow And Ice?



TRICO ICE and PEAK Snow Beast blades excel in snow and ice. They resist freezing and provide clear visibility. Their durable rubber and winter-specific design ensure smooth wiping in harsh conditions.



### How To Choose Wiper Blade Size For My Car?



Check your car manual or measure your current blades. Sizes range from 16" to 28". For winter blades, match exact size for optimal fit and performance, like TRICO Chill or BOSCH Clear Advantage.



### Are All-season Wiper Blades Effective In Winter?



All-season blades like PARRATI provide decent performance year-round. However, winter-specific blades offer better ice resistance and durability. For heavy snow, winter blades prevent freezing and ensure safer driving.



### Do Windshield Covers Protect Wiper Blades From Snow?



Yes, covers like EcoNour and FrostGuard Pro shield wipers from snow and ice build-up. They prevent freezing on blades and windshield, saving time during winter mornings and enhancing blade longevity.



### Can Rain-x Blades Handle Extreme Winter Weather?



Rain-X Total Clear and WeatherBeater blades perform well in various weather. Their rubber squeegees ensure smooth wiping. However, combining with a windshield cover improves performance in heavy snow and ice.



### How Often Should I Replace Winter Wiper Blades?



Replace winter blades every 6 to 12 months or after heavy use. Inspect for cracks or stiffness. Timely replacement ensures clear visibility and safe driving in snow and icy conditions.
