## Locate The Clock Controls

Finding the clock controls in a Toyota Highlander is the first step to setting the time. The controls vary by model year and trim, but they are usually easy to spot. Knowing where the clock controls are helps you adjust the time quickly.

Most Toyota Highlander models have clock settings integrated with the infotainment system or located near the dashboard. Some models use physical buttons, while others rely on touchscreen menus. Identifying these controls saves time and effort.

### Dashboard Button Location

In many Highlanders, the clock controls are near the radio or climate control buttons. Look for buttons labeled “Clock,” “Set,” or “H” and “M” for hours and minutes. These are often small and placed in a row.

### Infotainment Screen Settings

Newer Highlander models use the touchscreen for clock adjustments. Access the “Setup” or “Settings” menu on the screen. Inside, find “Clock” or “Time” options. Use the touchscreen to change hours and minutes.

### Owner’s Manual Reference

The owner’s manual provides clear diagrams and instructions. It shows the exact location of clock controls for your model year. Keep it handy for quick reference if you cannot find the controls.

After setting the correct time, look for the “Save” or “OK” button. Tap it to confirm the new time. The clock will update immediately on the display. Double-check the time to ensure it is correct. Your Toyota Highlander’s clock is now set and ready to use.

  


## Set The Clock On Older Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-the-clock-on-older-toyota-highlander-models-is-straightforward-but-requires-a-bit-of-familiarity-with-the-physical-controls-unlike-newer-touchscreen-systems-these-models-rely-on-manual-buttons-which-some-drivers-find-more-tactile-and-less-distracting&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting the clock on older Toyota Highlander models is straightforward but requires a bit of familiarity with the physical controls. Unlike newer touchscreen systems, these models rely on manual buttons, which some drivers find more tactile and less distracting. If you've ever struggled to keep your dashboard clock accurate, understanding these simple steps can save you time and frustration.

### Using Physical Buttons

Older Highlanders usually have small buttons labeled “H” for hours and “M” for minutes near the clock display. Press and hold the “Clock” or “Set” button until the time starts blinking, signaling it’s ready to be adjusted.

Then, tap the “H” button to move the hour forward. Each press changes the hour by one, so be patient and precise. Next, press the “M” button to set the minutes, again pressing once per minute adjustment.

Once the time looks correct, press the “Clock” or “Set” button again to save your changes. If you accidentally pass the desired time, don’t worry—you can cycle through the hours or minutes until you reach the right setting.

### Syncing With Radio Time

Some older Highlanders offer the option to sync the clock with the radio's time signal, especially models with AM/FM or satellite radio. This feature helps keep your clock accurate without manual adjustments.

To activate syncing, turn on your radio and tune to a station known for broadcasting time signals. Then, press and hold the “Clock” or “Set” button until the clock starts blinking, and select the sync option—usually done by pressing the “H” or “M” button.

If your radio station provides a reliable time signal, the clock will adjust itself automatically within a few minutes. This method can be a real time-saver, especially if you often cross time zones or experience daylight saving changes.




## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-your-toyota-highlanders-clock-can-save-you-time-and-frustration-many-drivers-face-simple-problems-that-prevent-the-clock-from-setting-correctly-or-keeping-the-right-time&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with your Toyota Highlander's clock can save you time and frustration. Many drivers face simple problems that prevent the clock from setting correctly or keeping the right time. Understanding these issues helps you fix them quickly without needing a mechanic.

### Clock Not Changing

If your clock refuses to change despite following the correct steps, check if you are pressing the right buttons firmly. Sometimes, the buttons may feel responsive but do not register a command due to wear or dirt buildup.

Try cleaning around the buttons gently with a soft cloth. If the problem persists, your vehicle’s system might need a reset. You can do this by turning off the car, opening the door for a minute, and then restarting it. This simple action often clears minor glitches.

Have you noticed if the clock changes when the car is running but not when it’s off? This detail can help identify whether the issue is with the power supply to the clock or the control panel itself.

### Time Reset After Engine Off

Does your clock reset every time you turn off the engine? This usually indicates a problem with the battery or the electrical system. A weak or failing battery might not hold the clock’s memory, causing it to lose settings.

Check your battery voltage or have it tested at a local auto shop. Also, inspect the fuse related to the clock or the radio system, as a blown fuse can disrupt power flow. Replacing a fuse is an easy fix you can do yourself with basic tools.

Think about when you last replaced your car battery. If it’s older than three years, this might be the root of your clock’s troubles. Addressing battery health not only fixes the clock but also prevents other electrical issues.

## Tips For Maintaining Accurate Time

Keeping the clock on your Toyota Highlander accurate helps you stay on schedule. It avoids confusion and missed appointments. Simple habits can keep the time precise. These tips ensure your clock shows the correct time every day.

### Regularly Check Time Settings

Check the clock often to confirm the time is right. Changes in battery or system resets may alter the settings. Compare the clock with your phone or watch regularly. Adjust the time if it is off by even a few minutes. This helps prevent any errors over time.

### Update After Daylight Saving Changes

Adjust the clock after daylight saving time starts or ends. The clock does not change automatically in most Toyota Highlander models. Set the time forward or backward by one hour as needed. This keeps your clock aligned with local time. Avoid arriving early or late because of wrong time.
