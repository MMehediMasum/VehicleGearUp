## Remove The Old Battery

Removing the old battery from your Jeep Compass key fob is a crucial step to ensure your remote continues working smoothly. It’s a simple task, but handling the small parts carefully can save you time and frustration later.

Before you start, make sure you have a clean, flat surface to work on. This prevents losing tiny components and helps you stay organized. Ready to see how easy it can be?

### Open The Key Fob Carefully

Locate the seam where the two halves of the key fob meet. Use a small flathead screwdriver or a coin to gently pry the fob apart. Avoid using too much force to prevent damaging the plastic casing.

Once the fob is open, you’ll see the battery sitting inside a small compartment. Notice how it’s positioned, so you remember the correct orientation for the new battery.

### Remove The Battery Without Damaging The Contacts

Use your fingers or a non-metal tool to lift the battery out carefully. Do not touch the metal contacts with your fingers as oils can cause corrosion and affect performance.

If the battery feels stuck, gently wiggle it free. Take your time—rushing this step can bend or break the contacts, which will cause more trouble.

### Dispose Of The Old Battery Properly

After removal, place the old battery in a safe container to avoid any contact with metals or moisture. You can recycle it at most electronics stores or battery drop-off points.

Keeping old batteries out of the trash protects the environment and keeps harmful chemicals away from your home. Have you checked where your nearest battery recycling station is?

## Reassemble The Key Fob

Reassembling the key fob is the last part of changing the battery. This step ensures your Jeep Compass key fob works correctly again. It requires care and attention to avoid damaging the small parts inside.

## Tips For Battery Maintenance

Maintaining the battery of your Jeep Compass key fob is important for smooth operation. A healthy battery ensures your key fob works every time. Small habits can extend the battery life significantly.

Proper care also prevents sudden battery failure. This saves time and avoids getting locked out. Here are some easy tips to keep your key fob battery in good shape.

### Store The Key Fob Away From Extreme Temperatures

Heat and cold can drain the battery quickly. Keep the key fob in a moderate temperature place. Avoid leaving it in direct sunlight or freezing cold.

### Remove The Battery When Not In Use For Long Periods

If you do not use your vehicle for weeks, take the battery out. This stops battery drain and avoids corrosion inside the fob. Store the battery separately in a dry place.

### Clean The Battery Contacts Regularly

Dirt and dust can block the battery contacts. Use a dry cloth to wipe the contacts gently. Clean contacts help the battery deliver power efficiently.

### Use The Correct Battery Type

Choose the exact battery model recommended for your key fob. Using the wrong battery can cause damage or poor performance. Check your owner’s manual for the correct type.

### Check Battery Life Periodically

Test the battery every few months. Replace it at the first sign of weakness. A weak battery can cause intermittent issues with your key fob functions.

### Avoid Excessive Button Pressing

Press buttons only when needed. Repeated pressing wastes battery power. Treat the key fob gently to make the battery last longer.
