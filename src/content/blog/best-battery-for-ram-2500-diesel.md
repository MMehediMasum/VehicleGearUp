## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Battery Type For Ram 2500 Diesel?



AGM batteries are ideal for Ram 2500 Diesel trucks. They offer high cold cranking amps, durability, and maintenance-free use. Models like ACDelco Gold 94RAGM and Weize Platinum AGM provide excellent performance and long 36-month warranties, ensuring reliability in tough conditions.



### How Long Do Ram 2500 Diesel Batteries Typically Last?



Most AGM batteries for Ram 2500 Diesel last between 3 to 5 years. Brands like MARXON and 1AUTODEPOT offer 36-month warranties, reflecting their expected lifespan. Proper maintenance and suitable battery cables also contribute to maximizing battery life and consistent starting power.



### What Size Battery Fits Ram 2500 Diesel Trucks?



BCI Group 94R or H7 size batteries are the standard fit for Ram 2500 Diesel models. These batteries measure approximately 12. 4" L x 6. 89" W x 7. 48" H and provide 12V, 80Ah, and 850 CCA, matching the truck’s power requirements perfectly.



### Are Custom Battery Cables Necessary For Ram 2500 Diesel?



Yes, custom battery cables enhance compatibility and performance for Ram 2500 Diesel trucks. Kits like those from Atstarte and Mighty Max ensure proper fitment for specific engine models and years, improving starting reliability and protecting battery connections from corrosion and wear.



### Can Agm Batteries Handle Extreme Cold For Ram 2500 Diesel?



AGM batteries, such as OPTIMA RedTop and Weize Platinum, perform well in extreme cold. They offer high cold cranking amps (around 800-850 CCA), ensuring reliable starts even in freezing temperatures. Their sealed design prevents acid leakage and improves durability.



### What Warranty Should I Expect With A Ram 2500 Diesel Battery?



Most premium AGM batteries for Ram 2500 Diesel come with a 36-month warranty. Brands like ACDelco, MARXON, and Weize back their products with this standard, providing peace of mind and protection against manufacturing defects or early battery failure.
