**Choosing the best carb spacer for your Edelbrock 1406 improves engine performance and throttle response. Carb spacers help with airflow, fuel atomization, and heat management.**



A carb spacer sits between the carburetor and intake manifold. It changes the distance and airflow path to enhance engine efficiency. For the Edelbrock 1406 carburetor, the right spacer balances power and fuel economy. Different materials like aluminum or phenolic affect heat insulation and durability.



Some spacers have open centers for better airflow, while others act as heat insulators to reduce fuel vaporization. Sizes range from 1 inch to 2 inches tall, affecting throttle response and torque. Choosing a spacer that fits your engine type, such as Chevy, Ford, or Mopar, matters. This guide helps you select the best carb spacer for your Edelbrock 1406 carburetor setup.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Carb Spacer For Edelbrock 1406?



The best carb spacer for Edelbrock 1406 is a 1" aluminum open carb spacer. It improves airflow and throttle response while reducing heat soak for better engine performance.



### How Does A Carb Spacer Improve Edelbrock 1406 Performance?



A carb spacer increases the distance between the carburetor and intake manifold. This enhances air-fuel mixture cooling, boosting power and throttle response on Edelbrock 1406 setups.



### Are Phenolic Carb Spacers Compatible With Edelbrock 1406?



Yes, 1" tall phenolic carb spacers fit Edelbrock 1406 carburetors. They provide excellent heat insulation, reducing heat soak and improving engine efficiency.



### Can I Use A Carburetor Spacer Adapter With Edelbrock 1406?



Yes, carburetor spacer adapters for Holley 4BBL and Carter AFB fit Edelbrock 1406. They help mount various carburetor types securely and optimize performance.



### What Are The Benefits Of A Heat Insulator Gasket Spacer?



Heat insulator gasket spacers prevent heat transfer to the carburetor. This minimizes fuel vaporization and improves cold starts, making them ideal for Edelbrock 1406 engines.



### Is An Aluminum Carb Spacer Better Than Phenolic For Edelbrock 1406?



Aluminum spacers offer durability and better heat dissipation, while phenolic spacers provide superior insulation. Choice depends on your performance needs and engine setup.
