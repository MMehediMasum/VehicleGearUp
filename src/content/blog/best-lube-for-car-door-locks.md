## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Lubricant For Car Door Locks?



Graphite lubricants like AGS Lock-Ease or Hillman Graphite Tube are ideal. They prevent freezing, reduce friction, and leave no residue. These lubricants protect against wear and rust, ensuring smooth lock operation and long-lasting performance in various weather conditions.



### How Does Graphite Lubricant Protect Car Locks?



Graphite lubricant forms a dry, slippery layer inside the lock cylinder. This reduces friction and prevents moisture buildup. It stops rust and freezing, ensuring smooth key insertion and turning. Graphite lubricants like Lucky Line and Choowin keep locks functioning properly without attracting dust or dirt.



### Can Dry Graphite Powder Be Used On Door Hinges?



Yes, dry graphite powder lubricants like Choowin or Horex White Lithium Grease work well on door hinges. They reduce squeaks and friction without attracting dust. These powders protect hinges from rust and wear, maintaining smooth and quiet operation for car doors and other mechanical parts.



### Are Graphite Lubricants Better Than Oil-based Ones?



Graphite lubricants are better for locks because they don't attract dirt or dust. Unlike oil-based lubricants, graphite stays dry and prevents clogging. It also protects against rust and freezing. This makes graphite lubricants like AGS Lock-Ease ideal for long-term lock maintenance.



### How Often Should I Apply Lock Lubricant To Car Doors?



Apply lock lubricant every 6 to 12 months or when locks feel stiff. Regular use of graphite lubricants like Hillman or Lucky Line ensures smooth operation. It also prevents wear, rust, and freezing, extending your car door lock's lifespan and reliability.
