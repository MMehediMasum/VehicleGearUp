## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Tire Size For Jeep Wrangler Jk Jl?



The best tire size for Jeep Wrangler JK JL is 245/75R17. This size balances off-road performance and on-road comfort well. It fits most 17-inch wheels designed for these models, providing good traction and durability.



### Which Wheels Fit Jeep Wrangler And Gladiator Models?



RockTrix 17-inch wheels with 5x5 bolt pattern and 71. 5mm bore fit both Jeep Wrangler JK JL and Gladiator JT. They offer offsets from -12mm to +12mm and backspace between 4. 5 to 5. 5 inches, ensuring compatibility and style.



### Are Yokohama Geolandar M/t Tires Good For Off-roading?



Yes, Yokohama Geolandar M/T LT255/75R17 tires excel in off-road conditions. Their aggressive tread design improves traction in mud and rocks. They are durable and provide reliable performance on Jeep Wranglers and similar trucks.



### What Offset Is Recommended For Jeep Wrangler 17-inch Wheels?



A wheel offset between -12mm to +12mm is ideal for Jeep Wrangler 17-inch wheels. Negative offsets increase wheel stance for better stability, while positive offsets fit stock suspension setups comfortably.



### Can I Use A 15-inch Wheel On Older Jeep Wranglers?



Yes, 15-inch wheels like the TACTIK Circle 8 Steel Wheel fit Jeep Wranglers from 1984 to 2006. Ensure the bolt pattern and offset match your vehicle specifications for proper fitment.



### Do Travelstar Ecopath At Tires Come With Warranty?



Yes, Travelstar Ecopath AT 245/75R17 tires include a road hazard warranty. This adds peace of mind by covering unexpected tire damage during normal use.
