## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls in your Honda Civic is the first step to changing the time. The clock controls are usually easy to find but their position can vary slightly by model year. Knowing where to look helps you adjust the clock quickly and without frustration.

The clock is often integrated into the dashboard display or the infotainment system. Identifying the right buttons or knobs will allow you to change the time settings easily.

### Check The Dashboard

Start by looking at the dashboard area around the radio or infotainment screen. The clock display is normally near the center of the dashboard. It might be part of the digital screen or a small separate display.

Look for a clock icon or digital numbers showing the current time. This is usually near the top or center of the dashboard. Some models have a dedicated clock display, while others show time on the infotainment screen.

### Identify Clock Buttons

Find buttons labeled “Clock,” “CLK,” “H,” or “M” near the time display. These buttons control the hour and minute settings. Sometimes, the clock controls are part of the infotainment menu or combined with audio system buttons.

Common button types include:

* Small push buttons marked with “H” for hour and “M” for minute
* A “Clock” or “Set” button to enter clock adjustment mode
* Knobs that can be turned or pressed to change the time

Knowing these buttons helps you adjust the clock without needing the car’s manual.




## Access The Clock Settings

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=accessing-the-clock-settings-on-a-honda-civic-is-simple-you-can-adjust-the-time-using-either-the-infotainment-system-or-manual-buttons&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Accessing the clock settings on a Honda Civic is simple. You can adjust the time using either the infotainment system or manual buttons. Both ways allow you to set the correct time quickly.

Knowing where to find these settings helps keep your dashboard clock accurate. This is useful after daylight saving changes or battery replacements.

### Using The Infotainment System

The infotainment system offers a touchscreen interface for clock adjustments. First, turn on the ignition to power the display. Then, follow these steps:

* Tap the “Home” button on the touchscreen.
* Select “Settings” from the menu.
* Choose “Clock” or “Time Settings.”
* Adjust the hours and minutes using the on-screen controls.
* Confirm the changes by tapping “Save” or “OK.”

This method is quick and uses a clear digital display. It suits newer Honda Civic models with advanced infotainment systems.

### Manual Button Method

Older Honda Civics or models without touchscreen displays use manual buttons. These buttons are usually near the dashboard clock. Follow these instructions:

* Turn the ignition to the “On” position.
* Locate the “H” button for hours and the “M” button for minutes.
* Press and hold the “H” button to change the hour.
* Press and hold the “M” button to set the minutes.
* Release the buttons once the correct time appears.

This method is straightforward and requires no touchscreen. It works well for Civics with a traditional clock display.

  


## Adjust The Time

Changing the time on a Honda Civic is quick and simple. Use the dashboard buttons to access clock settings, then adjust the hours and minutes. This helps keep your car’s clock accurate for daily use.

Adjusting the time on your Honda Civic is a straightforward task that ensures your dashboard clock is accurate and reliable. Whether you're adjusting for daylight saving time or setting the clock for the first time, knowing how to accurately set the time can save you from those occasional moments of doubt about the correct hour. Let's dive into the steps to adjust the time, focusing on setting the hours and minutes effectively.

### Set Hours

To begin setting the hours, locate the clock adjustment buttons on your Civic's dashboard. Typically, these buttons are marked with "H" for hours and "M" for minutes. Press and hold the "H" button. You'll notice the hour digits start to blink, indicating they are ready to be adjusted. Continue pressing the "H" button until you reach the desired hour. Be mindful of the AM and PM settings if your Civic uses a 12-hour clock format. This small detail can make a big difference in ensuring your time is set correctly.

### Set Minutes

Once the hours are set, it's time to adjust the minutes. Press and hold the "M" button. The minute digits will begin to blink, signaling that they are ready for adjustment. Press the "M" button repeatedly to cycle through the numbers until you reach the correct minute. This step ensures precision in your time setting, so take your time to get it right. After setting both the hours and minutes, double-check your settings to ensure accuracy. Imagine catching an important appointment because your clock is perfectly set! ## Save And Confirm Changes

After adjusting the time on your Honda Civic, saving and confirming the changes is crucial. This step locks in your new settings. It prevents the clock from resetting or displaying the wrong time later. Properly saving ensures your car’s system remembers the update every time you start the engine.

### Finalizing The Time

To finalize the time, press the “Set” or “Clock” button on your dashboard. Hold it for a few seconds until the display stops blinking. This action confirms your selection. The time will now be fixed on your chosen setting.

### Verifying The Update

Check the clock display after saving the changes. Make sure the hours and minutes match your current time. Restart your car to see if the time stays the same. If the clock resets, repeat the process carefully. Verification guarantees your Honda Civic shows the correct time every trip.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=fixing-the-time-on-your-honda-civic-can-sometimes-be-tricky-if-the-clock-doesnt-behave-as-expected-you-might-find-that-the-clock-refuses-to-change-or-that-it-resets-every-time-you-turn-off-the-engine&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Fixing the time on your Honda Civic can sometimes be tricky if the clock doesn’t behave as expected. You might find that the clock refuses to change or that it resets every time you turn off the engine. These issues can be frustrating, but understanding what causes them and how to address them can save you time and stress.

### Clock Not Responding

If your Civic’s clock isn’t responding to your inputs, the first thing to check is whether the buttons are working properly. Dirt or debris can get stuck around the buttons, making them unresponsive. Try cleaning around the clock buttons gently with a soft cloth or a small brush.

Another reason could be a minor glitch in the car’s electrical system. Sometimes, turning the ignition off and then back on can reset the system, allowing the clock to respond again. If the problem persists, inspect the fuse related to the clock or infotainment system; a blown fuse can cause the clock to freeze.

### Time Reset After Turning Off

Does your clock reset to a default time every time you switch off the car? This usually points to a power supply issue. The clock needs constant power from the car battery to keep the time, so if the battery is weak or the wiring is loose, the clock won’t hold the time.

Check if your battery is in good condition and fully charged. Also, look at the wiring connections behind the clock or infotainment unit. If you recently replaced the battery or disconnected the car’s electrical system, the clock might need to be reset manually.

Have you ever ignored a small issue like this only to realize later it was a sign of a bigger electrical problem? Paying attention to these symptoms early can prevent more costly repairs down the road.

## Tips For Different Honda Civic Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-time-on-your-honda-civic-can-vary-depending-on-the-model-year-different-models-have-unique-interfaces-and-buttons-which-means-the-process-isnt-always-the-same&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the time on your Honda Civic can vary depending on the model year. Different models have unique interfaces and buttons, which means the process isn't always the same. Knowing the right approach for your specific Civic ensures you set the clock quickly and accurately without frustration.

### Older Models

Older Honda Civics usually have a simpler setup with physical buttons for adjusting the clock. Typically, you’ll find a "Clock" or "H" (hour) and "M" (minute) button on the dashboard or radio panel.

* Press and hold the "Clock" button until the time display starts blinking.
* Use the "H" button to set the hour and the "M" button to set the minutes.
* Release the buttons once the correct time is displayed.

One tip: If the buttons don’t respond immediately, try pressing them gently a few times. Sometimes, older buttons can be a bit sticky. Have you noticed how even small changes in button pressure can make a difference?

### Latest Models

Newer Honda Civics often feature touchscreen displays or integrated infotainment systems for setting the clock. This can make the process seem more complicated, but it’s actually straightforward once you know where to look.

* Access the main menu on your touchscreen or infotainment system.
* Navigate to "Settings" or "System" and select "Clock" or "Time."
* Adjust the hour and minute using the touchscreen controls or dial.
* Confirm your changes to save the new time.

It’s easy to overlook the “Auto” time setting option, which syncs your clock to GPS or network time. Have you tried disabling this if your clock keeps resetting? Sometimes manual setting is more reliable, especially when traveling between time zones.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Clock On My Honda Civic?

To change the clock, press the “Menu” button on the dashboard. Select “Settings,” then “Clock. ” Use the touchscreen or buttons to adjust hours and minutes. Confirm the changes to update the time.

### Can I Change The Time On Honda Civic Without A Manual?

Yes, you can change the time using the infotainment system. Access “Settings,” then “Clock. ” Adjust the time using touchscreen controls or physical buttons. It’s simple and requires no manual.

### How To Set Daylight Saving Time On Honda Civic?

In the clock settings, enable “Daylight Saving Time. ” This option automatically adjusts the clock forward or backward by one hour. Make sure your vehicle’s software is updated for this feature.

### Why Won’t My Honda Civic Clock Update Automatically?

Automatic updates require GPS or internet connectivity. If your Civic lacks these, the clock won’t update automatically. Manually adjust the time via the settings menu for accuracy.
