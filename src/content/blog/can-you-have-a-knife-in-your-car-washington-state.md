## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can You Legally Carry A Knife In Your Washington Car?

Yes, you can carry a knife in your car in Washington. However, restrictions apply to blade length and intent. Knives over 3 inches or concealed for unlawful use may be illegal.

### What Knife Types Are Banned In Washington State Vehicles?

Washington bans switchblades and spring-assisted knives in vehicles. Fixed blades and folding knives with blades under 3 inches are generally allowed.

### Do You Need A Permit To Carry A Knife In Washington?

No permit is required to carry most knives in Washington cars. But intent to use a knife unlawfully can lead to criminal charges.

### Where Should You Store A Knife In Your Car Legally?

Knives should be stored in plain sight or in the glove compartment. Concealing a knife with intent to use unlawfully is prohibited.
