## Power Steering Assist Fault Explained

The Power Steering Assist Fault is a warning that appears in some Ford Focus models. It means the power steering system is not working correctly. This system helps drivers steer with less effort. When it fails, steering can become hard and unsafe. Understanding this fault helps in fixing it quickly.

The fault can be caused by different issues. Some are easy to fix, while others need a mechanic. Knowing the causes and symptoms helps identify the problem early. This keeps your car safe to drive.

### Common Causes

* Low power steering fluid levels
* Faulty power steering pump
* Damaged steering rack or gear
* Electrical issues in the steering system
* Worn-out sensors or control modules
* Battery or alternator problems
* Loose or broken drive belts

### Symptoms To Watch For

* Steering feels heavy or stiff
* Warning light on the dashboard
* Unusual noises when turning the wheel
* Vibrations in the steering wheel
* Car pulls to one side while driving
* Power steering fluid leaks under the car
* Delayed response when steering

### Turn Off The Engine

Begin by ensuring that your Ford Focus is parked in a safe location and on a flat surface. Turn off the engine completely and remove the keys from the ignition. This step is crucial to ensure your safety and prevent any electrical issues during the reset process.

### Disconnect The Battery

Next, pop the hood and locate the car battery. Using a wrench, carefully disconnect the negative battery terminal. This simple action will reset the car's electrical systems, potentially clearing the power steering assist fault.

### Wait And Reconnect

After disconnecting the battery, it's essential to be patient. Wait for about 15 minutes to allow the vehicle's systems to fully reset. Once the time has passed, reconnect the negative battery terminal securely.

### Start The Vehicle

With everything reconnected, get back into the driver's seat. Start the vehicle and let it idle for a few minutes. Check if the power steering assist fault warning has disappeared from the dashboard. ## Using Diagnostic Scanner

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-a-diagnostic-scanner-is-a-practical-way-to-reset-the-power-steering-assist-fault-on-a-ford-focus-this-tool-reads-the-cars-computer-system-and-identifies-error-codes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using a diagnostic scanner is a practical way to reset the power steering assist fault on a Ford Focus. This tool reads the car’s computer system and identifies error codes. It allows you to clear these faults safely without guesswork. The process requires a scanner compatible with your vehicle’s OBD-II port. A scanner simplifies troubleshooting and helps avoid unnecessary repairs.

### Connecting The Scanner

Locate the OBD-II port under the dashboard on the driver’s side. Plug the diagnostic scanner into this port firmly. Turn the car ignition to the “On” position without starting the engine. Wait for the scanner to power up and establish communication with the vehicle. Follow the scanner’s menu to select “Ford” or “Automatic” if available. This connection lets the scanner read the fault codes stored in the power steering system.

### Clearing The Fault Codes

After the scanner displays the error codes, select the option to clear or erase them. Confirm the action when prompted by the device. Clearing the codes resets the car’s computer memory related to power steering assist. Turn off the ignition and disconnect the scanner. Start the engine and check if the power steering light has gone off. This reset often resolves minor faults and restores normal steering assist function.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset Power Steering Assist Fault Ford Focus?

To reset the power steering assist fault, turn off the engine. Restart the car and steer fully left to right. This recalibrates the steering system and clears the fault message in many cases.

### What Causes Power Steering Assist Fault In Ford Focus?

Common causes include low power steering fluid, faulty sensors, or electrical issues. Sometimes a software glitch triggers the fault. Proper diagnosis is essential to identify the exact reason.

### Can I Drive With Power Steering Assist Fault Active?

You can drive, but steering may feel heavier. It’s safer to address the issue promptly. Ignoring the fault can lead to steering difficulties and possible damage.

### Does Resetting Power Steering Assist Require Special Tools?

No special tools are needed for basic reset procedures. However, advanced diagnostics might require an OBD2 scanner or Ford-specific software. Simple resets often involve just cycling the ignition and steering.
