**Finding the best cold air intake for your 3.5 Ecoboost can improve your truck’s power and efficiency. A quality intake boosts airflow, helping your engine breathe better.**



Cold air intakes replace the factory air filter and tubing with parts that allow cooler, denser air into the engine. Cooler air means more oxygen, which helps the engine produce more power and torque. This upgrade can also improve fuel economy and engine sound.



Many cold air intakes come with reusable filters and heat shields to protect the air from engine heat. Some popular options for the 3. 5 Ecoboost include K&N, Roush, aFe Power, and Volant. Choosing the right system depends on your truck’s model year and your performance goals.
