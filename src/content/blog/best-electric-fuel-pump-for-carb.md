## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Electric Fuel Pump For Carburetors?



The best electric fuel pump for carburetors offers low pressure between 3-9 PSI. It supports gasoline and diesel engines, ensuring smooth fuel flow and efficient engine performance.



### How Do I Choose The Right Fuel Pump Pressure?



Choose a fuel pump with pressure matching your carburetor’s specifications, usually between 2. 5 to 9 PSI. Too high pressure can damage the carburetor, while too low causes poor engine performance.



### Are Universal Electric Fuel Pumps Compatible With Lawn Mowers?



Yes, universal electric fuel pumps with 12V power and low-pressure ratings fit most lawn mowers. They often include fuel filters and 5/16" fuel lines for easy installation.



### What Flow Rate Should A Carburetor Electric Fuel Pump Have?



Ideal flow rates range from 30 to 110 GPH, depending on engine size. Higher flow suits larger engines, while smaller engines work well with pumps around 30-35 GPH.



### Do Electric Fuel Pumps Come With Fuel Filters Included?



Many electric fuel pump kits include built-in fuel filters to protect the carburetor from debris. This helps maintain engine health and prolongs the pump’s life.



### Can Electric Fuel Pumps Handle Both Gasoline And Diesel Engines?



Yes, certain universal electric fuel pumps are designed to work with gasoline and diesel. Always check pump specifications to ensure compatibility with your engine type.
