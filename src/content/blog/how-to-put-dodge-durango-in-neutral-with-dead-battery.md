## Locate The Shift Lock Override

Locating the shift lock override is essential to put your Dodge Durango in neutral when the battery is dead. This feature allows you to manually bypass the shift lock system. It helps move the gear lever without power from the battery.

The shift lock override is usually a small slot near the gear shift. Finding it is the first step to releasing the gear lever safely. Knowing its location saves time and avoids damage to the car’s interior.

### Find The Shift Lock Slot

Look closely around the gear shift lever area. The shift lock slot often appears as a small rectangular cover. It may blend in with the surrounding panel but stands out by its shape or slight indentation.

Use your fingers to feel for a cover that can be gently removed or pressed. Some models have a plastic cap that needs to be popped off to reveal the slot. The slot is where you insert a key or tool to release the lock.

### Tools You Might Need

* Flathead screwdriver or a small pry tool to remove the cover
* A car key or a similar flat object to press inside the slot
* A flashlight to see the slot clearly in low light

Having these tools ready makes the process faster. Use a gentle touch to avoid damaging the shift lock cover or nearby parts.

### Remove The Cover Panel

Locate the small cover panel near the gear shift on the center console. It often looks like a plastic cap or a small rectangle. Use a flathead screwdriver or a similar tool to gently pry it off. Be careful not to scratch or damage the surrounding area. Removing this cover reveals the override slot underneath.

### Insert The Override Tool

Use the key or a small screwdriver to insert into the exposed slot. Push down firmly while holding the brake pedal. This action releases the shift lock mechanism. While pressing, move the gear shift out of the park position. Now, the vehicle can be shifted into neutral even with a dead battery.

Alternative methods help you move your Dodge Durango if the battery is dead. These methods do not rely on the key turning the ignition. They offer practical solutions to shift the vehicle into neutral safely.

### Jump Starting The Battery

Jump starting the battery can give your Durango enough power to shift into neutral. Use jumper cables and a working vehicle or a jump starter pack. Connect the positive cable to the positive battery terminal. Attach the negative cable to a metal ground on the Durango. Start the working vehicle or the jump starter. Let it run for a few minutes to charge the dead battery. Try shifting your Durango into neutral once it has some power.

### Using A Tow Mode

Some Dodge Durangos have a tow mode that allows the vehicle to roll without engine power. Check the owner’s manual to find out if your model supports this feature. Tow mode usually disables the parking brake and allows the transmission to shift into neutral. Activate tow mode using the specific procedure in your manual. This method helps move the vehicle without needing the battery to power the shift.

Working around a vehicle with a dead battery demands caution. Make sure the parking brake is engaged to prevent any unexpected movement. If you need to push the vehicle, have someone assist you and communicate clearly to avoid accidents.
