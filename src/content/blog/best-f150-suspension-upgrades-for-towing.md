Upgraded suspension kits add support, reduce sway, and improve ride quality. Options include heavy-duty leaf springs, air bag kits, and helper springs designed for specific F150 models from 2003 to 2025.



These parts boost load capacity and maintain proper ride height. Choosing the correct suspension upgrade ensures better control, safer braking, and longer vehicle life. ## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Suspension Upgrades Improve F150 Towing Capacity?



Upgrading to heavy-duty leaf springs or airbag kits enhances load support. These upgrades reduce sagging and improve towing stability. Products like RAS® RoadActive and RETRUE air bags offer better load leveling and durability for Ford F150 towing performance.



### How Do Airbag Suspension Kits Benefit F150 Towing?



Airbag kits provide adjustable load support, improving ride quality and safety while towing. They reduce rear-end sag and distribute weight evenly. Kits like RETRUE and WINSBULL help maintain truck height and handling during heavy towing tasks.



### Are Leaf Spring Upgrades Good For Ford F150 Towing?



Yes, heavy-duty leaf spring kits increase suspension stiffness and load capacity. Kits such as BALOCEO and RAS® RoadActive enhance towing stability and off-road performance. They provide a 2-3 inch lift, improving clearance and handling under heavy loads.



### Which F150 Suspension Kits Fit 2009-2025 Models?



The RAS® RoadActive 3611-YHD and BALOCEO rear leaf spring kits fit 2009-2025 Ford F150 4WD models. RETRUE and WINSBULL airbag kits also support these model years, offering reliable towing suspension enhancement options.



### Can Rubber Helper Springs Improve F150 Towing?



Yes, rubber helper springs like the RiAiCiING system increase rear suspension support. They help carry up to 8,600 lbs, reducing sag and improving stability. These springs are compatible with 2015-2022 Ford F150 2WD and 4WD models.
