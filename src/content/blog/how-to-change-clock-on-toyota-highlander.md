**Is your Toyota Highlander’s clock showing the wrong time? It’s a small detail, but having the correct time on your dashboard can make a big difference in your daily drive.**

You don’t want to miss appointments or lose track of time just because the clock is off. Luckily, changing the clock on your Highlander is easier than you might think. In this guide, you’ll find simple, step-by-step instructions that anyone can follow.

Keep reading, and you’ll have your clock set perfectly in no time.




## Locate The Clock Controls

Finding the clock controls in your Toyota Highlander is the first step to adjusting the time. These controls can be a bit tricky to spot if you’re unfamiliar with the layout. Understanding where to look helps you save time and avoid frustration.

### Dashboard Buttons

Many Toyota Highlander models have physical buttons on the dashboard dedicated to clock settings. Look near the center console or around the radio display for buttons labeled “Clock,” “Set,” or “H” and “M.”

Pressing these buttons usually allows you to adjust the hour and minute manually. Sometimes, you may need to hold down a button for a few seconds to enter clock adjustment mode.

Have you noticed how some buttons feel intuitive while others don’t? Take a moment to familiarize yourself with these controls before trying to change the time.

### Touchscreen Settings

If your Highlander has a touchscreen infotainment system, the clock controls may be hidden in the settings menu. Tap the “Setup” or “Settings” icon on the home screen to start.

Look for a section labeled “Clock,” “Time,” or “General.” Inside, you’ll find options to change the time format (12-hour or 24-hour) and manually set the clock.

Using the touchscreen can give you more precise control, but it may require a few taps to find the right menu. Don’t rush—exploring the menus can reveal other useful features you didn’t expect.




## Adjust Time Manually

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-time-manually-on-a-toyota-highlander-is-straightforward-the-process-varies-depending-on-the-model-year-and-trim&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the time manually on a Toyota Highlander is straightforward. The process varies depending on the model year and trim. You can use physical buttons or the infotainment system to set the clock. Both methods allow quick changes without hassle.

### Using The Physical Buttons

Locate the clock or settings button on the dashboard. Press the button to enter the clock adjustment mode. Use the tuning knob or arrow buttons to change the hour and minutes.

* Press the clock button to start.
* Turn the knob or press arrows to adjust hours.
* Press again to switch to minutes.
* Adjust minutes using the same controls.
* Press the clock button once more to save.

The display will now show the updated time. This method works well for older Highlander models.

### Through The Infotainment System

For newer models, the touchscreen infotainment system offers an easy way to change time. Start by accessing the settings menu on the screen. Navigate to the clock or time section.

* Tap the “Settings” icon on the home screen.
* Select “General” or “Clock” from the options.
* Choose “Set Time” or “Adjust Clock.”
* Use the touchscreen controls to change hour and minutes.
* Confirm the new time by tapping “Save” or “OK.”

The system updates the clock instantly. It also syncs with GPS time if auto mode is enabled.

## Sync With Gps Or Phone

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=syncing-your-toyota-highlanders-clock-with-gps-or-your-phone-is-one-of-the-easiest-ways-to-keep-accurate-time-without-manual-adjustments-this-feature-uses-signals-from-satellites-or-your-mobile-device-to-automatically-update-the-clock-saving-you-time-and-preventing-errors&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Syncing your Toyota Highlander's clock with GPS or your phone is one of the easiest ways to keep accurate time without manual adjustments. This feature uses signals from satellites or your mobile device to automatically update the clock, saving you time and preventing errors. Have you ever noticed how your car’s clock can be off by a few minutes after a trip? Syncing can solve that problem effortlessly.

### Enabling Automatic Time Update

Start by accessing the settings menu on your Highlander’s infotainment screen. Look for the “Clock” or “Time” settings, then find the option labeled “Automatic Time Update” or “Sync with GPS/Phone.”

* Make sure your vehicle is in an area with a clear view of the sky to receive GPS signals.
* If syncing with your phone, ensure Bluetooth is connected and the phone’s date and time are accurate.
* Toggle the automatic update feature to “On.”

Your clock should now adjust itself based on the GPS or phone’s time. This is especially useful when crossing time zones or after daylight saving changes. Have you checked if your phone’s time zone is set to automatic? That setting can impact your car’s clock accuracy too.

### Troubleshooting Sync Issues

If your Highlander’s clock isn’t syncing correctly, start by verifying your GPS signal strength or Bluetooth connection. A weak GPS signal or a disconnected phone can prevent automatic updates.

* Try restarting your car’s infotainment system.
* Disconnect and reconnect your phone’s Bluetooth connection.
* Confirm that your phone’s date and time settings are set to “automatic.”
* Check for any software updates for your car’s infotainment system that might fix syncing glitches.

If these steps don’t work, consider resetting the clock manually and then re-enabling the automatic sync. Have you ever experienced a time mismatch after a software update? Sometimes, a quick reset is all it takes to get everything back on track.

## Save And Confirm Changes

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=saving-and-confirming-changes-is-crucial-after-adjusting-the-clock-on-your-toyota-highlander-without-saving-the-new-time-will-not-apply-and-you-will-have-to-reset-it-again&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Saving and confirming changes is crucial after adjusting the clock on your Toyota Highlander. Without saving, the new time will not apply, and you will have to reset it again. This step ensures your vehicle’s clock shows the correct time every time you start your car.

After setting the hour and minutes, the system waits for your confirmation. It is important to finalize your settings properly to avoid any errors. The process is quick and straightforward, making it easy for anyone to update the clock.

### Finalizing The Settings

Look for the “Set” or “OK” button on your dashboard or touchscreen. Press this button once to save the new time. Some models may require you to hold the button for a few seconds. Wait for the display to stop blinking, indicating the time is saved.

Do not turn off the car before saving the time. Turning off early might erase your changes. Always confirm the changes before exiting the menu.

### Verifying The Updated Time

After saving, check the clock display on the dashboard. The time should match what you set. If it shows the old time, repeat the process carefully.

Drive for a few minutes and turn off the car. When you restart, verify the clock again. This ensures the settings are stored in the vehicle’s memory.

## Tips For Different Model Years

Changing the clock on a Toyota Highlander varies by model year. Different years have different controls and menus. Understanding these differences makes the process easier and faster.

Some older models use manual buttons on the dashboard. Newer models rely on touchscreen menus or voice commands. Knowing the right method helps avoid frustration.

### Older Models

Older Toyota Highlander models usually have physical buttons near the clock display. Look for buttons labeled “H” and “M” for hour and minute adjustments. Press and hold the “Clock” or “Set” button before changing the time. The clock may be part of the radio or dashboard panel. Adjust the hour first, then the minutes. Release the button to save the new time.

* Find “Clock” or “Set” button on dashboard.
* Press and hold the button.
* Use “H” and “M” buttons to change time.
* Release button to save settings.

### Latest Versions

New Toyota Highlanders often have touchscreens and digital displays. Access the clock settings through the main menu on the screen. Tap “Settings” then “Clock” or “Time.” You can adjust the time manually or set it to sync with GPS or phone data. Some models allow voice commands to change the clock. Follow on-screen prompts to confirm your changes.

* Tap “Settings” on the touchscreen.
* Select “Clock” or “Time.”
* Adjust manually or sync with GPS/phone.
* Use voice command if available.
* Confirm changes on screen.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Set The Clock On A Toyota Highlander?

To set the clock, press the “Clock” button on the dashboard. Use the “Hour” and “Minute” buttons to adjust the time. Finally, press “Clock” again to save the settings.

### Can I Change The Clock Using The Touchscreen Display?

Yes, on newer Highlander models, access the clock settings via the infotainment menu. Select “Settings,” then “Clock,” and adjust time digitally.

### Why Won’t My Toyota Highlander Clock Update Automatically?

Automatic clock updates require GPS or a connected smartphone. If not connected, the clock must be set manually to show correct time.

### Is It Possible To Switch Between 12-hour And 24-hour Formats?

Yes, you can toggle between 12-hour and 24-hour formats. Find this option in the clock settings through the infotainment system or dashboard controls.
