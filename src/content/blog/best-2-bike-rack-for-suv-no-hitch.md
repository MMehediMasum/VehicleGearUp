This guide covers top 2-bike racks that do not require a hitch. It includes reliable options like the Allen Sports Deluxe and Thule Passage racks. Each model offers strong support, easy setup, and good bike protection. Choose the right rack to enjoy your rides without hassle or damage to your SUV.
