## Performance Specs

The new Honda Civic Type R delivers thrilling performance with impressive specs. It combines power, speed, and control to excite driving enthusiasts. Every detail in its design aims to boost driving dynamics and overall fun.

### Engine And Power Output

The heart of the Civic Type R is a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine. It produces 315 horsepower and 310 lb-ft of torque. This engine offers a strong balance of power and efficiency. It roars with aggressive sound, enhancing the sporty feel.

### Acceleration And Top Speed

The Civic Type R sprints from 0 to 60 mph in just 5.0 seconds. Its top speed reaches an impressive 169 mph. These numbers place it among the fastest in its class. Quick acceleration and high top speed make it ideal for both street and track.

### Transmission And Drivetrain

A six-speed manual transmission sends power to the front wheels. It features rev-matching technology for smooth gear shifts. The front-wheel-drive setup uses a limited-slip differential to improve traction. This drivetrain combination ensures sharp handling and control on curves.
