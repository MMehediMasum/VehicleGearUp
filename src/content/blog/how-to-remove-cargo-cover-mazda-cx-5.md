## Release The Cover From Side Slots

### Locate The Side Slots

Look near the rear seats on both sides. The slots are usually built into the vehicle’s interior trim. They hold the ends of the cargo cover firmly in place. Make sure you can reach them comfortably before trying to release the cover.

### Grip The Cover Ends Firmly

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Removing the cargo cover from your Mazda CX-5 should be straightforward, but sometimes you might hit a snag. Troubleshooting common issues can save you time and frustration. Let’s look at some typical problems and how you can fix them quickly.

### Dealing With A Stuck Cargo Cover

If your cargo cover feels stuck or won’t budge, don’t force it. Often, dirt or debris gets into the slots where the cover locks into place. Try gently wiggling the cover while pulling it out, and check for any visible obstructions.

Sometimes, a bit of cleaning with a soft cloth can help. If you notice something jammed inside, a small screwdriver or a pair of tweezers can carefully remove the blockage. Have you checked both sides of the cover for hidden catches?

### Difficulty Releasing The Cover Hooks

The hooks that secure the cargo cover might be stiff or hard to release. This usually happens if the hooks haven’t been moved in a while or if they’re slightly bent. Apply light pressure and try moving the hooks back and forth to loosen them.

Lubricating the hooks with a spray designed for car parts can also make a difference. Just be careful not to use too much, as it might stain your vehicle's interior. Have you tried using your fingers to feel if the hooks are catching on something?

### Cover Not Sliding Smoothly

If the cargo cover doesn’t slide smoothly when you try to remove it, check the rails it moves along. Dirt, small stones, or grime can build up inside these rails over time. Cleaning them out with a vacuum or a damp cloth often restores smooth movement.

Sometimes, the rails can become misaligned. Gently pressing or adjusting them can help the cover slide out without resistance. Have you inspected the rails carefully for any damage or warping?

### Cover Springs Or Mechanism Not Working Properly

Some Mazda CX-5 cargo covers use springs or retractable mechanisms that may wear out or get stuck. If the cover doesn’t retract properly or feels loose, the spring might be damaged or out of place. Try manually pulling the cover to its full length and then releasing it slowly to test the mechanism.

If the problem continues, the spring might need professional attention or replacement. You don’t want to risk breaking the mechanism by forcing it. Have you noticed any unusual sounds or resistance when using the cover?
