## Recommended Oil Types

Choosing the right oil for your 2015 Ford Explorer is more than just checking a label. The type of oil affects your engine’s performance, fuel efficiency, and longevity. Let’s break down the recommended oil types so you can make the best choice for your vehicle.

### Synthetic Vs Conventional Oils

Synthetic oils are designed to provide superior protection and performance under extreme conditions. They resist breakdown better than conventional oils, keeping your engine cleaner and running smoother. If you drive frequently in hot weather, tow heavy loads, or want to extend oil change intervals, synthetic oil is a smart option.

Conventional oils are less expensive and work fine for standard driving. However, they may break down faster and offer less protection in high-stress situations. Ask yourself: Do you want to save a little money now or protect your engine for the long haul?

### Viscosity Grades For 2015 Explorer

Viscosity indicates how thick or thin the oil is at different temperatures. The 2015 Explorer usually requires oil with a viscosity grade of 5W-20 or 5W-30. These grades ensure the oil flows well during cold starts and maintains protection when the engine heats up.

Using the wrong viscosity can cause increased engine wear or reduce fuel efficiency. It’s worth checking your owner’s manual or consulting a trusted mechanic to confirm the right grade. Have you ever noticed your engine running rough after an oil change? The viscosity might be the reason.

### Manufacturer's Oil Specifications

Ford recommends oils that meet specific standards such as the WSS-M2C930-A or WSS-M2C945-A for the 2015 Explorer. These specifications guarantee the oil meets performance and quality benchmarks set by the manufacturer. Using oil that doesn’t meet these specs can void warranties and harm your engine.

Look for oils labeled with Ford’s approvals or API SN/SM ratings to be safe. You can find this information on the oil container or ask your service provider. How often do you check the specs before buying oil for your car?

The 2015 Ford Explorer offers two main engine options, each with distinct oil needs to keep your vehicle running smoothly. Understanding these requirements helps you choose the right oil, which can improve engine performance and longevity. Let’s break down the specifics for each engine type so you can maintain your Explorer with confidence.

### 3.5l V6 Engine Oil Requirements

The 3.5L V6 engine in the 2015 Explorer typically calls for SAE 5W-20 motor oil. Using synthetic oil is highly recommended for better protection and efficiency, especially if you often drive in extreme temperatures or tow heavy loads.

Ford suggests oils that meet the WSS-M2C930-A specification for this engine. Regular oil changes every 7,500 miles or 6 months help keep deposits from building up and maintain engine health.

* Recommended oil type: SAE 5W-20 synthetic
* Oil specification: WSS-M2C930-A
* Change interval: Every 7,500 miles or 6 months

### 2.0l Ecoboost Engine Oil Requirements

The 2.0L EcoBoost engine requires a slightly different oil due to its turbocharged design. Ford recommends SAE 5W-30 synthetic oil that meets the WSS-M2C946-A specification.

This oil grade handles higher temperatures better, crucial for protecting the turbocharger and maintaining fuel efficiency. You should stick to oil changes every 7,500 miles or sooner if you drive aggressively or in harsh conditions.

* Recommended oil type: SAE 5W-30 synthetic
* Oil specification: WSS-M2C946-A
* Change interval: Every 7,500 miles or 6 months

## How To Check And Change Oil

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-how-to-check-and-change-the-oil-in-your-2015-ford-explorer-saves-you-time-and-money-it-also-keeps-your-engine-running-smoothly-and-extends-its-life&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing how to check and change the oil in your 2015 Ford Explorer saves you time and money. It also keeps your engine running smoothly and extends its life. Even if you’ve never done an oil change before, following clear steps can make the task manageable and even satisfying.

### Tools Needed

* Engine oil (5W-20 synthetic is recommended for the 2015 Ford Explorer)
* Oil filter compatible with your engine
* Oil filter wrench
* Socket wrench or a wrench set for the drain plug
* Drain pan to catch old oil
* Funnel to pour new oil without spills
* Clean rags or paper towels
* Car jack and jack stands or ramps for lifting the vehicle safely
* Gloves to keep your hands clean and protected

### Step-by-step Oil Change Process

Start by warming up your engine for a few minutes. Warm oil drains faster and more completely.

Raise your Ford Explorer safely using the jack and stands or ramps. Never rely on just the jack for support.

Place the drain pan under the oil drain plug. Use the socket wrench to loosen the plug and let the oil drain out fully. This might take several minutes.

Remove the old oil filter with the oil filter wrench. Be careful, as it may still contain oil. Before installing the new filter, lightly coat its rubber seal with fresh oil to ensure a tight seal.

Install the new oil filter by hand, turning it until it’s snug. Avoid over-tightening, which can damage the seal.

Replace and tighten the drain plug securely, but don’t strip the threads by over-tightening.

Lower the vehicle carefully and open the hood. Use the funnel to add the correct amount of new oil. Check your owner’s manual for the exact quantity.

After adding oil, check the level with the dipstick. Pull it out, wipe it clean, reinsert it fully, then pull it out again to see the oil level. Adjust if needed.

Start the engine and let it run for a minute, then check around the drain plug and filter for leaks. Turn off the engine and recheck the oil level once more.

### Disposing Of Used Oil Properly

Used oil is hazardous to the environment and should never be poured down drains or onto the ground.

Store the used oil in a clean, leak-proof container with a tight lid. You can use the empty oil container or any suitable bottle.

Take your used oil to a local recycling center, auto shop, or hazardous waste collection site. Many places accept used oil for free.

Handling disposal responsibly helps protect water sources and wildlife. What steps will you take to ensure your oil change is eco-friendly?

Understanding the common signs and risks can save you from costly repairs and keep your vehicle performing at its best.

### Signs Of Low Or Dirty Oil

Have you ever noticed your engine running louder or feeling rough? These can be early hints that your oil level is low or the oil has become dirty. Dirty oil loses its ability to lubricate and protect the engine parts effectively.

Other signs include:

* Oil warning light glowing on your dashboard
* Dark, thick oil visible on the dipstick instead of a clear amber color
* Increased engine temperature or overheating
* Decreased fuel efficiency or strange engine noises

Ignoring these signs can lead to severe engine wear. Regularly checking the oil level and condition helps you catch problems before they escalate.

### Engine Problems From Wrong Oil

Using the wrong type of oil in your 2015 Ford Explorer can cause serious engine issues. The oil’s viscosity and additive package are designed specifically for your engine’s needs.

Here’s what can happen if you use the incorrect oil:

* Reduced lubrication leading to increased friction and wear
* Oil breakdown under high temperatures causing sludge buildup
* Poor fuel economy due to improper engine protection
* Potential damage to seals and gaskets causing leaks
