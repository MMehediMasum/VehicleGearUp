## Locate The Clock Settings

Before you can change the time on your Honda Accord, the first thing you need to do is locate the clock settings. Finding these settings can vary slightly depending on your car’s model year and infotainment system, but it’s usually straightforward once you know where to look.

Knowing exactly where the clock controls are saves you time and frustration. Have you ever sat in your car trying to figure out how to adjust something simple, only to get distracted and give up? This part is easier than you think.

### Check The Dashboard Display Or Infotainment Screen

Most newer Honda Accords have a digital display where you can adjust settings, including the clock. This is often found in the center of your dashboard.

Look for a “Settings” or “Menu” button near the screen. Pressing this usually brings up various options including the clock.

### Use Physical Buttons Near The Radio Or Climate Controls

Older models might not have a touchscreen. Instead, they use physical buttons or knobs around the radio or climate control panel.

* Look for buttons labeled “Clock,” “H” (hour), and “M” (minute).
* Sometimes you need to hold down the “Clock” button until the time starts flashing.
* Then use the “H” and “M” buttons to adjust the time.

### Consult Your Owner’s Manual

If you can’t find the clock settings easily, your owner’s manual is a great resource. It provides step-by-step instructions tailored to your specific model year.

Many manuals are also available online if you don’t have a physical copy. This can save you the trouble of searching through menus blindly.




## Access The Infotainment System

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=accessing-the-infotainment-system-is-the-first-step-to-changing-the-time-on-your-honda-accord-this-system-controls-many-vehicle-settings-including-the-clock-and-understanding-how-to-navigate-it-makes-the-process-simple-and-quick&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Accessing the infotainment system is the first step to changing the time on your Honda Accord. This system controls many vehicle settings, including the clock, and understanding how to navigate it makes the process simple and quick. Whether you’re adjusting for daylight saving time or correcting the clock after a battery change, getting to the right menu is key.

### Locate The Main Display Screen

The infotainment system's main display screen is usually centered on your dashboard. It’s the touchscreen interface that shows options for audio, navigation, and vehicle settings.

Look for a large screen with clear icons or menus. If you’re unsure, check your owner’s manual or glance at the dashboard when you start your car to spot the display.

### Turn On The Infotainment System

Start your car or turn the ignition to the accessory position to power up the infotainment system. The screen should light up automatically.

If it doesn’t, press the power button near the display or on the dashboard. Sometimes the screen can be dimmed, so make sure it’s fully visible before proceeding.

### Access The Settings Menu

Once the screen is active, find the “Settings” icon. This is often represented by a gear symbol.

Tap the icon to open the settings menu. Here, you’ll find various options related to the system and vehicle preferences.

### Navigate To The Clock Settings

Inside the settings menu, look for “Clock” or “Time” options. Depending on your model year, it might be under “System” or “General.”

Use the touchscreen or rotary dial to select it. This will bring up the controls for adjusting your Honda Accord’s clock.

## Adjust Time Manually

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-time-manually-on-your-honda-accord-is-a-straightforward-task-that-puts-you-in-control-of-your-vehicles-clock-whether-daylight-saving-time-just-changed-or-youve-crossed-time-zones-knowing-how-to-update-the-clock-quickly-ensures-you-never-miss-an-appointment-or-run-late&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the time manually on your Honda Accord is a straightforward task that puts you in control of your vehicle’s clock. Whether daylight saving time just changed or you’ve crossed time zones, knowing how to update the clock quickly ensures you never miss an appointment or run late. You have two main ways to adjust the time: through the touchscreen controls or using the physical buttons, depending on your model and preference.

### Using The Touchscreen Controls

Many newer Honda Accords come equipped with a touchscreen interface, making time adjustment simple and intuitive. Start by tapping the “Home” button on the screen. Then, navigate to the “Settings” menu and select “Clock” or “System Settings.”

Here, you’ll see options to adjust the hour and minute. Use the plus (+) and minus (–) icons to set the correct time. Don’t forget to confirm or save your settings before exiting. This method lets you update your clock without fumbling with buttons, especially helpful when you’re in a hurry.

### Using Physical Buttons

If your Honda Accord doesn’t have a touchscreen or you prefer tactile feedback, physical buttons provide a reliable way to adjust the time. Look for the “Clock” or “H” (hour) and “M” (minute) buttons on the dashboard or near the stereo controls.

Press and hold the “Clock” button until the time display starts blinking. Then, press the “H” button to change the hour and the “M” button to adjust the minutes. Once the time is correct, press the “Clock” button again to lock in your settings.

Have you noticed how some cars reset their clocks automatically after a battery change, while others don’t? Knowing these manual steps saves you from driving with the wrong time and helps maintain your schedule perfectly.

## Troubleshoot Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-can-save-time-and-frustration-when-changing-the-time-on-a-honda-accord-sometimes-the-clock-may-not-update-correctly-or-settings-might-not-save-as-expected&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues can save time and frustration when changing the time on a Honda Accord. Sometimes the clock may not update correctly or settings might not save as expected. Identifying the problem quickly helps you fix it without extra hassle.

Most issues relate to simple errors in the process or minor system glitches. Knowing what to check makes the adjustment smoother. Below are common problems and easy ways to resolve them.

### Clock Not Updating

The clock may not change due to a few simple reasons:

* The ignition is off or not in the accessory position.
* The clock adjustment buttons are not held long enough.
* Battery power is low or disconnected recently.
* A software glitch in the vehicle’s system.

Start by turning the ignition to the accessory mode before adjusting. Press and hold the clock buttons firmly for a few seconds. Check the vehicle battery status; a weak battery can affect settings.

If the clock still does not update, try resetting the vehicle’s computer by disconnecting the battery for a few minutes. Reconnect and attempt the clock change again.

### Settings Not Saving

Settings may fail to save due to these causes:

* Not completing the entire clock setting process.
* Interference from other ongoing system updates.
* Faulty buttons or touchscreen issues.
* Problems with the vehicle’s internal memory.

Ensure you follow all steps fully and wait until the display confirms the change. Avoid adjusting settings during system updates or startup. If buttons seem unresponsive, inspect for dirt or damage.

For persistent problems, a visit to the dealership may help diagnose internal memory faults or software updates. Regular maintenance keeps the system working well.




## Tips For Maintaining Accurate Time

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-the-time-accurate-on-your-honda-accord-isnt-just-about-convenienceit-helps-you-stay-punctual-and-reduces-distractions-while-driving-small-habits-can-make-a-big-difference-in-maintaining-the-correct-clock-settings&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping the time accurate on your Honda Accord isn't just about convenience—it helps you stay punctual and reduces distractions while driving. Small habits can make a big difference in maintaining the correct clock settings. Let’s look at practical tips that make adjusting and keeping time on your Accord hassle-free and reliable.

### Regularly Check And Adjust After Battery Changes

Your car’s clock can reset every time you disconnect or replace the battery. Make it a habit to check the time right after any battery service. This simple step saves you from driving with the wrong time and unexpected schedule slips.

### Use Your Phone As A Time Reference

Since most phones sync automatically with official time servers, they make perfect references when setting your car clock. Compare your Accord’s clock with your phone’s time before making adjustments. This ensures you’re always setting the exact time, down to the minute.

### Be Mindful Of Daylight Saving Time Changes

Not all vehicles adjust automatically for daylight saving time. Check your Honda Accord’s clock twice a year during these changes. Staying ahead can prevent confusion and missed appointments.

### Keep Your Vehicle’s Software Updated

Some newer Honda Accord models update their clocks through software updates. Regularly update your infotainment system to fix bugs that might affect timekeeping. This keeps your clock running smoothly without manual resets.

### Avoid Setting The Clock While Driving

Adjusting your clock while on the road can distract you and increase the risk of accidents. Plan to set or correct the time when your vehicle is parked safely. Safety first, and you’ll avoid costly mistakes.

### Consider External Time Sync Devices

If precise time matters to you, consider using an external GPS time sync device. These gadgets automatically correct your car’s clock based on satellite signals. It’s a smart investment for those who value accuracy in their daily drive.

To change the time, press the "Menu" button on the dashboard. Navigate to "Clock" settings, then adjust hours and minutes using the tuning knob. Confirm your changes by pressing the "Menu" button again.

Your clock might not update due to a dead battery or system reset. Check your vehicle’s battery health. If the issue persists, consult your owner’s manual or a dealer for software updates.
