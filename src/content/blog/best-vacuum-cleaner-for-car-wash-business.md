## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Vacuum Capacity Is Best For Car Wash Businesses?



A 2 to 6-gallon capacity is ideal for car wash vacuums. It balances portability and sufficient dirt-holding space. Larger tanks reduce frequent emptying, improving efficiency during busy car detailing tasks.



### Are Wet/dry Vacuums Better For Car Detailing?



Yes, wet/dry vacuums handle both liquid spills and dry debris. This versatility is essential for thorough car cleaning, especially for interiors exposed to moisture and dirt.



### How Important Is Suction Power In Car Vacuums?



High suction power, around 2 to 5+ peak HP, ensures effective debris removal. Strong suction is crucial for deep cleaning carpets, seats, and hard-to-reach crevices in vehicles.



### Can Cordless Vacuums Handle Professional Car Wash Jobs?



Cordless vacuums like CarCraftKit offer portability but may have limited run time. They work well for quick cleanups but less so for continuous, heavy-duty car wash use.



### What Accessories Improve Vacuum Efficiency For Car Cleaning?



Nozzles, brushes, and extension hoses enhance reach and cleaning precision. Models like Giraffe Tools Grandstorm come with multiple attachments tailored for detailed car interiors.



### Are Wall-mounted Vacuums Practical For Car Wash Businesses?



Wall-mounted vacuums save floor space and offer easy access. Devices like BISSELL Garage Pro combine convenience with powerful suction, ideal for busy car wash environments.
