Hard tri-fold covers like XTWEEX and MOSTPLUS offer excellent security. They use lockable clamps and durable materials to protect your cargo. These covers are theft-resistant and waterproof, making them a great choice for safeguarding your belongings on the 2025 Honda Ridgeline.

### Do Tonneau Covers Affect Fuel Efficiency On Honda Ridgeline?



Yes, tonneau covers can improve fuel efficiency by reducing aerodynamic drag. Covers like soft roll-up and hard folding models help streamline airflow over the bed. This can lead to slight fuel savings, making them both practical and economical for 2025 Ridgeline owners.
