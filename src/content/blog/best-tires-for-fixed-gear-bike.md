## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Tire Size Is Best For Fixed Gear Bikes?



For fixed gear bikes, common tire sizes range from 700x23C to 700x35C. Choose based on riding style and terrain. Narrow tires like 700x23C offer speed, while wider ones like 700x35C provide comfort and grip.



### Are Foldable Tires Better For Fixed Gear Bikes?



Foldable tires, like MEGHNA’s 700C foldable series, are lightweight and easy to carry. They offer better performance and easier storage compared to wired tires, making them ideal for commuters and racers.



### Which Tires Are Most Durable For Fixed Gear Bikes?



Solid tires such as SENQI and CATAZER models offer excellent puncture resistance and durability. These are great for urban riding and rough surfaces, reducing the risk of flats significantly.



### Can I Use Hybrid Bike Tires On Fixed Gear Bikes?



Yes, hybrid tires like ZUKKA 700x32C fit fixed gear bikes well. They provide a balance of speed, comfort, and traction suitable for mixed terrains and daily commuting.



### What Tread Pattern Suits Fixed Gear Commuting Best?



Slick or smooth tread tires, like WTB ThickSlick, are ideal for city riding. They reduce rolling resistance and offer reliable grip on paved roads, enhancing speed and efficiency during commutes.



### Are Tubeless Tires Compatible With Fixed Gear Bikes?



Some fixed gear tires, such as CATAZER’s tubeless models, support tubeless setups. Tubeless tires reduce punctures and improve ride comfort but require compatible rims and proper installation.

If you want something tough for rougher paths, Catazer’s solid and tubeless options work well. The CYCLEZ 27-inch tires add a classic touch with smooth rides. Remember, tire size and type affect how your bike handles and feels. Pick tires that match your riding style and road conditions.
