## Locate The Shift Lock Release

Locating the shift lock release is essential when your Ford F150 battery dies. It helps you move the gear shift out of park without power. This small feature allows manual override, so you can put the truck in neutral. Knowing its location saves time and frustration during dead battery situations.

### Check The Gear Shift Area

Start by examining the gear shift area closely. The shift lock release is usually near the shifter. Look around the base or on the side of the gear lever. It might be a small slot or covered button that blends with the console. Use a flashlight if needed to see better in low light.

### Identify The Shift Lock Cover

The shift lock cover is a small plastic piece protecting the release button. It often looks like a removable panel or cap. Gently pry it open with a flat tool or your fingernail. Inside, you will find the shift lock release button or slot. Pressing this allows you to move the gear shift without using the ignition.
