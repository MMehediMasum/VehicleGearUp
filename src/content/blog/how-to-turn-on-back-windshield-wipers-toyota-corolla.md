If you’re unsure about the exact steps or want a quick refresher, this guide is made just for you. Keep reading, and you’ll learn how to easily activate your rear wipers so you can drive with confidence, no matter the weather.




## Locate The Wiper Control

Locating the wiper control in your Toyota Corolla is the first step to ensuring clear visibility during rainy or snowy conditions. Knowing exactly where to find and how to recognize the control will save you time and reduce any driving distractions. Let's break down how you can quickly identify this essential feature.

### Identify The Wiper Lever

The wiper lever is usually positioned on the right side of the steering wheel. It looks like a small stalk that you can move up or down to activate different wiper speeds.

Try feeling around the steering column without looking. You'll notice the lever often has symbols for wiper functions like intermittent, low, and high speeds. This design helps you operate the wipers without taking your eyes off the road.

### Check The Dashboard Indicators

After you move the wiper lever, glance at your dashboard. Most Toyota Corollas have a small icon that lights up when the wipers are on.

This indicator confirms the system is active and lets you know if the wipers are running at the speed you've selected. Have you ever wondered why some drivers keep fiddling with controls? Dashboard indicators help prevent that by giving clear feedback.

## Activate Rear Wipers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=activating-the-rear-windshield-wipers-on-your-toyota-corolla-is-straightforward-and-essential-for-maintaining-clear-visibility-during-rainy-or-snowy-conditions-knowing-how-to-control-these-wipers-ensures-safer-driving-and-better-reaction-times&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Activating the rear windshield wipers on your Toyota Corolla is straightforward and essential for maintaining clear visibility during rainy or snowy conditions. Knowing how to control these wipers ensures safer driving and better reaction times. Let’s walk through how you can easily turn on and adjust the rear wipers to suit your needs.

### Use The Rear Wiper Switch

Locate the rear wiper switch on the end of the turn signal stalk or as a separate control on the dashboard, depending on your Corolla model. Usually, this switch has a few positions, such as Off, Intermittent, and On.

To activate the rear wiper, simply twist or push the switch to the desired setting. You’ll notice the wiper blade start moving across the rear windshield, clearing away water or debris.

Have you ever had to squint through a foggy rear window during heavy rain? Activating the rear wiper quickly solves that problem and helps you stay focused on the road behind you.

### Adjust The Wiper Speed

Some Toyota Corolla models allow you to adjust the speed of the rear wipers. This is particularly useful when the weather conditions are variable.

Look for a dial or additional switch settings near the rear wiper control. You can typically choose between intermittent wipes or continuous wiping at a steady pace.

Try adjusting the speed to match the intensity of the rain. For light drizzle, intermittent mode saves wear on the wiper blades, while continuous mode is better for heavy downpours.

## Operate The Washer Fluid

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=operating-the-washer-fluid-in-your-toyota-corolla-helps-keep-the-rear-windshield-clear-it-improves-visibility-during-rain-mud-splashes-or-dust&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Operating the washer fluid in your Toyota Corolla helps keep the rear windshield clear. It improves visibility during rain, mud splashes, or dust. Using the washer fluid correctly is simple and ensures safe driving.

### Engage The Rear Washer

To activate the rear washer, find the wiper control stalk on the right side of the steering wheel. Push the stalk away from you to spray washer fluid on the rear windshield. Hold it briefly to release a steady stream of fluid. The rear wiper will automatically swipe the glass, cleaning the surface. Release the stalk to stop the washer and wiper action.

### Refill Washer Fluid Reservoir

Open the hood of your Toyota Corolla. Locate the washer fluid reservoir, usually marked with a windshield symbol. Check the fluid level through the translucent container. If low, open the reservoir cap and pour in the washer fluid. Use a funnel to avoid spills. Fill until the fluid reaches near the top. Close the cap tightly before closing the hood. Regular refills keep the washer system ready for use.




## Troubleshoot Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-on-the-back-windshield-wipers-in-a-toyota-corolla-is-simple-use-the-control-stick-near-the-steering-wheel-and-twist-it-to-activate-the-rear-wipers&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning on the back windshield wipers in a Toyota Corolla is simple. Use the control stick near the steering wheel and twist it to activate the rear wipers. Check the fuse and fluid levels if the wipers don’t work properly.

When the back windshield wipers on your Toyota Corolla don't work, frustration mounts quickly. Identifying and resolving common issues can save time and stress. Here’s how to troubleshoot effectively.

### Check For Wiper Blade Damage

Start by inspecting the wiper blades. Damaged blades won't clean properly. Look for cracks or tears. Replace them if necessary. New blades ensure a clear view in bad weather.

### Inspect Fuse And Electrical Connections

Fuses protect your wipers from electrical surges. Check the vehicle's manual to locate the fuse box. Inspect the wiper fuse for damage. Replace it if it's blown. Check the electrical connections. Loose or corroded connections can stop wipers. Tighten them to restore function. Clean any corrosion with a wire brush. These simple checks often resolve wiper issues. Keep the wipers in top condition for a safer drive.  


## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-back-windshield-wipers-on-your-toyota-corolla-ensures-clear-visibility-and-safe-driving-proper-care-increases-the-wipers-efficiency-and-lifespan&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the back windshield wipers on your Toyota Corolla ensures clear visibility and safe driving. Proper care increases the wipers' efficiency and lifespan. Simple maintenance habits keep your rear windshield clean and wipers functioning well.

### Clean The Rear Windshield Regularly

Dirt, dust, and grime build up quickly on the rear windshield. Wiping it down often prevents scratches and streaks. Use a soft cloth and a mild glass cleaner for best results. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the glass or wiper blades. Clean surfaces allow wipers to work smoothly and remove water effectively.

### Replace Wiper Blades Periodically

Wiper blades wear out over time due to weather and use. Replace them every 6 to 12 months for optimal performance. Look for cracks, tears, or stiff rubber on the blades. New blades clear water better and reduce noise. Changing blades regularly keeps your rear view clear in rain or snow.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Activate Rear Wipers On Toyota Corolla?

To activate rear wipers, turn the rear wiper control knob on the right stalk. Rotate it once for intermittent wipe and further for continuous wipe.

### Where Is The Rear Wiper Control In Toyota Corolla?

The rear wiper control is located on the right-hand stalk beside the steering wheel. It’s a rotating knob or switch for easy access.

### Can I Adjust Rear Wiper Speed On Toyota Corolla?

No, Toyota Corolla rear wipers have fixed speeds: intermittent and continuous. Speed adjustment is not available for the rear wiper.

### Why Won’t My Toyota Corolla Rear Wiper Turn On?

Check if the rear wiper switch is off or if the fuse is blown. Also, inspect the wiper blade and motor for damage.
