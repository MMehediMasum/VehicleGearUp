## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Dot 4 Brake Fluid Ideal For Motorcycles?



DOT 4 brake fluid has a higher boiling point than DOT 3, making it safer under heavy braking. It protects ABS, disc, and drum systems in motorcycles effectively. This ensures consistent performance and prevents brake fade in demanding conditions.



### Can Synthetic Dot 4 Brake Fluids Improve Braking Performance?



Yes, synthetic DOT 4 fluids, like Motul RBF 600, offer superior heat resistance and stability. They reduce moisture absorption and maintain consistent viscosity. This enhances brake responsiveness and longevity, especially in racing or high-performance motorcycles.



### How Often Should I Replace My Motorcycle’s Dot 4 Brake Fluid?



Brake fluid should be replaced every 1-2 years or as recommended by the manufacturer. Over time, moisture degrades DOT 4 fluid, reducing braking efficiency and increasing corrosion risks. Regular changes keep braking safe and reliable.



### Are Marine Dot 4 Brake Fluids Suitable For Motorcycles?



Marine DOT 4 fluids, like Quicksilver, meet similar standards and protect against corrosion. They can be used in motorcycles but check compatibility with your brake system first. Their anti-corrosion properties benefit riders in wet or humid environments.



### What Brands Of Dot 4 Brake Fluid Are Best For Motorcycles?



Top brands include STP, Lucas Oil, Liqui Moly, Motul, and Honda OEM. Each offers reliable protection, high boiling points, and compatibility with ABS and non-ABS systems. Choose based on your motorcycle’s needs and riding conditions for best results.
