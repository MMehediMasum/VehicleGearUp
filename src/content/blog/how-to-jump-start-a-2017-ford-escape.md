## Safety Precautions

Jump starting your 2017 Ford Escape can save you time and stress, but it requires caution. Ignoring safety precautions can lead to injury or damage to your vehicle. Before you connect the jumper cables, take a moment to prepare yourself and your surroundings.

### Wear Protective Gear

Always put on safety glasses to shield your eyes from potential sparks or battery acid. Thick gloves protect your hands from accidental contact with corrosive materials. Wearing closed-toe shoes adds another layer of safety, especially if tools or cables slip from your grip.

### Check Battery Condition

Look over the battery for cracks, leaks, or corrosion before attempting a jump start. A damaged battery can explode or leak harmful chemicals. If the battery terminals are covered in white or blue powder, gently clean them with a wire brush or cloth before proceeding.

### Avoid Sparks And Flames

Keep all flames, cigarettes, and sparks far away from the battery area. Batteries release flammable hydrogen gas that can ignite easily. Make sure your vehicle’s ignition is off and avoid touching the metal clamps together to prevent accidental sparking.

### Jumper Cables

You'll need a second vehicle with a fully charged battery. This will act as the power source to jump start your Escape. Ask a neighbor or friend for help, or if you're in a parking lot, consider asking a nearby driver. It's a great way to meet new people and lend a helping hand in return!

### Gloves And Eye Protection

## Locate Battery Terminals

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=locating-the-battery-terminals-is-the-first-step-to-jump-start-a-2017-ford-escape-knowing-the-exact-spots-helps-avoid-mistakes-and-keeps-you-safe&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Locating the battery terminals is the first step to jump start a 2017 Ford Escape. Knowing the exact spots helps avoid mistakes and keeps you safe. The terminals connect the battery to the car’s electrical system. Identifying them clearly ensures a smooth jump start process.

### Open The Hood

Find the hood release lever inside the car, usually near the driver’s side. Pull the lever to unlock the hood. Walk to the front of the vehicle and lift the hood carefully. Use the hood prop rod to keep it open. This gives you clear access to the engine compartment.

### Identify Positive And Negative Terminals

Look for the car battery, usually near the front of the engine bay. The battery has two terminals: positive and negative. The positive terminal often has a red cover or a plus (+) sign. The negative terminal is usually marked with a minus (–) sign and has a black cover or no cover at all.

Clean terminals make the jump start easier. Avoid touching both terminals at once. This prevents sparks and electrical shorts. Knowing these details helps jump start your Ford Escape safely and quickly.

## Connect Jumper Cables

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=connecting-jumper-cables-correctly-is-crucial-for-jump-starting-a-2017-ford-escape-safely-this-step-creates-the-electrical-path-needed-to-transfer-power-from-the-donor-vehicle-to-the-dead-battery&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Connecting jumper cables correctly is crucial for jump starting a 2017 Ford Escape safely. This step creates the electrical path needed to transfer power from the donor vehicle to the dead battery. Follow each connection carefully to avoid sparks or damage to either car.

### Attach Red Cable To Dead Battery

First, find the positive terminal on the dead battery. It usually has a plus sign (+) or is marked red. Clamp the red jumper cable securely onto this terminal. Make sure the connection is tight to avoid slipping.

### Attach Red Cable To Donor Battery

Next, locate the positive terminal on the donor battery. Attach the other end of the red cable to this terminal. Check that the clip holds firmly and does not touch any metal parts around the battery.

### Attach Black Cable To Donor Battery

Now, find the negative terminal on the donor battery. This terminal is usually marked with a minus sign (–) or is black. Connect the black jumper cable clamp to this terminal firmly. Ensure it is secure before proceeding.

### Attach Black Cable To Ground On Dead Vehicle

Finally, connect the black cable’s other end to a metal ground on the dead vehicle. Look for an unpainted metal surface away from the battery, like a bolt or bracket. This step helps prevent sparks near the battery.

## Start The Donor Vehicle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=starting-the-donor-vehicle-is-a-key-part-of-jump-starting-your-2017-ford-escape-this-vehicle-provides-the-power-needed-to-recharge-your-dead-battery&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Starting the donor vehicle is a key part of jump-starting your 2017 Ford Escape. This vehicle provides the power needed to recharge your dead battery. Make sure the donor vehicle’s engine is off before connecting the cables. Once connected, the donor engine needs to run to deliver power safely and steadily.

### Run Engine For A Few Minutes

Turn on the donor vehicle’s engine and let it run for 3 to 5 minutes. This allows the battery to build enough charge. A running engine produces the electrical current needed for the jump start. Avoid revving the engine. Keep it steady and smooth during this time.

### Check For Proper Connection

Look at each cable connection carefully. The red cable should connect to the positive (+) terminals on both batteries. The black cable must connect to the negative (-) terminal on the donor battery and a metal ground on the Ford Escape. A loose or wrong connection can cause sparks or damage. Make sure the clamps are tight and secure before proceeding.




## Start The 2017 Ford Escape

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=starting-a-2017-ford-escape-after-a-jump-start-requires-careful-attention-follow-simple-steps-to-ensure-the-engine-fires-up-safely&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Starting a 2017 Ford Escape after a jump start requires careful attention. Follow simple steps to ensure the engine fires up safely. Understanding the process helps avoid damage and gets the vehicle running smoothly.

### Turn Ignition Key

Insert the key into the ignition switch. Turn the key slowly to the "Start" position. Hold it there for a few seconds. This action sends power to the engine and electrical systems.

Do not turn the key for more than 10 seconds at a time. If the engine does not start, wait a moment before trying again. This prevents overheating the starter motor.

### Listen For Engine Response

Pay close attention to sounds from the engine. A strong cranking noise means the starter is working. If the engine catches, it will begin to run smoothly.

Weak or no sound may indicate poor battery connection or other issues. In that case, check the jumper cables and battery clamps. Ensure they are secure and making good contact.

## Disconnect Jumper Cables

Disconnecting the jumper cables is a critical step after successfully jump-starting your 2017 Ford Escape. Handling the cables in the right order prevents sparks and protects both vehicles’ electrical systems. Pay close attention to each connection to ensure safety and avoid any damage.

### Remove Black Cable From Ground

Start by carefully detaching the black cable connected to the ground point on your Ford Escape. This is usually a metal part away from the battery, like an unpainted bolt or engine block. Removing this cable first reduces the risk of sparks near the battery, which can be dangerous.

### Remove Black Cable From Donor Vehicle

Next, disconnect the black cable from the negative terminal of the donor vehicle’s battery. Make sure you pull the clamp off gently without touching other metal parts. This step ensures the circuit is fully broken on the donor side.

### Remove Red Cable From Donor Vehicle

Then, remove the red cable from the positive terminal on the donor vehicle. This cable carries the electrical current, so be cautious not to let it touch the black cable or any metal surface. Handling it carefully keeps you safe and prevents short circuits.

### Remove Red Cable From Dead Battery

Finally, take off the red cable from the positive terminal of your Ford Escape’s battery. This completes the disconnection process. By removing the cables in this specific order, you minimize the chance of sparks and protect both vehicles’ electrical systems.




## After Jump Starting

After successfully jump starting your 2017 Ford Escape, the job isn’t quite finished. What you do next can make a big difference in ensuring your battery stays healthy and your vehicle runs smoothly. Let’s look at the crucial steps to take immediately after jump starting.

### Keep Engine Running

Once the engine starts, don’t turn it off right away. Keep it running for at least 15 to 20 minutes. This allows the alternator to begin charging the battery and prevents it from dying again immediately.

Turning off the engine too soon could leave you stranded again. If you hear any unusual noises or notice the dashboard lights flickering, it might be a sign of a deeper electrical issue that needs attention.

### Drive To Recharge Battery

Simply idling isn’t enough to fully recharge your battery. Take your Ford Escape for a drive instead—ideally on the highway or open roads where you can maintain a steady speed. This helps the alternator generate more power and properly recharge the battery.

A short drive around the block won’t deliver enough charge. Think about your last long drive: Did your battery ever feel weak afterward? Driving after a jump start can prevent those frustrating moments.

### Check Battery Health

After you’ve driven and recharged the battery, it’s smart to check its health. Look out for slow engine starts, dim headlights, or warning lights on the dashboard. These signs suggest your battery might be near the end of its life.

You can also use a multimeter to measure voltage or visit a mechanic for a professional battery test. Catching battery problems early saves you from unexpected breakdowns and costly repairs.

  



## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Safely Jump Start A 2017 Ford Escape?

Connect the red clamp to the positive battery terminal of both cars. Attach the black clamp to the negative terminal of the booster car and a metal ground on the Escape. Start the booster car, then start the Escape after a few minutes.

### What Tools Do I Need To Jump Start My Ford Escape?

You only need a set of jumper cables and another vehicle with a fully charged battery to jump start your Ford Escape safely and effectively.

### Can I Use A Portable Jump Starter For A 2017 Ford Escape?

Yes, portable jump starters work well. Connect the device’s positive clamp to the battery’s positive terminal and the negative clamp to a grounded metal surface.

### How Long Should I Keep The Ford Escape Running After Jump Starting?

After jump starting, keep your Ford Escape running for at least 20 minutes. This helps recharge the battery fully and avoid future starting issues.
