## Tips For Battery Maintenance

Keeping your Jeep Grand Cherokee key fob battery in good shape can save you from unexpected lockouts and costly replacements. Simple habits and careful handling can extend your battery's life significantly. Let’s look at some practical ways to maintain your key fob battery effectively.

### Store Your Key Fob Away From Extreme Temperatures

Extreme heat or cold can drain your battery faster than normal. Avoid leaving your key fob on the dashboard or in direct sunlight for long periods. Instead, keep it in a cool, dry place when not in use to protect the battery’s longevity.

### Clean The Battery Contacts Regularly

Dirt and dust can build up on the battery contacts inside the key fob, reducing its performance. Use a soft cloth or a cotton swab with a bit of rubbing alcohol to gently clean the contacts. This simple step can improve the connection and extend battery life.

### Limit Excessive Button Pressing

Pressing buttons repeatedly or unnecessarily drains the battery faster. Are you someone who often presses the lock or panic button out of habit? Try to press only when needed to conserve power.

### Remove The Battery If You Won’t Use The Fob For A While

If you plan to leave your vehicle unused for an extended period, consider removing the battery from the key fob. This prevents slow battery drain and corrosion from potential leaks. It’s a straightforward way to keep your battery fresh when you return.

### Check Battery Voltage Periodically

Using a small multimeter, you can check your battery’s voltage to know when it’s time for a replacement. Batteries usually last around 2-3 years, but monitoring voltage helps you avoid surprises. Wouldn't you prefer to change the battery before the fob stops working?
