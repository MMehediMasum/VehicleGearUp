**If you own a Ford Explorer, you know how important it is to keep your engine running smoothly. One key part of that is using the right oil.**

But with so many options out there, how do you know which oil is best for your Explorer? Choosing the wrong oil can cause engine problems and costly repairs. You’ll discover exactly what oil your Ford Explorer needs and why it matters.

Keep reading to make sure you’re giving your vehicle the care it deserves.




## Recommended Oil Types

Choosing the right oil for your Ford Explorer is crucial for keeping your engine running smoothly and efficiently. Using the recommended oil types ensures optimal performance, better fuel economy, and longer engine life. Let’s break down what kind of oil suits your Explorer best and why it matters.

### Synthetic Vs Conventional Oils

Synthetic oils are engineered for higher performance and protection. They resist breakdown better than conventional oils, especially under extreme temperatures or heavy loads.

Conventional oils are less expensive but may require more frequent changes. If you drive mostly in mild conditions, conventional oil might work, but synthetic offers peace of mind for tougher drives.

Think about your driving habits: Do you often tow or drive in hot weather? Synthetic oil can handle those challenges more effectively.

### Viscosity Grades For Ford Explorer

Viscosity refers to how thick or thin the oil is. The right viscosity helps your engine parts move smoothly without extra friction.

Ford usually recommends 5W-20 or 5W-30 for most Explorer models. These grades provide a good balance for cold starts and high engine temperatures.

Check your owner’s manual to confirm the exact grade for your model year. Using the wrong viscosity can lead to poor fuel economy or engine wear.

### Oil Specifications And Standards

Look for oils that meet Ford’s specifications like WSS-M2C930-A or newer standards. These certifications mean the oil passes tests for quality and performance.

API ratings such as SN Plus or SP are also important. They indicate the oil’s ability to protect against engine deposits and wear.

Using oil without the correct specs is a gamble you don’t want to take with your Explorer’s engine health.




## Engine Oil Capacity

Knowing the engine oil capacity of your Ford Explorer is essential for proper maintenance. The right oil level keeps your engine running smoothly. Too little oil can cause damage, while too much oil may lead to leaks or other issues.

Each engine type in the Ford Explorer has a specific oil capacity. Checking and refilling oil correctly ensures your vehicle stays in good shape and performs well over time.

### Capacity By Engine Type

| Engine Type | Oil Capacity (with filter) |
| --- | --- |
| 2.3L EcoBoost 4-Cylinder | 5.7 quarts (5.4 liters) |
| 3.0L EcoBoost V6 | 7.0 quarts (6.6 liters) |
| 3.3L V6 Hybrid | 5.8 quarts (5.5 liters) |

### Checking And Refilling Oil

* Park your Explorer on level ground and turn off the engine.
* Wait a few minutes to let the oil settle.
* Pull out the dipstick, wipe it clean, then reinsert it fully.
* Remove the dipstick again and check the oil level.
* The oil should be between the minimum and maximum marks.
* Add oil slowly if the level is low, using the correct type.
* Recheck the level to avoid overfilling.
* Secure the dipstick and close the oil fill cap tightly.

## Oil Change Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-oil-in-your-ford-explorer-helps-keep-the-engine-running-smoothly-it-removes-old-oil-and-replaces-it-with-fresh-oil&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the oil in your Ford Explorer helps keep the engine running smoothly. It removes old oil and replaces it with fresh oil. This process protects the engine parts and improves performance. Knowing how to change the oil saves money and time. Follow the steps carefully to do it right.

### Tools Needed

* Socket wrench set
* Oil filter wrench
* Drain pan
* New oil filter
* New engine oil (check owner's manual for type and amount)
* Funnel
* Gloves
* Rags or paper towels
* Car jack and jack stands (if needed)

### Step-by-step Oil Change

* Warm up the engine for a few minutes to thin the oil.
* Park the vehicle on a level surface and turn off the engine.
* Use the jack and stands to lift the vehicle if needed.
* Place the drain pan under the oil drain plug.
* Use the socket wrench to remove the drain plug carefully.
* Allow the old oil to drain completely into the pan.
* Remove the old oil filter with the oil filter wrench.
* Apply a small amount of new oil to the gasket of the new filter.
* Install the new oil filter by hand until snug.
* Replace and tighten the drain plug securely.
* Use the funnel to pour new oil into the engine.
* Check the oil level with the dipstick and add more if needed.
* Start the engine and check for leaks.
* Turn off the engine and recheck the oil level.

### Disposing Of Used Oil

Used oil is harmful to the environment. Never pour it down drains or on the ground. Collect the oil in a sealed container. Take it to a local recycling center or auto shop that accepts used oil. Proper disposal keeps the environment safe and follows the law.

## Common Oil-related Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-common-oil-related-issues-can-save-you-time-and-money-while-keeping-your-ford-explorer-running-smoothly-oil-is-crucial-for-engine-health-and-problems-with-it-can-lead-to-serious-damage&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding common oil-related issues can save you time and money while keeping your Ford Explorer running smoothly. Oil is crucial for engine health, and problems with it can lead to serious damage. Recognizing early signs helps you act fast before minor issues turn into costly repairs.

### Oil Leaks And Causes

Oil leaks often start small but can quickly cause major trouble. You might notice dark spots under your vehicle or a burning oil smell. Common causes include worn gaskets, loose oil filters, or damaged oil pans.

Have you checked your oil level recently? Low oil due to leaks can lead to engine overheating and wear. Regular inspections under your Explorer can catch leaks early, saving you headaches.

### Low Oil Pressure

Low oil pressure is a warning sign your engine isn’t getting enough lubrication. It can result from a failing oil pump, clogged oil filter, or simply low oil levels. Ignoring this can cause engine parts to grind and wear out prematurely.

If your oil pressure light flickers or stays on, don’t delay—stop driving and check your oil. Using the right oil type and maintaining proper levels is key to avoiding this issue.

### Engine Noise And Oil Problems

Unusual engine noises often point to oil-related problems. Ticking or knocking sounds might mean oil isn’t reaching critical engine parts. This could be due to degraded oil, dirty filters, or oil starvation.

Have you noticed any new sounds from your engine? Changing your oil on schedule and using the correct viscosity can reduce noise and improve performance. Your ears can be the first alert system for oil issues, so listen carefully.

## Tips For Maximizing Engine Performance

Keeping your Ford Explorer’s engine running smoothly requires more than just using the right oil. Small habits can boost engine performance and extend its life. Focus on simple, regular care routines. These tips help your engine work better and last longer.

### Regular Oil Checks

Check your oil level often. Use the dipstick to see if oil is low or dirty. Top up with the correct oil type as needed. Clean oil lubricates engine parts and prevents wear. Change oil at recommended intervals to avoid engine damage.

### Using Additives

Additives can improve oil performance. Some reduce friction and protect against rust. Others help keep the engine clean by breaking down sludge. Use additives designed for your Ford Explorer’s engine. Follow product instructions carefully for best results.

### Maintaining Other Fluids

Engine oil is not the only fluid to watch. Coolant, transmission fluid, and brake fluid all affect engine health. Keep these fluids at proper levels and replace them as needed. Good fluid maintenance helps the engine run cooler and smoother.
