## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is A Weight Distribution Hitch For Half Ton Trucks?



A weight distribution hitch evenly spreads trailer tongue weight across the truck’s axles. This improves stability, steering, and braking. It reduces trailer sway and prevents rear-end sag on half ton trucks. Ideal for towing heavier loads safely and comfortably.



### How Does Sway Control Improve Towing Safety?



Sway control minimizes trailer side-to-side movement caused by wind or passing vehicles. It stabilizes the trailer and truck connection, enhancing control. Products like CURT TruTrack include built-in sway control for safer towing on highways and uneven roads.



### Which Hitch Fits 2-inch Shank Receivers Best?



CURT 17601, Husky 30849, and VEOVR models fit 2-inch shank receivers perfectly. These hitches provide strong towing capacity with compatible ball sizes, ensuring easy installation and optimal performance for half ton trucks.



### Can I Use A 14,000 Lbs Hitch On My Half Ton Truck?



While some 14,000 lbs hitches exist, verify your truck’s towing capacity first. Using a hitch rated above your truck’s limit can be unsafe. Always choose a hitch that matches or is below your truck’s maximum towing specs.



### What’s The Benefit Of A Round Bar Vs. Trunnion Bar Hitch?



Round bar hitches like Reese 49912 offer smoother sway control and easier maintenance. Trunnion bars provide higher weight capacity but may be heavier. Round bars are popular for half ton trucks due to their balance of performance and ease of use.



### How Do I Select The Right Drop Or Rise Shank?



Choose a shank drop or rise based on your trailer and truck height difference. Correct shank alignment ensures a level trailer and proper weight distribution. CURT offers various drop and rise sizes for precise fit and towing stability.
