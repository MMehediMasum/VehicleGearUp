## Locating Jack Points

Knowing exactly where to place the car jack on your 2021 Toyota Highlander can save you time and prevent damage. Locating the jack points is essential for safely lifting the vehicle. These spots are designed to support the weight without harming the frame or body.

### Front Jack Points

The front jack points are located just behind the front wheels, near the vehicle’s pinch welds. You’ll notice small notches or reinforced areas along the frame rail. These spots are made to hold the jack securely and keep the Highlander stable while lifted.

Always double-check the owner's manual to confirm the exact positions. Placing the jack too far forward or inward can slip or damage the undercarriage. Have you ever tried lifting your vehicle only to find the jack slipping? Knowing these points prevents that hassle.

### Rear Jack Points

The rear jack points sit behind the rear wheels, close to the pinch welds along the frame. Like the front, these points are reinforced and designed to handle the vehicle’s weight safely. They’re usually marked or slightly raised to help you spot them easily.

Using the rear jack points is crucial when changing a flat tire or performing maintenance on the back end. If you place the jack too far from these spots, you risk bending the frame or causing instability. How confident are you in identifying these points without a guide?

### Side Jack Points

Side jack points run along the bottom edges of the Highlander’s body, between the front and rear wheels. These are especially useful for lifting the vehicle when both wheels on one side need to be off the ground. Look for the small, reinforced sections near the pinch welds.

These points are handy if you need to lift the vehicle for brake work or suspension checks. The side jack points distribute the weight evenly, reducing the risk of damage. Have you ever wondered why some lifts feel unstable? It often comes down to missing these precise spots.

## Step-by-step Jack Placement

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-how-to-place-the-car-jack-correctly-on-your-2021-toyota-highlander-is-vital-for-safety-and-ease-this-guide-walks-you-through-each-stage-clearly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing how to place the car jack correctly on your 2021 Toyota Highlander is vital for safety and ease. This guide walks you through each stage clearly. Follow these steps to lift your vehicle without damage or risk.

### Preparing The Vehicle

Park the Highlander on flat, stable ground. Engage the parking brake to prevent movement. Turn off the engine and place wheel chocks behind the wheels. This stops the vehicle from rolling.

Remove the hubcap or wheel cover if needed. Locate the jack points under the vehicle. These are reinforced areas designed to support the jack’s weight safely.

### Positioning The Jack

Find the correct jack point near the tire you want to lift. The 2021 Highlander has specific jacking locations along the frame. Check your owner’s manual or look for small notches or marks on the rocker panels.

Place the jack’s saddle directly under the jack point. Make sure the jack sits flat and stable. Align it carefully to avoid slipping during lifting.

### Lifting The Highlander Safely

Start raising the jack slowly by turning the handle or pump lever. Watch the jack and vehicle for any signs of instability. Stop if the jack shifts or the vehicle tilts.

Lift the Highlander until the tire clears the ground. Avoid raising the vehicle higher than necessary. Once lifted, use jack stands for added support before working under the vehicle.

## Maintaining Your Jack

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-your-car-jack-is-just-as-important-as-knowing-where-to-place-it-on-your-toyota-highlander-2021-a-well-maintained-jack-ensures-safety-and-reliability-whenever-you-need-to-lift-your-vehicle&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining your car jack is just as important as knowing where to place it on your Toyota Highlander 2021. A well-maintained jack ensures safety and reliability whenever you need to lift your vehicle. Neglecting its upkeep can lead to malfunctions or even accidents during use.

### Inspection Routine

Regularly inspect your jack for any signs of wear or damage. Look closely for cracks, rust, or bent parts, especially around the lifting arm and base. If you spot any leaks in hydraulic jacks, don’t ignore them—these can cause sudden failure.

Check the moving parts and make sure they operate smoothly without sticking or grinding. Lubricate pivot points to keep the mechanism working effortlessly. Have you ever tried using a rusty jack only to find it won’t lift properly? Avoid that frustration by making inspections a habit.

### Storage Tips

Store your jack in a dry, clean place to prevent rust and dirt buildup. Avoid leaving it exposed to moisture or extreme temperatures, which can degrade the metal and seals. A dedicated storage bag or box can protect it from dust and accidental damage.

Keep the jack upright and secure to prevent it from tipping over and causing injury or damage. If your Toyota Highlander has a specific compartment for the jack, always return it there after use. This simple habit saves you time searching and helps maintain the jack’s condition over time.
