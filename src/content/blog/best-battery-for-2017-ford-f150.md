## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Battery Size For A 2017 Ford F150?



The best battery size for a 2017 Ford F150 is Group 48 (H6, L3) or Group 94R (H7). These sizes fit perfectly and offer optimal power and durability for your truck’s requirements.



### How Many Cold Cranking Amps (cca) Does The Ideal Battery Have?



An ideal battery for the 2017 Ford F150 should have at least 760 to 850 CCA. This ensures reliable starts in cold weather and consistent performance under heavy loads.



### Are Agm Batteries Better For The 2017 Ford F150?



Yes, AGM batteries are better due to their maintenance-free design, higher durability, and better resistance to vibration. They offer longer life and improved performance for Ford F150 trucks.



### Can I Replace My Ford F150 Key Fob Battery Myself?



Yes, you can easily replace your Ford F150 key fob battery using CR2025 batteries. Replacement kits are affordable and come with step-by-step instructions for a quick swap.



### What Warranty Period Is Typical For Ford F150 Batteries?



Most premium AGM batteries for the Ford F150 come with a 36-month warranty. This covers defects and ensures peace of mind for long-term battery performance.



### Which Brands Offer Reliable Batteries For The 2017 Ford F150?



Reliable battery brands include UPLUS, Motorcraft, ACDelco, Weize, and MARXON. These brands provide high-quality AGM batteries with excellent CCA ratings and solid warranties.
