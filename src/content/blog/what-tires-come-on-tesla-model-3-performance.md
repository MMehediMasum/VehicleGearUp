## Tire Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-tires-on-your-tesla-model-3-performance-is-crucial-not-only-for-safety-but-also-for-maximizing-the-vehicles-impressive-performance-proper-care-ensures-your-tires-last-longer-and-deliver-the-grip-and-efficiency-tesla-designed&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the tires on your Tesla Model 3 Performance is crucial not only for safety but also for maximizing the vehicle’s impressive performance. Proper care ensures your tires last longer and deliver the grip and efficiency Tesla designed. Let’s look at some practical tips to keep your tires in top shape.

### Tire Rotation And Alignment

Rotating your tires regularly helps even out wear and extends their lifespan. Tesla recommends rotating the Model 3 Performance’s tires every 6,250 miles or if tread depth difference exceeds 2/32 inch.

Alignment is equally important. Misaligned wheels can cause uneven tire wear and affect handling. Have your alignment checked if you notice your car pulling to one side or after hitting a pothole.

### Pressure Monitoring

Keeping the right tire pressure improves handling, range, and tire life. Your Tesla’s tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) alerts you if pressure is low, but checking manually once a month is smart.

Remember, tire pressure changes with temperature. Adjust pressure based on Tesla’s recommended levels found in the door jamb or owner’s manual, not just what feels right.

### Tread Wear Indicators

Watch the tread wear indicators embedded in your tires. These small bars become flush with the tread surface when it’s time to replace the tire.

Don’t wait for the indicator to show up. Regularly inspect tread depth with a gauge or a simple coin test. Worn tires reduce traction, especially in wet conditions, which can compromise your safety and performance.
