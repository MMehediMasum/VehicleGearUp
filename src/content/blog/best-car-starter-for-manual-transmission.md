## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Car Starter Compatible With Manual Transmissions?



A manual transmission starter must match the vehicle’s engine size and bolt pattern. It engages the flywheel directly to start the engine. Compatibility ensures smooth installation and reliable performance. Always check your vehicle model and transmission type before buying a starter.



### Can I Use The Same Starter For Ford And Mazda Manuals?



Some starters fit both Ford and Mazda models if engine specs and transmission types match. For example, the OEG Parts 12V starter fits Ford Explorer and Mazda B4000 manuals. Always verify part numbers and vehicle year ranges for compatibility.



### How Do I Know If A Starter Fits My Jeep Wrangler Manual?



Check the engine size and year range of your Jeep Wrangler. Starters like 17879N fit 4. 0L L6 manual transmissions from 2003 to 2006. Confirm the exact model and transmission type before purchasing to avoid fitment issues.



### Are Premium Starters Better For Manual Transmission Vehicles?



Premium starters offer higher durability and consistent performance. They are built to handle manual transmission requirements like higher torque during startup. Investing in a premium starter ensures longer life and fewer replacements for your vehicle.



### What Are Common Replacement Part Numbers For Manual Starters?



Common replacement numbers include F2TU-11000-AA for Ford and 06312-RAA-505 for Honda. These part numbers help ensure you get the correct starter model. Always cross-reference your vehicle’s OEM number before purchase.



### Can I Install A Manual Transmission Starter Myself?



Yes, with basic tools and mechanical knowledge, you can replace a manual transmission starter. Follow safety precautions, disconnect the battery, and reference the vehicle’s repair manual. If unsure, consult a professional mechanic for installation.
