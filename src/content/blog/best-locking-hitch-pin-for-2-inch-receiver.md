## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Locking Hitch Pin Fits A 2-inch Receiver?



A 5/8-inch diameter locking hitch pin is the standard size for 2-inch receivers. It fits most Class III and IV hitches securely, ensuring proper towing safety and theft protection.



### How Do Locking Hitch Pins Prevent Theft?



Locking hitch pins use durable locks and weatherproof designs. They secure the trailer hitch firmly, preventing unauthorized removal and theft of your trailer or accessories.



### Are Weatherproof Locking Hitch Pins Necessary?



Yes, weatherproof locking hitch pins resist rust and corrosion. This ensures long-lasting performance and reliable security in all weather conditions.



### Can Locking Hitch Pins Fit 2-1/2 Inch Receivers?



Many locking pins, like CZC AUTO and TowWorks, fit both 2-inch and 2-1/2-inch receivers. Always check product specs for compatibility before purchase.



### What Are The Benefits Of Keyed Alike Hitch Pins?



Keyed alike pins allow multiple locks to open with one key. This adds convenience when securing several trailers or accessories without carrying multiple keys.



### How To Choose The Best Locking Hitch Pin For Towing?



Look for durable materials, anti-theft features, weather resistance, and proper size (5/8" pin for 2" receiver). Trusted brands like Rhino USA and CURT offer reliable options.
