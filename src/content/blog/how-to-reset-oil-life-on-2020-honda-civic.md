## Check Oil Life Monitor

The oil life monitor in your 2020 Honda Civic helps track the engine oil's condition. It shows a percentage that tells you how much oil life remains. Checking this monitor is important before resetting the oil life. It ensures the system correctly reflects your vehicle’s maintenance needs.

Reading the oil life monitor is quick and easy. It keeps you informed about when to change the oil next. This helps protect your engine and keeps your car running smoothly.

### Locate The Oil Life Monitor Display

The oil life monitor is visible on the dashboard screen. Usually, it appears on the multi-information display between the speedometer and tachometer. This display shows vital info like fuel economy and maintenance alerts.

### Turn On The Ignition Without Starting The Engine

To view the oil life, press the ignition button once. Do not start the engine. This action powers the dashboard and reveals the oil life monitor on the screen.

### Use The Buttons On The Steering Wheel

Press the “Menu” or “Home” button on the steering wheel controls. Navigate through the options until you find the “Maintenance Info” or “Vehicle Settings.” The oil life percentage will be displayed here.

### Understand The Oil Life Percentage

The oil life starts at 100% after an oil change. It decreases with driving and time. When the percentage reaches 15% or less, plan to change the oil soon. Resetting the oil life monitor should be done only after completing an oil change.

## Reset Oil Life Percentage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-life-percentage-on-a-2020-honda-civic-helps-keep-track-of-engine-maintenance-it-ensures-the-oil-life-monitor-shows-accurate-data-after-an-oil-change&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil life percentage on a 2020 Honda Civic helps keep track of engine maintenance. It ensures the oil life monitor shows accurate data after an oil change. This process is simple and can be done using the steering wheel controls. Follow these easy steps to reset the oil life percentage correctly.

### Use Steering Wheel Controls

Turn the ignition switch to the ON position. Do not start the engine. Use the left or right button on the steering wheel to find the “Settings” menu on the display. Scroll through the options until you see “Vehicle Settings.” Select it by pressing the center button. Find “Maintenance Info” and press the center button again. Scroll to “Oil Life” and select it. Press and hold the center button until the oil life percentage starts to blink. Release the button once the blinking begins.

### Confirm Reset

Press the center button again to confirm the reset. The oil life percentage will reset to 100%. Turn off the ignition switch. Turn it back on to check the new oil life reading. The display should now show 100% oil life. This confirms the reset was successful and your car is ready for the next oil change interval.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-life-on-your-2020-honda-civic-usually-goes-smoothly-but-sometimes-things-dont-work-as-expected-you-might-find-the-oil-life-indicator-refuses-to-reset-or-encounter-unexpected-dashboard-warnings&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil life on your 2020 Honda Civic usually goes smoothly, but sometimes things don’t work as expected. You might find the oil life indicator refuses to reset or encounter unexpected dashboard warnings. These issues can be frustrating, but understanding why they happen helps you fix them faster.

### Oil Life Not Resetting

If your oil life percentage won’t reset after following the usual steps, check if your ignition is fully in the ON position, not just accessory mode. Sometimes, the system needs the engine running or the car in Park to accept the reset command.

Another common cause is a software glitch. Try turning off the car completely, wait a few minutes, and then attempt the reset again. If that doesn’t work, it might mean the onboard computer needs a system update or a deeper diagnostic check.

Have you ever noticed the reset option grayed out or unresponsive? This can happen if the oil change reminder sensor is malfunctioning or if the oil has not been changed properly. In such cases, a quick visit to your mechanic can save you from guessing and ensure your oil life monitor works reliably.

### Dashboard Warning Lights

After resetting the oil life, seeing warning lights on your dashboard can be alarming. A persistent oil pressure or check engine light might signal an underlying issue unrelated to the reset procedure.

Sometimes, a warning light stays on because the reset didn’t fully register in the car’s system. You could try disconnecting the battery for a few minutes to force a full system reboot, but be cautious as this may reset other settings.

Ignoring these warning lights isn’t wise. They often point to problems that need immediate attention, such as low oil levels or sensor faults. If the lights don’t turn off after a reset and a short drive, it’s best to get a professional inspection.

### How Do I Reset Oil Life On 2020 Honda Civic?

To reset oil life, turn the ignition on without starting the engine. Press the accelerator pedal fully three times within 10 seconds. The oil life indicator will reset to 100%. This process helps maintain accurate oil change reminders.

Reset the oil life indicator right after an oil change. This keeps track of your vehicle’s maintenance schedule. It ensures timely alerts for future oil changes, helping maintain engine performance and longevity.
