## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Wheel Spacers On Ram 1500?



Wheel spacers improve vehicle stability, enhance handling, and allow wider tires. They also provide a more aggressive stance and better brake clearance for Ram 1500 trucks.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size Wheel Spacer For Ram 1500?



Select spacers based on bolt pattern, hub bore size, and thickness. For Ram 1500, common sizes include 1. 5" and 2" with 6x5. 5 or 5x5. 5 bolt patterns.



### Are Forged Wheel Spacers Better For Ram 1500 Trucks?



Yes, forged spacers offer superior strength, durability, and safety. They resist bending and cracking better than cast spacers for Ram 1500 applications.



### Can I Install Wheel Spacers Myself On A Ram 1500?



Yes, with proper tools and torque specs, installation is straightforward. However, professional help ensures safety and correct fitment for Ram 1500 spacers.



### Do Wheel Spacers Affect Ram 1500’s Hub Centric Fit?



Hub centric spacers maintain perfect wheel alignment and reduce vibrations. Choose spacers with matching hub bore sizes for a smooth ride on Ram 1500.



### Will 2-inch Wheel Spacers Fit All Ram 1500 Models?



No, fitment depends on model year and bolt pattern. Verify spacer specs like 5x5. 5 or 6x5. 5 bolt patterns for your specific Ram 1500 year.
