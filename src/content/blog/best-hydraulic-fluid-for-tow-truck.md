## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Hydraulic Fluid Is Best For Tow Trucks?



The best hydraulic fluid for tow trucks is multi-viscosity, anti-wear, and suitable for extreme temperatures. Products like TRIAX Powerflow MV 46 and DOGNNAK Kubota Super UDT2 offer durability and protection for hydraulic, transmission, and brake systems in tow trucks.



### Can I Use Tractor Hydraulic Fluid In Tow Trucks?



Yes, fluids like DOGNNAK Kubota Super UDT2 are designed for both tractor and tow truck hydraulic systems. They provide excellent protection for transmissions, differentials, and wet brakes, making them versatile for various heavy equipment applications.



### How Often Should Hydraulic Fluid Be Changed In Tow Trucks?



Hydraulic fluid should be changed every 1,000 to 2,000 hours or as recommended by the manufacturer. Using fluids like CAM2 PROMAX AW 32 with extended life can help maximize fluid longevity and ensure consistent hydraulic system performance.



### What Are The Benefits Of Synthetic Hydraulic Fluids?



Synthetic hydraulic fluids, like STAR BRITE Star Tron Premium, offer better temperature stability and reduced foaming. They improve system efficiency, reduce wear, and extend fluid life, especially in extreme cold or heat conditions common in tow truck operations.



### Is Anti-wear Hydraulic Fluid Necessary For Tow Trucks?



Yes, anti-wear hydraulic fluids such as TRIAX Powerflow MV 46 protect hydraulic components from friction and damage. This extends equipment life and reduces maintenance costs, ensuring reliable tow truck hydraulic system performance.



### Can Hydraulic Fluid Also Lubricate Tow Truck Transmissions?



Many hydraulic fluids like Lucas Oil Universal Hydraulic & Transmission Fluid are formulated to lubricate transmissions and differentials. This dual-purpose fluid simplifies maintenance and provides comprehensive protection for tow truck drivetrains.
