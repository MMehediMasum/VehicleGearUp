### Can Leather Dye Fix Scratches On Car Seats?



Yes, leather dye products like Leather Hero and Furniture Clinic recoloring balms fill in scratches and restore faded areas, making car seats look new again.

### How Long Does Leather Dye Last On Car Seats?

### Can I Use Leather Dye On Furniture And Shoes Too?



Yes, most leather dyes like TOMBARINGR and NIUB are multipurpose. They work well on furniture, shoes, bags, and car seats for consistent color restoration.
