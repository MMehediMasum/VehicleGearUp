## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Microfiber Towels Best For Car Windows?



Microfiber towels are soft, absorbent, and lint-free. They clean without scratching glass or leaving streaks. Their fine fibers trap dirt and moisture effectively. This ensures a clear, spotless finish on car windows.



### How Do Waffle Weave Towels Improve Glass Cleaning?



Waffle weave towels have a unique textured surface. This texture increases absorbency and scrubbing power. They remove grime and fingerprints quickly. Plus, they dry surfaces without streaks or lint, ideal for car windows and mirrors.



### Can Microfiber Towels Be Reused After Washing?



Yes, microfiber towels are highly durable and reusable. They maintain cleaning performance for hundreds of washes. Proper washing without fabric softeners keeps them effective and streak-free. This makes them cost-efficient for long-term car care.



### Which Towel Size Is Best For Cleaning Car Windows?



Medium to large towels (16x16 to 25x36 inches) are ideal. They cover more surface and reduce cleaning time. Towels like Chemical Guys’ 24x16 inch or Griot’s 16x16 inch offer perfect balance for easy handling and efficient cleaning.



### Are Lint-free Towels Necessary For Car Window Cleaning?



Absolutely, lint-free towels prevent fibers from sticking on glass. They ensure a crystal-clear, streak-free finish without residue. Towels like Meguiar’s Perfect Clarity or The Rag Company’s Diamond Weave are top choices for lint-free cleaning.



### How To Maintain Microfiber Towels For Best Results?



Wash towels separately in warm water with mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners to preserve fibers. Air dry or tumble dry low heat. Proper care keeps towels absorbent, soft, and streak-free for all your car window cleaning needs.
