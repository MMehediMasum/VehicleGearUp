## Remote Start Basics

Starting your Ford Explorer remotely can change how you prepare for a drive. Knowing the basics of remote start helps you use this feature effectively and safely. Let’s break down what remote start means and why it’s worth trying.

### What Is Remote Start

Remote start lets you start your Ford Explorer’s engine from a distance using a key fob or a smartphone app. This means you can warm up or cool down your vehicle before you even get inside. It’s not just about convenience; it’s about controlling your car’s environment ahead of time.

Think about a freezing winter morning. Instead of shivering as you hop in, your car is already cozy and defrosted. Or on a hot day, the interior is cool and comfortable before you step in. Remote start makes these moments possible with just a press of a button.

### Benefits Of Remote Starting Your Ford Explorer

* **Comfort:**Enjoy a perfect temperature inside your Explorer before driving, no matter the weather outside.
* **Safety:**The engine runs only when the vehicle is locked, so you avoid leaving your car vulnerable.
* **Convenience:**Start your vehicle from your home, office, or anywhere within range, saving time and effort.
* **Efficiency:**Warming or cooling the car ahead helps the vehicle’s systems work better when you start driving.

Have you noticed how much easier your day gets when your car is ready before you are? Remote start removes the hassle of waiting inside a cold or hot vehicle. You might wonder why more people don’t use this feature regularly.

## Using The Key Fob

### Locating Remote Start Button

The remote start button is usually on your Ford Explorer’s key fob. It’s often a circular or curved arrow symbol, separate from the lock and unlock buttons.

Some key fobs have this button on the back, while others place it below the lock button. Knowing exactly where it is helps you avoid confusion when you're in a hurry.

Take a moment to check your key fob now. Have you noticed the button’s location without needing to look closely? This small detail can make a big difference in ease of use.

### Step-by-step Remote Start Process

* **Press the Lock Button:**Ensure your Explorer is locked by pressing the lock button on your key fob once. The vehicle’s lights usually flash to confirm it’s locked.
* **Press the Remote Start Button Twice:**Quickly press the remote start button two times in a row. The engine should start, and the parking lights will turn on.
* **Enter Your Vehicle:**After remote starting, unlock the door and get inside within 10 minutes. If you don’t, the engine will automatically shut off.
* **Press the Brake:**To fully take control, press the brake pedal once you’re inside. This action disables the remote start mode and switches the vehicle to normal operation.

Did you know some Ford Explorers allow you to remote start multiple times in a row? This can extend the warm-up or cool-down period, which is especially useful in extreme weather.

Try practicing this process a few times in your driveway. It can feel tricky at first, but the more you do it, the more natural it will become. What’s your favorite part about using remote start?

## Using The Fordpass App

The FordPass app offers a simple way to remote start your Ford Explorer. It connects your phone with your vehicle. This lets you control many features from a distance. You can warm up or cool down the cabin before you get in. The app works on most smartphones and is easy to use.

### Setting Up Fordpass

Download the FordPass app from your app store. Open the app and create a new account. Enter your vehicle’s VIN to link it to your profile. Follow the instructions to verify your identity. Make sure your Ford Explorer is compatible and has the remote start feature enabled.

Allow the app to access your location and send notifications. This helps with security and communication. Check for any updates to keep the app working smoothly. Keep your login details safe for easy access.

### Starting Your Vehicle Remotely Via App

Open the FordPass app on your phone. Select your Ford Explorer from the list of connected vehicles. Look for the “Remote Start” button in the app’s dashboard. Tap the button to start your vehicle’s engine remotely.

The vehicle will start and run for a preset time, usually 10 minutes. You can extend this time using the app if needed. The app will notify you once the engine starts. Use this feature to preheat or cool your Explorer before driving.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=remote-starting-your-ford-explorer-makes-cold-mornings-easier-and-saves-time-sometimes-the-remote-start-may-not-work-as-expected&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Remote starting your Ford Explorer makes cold mornings easier and saves time. Sometimes, the remote start may not work as expected. Understanding common problems helps fix these issues quickly.

Many remote start issues stem from simple causes. Checking basic elements first saves frustration. Here are some common problems and ways to address them.

### Remote Start Not Working

The remote start may fail due to several reasons. Ensure the vehicle is in park and the doors are closed. The hood must be fully latched for the system to activate. The engine won’t start if the alarm system is triggered.

Check if the remote key fob battery is weak or dead. A low battery can prevent the signal from reaching the car. Sometimes, the remote start system needs to be reset. Consult the vehicle manual for reset instructions.

* Verify the car is in park and doors are closed
* Check the hood latch is fully secured
* Replace the key fob battery if needed
* Reset the remote start system as per manual

### Battery And Signal Problems

The vehicle battery may impact remote start performance. A weak or dead battery can stop the engine from starting remotely. Test the battery voltage and replace it if below recommended levels.

Signal interference can block the remote start command. Nearby electronic devices or tall buildings may weaken the remote’s signal. Try standing closer to the vehicle or removing obstacles between the remote and car.

* Check vehicle battery health and voltage
* Replace battery if it is weak or old
* Avoid signal interference by moving closer
* Keep remote and vehicle line of sight clear




## Safety Tips For Remote Start

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-the-remote-start-feature-on-your-ford-explorer-can-be-incredibly-convenient-especially-during-extreme-weather-however-safety-should-always-be-your-top-priority&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using the remote start feature on your Ford Explorer can be incredibly convenient, especially during extreme weather. However, safety should always be your top priority. Understanding how to use this feature responsibly helps protect you, your vehicle, and those around you.

### Avoiding Accidental Starts

Have you ever pressed a button and wondered if you just started the engine by mistake? Accidental starts can waste fuel and create unnecessary noise, but more importantly, they can cause safety hazards.

To prevent this, always keep your key fob out of reach of children and pets. Consider using protective cases or key holders that make it harder to press buttons accidentally.

Also, double-check your vehicle’s status before activating remote start. Many Ford Explorers require the doors to be locked first, which is a built-in safety step, but it’s wise to confirm this yourself.

### Vehicle Security Measures

Your Ford Explorer’s remote start system includes built-in security features, but you can add more layers of protection. For example, ensure your vehicle is parked in a safe, well-lit area when using remote start.

Always lock the doors before activating remote start. This prevents unauthorized access while the engine is running. Some models automatically disable the remote start if the hood is open, so make sure the hood is securely closed.

Consider enabling alerts on your smartphone, if your Explorer supports it, to notify you if someone tampers with your vehicle. This proactive step can save you from potential theft or damage.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Activate Remote Start On A Ford Explorer?

To activate remote start, press the lock button twice on your key fob. Then, press and hold the remote start button for 3 seconds. The engine will start remotely, allowing you to warm or cool the vehicle before entering.

### Can I Use Remote Start In Extreme Weather Conditions?

Yes, remote start works in cold or hot weather. It helps preheat or cool your Ford Explorer, improving comfort. However, ensure the vehicle is parked safely with no obstructions around.

### Is Remote Start Available On All Ford Explorer Models?

Remote start is available on most recent Ford Explorer trims. Check your owner’s manual or contact your dealer to confirm if your model supports this feature. Some older models may require aftermarket installation.

### How Long Does The Remote Start Run Before Shutting Off?

The remote start system runs the engine for up to 10 minutes. You can extend this by repeating the remote start sequence. After that, the engine shuts off automatically for safety reasons.
