## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Microfiber Towels Best For Drying Cars?



Microfiber towels absorb water quickly without scratching surfaces. They prevent streaks, water spots, and lint residue. Their softness protects car paint, making them ideal for drying cars, SUVs, and trucks efficiently.



### How Do Gsm Ratings Affect Car Drying Towels?



GSM (grams per square meter) measures towel density. Higher GSM towels, like 1200-1400, absorb more water and last longer. They offer better softness, durability, and faster drying without leaving streaks or scratches.



### Can Large Towels Dry Suvs And Trucks Effectively?



Yes, large towels (24"x31" or bigger) cover more surface quickly. They reduce drying time and minimize streaks or water spots. Towels like KKV 48"x24" or HOMEXCEL 31"x24" are perfect for big vehicles.



### Are Lint-free Towels Better For Car Drying?



Lint-free towels prevent tiny fibers from sticking to the car surface. This keeps your vehicle spotless and scratch-free. Products like HOMEXCEL and Blackline offer lint-free microfiber towels for a clean finish.



### How To Maintain Microfiber Drying Towels For Longevity?



Wash towels separately in cold water with mild detergent. Avoid fabric softeners and bleach as they reduce absorbency. Air dry or tumble dry low to keep towels soft and effective longer.



### Why Choose Double-twist Pile Towels For Drying Cars?



Double-twist pile towels hold more water and dry faster. They provide a plush, scratch-free surface that traps dirt and moisture effectively. Brands like ExoForma and SMINDER use this design for superior drying results.
