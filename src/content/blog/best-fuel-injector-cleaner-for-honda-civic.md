Yes, fuel injector cleaners remove deposits that block fuel flow, improving combustion. This leads to better fuel efficiency and smoother engine operation. Products like STP Ultra 5 In 1 and Royal Purple are designed to optimize fuel system performance.



### What Tools Help Clean Honda Civic Fuel Injectors Effectively?
