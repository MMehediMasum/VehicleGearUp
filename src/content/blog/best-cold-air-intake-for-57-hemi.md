Several top brands make cold air intakes designed specifically for the 5. 7 Hemi engine. These products fit popular trucks like the Ram 1500 and Dodge Charger. Selecting the right intake depends on your needs, such as towing or daily driving. This guide covers reliable options like Rough Country, K&N, MOOSUN, and PHILTOP, helping you find a good fit for your vehicle and driving style.
