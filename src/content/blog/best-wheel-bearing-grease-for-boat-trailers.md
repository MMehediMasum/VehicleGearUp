## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Marine Grease Ideal For Boat Trailer Bearings?



Marine grease resists water, salt, and corrosion to protect bearings. It offers strong adhesion and high-temperature tolerance, ensuring durability and smooth operation in wet environments. Products like STAR BRITE and Lucas Oil marine greases are formulated specifically for these conditions.



### How Often Should I Re-grease Boat Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Re-grease your boat trailer wheel bearings at least once per boating season. If you frequently expose them to water or salt, grease more often. Regular maintenance prevents corrosion, reduces wear, and extends the life of your bearings.



### Can I Use Automotive Grease On Boat Trailer Bearings?



Automotive grease may lack water resistance and corrosion protection needed for marine use. Use marine-grade grease like Sta-Lube or Quicksilver for boat trailers. These greases withstand water exposure and harsh marine environments better than standard automotive lubricants.



### Are Grease Gun Cartridges Compatible With All Boat Trailers?



Most marine grease gun cartridges, such as STAR BRITE and the lithium complex types, fit standard grease guns. They are designed for easy application on boat trailer bearings, making maintenance simpler and more efficient.



### What Temperature Range Should Wheel Bearing Grease Handle?



Wheel bearing grease for boat trailers should handle temperatures from -20°F to 300°F. High-temp greases like AtomLube Ultra Heavy Duty ensure protection during hot operation and cold storage, maintaining lubrication and bearing performance year-round.
