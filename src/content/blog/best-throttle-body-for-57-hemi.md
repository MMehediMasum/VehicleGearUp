## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Throttle Body For 5.7 Hemi Engines?



The SCITOO and A-Premium throttle bodies are top choices. They fit Dodge Ram 1500, 2500, 3500, and Durango 5. 7L models. Both offer reliable performance and easy installation, enhancing throttle response and engine efficiency.



### How Do I Know Which Throttle Body Fits My 5.7 Hemi?



Check your vehicle’s year and model against product compatibility. Most throttle bodies listed fit Dodge Ram 1500-3500 (2003-2012) and Durango (2004-2009). Verify part numbers like 53032801AC or 5161805AA for exact matches.



### Can A Throttle Body Spacer Improve 5.7 Hemi Performance?



Yes, spacers like the RJSPHH model can boost airflow and throttle response. They help improve horsepower and torque by optimizing air intake, making your 5. 7 Hemi run smoother and more efficiently.



### Are Electronic Throttle Bodies Better For 5.7 Hemi Trucks?



Electronic throttle bodies, such as IRONTREE and A-Premium, provide precise control. They improve throttle response and fuel efficiency compared to older mechanical types. These are ideal for modern 5. 7 Hemi Dodge and Ram models.



### What Is The Difference Between 80mm And 90mm Throttle Bodies?



80mm and 90mm refer to the diameter of the throttle body opening. Larger 90mm models, like ROADFAR, allow more air for better performance in high-powered 5. 7 Hemi engines. 80mm suits stock or mild upgrades.



### How Often Should I Replace My 5.7 Hemi Throttle Body?



Replace the throttle body if you experience poor throttle response or check engine lights. Generally, inspect every 60,000 miles, but replacement depends on wear and vehicle condition for optimal performance.
