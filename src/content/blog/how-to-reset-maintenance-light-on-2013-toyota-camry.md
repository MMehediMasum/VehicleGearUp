**Is your 2013 Toyota Camry’s maintenance light on, and you’re not sure how to reset it? You’re not alone.**

That little light can be confusing and even a bit frustrating when it stays on after a routine service. But don’t worry—resetting it is easier than you think. In this guide, you’ll learn simple, step-by-step instructions to turn off that maintenance light yourself.

Keep reading, and you’ll save time, avoid unnecessary trips to the mechanic, and get your Camry back to running smoothly in no time.




## Locate The Maintenance Light

Before you reset the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Camry, you need to know exactly where to find it. This light is your car’s way of reminding you to perform essential maintenance tasks, like oil changes. Knowing its location helps you quickly check and reset it without confusion.

### Dashboard Location Of The Maintenance Light

The maintenance light is usually located on the dashboard, near the speedometer or the odometer display. On the 2013 Toyota Camry, it often appears as a small wrench icon or a message that says “Maintenance Required.”

Look closely at the cluster of gauges right in front of you when you sit in the driver’s seat. If the light is on, it will be brightly lit, making it easy to spot.

### Understanding The Maintenance Light Symbols

Sometimes the maintenance light is not just a simple icon but a symbol that might be confusing at first. The wrench icon is the most common, but some models might also display messages related to oil changes or tire pressure.

Pay attention to what the symbol looks like or what message appears. This will help you confirm that you have found the right indicator before trying to reset it.

### Using The Owner’s Manual To Confirm The Light

If you’re unsure about the location or meaning of the maintenance light, your best resource is the owner’s manual. It provides clear diagrams and explanations for every dashboard light on your Camry.

Keep the manual handy in your glove compartment. This way, you can quickly reference it whenever a new light appears, saving you time and stress.

## Prepare The Vehicle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=ensure-the-vehicle-is-parked-and-the-engine-is-off-before-starting-turn-the-ignition-to-the-on-position-without-starting-the-engine&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Ensure the vehicle is parked and the engine is off before starting. Turn the ignition to the "ON" position without starting the engine. This prepares the system to reset the maintenance light safely.

Preparing your vehicle is the first step in resetting the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Camry. This process involves a few simple actions, ensuring your vehicle is in the correct state before you begin. Following these steps will help you avoid mistakes and ensure a smooth reset.

### Turn Off The Engine

Start by turning off the engine completely. This is crucial to prevent any electrical issues. Ensure the car is in park mode. Remove the key from the ignition. Doing this ensures safety and readiness for the next steps.

### Set The Odometer To Trip A

Next, set the odometer to Trip A. This involves pressing the trip button on the dashboard. Keep pressing until you see the Trip A display. This position is necessary to reset the maintenance light successfully. Ensure the display is clear and readable.

## Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-maintenance-light-on-your-2013-toyota-camry-is-a-straightforward-process-that-you-can-complete-in-just-a-few-minutes-this-reset-lets-your-vehicle-know-that-routine-maintenance-has-been-completed-helping-you-keep-track-of-when-the-next-service-is-due&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Camry is a straightforward process that you can complete in just a few minutes. This reset lets your vehicle know that routine maintenance has been completed, helping you keep track of when the next service is due. If you've just changed your oil or performed other scheduled tasks, resetting the light is the final step to ensure your dashboard reflects the current status.

### Press And Hold The Trip Reset Button

Locate the trip reset button on your Camry's dashboard, usually near the speedometer. Press and hold this button firmly. It’s important to maintain steady pressure throughout the next steps to avoid interrupting the reset.

### Turn The Ignition To On

While still holding the trip reset button, turn the ignition key to the “On” position. This powers the dashboard without starting the engine. Keeping the button pressed during this phase signals the system to prepare for the reset.

### Wait For The Light To Flash

Watch the maintenance light closely after turning the ignition on. It will start to flash or blink, indicating the reset process is underway. This usually takes about 10 seconds, so be patient and keep holding the button.

### Release The Button

Once the maintenance light stops flashing and remains off, you can release the trip reset button. This confirms that the system has successfully reset the maintenance reminder. Turn off the ignition and then start your engine to double-check that the light stays off.




## Verify The Reset

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-resetting-the-maintenance-light-on-your-2013-toyota-camry-it-is-important-to-confirm-the-reset-worked-properly-verifying-the-reset-ensures-your-vehicles-system-recognizes-that-the-maintenance-has-been-completed&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After resetting the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Camry, it is important to confirm the reset worked properly. Verifying the reset ensures your vehicle’s system recognizes that the maintenance has been completed. This prevents confusion and false alerts in the future.

Checking the reset is simple and takes just a few moments. Doing this step helps maintain accurate vehicle records and keeps your dashboard clear.

### Turn The Ignition To The On Position

Start by turning the key to the “On” position without starting the engine. The dashboard lights will come on. Look closely at the maintenance light to see if it is off.

### Observe The Maintenance Light

The light should turn off completely. If it stays on or blinks, the reset may not have worked. You might need to repeat the reset process or check for other issues.

### Drive The Vehicle Briefly

Take your Camry for a short drive. This helps the car’s computer update its records. After driving, check the maintenance light again to confirm it remains off.

### Use The Dashboard Display Menu

* Press the “Trip” or “Menu” button on the dashboard.
* Navigate to the maintenance or settings screen.
* Look for a message confirming the maintenance light reset.

This menu can provide extra confirmation that the reset was successful.

  


## Troubleshooting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-the-maintenance-light-on-a-2013-toyota-camry-can-save-time-and-avoid-confusion-sometimes-the-light-may-not-reset-as-expected-or-might-come-back-quickly-after-resetting&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting the maintenance light on a 2013 Toyota Camry can save time and avoid confusion. Sometimes, the light may not reset as expected or might come back quickly after resetting. Understanding common issues helps fix the problem efficiently.

### Light Doesn’t Reset

The maintenance light may fail to reset due to several reasons. The reset procedure might not have been done correctly. Ensure the ignition is turned to the "ON" position without starting the engine during reset.

Battery issues can also affect the reset process. A weak or dead battery can cause the system to malfunction. Check the battery health and connections before resetting the light.

Faulty sensors or electrical problems may prevent the light from turning off. Inspect fuses and wiring related to the maintenance system for damage or loose connections.

### Maintenance Required Light Returns Quickly

The light coming back soon after reset indicates an unresolved issue. The vehicle might need actual maintenance or repairs. Check oil level, oil quality, and other fluids first.

Resetting the light without addressing the required service can trigger the warning again. Follow the recommended maintenance schedule strictly. Use genuine Toyota parts for replacements.

Sometimes, the system may detect a sensor error causing false alerts. Use an OBD-II scanner to read any stored trouble codes. This helps identify hidden problems.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Maintenance Light On A 2013 Toyota Camry?

To reset the maintenance light, turn the ignition to "ON" without starting the engine. Press and hold the trip meter reset button until the light flashes and turns off.

### When Should I Reset The Maintenance Light On My Camry?

Reset the maintenance light after performing scheduled maintenance like oil changes or inspections. This ensures accurate reminders for future service.

### Can I Reset The Maintenance Light Without Tools?

Yes, no tools are needed. Use the trip meter reset button on the dashboard and follow the reset procedure.

### What If The Maintenance Light Stays On After Resetting?

If the light stays on, check if maintenance is overdue or consult a mechanic. The sensor may need professional diagnosis.
