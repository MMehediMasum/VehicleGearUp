Yes, some natural leather cleaners like Chemical Guys SPI\_103\_16 work on synthetic leather too. They gently clean and condition without harming pleather or faux finishes. Always check product labels for compatibility before use.



### How Often Should I Clean Synthetic Leather Car Seats?



Clean synthetic leather car seats every 1-2 months to maintain appearance and prevent dirt buildup. Regular cleaning prolongs seat life and keeps interiors fresh. Use gentle, synthetic-safe products like Lexol or Armor All.



### Do Leather Conditioners Work On Synthetic Leather?



Certain leather conditioners, such as Chemical Guys SPI\_109\_16, work on synthetic leather by keeping it soft and flexible. They protect against cracking and fading. Ensure the conditioner states compatibility with faux or pleather materials.

### Can I Remove Odors From Synthetic Leather Seats?
