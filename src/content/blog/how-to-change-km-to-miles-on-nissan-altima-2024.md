## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Changing the units from kilometers to miles on your Nissan Altima 2024 should be simple. Yet, some common issues may arise during the process. Understanding these problems helps fix them quickly. Here are some troubleshooting tips to solve common unit change problems.

### Settings Not Saving

Sometimes, the car might not save the new unit settings. This happens if the system does not register the change properly. To fix this:

* Ensure the ignition is on while changing settings.
* Use the main menu to confirm the change before exiting.
* Check if the car’s software needs an update.
* Try switching units again after restarting the car.

These steps help the system remember your preferences.

### Display Not Updating

The display might still show kilometers even after changing to miles. This can confuse drivers. Follow these tips to update the display:

* Turn off the engine, then turn it back on.
* Navigate back to the unit settings and switch again.
* Check the display brightness and contrast settings.
* Look for any dashboard error messages.

These simple checks often fix display issues quickly.

### Resetting To Default Units

Sometimes, the system resets to kilometers by itself. This problem may occur after a software glitch or battery change. To reset units to default:

* Access the settings menu on the dashboard.
* Find the unit measurement option.
* Select the preferred unit, miles or kilometers.
* Save the settings and exit the menu.

Perform this reset after any major car update or service to avoid unit problems.
