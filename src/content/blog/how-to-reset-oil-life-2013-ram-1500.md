## Locate The Oil Life Monitor

Knowing where to find the Oil Life Monitor in your 2013 Ram 1500 is essential before you can reset it. This monitor tracks the condition of your engine oil and alerts you when it’s time for a change. Locating it quickly saves time and helps you maintain your truck’s performance.

### Understanding The Dashboard Display

The Oil Life Monitor is usually displayed on the truck’s instrument cluster. Look for a small screen located between the speedometer and tachometer. This screen shows important information including your oil life percentage.

Sometimes the display might be set to show other data. Use the buttons on your steering wheel or dashboard to scroll through different screens until you see the oil life status. If you’ve never explored these buttons, take a moment to familiarize yourself now—it makes maintenance easier.

### Using The Vehicle Information Center (vic)

The Vehicle Information Center, or VIC, is your main tool for checking oil life. On your 2013 Ram 1500, it’s accessed via the controls on the steering wheel or the dashboard. Once inside the VIC menu, you can find the oil life percentage clearly displayed.

Don’t worry if this sounds complicated. The menu is designed to be user-friendly, with clear labels and simple navigation. You’ll find that accessing the Oil Life Monitor becomes second nature after a couple of tries.

### Checking The Owner’s Manual For Specifics

If you’re still unsure where to find the Oil Life Monitor, your Ram’s owner’s manual is a great resource. It provides detailed diagrams and instructions specific to your model year. This can save you time and prevent frustration.

Having the manual on hand is useful not just for oil life but for other maintenance tasks too. It’s like having a personal guide tailored to your truck’s features and functions.

Turning on the ignition is a crucial first step in resetting the oil life on your 2013 Ram 1500. It wakes up the vehicle’s electrical system, allowing you to access the dashboard settings without starting the engine. This simple action prepares your truck to receive the reset command.

### Locate The Ignition Switch

Before you turn the ignition, make sure you know where the key slot or start button is located. In the 2013 Ram 1500, the ignition switch is typically on the right side of the steering column. If your truck has a push-button start, the button is usually on the dashboard near the steering wheel.

### Insert The Key Or Press The Start Button

Insert your key into the ignition and turn it to the “On” position without starting the engine. If you have a push-button start, press the button once without pressing the brake pedal. This will activate the vehicle’s electrical systems, including the dashboard display where you’ll reset the oil life.

### Check The Dashboard Display

Look at your dashboard to confirm that the ignition is on. You should see the instrument cluster light up, and the oil life indicator should be visible. If the display doesn’t turn on, try turning the ignition off and on again or check your vehicle’s battery.

### Why Is Turning On The Ignition Important?

Have you ever wondered why your truck needs to be in the “On” position but not fully started? This step allows you to reset settings without running the engine, which saves fuel and reduces emissions. It’s a small but smart way to manage your vehicle’s maintenance efficiently.

## Reset The Oil Life

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-life-on-your-2013-ram-1500-is-essential-after-an-oil-change-to-keep-your-trucks-maintenance-system-accurate-this-process-helps-the-vehicle-monitor-when-the-next-oil-change-is-due-ensuring-your-engine-runs-smoothly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil life on your 2013 Ram 1500 is essential after an oil change to keep your truck's maintenance system accurate. This process helps the vehicle monitor when the next oil change is due, ensuring your engine runs smoothly. Resetting is straightforward and only takes a few moments once you know where to look.

### Access The Reset Option

Start by turning the ignition to the "ON" position without starting the engine. Use the buttons on your steering wheel or dashboard to navigate the menu on the instrument cluster display.

Look for the “Vehicle Info” or “Settings” menu, then find the “Oil Life” option. This menu is where you can access the reset feature for your oil life indicator.

### Confirm The Reset

Once you highlight the “Oil Life” option, press the “OK” or “Reset” button to begin the reset process. The display will prompt you to confirm that you want to reset the oil life percentage.

Confirm your choice by pressing the button again, and you’ll see the oil life percentage return to 100%. This confirms that the system is now ready to track your next oil change accurately.

## Verify The Reset

After resetting the oil life on your 2013 Ram 1500, it's crucial to verify that the reset was successful. This step ensures your truck accurately tracks its maintenance schedule, preventing premature oil change alerts or missed reminders. Taking a moment to confirm the reset can save you from unnecessary service visits and keep your engine running smoothly.

### Check The Instrument Cluster Display

Turn your ignition to the "On" position without starting the engine. Look at the instrument cluster where the oil life percentage is displayed. If the reset worked, you should see a fresh 100% oil life indication.

If the display still shows the old percentage or an error message, it means the reset didn't register. Try performing the reset process again and recheck the display.

### Use The Steering Wheel Controls

Navigate through the menu using the buttons on your steering wheel. Find the "Vehicle Info" or "Oil Life" screen. Confirm that the oil life reads 100% or near full, indicating the reset was successful.

This method also confirms that your truck’s computer has properly registered the reset, giving you peace of mind.

### Restart The Engine To Confirm

After verifying the oil life on the display, start the engine and let it run for a few moments. Check the oil life indicator again to ensure it remains at 100%. If it drops or behaves oddly, there might be a system glitch or an incomplete reset.

Have you noticed your oil life percentage reset but then quickly dropped after starting the engine? This could signal an underlying issue worth investigating.

## Tips For Maintaining Oil Life

Keeping your 2013 Ram 1500’s oil life in check is more than just a routine—it’s about protecting your engine and getting the best performance. Simple habits can extend oil life and save you money on repairs. What small changes could you make today to keep your truck running smoothly?

### Regularly Check Oil Levels

Checking your oil level often helps catch leaks or burns early. Use the dipstick to verify the oil is between the minimum and maximum marks. If you spot the level dropping faster than usual, it might signal an engine issue that needs attention.

### Use The Right Oil Type

Your Ram 1500’s manual recommends specific oil grades. Sticking to these grades ensures proper lubrication and prevents engine wear. Using the wrong oil can reduce efficiency and even damage parts over time.

### Drive Smart To Extend Oil Life

Hard acceleration and frequent short trips can degrade oil faster. Try to avoid aggressive driving and give your engine enough time to warm up. Have you noticed how smoother driving affects your oil change intervals?

### Keep Your Engine Clean

Dust and debris around the engine can contaminate the oil. Regularly cleaning the engine bay prevents grime from entering oil components. This simple step keeps your oil cleaner for longer and supports engine health.

### Change Oil Filters With Every Oil Change

Oil filters trap dirt and particles that could harm your engine. Changing the filter during every oil change ensures the new oil stays clean. Skipping this can reduce the effectiveness of fresh oil and shorten its life.






## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset Oil Life On A 2013 Ram 1500?

To reset oil life, turn ignition to ON without starting. Press the accelerator pedal fully three times within 10 seconds. The oil life indicator will reset to 100%. This method works for most 2013 Ram 1500 models.

### Why Is Oil Life Reset Important For My Ram 1500?

Resetting oil life ensures accurate maintenance reminders. It helps track when the next oil change is due. This keeps your engine running smoothly and maintains warranty coverage. Ignoring resets may lead to incorrect service alerts.

### Can I Reset Oil Life Without Tools Or Equipment?

Yes, resetting oil life on a 2013 Ram 1500 requires no special tools. The process is done through the vehicle’s ignition and accelerator pedal. It’s simple and can be completed within minutes.

### How Often Should I Reset Oil Life On My Ram 1500?

Reset oil life after every oil change. Typically, this is every 5,000 to 7,500 miles, depending on driving conditions. Proper resets keep maintenance alerts accurate and help maintain vehicle performance.
