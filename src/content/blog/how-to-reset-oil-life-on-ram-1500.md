## Check Oil Life Monitor

The oil life monitor on a Ram 1500 helps track when to change the oil. It shows the remaining oil life as a percentage. This system makes maintenance easier and helps protect the engine.

Checking the oil life monitor lets you know if the oil needs changing. It also confirms if the monitor reset worked correctly after an oil change. This step is important to keep your truck running smoothly.

### Locate The Oil Life Monitor Display

The oil life monitor appears on the dashboard screen. It is usually found in the instrument cluster. Look for a menu or icon labeled "Oil Life" or "Vehicle Info."

### Turn The Ignition On Without Starting

Switch the key to the "On" position without starting the engine. This powers the dashboard and lets you access the monitor. The oil life percentage will then display on the screen.

### Check The Oil Life Percentage

Read the oil life percentage shown on the display. A full oil life reads 100%. Lower numbers mean the oil needs changing soon. If the oil was just changed, the number should reset close to 100%.

### Use The Steering Wheel Controls

Navigate through the menu using buttons on the steering wheel. Press the up or down arrows to find the oil life screen. Use the "OK" or "Select" button to confirm your choice.




## Prepare Vehicle For Reset

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparing-your-ram-1500-for-an-oil-life-reset-ensures-the-process-goes-smoothly-taking-a-few-simple-steps-before-the-reset-helps-avoid-errors&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparing your Ram 1500 for an oil life reset ensures the process goes smoothly. Taking a few simple steps before the reset helps avoid errors. It also confirms that your vehicle is ready for accurate oil monitoring.

Start by turning off the engine. The vehicle should be parked on a flat surface. This prevents any issues during the reset.

### Turn Off The Engine And Remove The Key

Make sure the engine is completely off. Take out the key from the ignition. This stops any electrical activity in the vehicle. It sets the system in a reset-ready state.

### Check The Vehicle’s Oil Level

Pull out the dipstick and wipe it clean. Reinsert it fully, then pull it out again to check oil. The oil should be at the proper level. Low or dirty oil can cause problems later.

### Set The Ignition To The “on” Position

Insert the key without starting the engine. Turn it to the “On” position. This powers the dashboard and systems. It allows access to the oil life reset function.

### Ensure Dashboard Lights Are Functional

Look at the dashboard to confirm all lights work. This includes the oil change indicator. A clear display helps with the reset process.

## Reset Using Instrument Cluster

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=access-the-instrument-cluster-menu-using-the-steering-wheel-controls-navigate-to-the-oil-life-reset-option-and-confirm-to-reset-the-oil-life-on-your-ram-1500&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Access the instrument cluster menu using the steering wheel controls. Navigate to the oil life reset option and confirm to reset the oil life on your Ram 1500. This simple process keeps your vehicle’s maintenance alerts accurate.

Resetting the oil life on a Ram 1500 using the instrument cluster is straightforward. This method involves a few simple steps. It ensures your vehicle's maintenance schedule remains accurate. Follow the instructions below for a successful reset.

### Turn Ignition On

Ensure the vehicle is in park. Do not start the engine. Turn the ignition to the "On" position. This lights up the dashboard and activates the instrument cluster.

### Navigate To Oil Life Menu

Locate the steering wheel controls. Use the arrow buttons to find the "Vehicle Info" menu. Scroll through the options until you see "Oil Life." The current oil life percentage will display here.

### Perform Reset Sequence

Press and hold the "OK" button on the steering wheel. Hold until the oil life percentage resets to 100%. This confirms the oil life has been successfully reset. Release the button and turn off the ignition. Your Ram 1500 is now ready for the next oil change cycle.

## Reset Using Uconnect System

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-uconnect-system-in-your-ram-1500-offers-a-simple-way-to-reset-the-oil-life-monitor-this-system-uses-a-touchscreen-interface-that-lets-you-manage-vehicle-settings-quickly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The Uconnect system in your Ram 1500 offers a simple way to reset the oil life monitor. This system uses a touchscreen interface that lets you manage vehicle settings quickly. Resetting the oil life alert through Uconnect helps keep track of your maintenance schedule without extra tools.

### Access Vehicle Settings

Turn on your Ram 1500 and wait for the Uconnect screen to light up. Tap the “Settings” icon on the home screen. This will open the main menu for your vehicle’s controls and preferences.

### Locate Oil Life Option

Scroll through the settings menu to find the “Oil Life” or “Vehicle Info” section. Select it to view current oil life percentage and maintenance alerts. This screen shows how much oil life remains before your next change.

### Confirm Reset

Press the “Reset” button on the oil life screen. A confirmation message will appear asking if you want to reset the oil life. Confirm your choice by selecting “Yes” or “OK.” The oil life percentage will return to 100%, indicating a successful reset.

  


## Verify Oil Life Reset

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-resetting-the-oil-life-on-your-ram-1500-its-important-to-verify-that-the-reset-was-successful-this-step-ensures-your-trucks-maintenance-system-is-accurately-tracking-your-oils-condition&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After resetting the oil life on your Ram 1500, it’s important to verify that the reset was successful. This step ensures your truck’s maintenance system is accurately tracking your oil’s condition. Skipping this can lead to confusion about when your next oil change is due, potentially affecting engine health.

### Check The Instrument Cluster Display

Turn on your Ram 1500 without starting the engine. Look at the instrument cluster where oil life is shown. It should read 100% or display a full reset status.

If you see a lower percentage or an error message, it means the reset didn’t work. You’ll need to try the reset process again. This quick check saves you from missing timely oil changes.

### Use The Uconnect System

Your Ram 1500’s Uconnect screen also shows oil life information. Navigate through the menu to the vehicle info section. Here, you can confirm the oil life percentage.

Seeing 100% on Uconnect confirms the reset. If not, it’s a signal to review the reset steps. This digital confirmation gives you peace of mind about your truck’s maintenance status.

### Test Drive And Recheck

Take a short drive after resetting the oil life. Then, recheck the oil life display. Sometimes the system needs a bit of driving time to update the reset information fully.

Have you ever found your oil life didn’t reset properly until after a drive? This little trick can save you from unnecessary repeat resets. It also confirms that your Ram 1500’s monitoring system is working as it should.

## Tips For Maintaining Oil Life

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-oil-life-of-your-ram-1500-ensures-the-engine-runs-smoothly-good-habits-help-protect-engine-parts-and-improve-fuel-efficiency&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the oil life of your Ram 1500 ensures the engine runs smoothly. Good habits help protect engine parts and improve fuel efficiency. Simple steps extend oil life and keep your truck reliable.

### Regular Oil Changes

Change the oil on time as per the owner’s manual. Old oil loses its ability to lubricate and clean the engine. Fresh oil prevents wear and keeps the engine cool. Set reminders to avoid missing oil changes.

### Use Recommended Oil Type

Choose the oil grade suggested by Ram for your 1500 model. Using the right oil helps maintain engine performance and protects parts. Avoid mixing different oil types, as this can reduce effectiveness.

### Monitor Driving Conditions

Driving in tough conditions affects oil life. Stop-and-go traffic, hot weather, and dusty roads cause oil to degrade faster. Adjust oil change intervals if you drive in these conditions often. Check oil levels regularly to catch problems early.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset Oil Life On Ram 1500?

To reset oil life on a Ram 1500, turn the ignition to ON without starting. Press the accelerator pedal fully three times within 10 seconds. The oil life indicator will reset to 100%.

### When Should I Reset The Oil Life Indicator?

Reset the oil life indicator only after an oil change. This ensures accurate maintenance tracking. Resetting too early can cause incorrect service alerts.

### Can I Reset Oil Life Without A Scan Tool?

Yes, you can reset oil life manually via pedal steps. No scan tool is needed for most Ram 1500 models from 2013 onwards.

### What If Oil Life Won’t Reset On Ram 1500?

If oil life won’t reset, check if the ignition was ON without starting. Repeat the accelerator pedal steps. If still fails, consult a dealer or use diagnostic tools.
