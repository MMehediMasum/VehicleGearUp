**Have you noticed a crack on your car windshield and wondered if it’s something you can fix yourself? That small crack might seem harmless now, but if left untreated, it can spread and become a costly problem.**

The good news is, repairing a crack in your windshield is often easier and cheaper than you think. In this guide, you’ll discover simple steps you can take right away to stop that crack from growing. Keep reading to learn how to save your windshield—and your wallet—before it’s too late.




## Identify The Crack Type

Identifying the type of crack in your car windshield is the first step to effective repair. Different cracks need different approaches and materials. Understanding the crack type helps decide whether you can fix it yourself or need professional help.

Each crack looks unique and grows differently. Spotting the exact type can save time and money. Here are the common types of windshield cracks you should know.

### Hairline Cracks

Hairline cracks are very thin and long. They often appear due to temperature changes or minor impacts. These cracks are usually less than a quarter inch wide but can spread quickly. Repair kits work well for small hairline cracks if treated early.

### Bullseye Cracks

Bullseye cracks look like a circle with a dark center. They happen when an object hits the glass straight on. The impact creates a round shape with small cracks spreading out. These cracks can weaken the windshield, so prompt repair is important.

### Star Breaks

Star breaks have lines radiating from the impact point, like a star. They form after a sharp hit or rock chip. These cracks can spread fast with vibrations or heat. Fixing star breaks quickly prevents the damage from growing larger and more costly.

## Gather Necessary Tools

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=before-you-start-repairing-that-crack-in-your-car-windshield-gathering-the-right-tools-is-essential-having-everything-on-hand-saves-time-and-prevents-frustration-during-the-repair&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Before you start repairing that crack in your car windshield, gathering the right tools is essential. Having everything on hand saves time and prevents frustration during the repair. Missing a key item can affect the quality of the fix and possibly cause more damage.

### Windshield Repair Kit

A windshield repair kit is your main tool. These kits usually include resin, an applicator, curing strips, and sometimes a small syringe or injector.

Choose a kit that matches the size and type of crack you’re dealing with. Some kits work better for small chips, while others handle longer cracks.

Look for kits with clear instructions and good customer reviews. I once tried a cheap kit without clear directions, and the resin didn’t fill the crack properly, leaving bubbles.

### Clean Cloth

A clean cloth is crucial for prepping the windshield surface before repair. Dust, dirt, or moisture can stop the resin from bonding properly.

Use a lint-free cloth to wipe around the crack gently. Avoid paper towels, which can leave fibers behind.

Remember, even a tiny speck of dirt can ruin the repair. Have a microfiber cloth ready and keep it clean throughout the process.

### Safety Equipment

Safety should never be overlooked. Working with resin and glass fragments requires some protective gear.

Wear safety glasses to shield your eyes from shards or splashes. Gloves protect your hands from sticky resin and sharp edges.

Think about the ventilation in your workspace too. Resin fumes can be strong, so a well-ventilated area or a mask can make your repair safer and more comfortable.

  


## Prepare The Windshield

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=clean-the-windshield-thoroughly-to-remove-dirt-and-dust-dry-the-area-completely-to-ensure-the-repair-resin-sticks-well&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Clean the windshield thoroughly to remove dirt and dust. Dry the area completely to ensure the repair resin sticks well. Avoid touching the cracked spot to prevent oils from hands interfering.

Preparing the windshield is a crucial step in the repair process. Before diving into fixing that annoying crack, ensure your windshield is prepped for optimal results. This not only makes the repair more effective but also extends the life of your windshield. Let's get started with the basics.

### Clean The Area

Begin by cleaning the area around the crack. Use a soft, lint-free cloth and a mild glass cleaner. This removes any dirt or debris that could interfere with the repair process. Ensure you clean the area gently to avoid expanding the crack.

### Dry The Surface

Once cleaned, it's essential to dry the surface thoroughly. Moisture can hinder the adhesive properties of repair resin. Use a dry, clean cloth or let it air dry if you have time. If you're in a hurry, a gentle blow with a hairdryer can speed things up.

### Protect Surroundings

It's important to protect the area surrounding the crack. Use masking tape or a similar material to guard the windshield wipers and car paint from any resin spills. This ensures that only the crack is treated, maintaining the appearance of your car. By preparing your windshield correctly, you not only make the repair process smoother but also increase its effectiveness. ## Apply The Resin

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=applying-resin-is-a-key-part-of-fixing-a-cracked-windshield-this-clear-liquid-fills-the-crack-and-stops-it-from-spreading&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Applying resin is a key part of fixing a cracked windshield. This clear liquid fills the crack and stops it from spreading. Proper application ensures a smooth and strong repair. Follow these steps carefully to apply the resin correctly.

### Position The Applicator

Place the applicator over the crack. Make sure it fits snugly on the glass. The applicator acts like a small syringe to inject resin into the crack. Check that it seals tightly to avoid leaks.

### Inject The Resin

Slowly press the resin into the crack. Use steady pressure to push the liquid deep inside. Fill the crack fully to prevent air pockets. Keep injecting until resin oozes out of the crack ends.

### Remove Air Bubbles

Air bubbles weaken the repair. Use a pin or needle to pop visible bubbles. Press the applicator gently to push out trapped air. Repeat this step until the resin looks smooth and clear.

## Cure The Resin

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-filling-the-crack-in-your-car-windshield-with-resin-curing-it-properly-is-crucial-this-step-hardens-the-resin-and-restores-the-glasss-strength&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After filling the crack in your car windshield with resin, curing it properly is crucial. This step hardens the resin and restores the glass’s strength. Proper curing prevents the crack from spreading and ensures a smooth finish. Follow these tips to cure the resin effectively.

### Use Uv Light

UV light helps the resin harden quickly. Many resin kits come with a small UV lamp. Hold the light close to the repaired area and move it slowly over the crack. Make sure the resin gets full exposure to the UV light for about 5 to 10 minutes. If you don’t have a UV lamp, park the car in direct sunlight for natural curing.

### Allow Proper Drying Time

After using UV light, let the resin dry completely. Avoid touching or disturbing the repair during this time. Drying can take from 30 minutes to a few hours depending on resin type and weather. Check the resin’s instructions for exact drying times. Patience here prevents weak spots and ensures a clear finish.

## Finish And Inspect

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=finishing-and-inspecting-your-windshield-repair-is-just-as-important-as-the-initial-fix-this-stage-ensures-that-the-crack-is-properly-sealed-and-the-surface-looks-smooth-and-clear&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Finishing and inspecting your windshield repair is just as important as the initial fix. This stage ensures that the crack is properly sealed and the surface looks smooth and clear. Taking your time here can save you from future problems and give your car a clean, safe finish.

### Remove Excess Resin

After the resin has cured, you’ll often find some extra resin sitting on the surface. Use a razor blade or plastic scraper held at a low angle to carefully remove this excess. Be gentle to avoid scratching the glass or damaging the repair area.

Keep your strokes smooth and consistent. Removing this extra resin creates a flush surface that blends with the rest of the windshield, improving both appearance and safety.

### Polish The Surface

Polishing the repaired area helps restore clarity and smoothness. Use a fine polishing compound and a soft cloth or a polishing pad.

Apply the compound in small circular motions. This step can make a big difference, making the repair almost invisible and improving your view while driving.

### Check For Flaws

Take a close look at the repair under different lighting conditions. Look for bubbles, cracks, or areas where the resin didn’t fully penetrate.

Does the repaired spot feel even with the rest of the glass? If not, it might need a little more work before you consider the job done.

Have you noticed any subtle distortions or cloudiness? These can affect visibility and safety, so addressing them now will pay off later.

## When To Seek Professional Help

Knowing when to seek professional help for a cracked windshield can save you time and money. Some cracks need expert repair to keep your car safe. Ignoring serious damage can lead to bigger problems and costly replacements. Here are key signs that call for professional attention.

### Large Or Deep Cracks

Large cracks are often longer than a few inches. Deep cracks may reach through the glass layers. These cracks weaken the windshield’s strength. Professionals have the tools and skills to fix or replace these safely. DIY fixes cannot handle such damage well.

### Cracks In Driver’s Line Of Sight

Cracks blocking the driver’s clear view pose a safety risk. They can distract or obscure vision during driving. Professionals can repair or replace glass without leaving marks. This ensures your view stays clear and safe on the road.

### Multiple Or Spreading Cracks

Cracks that multiply or spread fast show the glass is unstable. This often happens due to temperature changes or vibrations. Experts can assess the damage and prevent it from worsening. Quick professional action stops further risks and costly repairs.

## Prevent Future Cracks

Preventing future cracks in your car windshield helps keep it safe and clear. Small cracks can grow fast and cause bigger problems. Taking simple steps protects your windshield and saves money on repairs. Focus on how to avoid damage from heat, driving, and weather exposure.

### Avoid Extreme Temperatures

Glass expands and contracts with temperature changes. Sudden heat or cold can stress the windshield. Park your car in shaded areas during hot days. Use sunshades inside the car to reduce heat. In winter, avoid pouring hot water on the windshield. Let ice melt naturally or use a scraper carefully.

### Maintain Safe Driving Distance

Keep a good distance from other vehicles on the road. Rocks and debris can hit your windshield from cars ahead. Driving too close increases the risk of chips and cracks. Slow down on gravel roads or construction zones. Smooth driving helps avoid sudden impacts that harm the glass.

### Use Protective Covers

Cover your windshield when parked for long periods. Protective covers shield the glass from sun, dust, and hail. Choose covers made for windshields that fit well. A cover reduces temperature swings and blocks small debris. Store the cover properly to keep it clean and ready to use.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Can I Fix A Small Crack In My Windshield?

You can repair small cracks using a windshield repair kit. Clean the area, apply resin, and cure it with UV light. This prevents the crack from spreading and restores structural integrity.

### Is Diy Windshield Crack Repair Effective?

DIY repair kits are effective for minor cracks under six inches. They save money and delay the need for replacement. However, professional repair is better for larger or complex cracks.

### How Long Does Windshield Crack Repair Take?

Windshield crack repair typically takes 30 to 60 minutes. The process involves cleaning, resin injection, and curing. Quick repair prevents crack expansion and restores visibility promptly.

### When Should I Replace My Cracked Windshield?

Replace your windshield if cracks exceed six inches or impair vision. Extensive damage weakens safety and cannot be fixed properly. Professional assessment helps determine replacement necessity.
