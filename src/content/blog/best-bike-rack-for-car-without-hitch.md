**Finding the best bike rack for a car without a hitch can be tricky. Many bike racks fit only vehicles with hitch receivers.**



Carrying bikes without a hitch requires racks that attach to the trunk or roof. Trunk-mounted racks like the Allen Sports Deluxe 2-Bike or Bell Cantilever 300 offer easy installation and secure transport. Roof racks also work well but need crossbars.



Some racks fit SUVs, sedans, and hatchbacks, making them versatile. Look for racks with strong straps, padded hooks, and good weight capacity. Brands like MaxxHaul, Yakima, and Hollywood Racks provide durable options. Choose a rack that fits your car type and bike size. This guide helps you find reliable, easy-to-use bike racks without needing a hitch receiver.
