## Jeep Grand Cherokee Basics

The Jeep Grand Cherokee is a popular SUV known for its strong performance and rugged design. It appeals to drivers who want both comfort and off-road ability. Understanding its basics helps you grasp how heavy it is and why weight matters. The Grand Cherokee comes in several models, each with different engines and trim levels. These factors influence the vehicle's overall weight and capabilities.

### Model Variants

The Grand Cherokee offers a few main model variants. These include the standard Grand Cherokee and the Grand Cherokee L, which has three rows of seats. Each variant varies in size and weight. The standard model is lighter, while the L version is larger and heavier due to extra seating and space.

### Engine Options

This SUV has multiple engine choices. Options range from a fuel-efficient V6 to powerful V8 engines. Some models include a diesel engine for better torque. Engine size affects weight because bigger engines weigh more. This also impacts fuel economy and towing capacity.

### Trim Levels

Trim levels add features and change the weight too. Basic trims focus on essentials and weigh less. Higher trims include luxury and technology upgrades that increase weight. Examples include the Laredo, Limited, Overland, and Summit trims. Each trim level offers different equipment and comfort options.
