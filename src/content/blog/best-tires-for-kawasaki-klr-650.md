### Which Tire Size Fits Kawasaki Klr 650 Rear Wheel?



The standard rear tire size for Kawasaki KLR 650 is 130/90-17 (68R). Tusk Dsport® and Dunlop D606 both match this size. This size ensures optimal performance and stability for adventure and dual sport riding.



### Are Tube Type Tires Recommended For Klr 650?



Yes, tube type tires like Tusk Dsport® and Kenda K270 are popular for KLR 650. They offer easier repairs on rough trails. Tube type tires maintain air pressure well during long rides and rough terrains.



### What Is The Difference Between Tusk Dsport® And Waypoint Adventure Tires?

Yes, the MICHELIN Anakee Adventure tire 90/90-21 fits the KLR 650 front wheel. It offers excellent handling on pavement and gravel. This tire enhances stability and control during adventure rides.



### What Maintenance Is Recommended For Klr 650 Tires?
