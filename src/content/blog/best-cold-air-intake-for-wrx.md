Cold air intakes replace the stock air filter and tubing with a design that brings cooler air into the engine. Cooler air is denser, which helps the engine burn fuel more efficiently. This leads to more horsepower and quicker acceleration.



Several brands offer quality cold air intakes for WRX models from different years. Options include AEM, Mishimoto, K&N, RSG, Injen, and Rtunes Racing. Each product fits specific WRX years and provides unique benefits like better airflow, durable filters, and easy installation. Choosing the right intake depends on your model year and performance goals. This guide will help you find the best fit for your WRX.
