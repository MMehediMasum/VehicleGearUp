## Locate The Infotainment System

The infotainment screen sits in the center of the dashboard. It is usually a touchscreen display. This screen shows menus for audio, navigation, and car settings.

### Identifying The Central Display Screen

The central display is a flat screen, about 7 to 10 inches wide. It is located just above the main air vents. The screen stays dark when the car is off and lights up after starting the engine.

### Finding The Control Knobs And Buttons

Below or near the display, you will find control knobs and buttons. These allow you to navigate through menus if the screen is not touch-sensitive. Look for a round knob or buttons labeled with icons like “Menu” or “Settings.”

## Troubleshoot Common Issues

Dealing with time-setting issues on your Audi Q5 can be frustrating, especially if the clock refuses to update correctly. Troubleshooting these common problems doesn’t have to be complicated. Understanding simple fixes can save you time and keep your dashboard accurate.

### Time Not Updating

If your Audi Q5's clock isn’t updating, start by checking if the time zone and daylight saving settings are correct. Sometimes, the system may default to a different region, causing the time to display inaccurately.

Another cause could be a weak connection between the car’s battery and the infotainment system. If the battery was recently disconnected or replaced, the clock might need manual resetting.

Consider this: have you noticed your clock freezing after a software update? This can happen if the system encounters bugs during the update process. In such cases, a system reset often helps.

### System Reset Tips

Resetting the system can resolve many time display problems. You can usually reset the infotainment system by holding down the power button for about 10 seconds until the screen goes black and restarts.

If that doesn’t work, try disconnecting the car battery for a few minutes, then reconnect it. This hard reset can clear glitches that prevent the clock from updating properly.

Remember, after a reset, you might need to re-enter your preferences, including radio presets and paired devices. Keep your manual handy for specific reset instructions tailored to your Audi Q5 model year.
