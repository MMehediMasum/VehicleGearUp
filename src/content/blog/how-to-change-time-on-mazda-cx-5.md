**Is your Mazda CX-5 showing the wrong time? It’s frustrating when your clock doesn’t match your schedule.**

But don’t worry—you can fix it quickly. Whether daylight saving time changed or you just want the right time on your dashboard, this guide will walk you through every step. By the end, you’ll have your Mazda’s clock set perfectly, so you never miss a beat.

Keep reading to find out exactly how to change the time on your Mazda CX-5 without any hassle.



## Locate The Clock Settings

Locating the clock settings on your Mazda CX-5 is simple and quick. The clock is part of the vehicle’s infotainment system. Knowing where to find it helps you adjust the time easily.

This section guides you through accessing the clock settings. Follow these steps to set the correct time without hassle.

### Accessing The Infotainment System

Start your car and turn on the infotainment screen. The main menu appears on the display. Use the control knob or touchscreen to navigate through options. The system is user-friendly and clear.

Look for the settings icon. It usually looks like a gear or wrench. Select this icon to open the settings menu. This area contains all adjustable features of your Mazda CX-5.

### Finding The Clock Menu

Inside the settings menu, find the option labeled "Clock" or "Time." This menu controls the vehicle’s clock settings. Click on it to open the clock settings screen.

You can now see options to adjust hours and minutes. Use the control knob or touchscreen to change the time. Confirm your changes to save the new time setting.




## Adjusting Time Manually

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-time-manually-on-your-mazda-cx-5-is-a-simple-process-it-allows-you-to-set-the-correct-hour-and-minute-without-relying-on-automatic-updates&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the time manually on your Mazda CX-5 is a simple process. It allows you to set the correct hour and minute without relying on automatic updates. This is useful when traveling or after a power reset.

The following instructions will help you change the time quickly and accurately using the vehicle’s controls.

### Selecting Hour And Minute

Start by accessing the clock settings on the infotainment screen or dashboard menu. Use the control knob or touchscreen to highlight the time adjustment option.

* Turn the knob or tap to select the hour field first.
* Rotate or tap to increase or decrease the hour value.
* Confirm the hour and move to the minute field.
* Adjust the minutes the same way by rotating or tapping.

Ensure the time format (12-hour or 24-hour) matches your preference before proceeding.

### Saving The Changes

After setting the correct hour and minute, find the save or confirm button on the screen or control panel.

* Press the button to lock in your new time settings.
* Wait for the system to update the clock display.
* Double-check the time shown to confirm accuracy.

Changes take effect immediately and remain until adjusted again.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-problems-helps-keep-your-mazda-cx-5-clock-accurate-and-reliable-small-issues-can-stop-the-clock-from-updating-properly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common problems helps keep your Mazda CX-5 clock accurate and reliable. Small issues can stop the clock from updating properly. Understanding these problems saves time and frustration. Simple checks often fix the issue quickly.

### Clock Not Updating

The clock may not update due to a weak battery or software glitches. Verify your vehicle’s battery is charged and in good condition. Check if the clock settings were saved correctly after adjustment. Sometimes, incorrect time zone settings cause the clock to display wrong time.

* Ensure the ignition is fully on while setting the clock.
* Double-check the time zone and daylight saving settings.
* Look for system updates in your vehicle’s infotainment menu.

### Resetting The System

Resetting the system can solve persistent clock errors. This resets the software controlling the clock and infotainment system. Use this if the clock does not respond to normal settings changes. The reset process differs by model year, so consult the owner’s manual for steps.

* Turn off the vehicle and open the driver’s door.
* Press and hold the power button for about 10 seconds.
* Wait for the system to reboot and the Mazda logo to appear.
* Set the clock again after the reset completes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Set The Clock On A Mazda Cx-5?

To set the clock, start your Mazda CX-5 and access the infotainment system. Navigate to the settings menu, select "Clock," then adjust the hours and minutes using the touchscreen or control knob. Save your changes to update the time.

### Can I Change Time On Mazda Cx-5 Manually?

Yes, you can manually change the time via the infotainment system. Use the touchscreen or control dial to access the clock settings. Adjust the time manually, then confirm to save the new settings immediately.

### Does Mazda Cx-5 Update Time Automatically?

Mazda CX-5 models with GPS or connected services can update time automatically. Ensure your vehicle’s system has location and network access enabled for this feature to work properly.

### Why Is My Mazda Cx-5 Clock Incorrect?

The clock may be incorrect due to lost settings after battery disconnection or time zone changes. Reset the clock manually through the infotainment system to fix this issue quickly.
