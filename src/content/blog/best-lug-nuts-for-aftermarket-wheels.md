## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Lug Nuts Fit Aftermarket Ford Fusion Wheels?



M12x1. 5 lug nuts with a 19mm hex size fit Ford Fusion aftermarket wheels. These nuts ensure a secure and proper fit for most Ford Fusion models.



### Are Closed-end Or Open-end Lug Nuts Better For Aftermarket Wheels?



Closed-end lug nuts protect threads from dirt and corrosion, ideal for aftermarket wheels. Open-end nuts allow longer studs but offer less protection.



### Can M12x1.5 Lug Nuts Fit Multiple Car Brands?



Yes, M12x1. 5 lug nuts fit various brands like Ford, Honda, Toyota, and Lexus. Always check compatibility with your specific wheel and vehicle model.



### What Is The Advantage Of Spline Lug Nuts For Aftermarket Wheels?



Spline lug nuts provide better grip and security, reducing risk of loosening. They often require a special key, enhancing theft protection for aftermarket wheels.



### Are Chrome Lug Nuts Durable For Everyday Driving?



Chrome lug nuts are corrosion-resistant and durable, suitable for daily driving. They also add a polished, stylish look to aftermarket wheels.



### How Many Lug Nuts Are Needed For A Complete Aftermarket Wheel Set?



Typically, 20 lug nuts are sold in sets, enough for 4 wheels with 5 nuts each. Always verify your wheel’s lug count before purchasing.
