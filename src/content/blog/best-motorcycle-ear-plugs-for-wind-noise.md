Soft silicone plugs like EARasers provide a good fit inside the ear canal. Helmet ear covers, such as those from MOTO4U and GRIRIW, block wind noise around the ears while allowing breathability.



Removable ear pads from Milisten and POPETPOP add extra comfort and warmth in cold weather. Cabilock’s soft ear pads fit inside helmets to reduce noise and improve comfort. Choosing the right ear plugs or covers helps riders enjoy quieter, safer rides every time.
