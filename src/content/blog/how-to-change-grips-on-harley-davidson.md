## Tools Needed

Changing grips on a Harley Davidson requires the right tools for a smooth job. Having the correct tools makes the process easier and safer. The tools fall into two main groups: basic hand tools and specialized grip tools. Each group serves a specific purpose during the grip replacement.

### Basic Hand Tools

* Screwdrivers – flathead and Phillips for removing screws and covers
* Allen wrenches – to loosen bolts holding the grips or controls
* Utility knife – to cut old grips carefully without damaging the handlebar
* Razor blade – to remove stubborn grip residue
* Clean cloth – to wipe down the handlebar before new grips
* Soap and water or rubbing alcohol – for cleaning the handlebar surface

### Specialized Grip Tools

* Grip puller tool – designed to remove grips without damage
* Compressed air gun – helps slide grips off using air pressure
* Grip adhesive or glue – secures new grips in place
* Rubber mallet – gently taps grips into place without harm
* Heat gun (optional) – softens grips for easier removal or fitting

### Material Options

Grips come in different materials. Each material offers unique benefits. Rubber grips provide good cushioning and reduce vibration. They are soft and easy to hold. Leather grips look classic and add style. They can get slippery when wet. Metal grips offer a strong, durable feel. They may get hot or cold with weather. Foam grips are light and comfortable. They absorb shocks well but wear out faster.

### Grip Sizes

Choosing the right grip size is important for control. Grips come in different diameters and lengths. Thin grips fit smaller hands better. Thick grips help riders with larger hands. Length matters for extra hand space or control. Measure your hand size before buying. The right size reduces hand fatigue and improves safety.

### Style And Comfort

Grips should match your bike’s look and your comfort needs. Some grips have textured patterns for better hold. Others are smooth and simple for a clean look. Comfort grips reduce hand strain on long rides. Some grips include heating elements for cold weather. Pick a style that fits your personality and riding style. Comfort and style go hand in hand.

Old grips can be tricky to remove but soaking them with rubbing alcohol helps loosen the glue. Gently twisting and pulling will free the grips without damaging the handlebars. Use a small flat tool carefully to lift stubborn edges.

Removing the old grips from your Harley Davidson can feel like a daunting task. But with a little patience and the right techniques, you can do it smoothly. This process is an essential step in ensuring a perfect fit for your new grips.

### Loosening Techniques

Start by ensuring your bike is securely parked. You don't want it shifting while you're working. Use a screwdriver to carefully pry open the end of the grip. Insert it gently to avoid damaging the handlebar. If the grip is stubborn, try using a bit of lubricant like WD-40 to ease the process. Rotate the grip back and forth to break the seal. This twisting motion helps loosen the grip, making removal easier. Be cautious not to apply excessive force, as this could harm the throttle tube or handlebars.

Once the grip is off, you'll likely notice some adhesive residue left behind. This sticky mess can be a bit of a nuisance. Use a clean cloth and a mild adhesive remover to wipe it away. Products like Goo Gone work well without damaging the chrome or paint. If you prefer a more natural solution, a mix of baking soda and water can also do the trick. ## Installing New Grips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-new-grips-on-your-harley-davidson-adds-comfort-and-style-the-process-needs-care-for-a-secure-fit-that-lasts&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing new grips on your Harley Davidson adds comfort and style. The process needs care for a secure fit that lasts. Follow these steps to set up your grips properly and enjoy a smooth ride.

### Applying Adhesive

Use a grip adhesive made for motorcycles. Apply a thin layer inside the grip tube. Avoid using too much; excess glue can cause slipping. Wait a few seconds for the adhesive to get tacky. This helps the grip stick firmly to the handlebar.

### Sliding Grips Into Place

Push the grip onto the handlebar with steady pressure. Twist slightly to help it slide on easily. Keep pushing until the grip reaches the handlebar end. If the grip is tight, use a little rubbing alcohol to ease the slide. Alcohol dries fast and won’t harm the adhesive.

### Aligning Correctly

Check the grip’s position before the adhesive sets. Align any patterns or logos so they face the right way. Make sure the throttle grip can move freely without binding. Hold the grip firmly in place for a minute or two. This ensures it bonds well and stays put during rides.

## Maintenance Tips

Proper maintenance keeps your Harley Davidson grips comfortable and safe. Regular care extends their life and keeps them looking fresh. Follow simple steps to maintain your grips after installation.

### Cleaning New Grips

Clean new grips gently before use. Use mild soap and warm water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage rubber or leather. Rinse with clean water and dry fully with a soft cloth. This removes any factory residue and improves grip texture.

### Regular Inspection

Check grips often for wear and tear. Look for cracks, tears, or loose parts. Worn grips reduce control and comfort. Replace grips immediately if damaged. Tighten any loose screws or clamps. Regular inspection prevents accidents and keeps your ride smooth.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Remove Old Grips On A Harley Davidson?

To remove old grips, use a flat screwdriver to gently pry under the grip’s edge. Apply rubbing alcohol or grip glue remover. Twist and pull the grip off carefully to avoid damaging the handlebar.

### What Tools Are Needed To Change Harley Davidson Grips?

You need a flathead screwdriver, rubbing alcohol or grip adhesive remover, new grips, and grip glue or adhesive. Optional tools include compressed air and a utility knife for stubborn grips.

### How To Install New Grips On A Harley Davidson?

Apply rubbing alcohol or grip glue inside the new grips. Slide them onto the clean handlebars quickly before the glue dries. Adjust the position, then let the adhesive set for at least an hour.

### Can I Change Grips Without Damaging The Handlebars?

Yes, using proper tools and gentle techniques prevents damage. Avoid sharp tools that scratch the bars. Use lubricants like rubbing alcohol or grip remover to ease grip removal.
