The best battery size for a 2020 Ram 1500 is Group 94R or H7. These sizes fit perfectly and provide optimal power. Choosing the right size ensures compatibility and reliable starting performance for your truck.



### How Long Does The Acdelco Gold 94ragm Battery Last?



The ACDelco Gold 94RAGM battery offers a 36-month warranty. It typically lasts around 3 years with proper maintenance. Its AGM technology enhances durability and performance in extreme weather conditions.



### Are Agm Batteries Better For Ram 1500 Trucks?



Yes, AGM batteries like the ACDelco Gold and UPLUS models offer better durability. They handle vibrations well and have faster recharge times. AGM batteries also provide superior cold-cranking amps for reliable starts in cold weather.



### Can I Use Maintenance-free Batteries In My Ram 1500?



Absolutely, maintenance-free AGM batteries such as Mighty Max MM-H7 are ideal for Ram 1500. They require no water refilling and resist leaks. These batteries offer convenience and long-lasting power without regular upkeep.



### What Is The Cold Cranking Amp (cca) Requirement For Ram 1500 Batteries?



Ram 1500 batteries typically require around 760 to 850 CCA. Higher CCA ratings ensure reliable engine starts in cold weather. Batteries like the 1AUTODEPOT AGM with 850 CCA are excellent choices for winter conditions.
