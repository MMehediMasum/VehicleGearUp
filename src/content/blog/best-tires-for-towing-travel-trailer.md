## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Tires For Towing A Travel Trailer?



The best tires for towing travel trailers include heavy-duty, load-rated tires like Free Country Grand Ride and Libra Trailer Parts. They offer durability, high load capacity, and resistance to wear, ensuring safe and smooth towing over long distances.



### How Do Trailer Ramps Help With Tire Changes?



Trailer ramps like Camco Trailer Aid Plus lift tires for easy access. They reduce the effort needed to change tires without unloading cargo, making tire maintenance quicker and safer for travel trailers with tandem axles.



### What Load Range Is Ideal For Trailer Tires?



Load ranges C, D, and E are common for trailer tires, with higher ranges supporting heavier loads. For towing, choose tires with at least Load Range C or above for better durability and safety.



### Why Choose Pneumatic Tires For Trailer Dollies?



Pneumatic tires, like those on MaxxHaul trailer dollies, provide better shock absorption and maneuverability. They help move heavy trailers smoothly over rough surfaces and reduce strain during loading and unloading.



### Are Steel-belted Radial Tires Good For Travel Trailers?



Yes, steel-belted radial tires like those from Free Country offer enhanced stability and longer tread life. They improve fuel efficiency and provide better handling compared to bias-ply tires on travel trailers.



### How Important Is Tire Size For Towing Trailers?



Tire size affects load capacity and stability. Sizes like ST205/75R14 or ST205/75R15 are popular for travel trailers, balancing strength and ride comfort. Always match tire size to manufacturer recommendations for safe towing.
