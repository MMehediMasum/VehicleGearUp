## Locate The Maintenance Light

The maintenance light on a 2013 Toyota Highlander signals when regular service is due. Knowing where this light appears helps you reset it properly after maintenance. The light stays on as a reminder to keep your vehicle in good condition.

Locating the maintenance light is the first step in resetting it. This light is part of the dashboard display, designed to catch your attention easily. It looks like a small icon or message that appears among other indicators.

### Dashboard Location

The maintenance light is usually found within the instrument cluster. This cluster sits right behind the steering wheel. It includes the speedometer, fuel gauge, and other important lights.

Look closely for a wrench or the words “Maintenance Required.” This is the symbol Toyota uses for this alert.

### Digital Display Screen

Some Highlander models use a digital screen to show the maintenance reminder. This screen is part of the instrument panel. It shows text alerts and symbols clearly.

Check the center of the dashboard where the screen is located. The maintenance light may blink or stay steady until reset.

### Indicator Light Color

The maintenance light typically glows orange or yellow. This color signals caution, not an emergency. It reminds you to schedule service soon.

Understanding this color helps you identify the light quickly among other dashboard signals.

## Use The Odometer Button

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-odometer-button-is-key-to-resetting-the-maintenance-light-on-a-2013-toyota-highlander-it-allows-you-to-clear-the-alert-without-special-tools&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The odometer button is key to resetting the maintenance light on a 2013 Toyota Highlander. It allows you to clear the alert without special tools. This method is quick and easy to perform. Follow the simple steps below to use the odometer button effectively.

### Press And Hold The Odometer

Locate the odometer button on the dashboard. Press and hold it firmly. Keep holding the button until the maintenance light starts blinking. This usually takes about 10 seconds. The blinking shows the reset process has begun.

### Turn The Ignition To On

While still holding the odometer button, turn the ignition key to the "On" position. Do not start the engine. Keep holding the button for a few more seconds. Release the button once the maintenance light turns off. This confirms the reset was successful.




## Reset The Maintenance Light

Resetting the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Highlander is an essential step after completing routine services like oil changes. This indicator helps you keep track of when your vehicle needs attention, but it won’t turn off automatically. Clearing it ensures your dashboard is accurate and prevents unnecessary distractions while driving.

### Wait For The Light To Blink

Start by turning the ignition to the "ON" position without starting the engine. Press and hold the trip reset button located on the dashboard. Keep holding it as you watch the maintenance light; it will start to blink after about 10 seconds.

This blinking signals that the system is ready to be reset. Have you noticed how holding the button feels like a small but satisfying pause? It’s a simple action that tells you the car’s computer is listening.

### Release The Button

Once the maintenance light begins to blink, release the trip reset button immediately. The light should stop blinking and then turn off, confirming the reset is complete.

If the light stays on, repeat the process. It’s a quick reset that puts you back in control of your vehicle’s maintenance schedule without needing a mechanic. Don’t you appreciate how a few seconds can save you a trip to the shop?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Maintenance Light On A 2013 Toyota Highlander?

To reset the maintenance light, turn the ignition to "ON" without starting. Press the odometer button until "TRIP A" displays. Turn the ignition off, then press and hold the odometer button. Turn ignition back to "ON" while holding the button.

Release when the light resets.

### What Tools Are Needed To Reset The Maintenance Light?

No tools are required to reset the maintenance light on a 2013 Toyota Highlander. The process involves using the ignition switch and odometer/trip button only. This makes it simple to perform at home without any specialized equipment.

### How Often Should I Reset The Maintenance Light?

Reset the maintenance light after each scheduled service or oil change. This keeps your vehicle’s maintenance reminders accurate. Typically, the light resets every 5,000 miles or as specified in the owner’s manual.

### Can Resetting The Light Fix Other Maintenance Issues?

No, resetting the light only clears the maintenance reminder. It does not fix mechanical or service-related problems. Always perform recommended maintenance before resetting the light to ensure your vehicle runs safely.
