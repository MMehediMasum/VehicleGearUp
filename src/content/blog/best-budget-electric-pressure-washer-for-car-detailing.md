Most models offer 1. 5 to 2 hours of continuous use per full charge. Some come with dual batteries for extended cleaning sessions. Battery capacity ranges from 21V to 48V, ensuring powerful and long-lasting performance for car detailing and other cleaning tasks.



### Are These Pressure Washers Suitable For Delicate Car Surfaces?



Yes, adjustable nozzles allow you to control water pressure. This feature helps protect delicate paint and surfaces from damage. The 6-in-1 nozzle design offers versatility, making them ideal for gentle yet effective car detailing.



### How Portable Are These Cordless Pressure Washers For Outdoor Use?



They are highly portable and lightweight, designed for easy transport. Cordless operation removes the hassle of cords or hoses. Compact size and included hoses make them perfect for outdoor cleaning like cars, bikes, patios, and garden areas.



### What Water Pressure Do These Budget Washers Typically Provide?



They provide between 750 PSI to 980 PSI of water pressure. This range is powerful enough for car detailing but safe for most surfaces. Adjustable nozzles help fine-tune pressure for different cleaning needs.



### Can These Pressure Washers Be Used For Multiple Cleaning Tasks?



Yes, they are versatile and great for cars, bikes, patios, floors, and gardens. The included 6-in-1 nozzles and adjustable settings support various cleaning applications. These washers combine convenience with multi-surface cleaning efficiency.



### How Long Does It Take To Recharge The Batteries?



Recharge time usually ranges from 3 to 5 hours depending on battery capacity. Dual battery models allow alternating use, minimizing downtime. Fast charging ensures you’re ready for your next cleaning task quickly.
