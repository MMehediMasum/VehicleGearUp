### Accessing The Menu

Turn the ignition to the “On” position without starting the engine. Locate the buttons on the steering wheel’s right side. Press the menu button to open the main display. The screen will show various options like “Fuel Economy,” “Settings,” and “Service.”

### Navigating To Service

Use the up and down arrows to scroll through the menu. Highlight the “Service” option. Press the OK button to enter the service menu. Here, you will find options related to oil life and maintenance reminders.

### Resetting The Indicator

Scroll to “Perform Service Reset” or a similar option. Press and hold the OK button for a few seconds. The display will show a confirmation message. Confirm the reset by pressing OK again. The service indicator will now turn off. Turn off the ignition and start the engine to check the reset.

## Manual Reset Method

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-manual-reset-method-is-essential-for-resetting-the-perform-service-indicator-on-the-2018-ram-2500-this-technique-involves-a-straightforward-process-without-using-specialized-tools&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The manual reset method is essential for resetting the 'Perform Service' indicator on the 2018 Ram 2500. This technique involves a straightforward process without using specialized tools. It can be performed by most vehicle owners comfortably at home. Understanding how to manually reset the service light can save time and trips to the mechanic. Below, we explore the steps involved in the manual reset method.

### Battery Disconnect Procedure

Disconnecting the battery is a common reset method. It helps clear minor electronic glitches in your vehicle. Follow these simple steps:

* Turn off the ignition and remove the key from the ignition switch.
* Open the hood and locate the battery.
* Use a wrench to loosen the negative battery cable clamp.
* Carefully remove the negative cable from the battery terminal.
* Wait for about 10 minutes to allow the system to reset.
* Reconnect the negative cable and tighten the clamp securely.

Once reconnected, start the engine and check if the service light has reset. This method can resolve many minor issues. It's a quick fix for the service indicator.

### Potential Risks

While the battery disconnect procedure is simple, it carries potential risks. Disconnecting the battery may reset radio and clock settings. You might lose saved preferences in your vehicle’s system. Additionally, the vehicle’s onboard computer may need recalibration. This may affect the vehicle's performance temporarily.

Always handle battery connections with care. Ensure all electrical components are off before disconnecting the battery. Be cautious to avoid damage or injury.

## Troubleshooting Reset Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-service-light-on-your-2018-ram-2500-can-be-straightforward-but-sometimes-issues-pop-up-that-make-the-process-tricky-troubleshooting-these-problems-helps-ensure-your-trucks-maintenance-reminders-stay-accurate&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the service light on your 2018 Ram 2500 can be straightforward, but sometimes issues pop up that make the process tricky. Troubleshooting these problems helps ensure your truck’s maintenance reminders stay accurate. Let’s look at common reset problems and when it might be time to get professional help.

### Common Problems

One frequent issue is the service light not turning off even after following the reset steps. This can happen if the ignition is not in the correct position during the reset or if the procedure isn’t completed within the required time frame.

Another problem is the display showing an error or no response at all. This often points to a communication glitch between the truck’s computer and the dashboard. Sometimes, a weak battery or low voltage can interfere with the reset process.

If you notice the service light coming back on quickly after a reset, it could mean the system detected an unresolved maintenance need or a sensor malfunction. Ignoring this may lead to more serious issues down the road.

### When To Visit A Dealer

If you’ve tried resetting the service light multiple times without success, it might be time to visit your dealer. They have specialized diagnostic tools that can read error codes and pinpoint hidden problems.

Dealers can also update your truck’s software if outdated firmware is causing the reset failure. Don’t overlook this step—software updates can fix bugs that prevent proper reset functions.

Ask yourself: Are you comfortable troubleshooting this on your own, or is the issue beyond simple fixes? When in doubt, professional help can save you time and avoid potential damage.
