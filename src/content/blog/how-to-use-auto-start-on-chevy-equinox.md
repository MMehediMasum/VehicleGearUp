## Activating Auto Start

Activate Auto Start on your Chevy Equinox by using the key fob or the vehicle’s touchscreen menu. This feature lets you start the engine remotely, warming or cooling the car before you enter. Ensure the vehicle is locked and follow simple prompts for easy use.

Activating the Auto Start feature on your Chevy Equinox is straightforward. This feature lets you start your car remotely. It ensures the cabin is comfortable before you enter. Auto Start is especially useful during extreme weather. Learn how to activate it using simple steps.

### Using The Key Fob

Your key fob is a powerful tool. Ensure you're within 200 feet of the vehicle. First, press the lock button on the key fob. Hold it for a few seconds. Next, press and hold the remote start button. It's usually marked with a circular arrow. The engine will start. You will hear the engine start and see the lights flash.

### Steps On The Infotainment System

The Infotainment System offers another way. Begin by accessing the settings menu. Locate the "Vehicle" settings option. Tap on it. Find "Remote Start" in the list. Select it and enable the feature. Confirm your selection. Your Equinox is now ready for remote start using the system.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-the-auto-start-feature-on-your-chevy-equinox-can-make-your-mornings-easier-but-sometimes-it-doesnt-work-as-expected-troubleshooting-these-common-issues-can-save-you-time-and-frustration&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using the auto start feature on your Chevy Equinox can make your mornings easier, but sometimes it doesn’t work as expected. Troubleshooting these common issues can save you time and frustration. Understanding why the system fails to activate or what limits its operation helps you get the most out of this convenient feature.

### When Auto Start Doesn’t Activate

If your Chevy Equinox’s auto start doesn’t activate, the first thing to check is whether all conditions for activation are met. The vehicle usually won’t start remotely if the hood is open, the doors are unlocked, or if the key fob is inside the car.

Another common reason is if the engine is already running or if the vehicle is in gear. Have you noticed the remote start button flashing or your car’s lights behaving oddly? These are signals that the system detected a problem and stopped the start process.

Try locking the doors with the key fob before attempting the remote start again. If it still doesn’t work, you might want to reset the system by locking and unlocking the vehicle manually, then try the remote start once more.

### Battery And Range Limitations

Your key fob’s battery plays a crucial role in the auto start function. A weak or dead battery might prevent the signal from reaching your Equinox. Have you changed your key fob battery recently? It’s a simple fix that often resolves remote start failures.

The distance between you and your vehicle also matters. The remote start has a limited range, usually up to 300 feet. If you’re too far away, the signal won’t reach your car. Check your surroundings—interference from buildings or other electronics can reduce the effective range.

Additionally, extremely cold or hot weather can affect battery performance, both in the key fob and the vehicle. If your car doesn’t start remotely during harsh weather, consider these environmental factors before assuming a system malfunction.

## Safety Tips For Auto Start Use

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-the-auto-start-feature-on-your-chevy-equinox-can-make-your-mornings-easier-and-your-drives-more-comfortable-however-its-important-to-prioritize-safety-while-enjoying-this-convenience&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using the auto start feature on your Chevy Equinox can make your mornings easier and your drives more comfortable. However, it's important to prioritize safety while enjoying this convenience. Being aware of potential risks and taking simple precautions can protect you and others around you.

### Avoiding Carbon Monoxide Risks

Never use the auto start function in an enclosed space like a garage. Carbon monoxide is a dangerous, odorless gas that can build up quickly and cause serious harm. Even if the garage door is open, ensure there's plenty of ventilation before starting your vehicle remotely.

Think about how close your car is to your home’s windows or air vents. Exhaust fumes can enter your living space and pose health risks. Always park your Equinox in a well-ventilated area to keep your family safe.

### Locking The Vehicle During Remote Start

Make sure your Chevy Equinox is locked before using the remote start. This keeps your vehicle secure and prevents unauthorized access while the engine is running. Many owners overlook this simple step, but it’s crucial for your safety and peace of mind.

Did you know that some auto start systems automatically lock the doors? If yours doesn’t, develop a habit of locking manually before activating remote start. This small action protects your belongings and reduces the risk of theft.
