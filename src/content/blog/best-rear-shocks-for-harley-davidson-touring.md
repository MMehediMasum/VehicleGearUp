## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Rear Shocks For Harley Davidson Touring?



The best rear shocks for Harley Davidson Touring offer durability, comfort, and improved handling. Options include Progressive Suspension, Mofun, TCMT, and WOWTK. These shocks fit various Harley models and years, enhancing ride quality and performance on long rides and rough roads.



### How Do Adjustable Rear Shocks Benefit Harley Touring Riders?



Adjustable rear shocks let riders customize preload and damping settings. This improves ride comfort and stability based on load and road conditions. Brands like Mofun and WeiSen offer preload adjustable shocks for Harley Touring models, allowing a smoother and safer riding experience.



### Are Air Ride Suspension Kits Good For Harley Touring Bikes?



Yes, air ride suspension kits like NEWZQ and XFMT provide superior comfort and load handling. They allow on-the-fly ride height adjustment and better shock absorption. These kits improve touring performance and reduce rider fatigue on long journeys.



### Can I Use Lowering Rear Shocks On My Harley Touring?



Lowering rear shocks like Mofun and Wsays kits are compatible with Harley Touring models. They lower the bike’s center of gravity, improving stability and rider confidence. These shocks are preload adjustable to maintain comfort after lowering.



### What Makes Remote Reservoir Shocks Suitable For Harley Touring?



Remote reservoir shocks, such as TCMT and Mofun models, offer better heat dissipation and consistent damping. This results in improved shock performance during long rides. They handle heavy loads and rough terrain effectively, enhancing Harley Touring suspension reliability.



### Are Progressive Suspension Shocks Reliable For Harley Touring?



Progressive Suspension shocks are highly reliable and heavy-duty. Their 12-inch length and black anodized finish provide durability and style. They deliver excellent ride comfort and stability, making them a popular choice for Harley Davidson Touring motorcycles.
