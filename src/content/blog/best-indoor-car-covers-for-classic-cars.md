## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Indoor Classic Car Covers?



Indoor car covers for classic cars are usually made of soft, breathable fabrics like polyester or satin. These materials prevent scratches and allow moisture to escape, protecting your car from dust and humidity without causing damage to the paint or interior.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size For A Classic Car Cover?



Measure your classic car’s length accurately, then select a cover that fits within that range. Most covers list compatible car lengths, ensuring a snug fit that protects without excess fabric causing wrinkles or dust accumulation.



### Are Waterproof Car Covers Necessary For Indoor Storage?



Waterproof covers are not essential indoors but offer extra protection against spills and humidity. Breathable, dustproof covers are typically sufficient for indoor storage, preventing moisture buildup while keeping dust and dirt off your classic car.



### Can I Use Outdoor Car Covers Indoors For Classic Cars?



Yes, outdoor covers can be used indoors but may be bulkier. Indoor-specific covers are lighter and softer, designed to prevent scratches and allow better airflow, making them ideal for protecting classic cars in garages or showrooms.



### How Often Should I Clean My Indoor Car Cover?



Clean your indoor car cover every 3-6 months or when visibly dirty. Regular cleaning prevents dust buildup and maintains fabric breathability, ensuring your classic car stays protected without trapped moisture or dirt that can cause damage.



### Do Custom Car Covers Offer Better Protection For Classic Cars?



Custom covers provide a perfect fit tailored to your car’s exact dimensions. This reduces fabric movement, preventing scratches and dust accumulation, and often includes features like UV protection and water resistance, offering superior protection for valuable classic cars.
