Many options come with fan shrouds and relay kits to improve airflow and control. Brands like OHOB-COOL, Smileracing, and ECMRAD offer reliable radiators designed for Chevy S10 V8 swaps. These radiators fit models from 1982 to 2005 and work with popular V8 engines like the 5. 7L Corvette. Choosing the right radiator ensures your truck stays cool and runs smoothly on every drive.
