## Locating The Power Controls

Knowing where the power controls are in your Chevy Equinox EV is the first step to confidently turning off your vehicle. These controls are designed for ease of use, but they might not be immediately obvious if you're new to electric vehicles. Understanding exactly where to find the ignition button and how to use the key fob can save you time and frustration.

### Finding The Ignition Button

The ignition button in the Chevy Equinox EV is usually located on the dashboard, near the steering wheel. It’s a round, clearly marked button that you press to start or stop the car.

If you’ve ever driven a push-to-start car, this will feel familiar. However, some drivers initially search for a traditional key slot and miss this simple button.

Try looking just to the right or left of the steering column. Pressing the ignition button once will turn off the vehicle, but make sure your car is in park first. Have you noticed how quickly the car responds to this button? That’s part of the electric car’s design for smooth operation.

### Using The Key Fob

Your Chevy Equinox EV’s key fob does more than just lock and unlock the doors. It also has a feature to remotely turn off the vehicle in some models.

Look for a button on the fob marked with a power or engine icon. Holding this button down for a few seconds can power down your car without needing to be inside it.

This is especially useful if you want to ensure the vehicle is off from a distance. Have you ever forgotten to turn off your car and wished you could do it remotely? The key fob makes that worry disappear.

## Shutting Down The Vehicle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=shutting-down-the-chevy-equinox-ev-is-simple-and-safe-proper-shutdown-helps-protect-the-vehicles-battery-and-systems&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Shutting down the Chevy Equinox EV is simple and safe. Proper shutdown helps protect the vehicle’s battery and systems. Follow the right steps to turn off the car smoothly.

### Pressing The Power Button

Locate the power button on the dashboard or center console. Press the button once firmly to switch off the vehicle. The screen and engine will shut down quickly. Hold the button if the car does not turn off immediately.

### Ensuring The Vehicle Is In Park

Check the gear shift lever position before turning off the car. Make sure the vehicle is in the “Park” (P) mode. The car will not shut down if it is in any other gear. This step prevents accidental movement and ensures safety.

## Turning Off Ev Features

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-off-the-ev-features-in-your-chevy-equinox-ev-allows-more-control-over-how-the-vehicle-operates-these-features-help-save-energy-but-sometimes-you-may-want-to-disable-them&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning off the EV features in your Chevy Equinox EV allows more control over how the vehicle operates. These features help save energy but sometimes you may want to disable them. Understanding how to turn off EV functions helps you drive exactly how you prefer.

### Disabling Regenerative Braking

Regenerative braking recovers energy when slowing down. To disable it, locate the settings on your dashboard menu. Select the regenerative braking option and switch it off. This stops the car from slowing down automatically when you release the accelerator. It also reduces energy recovery to the battery.

### Switching Off Ev Mode

EV mode uses only the electric motor to power the vehicle. To turn it off, press the EV mode button usually found near the gear selector. The car will then use the gasoline engine along with the electric motor. This helps in situations needing more power or longer driving range.

### Vehicle Won't Turn Off

Imagine you're ready to leave your car, but the engine just won't shut down. This can be frustrating and worrying. ## Safety Tips For Powering Down

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=powering-down-your-chevy-equinox-ev-safely-is-crucial-to-avoid-any-unexpected-issues-or-accidents-taking-a-few-simple-precautions-can-protect-both-you-and-your-vehicle&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Powering down your Chevy Equinox EV safely is crucial to avoid any unexpected issues or accidents. Taking a few simple precautions can protect both you and your vehicle. Let’s look at some safety tips that help you turn off your EV securely and with confidence.

### Avoiding Accidental Starts

Accidental starts can happen if you’re not careful, especially with an electric vehicle’s silent engine. Always make sure the key fob is out of reach or turned off before exiting the car. This prevents the vehicle from starting unexpectedly.

Double-check that your foot is off the accelerator pedal before turning off the car. Even a slight touch can cause the vehicle to move unintentionally. Have you ever felt the car lurch when you thought it was off? That’s why a careful shutdown is essential.

Also, avoid leaving electronic devices or heavy objects near the start button or gear selector. These can accidentally press the controls and cause the car to start or shift gears. Keeping your space clear reduces the risk of surprises.

### Proper Use Of Parking Brake

The parking brake is your best friend when securing your Equinox EV. Always engage it fully after you park, even on flat surfaces. This extra step prevents the car from rolling, especially if the automatic park feature fails.

If you park on a slope, turn your wheels toward the curb or roadside. This simple action adds a layer of protection against rolling downhill. Have you noticed how a small change in wheel direction can prevent a big problem?

Remember to release the parking brake gently when you start driving again. Pulling it off abruptly can damage the brake system over time. Treat your parking brake with care to keep it working smoothly for years.
