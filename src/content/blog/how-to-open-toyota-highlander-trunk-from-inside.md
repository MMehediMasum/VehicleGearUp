## Locate The Trunk Release Lever

Finding the trunk release lever inside your Toyota Highlander is the first step to open the trunk safely. This lever allows you to open the trunk without exiting the vehicle. It is designed to be easy to find and use, especially in emergencies.

### Check The Rear Seat Area

The trunk release lever is usually located near the rear seats. Look around the lower part of the back seats or near the floor. Sometimes it might be on the side panel beside the seats. Make sure to check both the driver and passenger sides carefully.

### Identify The Lever Design

The lever often has a simple design with a clear icon of a car trunk. It may be a small handle or a pull tab. The color is usually bright, such as red or yellow, to stand out against the interior. Recognizing this lever helps you quickly open the trunk from inside.

## Access Trunk Via Rear Seats

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=accessing-the-trunk-of-a-toyota-highlander-from-inside-the-vehicle-is-simple-and-useful-this-method-helps-when-the-trunk-is-locked-or-when-carrying-large-items-that-require-more-space&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Accessing the trunk of a Toyota Highlander from inside the vehicle is simple and useful. This method helps when the trunk is locked or when carrying large items that require more space. The key is using the rear seats as a pathway to the trunk.

Folding down the rear seats opens a direct route to the trunk. This way, you can reach inside without needing to open the trunk from outside.

### Folding Down Rear Seats

Start by locating the seat release levers on the top or side of the rear seats. Pull the lever to unlock the seatback. Push the seat forward and fold it down flat. This creates a larger opening between the cabin and the trunk. Repeat for both sides if needed. Make sure the seats are securely folded before reaching through.

### Reaching The Trunk From Inside

With the seats folded, lean into the opening carefully. Use your hands to feel for the trunk latch or any item inside. You can pull items out or push objects deeper into the trunk space. This method works well for retrieving forgotten items or adjusting cargo. Always be cautious not to hurt yourself on sharp edges.




## Using The Key Fob Inside The Vehicle

Using the key fob inside the Toyota Highlander makes opening the trunk simple and quick. It saves time and effort, especially when your hands are full. This method works even if you are seated inside the vehicle.

The key fob contains several buttons, including one for the trunk. Knowing where this button is helps you access the trunk without leaving your seat.

### Locate The Trunk Button On Key Fob

The trunk button usually has a symbol of a car with an open trunk. It is often found below the lock and unlock buttons.

The button may be slightly recessed to prevent accidental presses. Feel for a small raised icon or text indicating the trunk.

### Activate The Button From Inside

Hold the key fob comfortably in your hand while sitting in the vehicle. Press and hold the trunk button for about two seconds.

Listen for the trunk release sound or a click from the back of the car. This confirms the trunk is opening.

Some models may require the vehicle to be in park mode for the trunk to open from the key fob inside.

## Safety Tips While Opening The Trunk

### Ensure Vehicle Is Stationary

Always make sure your Toyota Highlander is completely stopped before opening the trunk from inside. Even a slight movement can cause instability or unexpected shifts of items inside the trunk. Imagine trying to open the trunk while the car is rolling — it’s not only unsafe but can also lead to accidents.

Engage the parking brake and put the gear in park. This simple step prevents the vehicle from moving and keeps you safe. Have you ever found yourself in a rush and skipped this? It’s worth the extra second to avoid serious consequences.

### Avoid Obstructions In Trunk Area

Check the trunk space for any objects that could block the door or cause injury when opening it from inside. Loose items might shift and hit you when you pull the latch. Keeping the trunk organized minimizes this risk and makes the process smoother.

Clear any heavy or sharp items before attempting to open the trunk. It’s surprising how small clutter can turn into a hazard in confined spaces. How often do you check your trunk for safety before climbing in?
