## Tools Needed

## Insert The New Battery

## Test The Key Fob

Testing the key fob is a simple way to check if the battery needs replacing. It helps confirm whether the key fob still works properly. This step saves time and avoids unnecessary battery changes. Follow these tips to test your Toyota Corolla key fob effectively.

### Check Lock And Unlock Functions

Press the lock button on your key fob. Listen for the car doors to lock or look for blinking lights. Next, press the unlock button. The doors should unlock, and lights may flash. If these functions do not respond, the battery might be weak or dead.

Try standing close to the car during this test. Sometimes distance affects the signal. Check the buttons for any physical damage. Damaged buttons can also stop the fob from working well.

### Replace Battery If Necessary

If the lock and unlock functions fail, replace the battery. Use a small screwdriver to open the key fob case carefully. Remove the old battery and insert a new one of the same type. Make sure the battery is placed correctly with the right polarity.

After replacement, test the key fob again. Confirm the lock and unlock buttons work smoothly. Dispose of the old battery properly to protect the environment.




## Tips For Battery Maintenance

Keeping your Toyota Corolla key battery in good shape extends its life. Proper care avoids frequent replacements and saves hassle. A few simple habits help maintain battery power and reliability.

Regular checks and cleanings prevent dirt buildup. Avoid leaving the key in extreme temperatures. These steps support steady battery performance and reduce failure risks.

### Keep The Battery Contacts Clean

Dirt or corrosion on contacts stops proper energy flow. Use a soft cloth or cotton swab with rubbing alcohol. Gently clean the metal parts inside the key fob. Do this every few months or when you notice issues.

### Store The Key In A Cool, Dry Place

Heat and moisture damage the battery faster. Avoid leaving the key inside the car on hot days. Keep the key away from water or humid areas. A cool, dry place helps the battery last longer.

### Use The Key Sparingly

Press buttons only when needed. Constant use drains the battery quickly. Turn off unnecessary alerts or sounds linked to the key fob. This practice conserves battery power for crucial moments.

### Replace The Battery Promptly

Don’t wait for the key to stop working completely. Change the battery at the first sign of weakness. Weak signals or delayed responses mean the battery is low. Timely replacement avoids lockouts or key failure.
