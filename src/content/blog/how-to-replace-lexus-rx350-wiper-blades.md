## Tools Needed

Before you start replacing the wiper blades on your Lexus RX350, gathering the right tools is key. Having everything ready saves time and spares you from last-minute trips to the store. It also makes the process smoother and less frustrating.

Are you curious if you really need special tools, or can you manage with what’s already in your car? Let’s break down exactly what you’ll need.

### New Wiper Blades

Obviously, you need the correct replacement blades designed for the Lexus RX350. The size matters—usually 24 inches for the driver side and 19 inches for the passenger side, but double-check your owner’s manual or measure your old blades.

Choosing high-quality blades can improve your visibility dramatically, especially in heavy rain or snow.

### Flathead Screwdriver

A small flathead screwdriver is handy for releasing the locking mechanism that holds the wiper blade in place. It’s a simple tool but crucial for avoiding damage to the wiper arm.

If you don’t have one, they’re inexpensive and can be found at any hardware store. You might be surprised how often this tool comes in handy beyond just wiper blade replacement.

### Clean Cloth Or Paper Towels

Wiping the wiper arm and windshield before installing new blades ensures a clean fit. Dirt or debris can cause streaking or reduce the blade’s lifespan.

Keep a cloth or some paper towels within reach to give the arm a quick clean-up before attaching the new blade.

### Gloves (optional)

Wearing gloves protects your hands from dirt and any sharp edges on the wiper arm. It also gives better grip when handling small clips and parts.

While not mandatory, gloves can make the task more comfortable, especially in cold weather.

### Blade Size For Lexus Rx350

The Lexus RX350 uses different wiper blade sizes for the driver and passenger sides. The driver’s side blade is usually longer than the passenger’s side. Using the correct size ensures full windshield coverage and proper contact with the glass.

* Driver side blade size: 26 inches
* Passenger side blade size: 20 inches

Check your vehicle’s manual or measure your existing blades to confirm the size. Incorrect size causes poor wiping and may damage the wiper arm or windshield.

### Types Of Wiper Blades

Wiper blades come in several types. Each type has benefits suited for different driving conditions and preferences. Choosing the right type improves your car’s performance and comfort.

* **Traditional Frame Blades:**Metal frames hold the rubber blade. They are affordable and easy to replace but may wear unevenly.
* **Beam Blades:**One-piece design with no external frame. They offer better contact on curved windshields and resist ice buildup.
* **Hybrid Blades:**Combine frame and beam blade features. They provide good flexibility and durability.

Select a blade type that matches your climate and driving habits. Beam blades are excellent for snowy or rainy regions, while traditional blades work well in mild weather.

## Removing Old Wiper Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=removing-the-old-wiper-blades-from-your-lexus-rx350-is-simpler-than-it-might-seem-with-the-right-approach-you-can-do-it-quickly-and-without-frustration&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Removing the old wiper blades from your Lexus RX350 is simpler than it might seem. With the right approach, you can do it quickly and without frustration. This part is crucial because improperly removed blades can damage the wiper arm or windshield.

### Locate The Release Tab

First, identify the small release tab on the wiper blade where it connects to the arm. It’s usually a little button or lever that you can press with your fingers. You might need to angle the wiper arm slightly away from the windshield to get better access to this tab.

Have you noticed how some release tabs can feel stuck or hard to press? If you do, try gently wiggling the blade back and forth while pressing the tab. This often loosens the grip and makes the blade slide off more easily.

### Detach The Blade From The Arm

Once you press the release tab, slide the wiper blade downward, away from the arm’s hook. It should unclip smoothly without forcing it. If it resists, double-check that you are pressing the release tab fully.

Keep the wiper arm supported with your other hand as you remove the blade to avoid it snapping back and damaging your windshield. Removing the blade carefully prevents bending the arm or scratching the glass. Have you ever had a wiper arm snap back unexpectedly? Taking this precaution saves you from costly repairs.

## Installing New Wiper Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-new-wiper-blades-on-your-lexus-rx350-is-a-straightforward-task-that-ensures-clear-visibility-during-rain-or-snow-paying-attention-to-how-the-new-blades-fit-will-save-you-time-and-frustration&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing new wiper blades on your Lexus RX350 is a straightforward task that ensures clear visibility during rain or snow. Paying attention to how the new blades fit will save you time and frustration. Let’s look at how to properly position and secure the blades for smooth operation.

### Align The New Blade

Hold the new wiper blade next to the arm to check the correct alignment. The blade’s curve should match the windshield’s contour to maximize contact. This ensures efficient wiping without streaks or missed spots.

Take a moment to compare the attachment points on the blade with the wiper arm hook. Are they designed to fit together easily? Confirming this before snapping the blade in place avoids damage or improper installation.

### Snap The Blade Into Place

Once aligned, gently push the blade’s connector into the wiper arm hook until you hear a click. This sound means the blade is securely locked. Avoid forcing it, as too much pressure can break the plastic components.

After installation, test the blade by moving the wiper arm manually or turning on the wipers briefly. Is the blade moving smoothly without wobbling? If not, double-check the connection or reposition the blade.




## Testing The New Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=testing-the-new-wiper-blades-is-essential-after-installation-this-step-ensures-safety-and-clear-visibility-on-the-road&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Testing the new wiper blades is essential after installation. This step ensures safety and clear visibility on the road. Proper testing helps confirm the blades work smoothly without damage or noise.

### Check For Proper Movement

Turn on the wipers and watch their motion closely. The blades should move evenly across the windshield. Look for any jerky or uneven movements that may cause streaks. Smooth and consistent motion means the blades are installed correctly. Listen for unusual sounds like squeaking or scraping, which indicate adjustment is needed.

### Inspect For Streaks Or Smudges

Spray water or windshield cleaner on the glass. Activate the wipers and observe the cleaning effect. New blades should leave the glass clear and free of streaks. Smudges or missed spots show the blades may be dirty or improperly fitted. Repeat wiping to check if streaks disappear or persist. Clean and smooth wiping means the new blades function well.

## Maintenance Tips

Proper maintenance keeps your Lexus RX350 wiper blades working well. Regular care improves visibility and safety. It also extends the life of the blades. Follow simple steps to maintain your wipers for the best performance.

### Regular Cleaning

Clean your wiper blades often to remove dirt and grime. Use a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water. Gently wipe the rubber edge to avoid damage. Dirt buildup can cause streaks and reduce wiping power. Clean the windshield too for smooth blade movement.

### When To Replace Again

Check your wiper blades every few months for wear. Replace them if you see cracks, tears, or missing rubber. Notice streaks or skipping during use. These signs mean the blades no longer clean well. Replace blades at least once a year to ensure clear vision.
