## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hydraulic Brakes Best For E-bikes?



Hydraulic brakes offer superior stopping power and modulation. They handle high speeds and heavier e-bike weight better than mechanical brakes. These brakes provide consistent performance in wet or muddy conditions, ensuring safety and control on diverse terrains.



### How Do 2-pin Power-off Sensors Improve Brake Safety?



2-PIN power-off sensors cut motor power when braking. This prevents motor drag and enhances stopping efficiency. They provide quicker response times and reduce the risk of accidents by ensuring motor and brake coordination on e-bikes.



### Are 4-piston Calipers Better Than 2-piston For E-bikes?



4-piston calipers provide stronger and more even braking force. They improve heat dissipation and reduce brake fade on steep descents. For heavier e-bikes or aggressive riding, 4-piston brakes offer enhanced control and reliability over 2-piston models.



### Why Choose Pre-bled Hydraulic Brake Kits?



Pre-bled kits come filled with mineral oil and are ready to install. They save time and prevent air bubbles in the brake lines. This ensures immediate, smooth brake performance without extra maintenance or bleeding after setup.



### What Rotor Size Is Ideal For E-bike Hydraulic Brakes?



160mm rotors balance weight and braking power for most e-bikes. Larger rotors like 180mm offer more stopping force but add weight. Choose rotor size based on your riding style and bike weight for optimal braking efficiency.
