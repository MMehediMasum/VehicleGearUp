Ceramic brake pads provide quiet operation, less brake dust, and longer lifespan. They handle heat well, making them ideal for Ram 2500 Diesel trucks used in towing and heavy-duty tasks. Ceramic pads like Detroit Axle and Bosch QuietCast enhance braking efficiency and reduce maintenance.

Yes, semi-metallic pads like Bendix Priority1 offer strong braking performance and excellent heat dissipation. ### What Brake Pads Fit Ram 2500 Diesel From 2019 To 2024?

### How Often Should Ram 2500 Diesel Brake Pads Be Replaced?



Brake pads on a Ram 2500 Diesel should be checked every 20,000 to 30,000 miles. Replacement depends on driving style, load, and pad material. Regular inspection prevents brake failure and maintains stopping power, especially for trucks used in towing and heavy-duty applications.
