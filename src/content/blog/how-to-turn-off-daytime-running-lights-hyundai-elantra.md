## Daytime Running Lights Basics

Daytime Running Lights (DRLs) are an important safety feature on many modern vehicles. They help increase your car’s visibility during the day. Understanding the basics of DRLs can help you manage them better, especially on your Hyundai Elantra.

### Purpose Of Drls

DRLs are lights that turn on automatically when the car is running. They make your vehicle easier to see for other drivers and pedestrians. This reduces the chance of daytime accidents. DRLs do not replace headlights but work alongside them.

These lights use less power than regular headlights. They are designed to stay on during the day to improve safety. DRLs activate without the need for driver input. This feature helps keep you safe without extra effort.

### Drls On Hyundai Elantra

The Hyundai Elantra comes with DRLs as a standard safety feature. These lights turn on automatically when you start the engine. They provide better visibility in daylight conditions. The Elantra uses LED lights for its DRLs, which are bright and energy-efficient.

In some models, you can control the DRLs through the vehicle’s settings. Some drivers prefer to turn them off in certain situations. Knowing how DRLs work on the Elantra helps you decide when to use or disable them.
