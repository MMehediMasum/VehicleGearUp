## Reasons Key Fob Might Fail

Key fobs are designed to make starting your Ford Explorer quick and effortless. But what happens when your key fob doesn’t work? Understanding why it might fail can save you frustration and help you find a workaround to start your vehicle without it.

### Battery Issues

One of the most common reasons your key fob might fail is a dead or weak battery. Batteries don’t last forever, and if your fob’s battery is low, the signal it sends to your vehicle can be too weak to register.

Have you ever experienced a fob that only worked when it was extremely close to the car? That’s a classic sign of battery trouble. Replacing the battery is often a simple fix that gets your key fob back in action quickly.

### Signal Interference

Sometimes your key fob is fine, but the signal just doesn’t reach your Explorer. This can happen if you’re near devices that emit strong radio waves or electromagnetic interference, like certain security systems or even large metal objects.

Think about times when your fob works perfectly outside but fails when inside your garage or near a busy street. This interference can block or scramble the communication between your key fob and the car’s receiver.

### Key Fob Damage

If your key fob has been dropped, exposed to water, or physically damaged, it may stop working entirely. Internal components can break or short-circuit, making the fob unable to send any signal at all.

Consider how you handle your key fob daily. A single spill or fall might be the culprit behind its failure. In cases like this, repairs or replacement might be necessary to regain full functionality.

### Using A Spare Key Fob

Most Ford Explorers come with a spare key fob. Keep this in a safe place for emergencies. To start the vehicle:

* Hold the spare key fob close to the start button.
* Press the brake pedal firmly.
* Push the start button while keeping the spare fob near it.

The system will recognize the spare fob’s signal. The engine should start without issues. This method works even if the main key fob battery is dead.

### Emergency Start Procedure

If no spare fob is available, use the emergency start option. Ford Explorers have a special way to start the car without the key fob signal. Follow these steps:

* Place the key fob directly on the push start button.
* Press the brake pedal.
* Hold the start button down for a few seconds.

This action allows the car to detect the key fob’s chip inside the fob. The engine will start despite the usual key fob signal being weak or missing.

  


## Preventing Key Fob Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preventing-key-fob-issues-is-the-best-way-to-avoid-the-hassle-of-starting-your-ford-explorer-without-the-key-fob-a-malfunctioning-key-fob-can-leave-you-stranded-or-force-you-to-rely-on-backup-methods-that-might-not-be-convenient&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preventing key fob issues is the best way to avoid the hassle of starting your Ford Explorer without the key fob. A malfunctioning key fob can leave you stranded or force you to rely on backup methods that might not be convenient. Taking simple, consistent actions can keep your key fob working smoothly and save you time and stress.

### Regular Battery Checks

The battery inside your key fob is small but powerful, and it doesn’t last forever. Checking the battery regularly helps you catch problems before they leave you locked out. Try to test the key fob every few months by pressing the buttons and watching for the expected response from your car.

If you notice any delay or no response, it’s time to replace the battery. Keep a small pack of CR2032 batteries handy—these are the most common type for Ford Explorer key fobs. Changing the battery yourself is straightforward and can save you a trip to the dealer.

### Safe Storage Tips

Where you keep your key fob affects how well it works. Avoid placing it near metal objects like keys or coins, which can interfere with the signal. Also, keep your key fob away from extreme temperatures and moisture, as these can damage the internal electronics.

Consider using a dedicated pouch or case to protect your key fob. This simple step prevents scratches and accidental button presses. Ask yourself: do you have a habit of tossing your key fob in the same pocket or bag with other items? Changing that habit could extend your key fob’s life.
