## Resetting Service Light For Older Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=older-honda-accord-models-often-require-a-simple-button-press-to-reset-the-service-light-holding-the-trip-button-while-turning-the-ignition-can-clear-the-reminder&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Older Honda Accord models often require a simple button press to reset the service light. Holding the trip button while turning the ignition can clear the reminder. This quick reset helps keep your dashboard accurate after maintenance.

Resetting the service light on older Honda Accord models can seem tricky. But don't worry. With a few simple steps, you can do it yourself. This process ensures your vehicle runs smoothly. Follow the steps below to reset the service light without any hassle.

### Turn Ignition On Without Starting

Begin by sitting in the driver's seat. Insert the key into the ignition. Turn the ignition to the "ON" position. Do not start the engine. This step powers your car's system.

### Use Odometer Button

Locate the odometer button on your dashboard. Press and hold the button. While holding, switch the ignition off, then back to "ON." Continue holding the button until the service light blinks. This might take a few seconds.

### Confirm Reset

Release the odometer button. Check if the service light is off. If it stays off, the reset is successful. If not, repeat the steps. Make sure each step is completed correctly.

## Resetting Service Light For Newer Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-service-light-on-newer-honda-accord-models-is-a-simple-process-it-helps-keep-track-of-your-vehicles-maintenance-schedule&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the service light on newer Honda Accord models is a simple process. It helps keep track of your vehicle’s maintenance schedule. The steps use the car’s digital display and menu system. No special tools are needed.

This reset clears the service reminder after maintenance. Follow these easy steps to reset the service light quickly and correctly.

### Access Vehicle Settings

Turn on the ignition without starting the engine. Use the buttons on the steering wheel to open the main menu. Find the “Settings” or “Vehicle” option in the display. Press “Enter” or “OK” to access the vehicle settings.

### Navigate To Maintenance

Scroll through the menu to find “Maintenance.” This section shows service reminders and alerts. Select “Maintenance” to open the maintenance options. Look for the service light or oil life reset feature.

### Reset Service Reminder

Select the “Reset” or “Clear” option for the service reminder. Confirm your choice by pressing “OK” or “Enter.” The service light will turn off, indicating the reset is complete. Turn off the ignition and then restart the car to check the reset.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-the-service-light-on-a-honda-accord-can-save-time-and-reduce-frustration-the-service-light-may-not-reset-as-expected-or-display-error-messages-after-resetting-attempts&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with the service light on a Honda Accord can save time and reduce frustration. The service light may not reset as expected or display error messages after resetting attempts. These problems often have simple solutions that anyone can try before visiting a mechanic.

### Service Light Won't Reset

Sometimes, the service light stays on even after completing maintenance. This can happen if the reset process was not done correctly. Make sure the ignition is in the right position during the reset steps. Check for worn or dirty sensors that might send false signals.

Another cause is a low battery or loose connection. Ensure the car battery is fully charged. Inspect the fuses related to the dashboard and sensors. A faulty sensor or module may require professional diagnosis.

### Error Messages After Reset

After resetting the service light, error messages may appear on the dashboard. These messages can indicate sensor malfunctions or system errors. Common errors include the maintenance minder showing incorrect mileage or warnings about engine systems.

Clear the error codes with an OBD-II scanner if available. Sometimes, a short drive allows the system to recalibrate and clear the messages. Persistent errors may need a visit to a service center for deeper diagnostics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset Service Light On Honda Accord?

To reset the service light, turn the ignition to ON without starting. Press and hold the trip meter reset button until the light blinks and goes off. Release the button and turn off the ignition. This resets the service light on most Honda Accord models.

### Why Won't My Honda Accord Service Light Reset?

If the service light won’t reset, check the ignition position and button hold time. Make sure the trip meter is selected. Sometimes, a system fault or sensor issue can prevent reset. Consult the owner’s manual or a professional if problems persist.

### How Often Should I Reset The Service Light?

Reset the service light only after completing scheduled maintenance. This usually happens every 5,000 to 7,500 miles, depending on your driving habits. Regular resets help keep track of upcoming service needs and maintain vehicle health.

### Can I Reset Service Light Without Tools?

Yes, you can reset the service light manually using the trip meter button. No special tools or devices are needed. Follow the specific steps in your Honda Accord’s manual for accurate resetting.
