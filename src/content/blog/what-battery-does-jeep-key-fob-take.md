## Jeep Key Fob Battery Types

Your Jeep key fob relies on a small but powerful battery to keep it working smoothly. Understanding the types of batteries used can save you time and hassle when it's time for a replacement. Let’s look at the common battery models and their specifications to help you choose the right one for your Jeep key fob.

### Common Battery Models

Most Jeep key fobs use coin cell batteries, which are easy to find and replace. The most popular models include:

* **CR2032:**Widely used in many Jeep key fobs, this 3-volt battery is known for its long life and reliability.
* **CR2025:**Slightly thinner than the CR2032, it fits some Jeep key fobs depending on the design.
* **CR2016:**Less common but still found in certain older or specific Jeep models.

Have you ever grabbed the wrong battery size? It’s a common mistake that can lead to frustration and wasted money.

### Battery Sizes And Specifications

| Battery Model | Diameter (mm) | Thickness (mm) | Voltage (V) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CR2032 | 20 | 3.2 | 3 |
| CR2025 | 20 | 2.5 | 3 |
| CR2016 | 20 | 1.6 | 3 |

Choosing the right size matters because even a tiny difference in thickness can prevent the battery from fitting or making proper contact.

Next time your key fob starts acting up, check the battery size and model carefully before buying a replacement. This small step can save you a lot of trouble and keep your Jeep running smoothly.

## How To Identify Your Jeep Key Fob Battery

Knowing the exact battery type for your Jeep key fob helps avoid buying the wrong one. Identifying the battery is simple and quick. You can check the key fob model or find battery details inside the fob itself. Both ways give clear clues about the correct battery to use.

### Checking The Key Fob Model

Look at the back of your Jeep key fob. The model number is usually printed there. It may be a combination of letters and numbers. Write down this model number for reference.

Search online for the key fob model. Official Jeep sites or user forums often list compatible batteries. This step helps confirm the battery type before opening the fob.

### Locating Battery Information

Open the key fob carefully with a small flat tool. Inside, you will see a round, flat battery. The battery usually has a code printed on it.

Common codes include CR2032, CR2025, or CR1632. These codes indicate the battery size and voltage. Note this code to buy the correct replacement battery.

* Handle the battery gently to avoid damage.
* Make sure the new battery matches the code exactly.
* Replace the battery with the same side facing up as before.

## Step-by-step Battery Replacement

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=replacing-the-battery-in-your-jeep-key-fob-is-easier-than-many-expect-knowing-how-to-do-it-yourself-can-save-you-time-and-a-trip-to-the-dealer&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Replacing the battery in your Jeep key fob is easier than many expect. Knowing how to do it yourself can save you time and a trip to the dealer. Let’s walk through the process step-by-step so you feel confident handling it on your own.

### Tools Needed

* Small flathead screwdriver or a plastic pry tool
* Replacement battery (usually a CR2032 button cell, but double-check your model)
* Soft cloth or towel to work on

Having these tools ready before you start will make the process smoother and prevent accidental damage to your key fob.

### Opening The Key Fob

Begin by locating the seam where the two halves of the fob meet. Use your flathead screwdriver or pry tool gently to separate the halves.

Applying too much force can break the plastic casing, so take your time and work around the edges carefully. Have you ever tried to open something fragile without the right tools? It rarely ends well.

### Removing The Old Battery

Once the fob is open, you’ll see the battery sitting in a small compartment. Note how the battery is positioned—usually with the positive side facing up.

Use your fingers or the screwdriver tip to lift the old battery out gently. Avoid touching other components inside to prevent damage or misalignment.

### Installing The New Battery

Place the new battery in the compartment exactly as the old one was positioned. Make sure it sits flat and secure in its slot.

Before snapping the fob back together, test the buttons to confirm the battery is working. This quick check can save you from reopening the fob unnecessarily.

## Troubleshooting Key Fob Battery Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=dealing-with-a-jeep-key-fob-that-suddenly-stops-working-can-be-frustrating-especially-when-you-rely-on-it-daily-before-rushing-to-replace-the-battery-its-important-to-troubleshoot-the-issue-carefully&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Dealing with a Jeep key fob that suddenly stops working can be frustrating, especially when you rely on it daily. Before rushing to replace the battery, it’s important to troubleshoot the issue carefully. Understanding the signs of a weak battery and knowing when replacement isn’t the real fix can save you time and unnecessary expenses.

### Signs Of A Weak Battery

You might notice your key fob's response time slowing down or having to press buttons multiple times for it to register. Sometimes, the key fob may only work intermittently or at very close range. Another common sign is when the LED light on your fob dims or fails to light up altogether.

These symptoms indicate that the battery is losing power, but they don't always guarantee the battery is the sole cause. Have you ever experienced your key fob acting up in cold weather? Batteries can weaken more quickly in low temperatures, causing unexpected behavior.

### When Replacement Isn't The Solution

Replacing the battery might seem like the obvious fix, but if the key fob still doesn’t work after a fresh battery, other issues could be at play. Circuit board damage, worn-out buttons, or signal interference can all mimic battery problems.

Before buying a new battery, check for dirt or corrosion inside the key fob. Cleaning the contacts gently with rubbing alcohol can often restore proper function. Also, consider resetting your key fob or reprogramming it, especially if it lost connection to your Jeep after a battery change.

Could your key fob battery issue be a sign of something more complex? Testing with a multimeter or consulting with a professional can help pinpoint the exact problem, so you don’t waste money on unnecessary replacements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Type Of Battery Does A Jeep Key Fob Use?

Jeep key fobs typically use a CR2032 lithium coin battery. This battery is small, durable, and widely available. It powers the remote functions like locking and unlocking your vehicle. Always check your fob’s manual to confirm the exact battery model.

### How Do I Replace The Battery In My Jeep Key Fob?

To replace the battery, gently pry open the key fob using a small flat tool. Remove the old battery and insert a new CR2032 battery with the positive side facing up. Snap the fob back together and test its functions to ensure proper installation.

### How Long Does A Jeep Key Fob Battery Last?

A Jeep key fob battery typically lasts between 1 to 3 years. Battery life depends on usage frequency and environmental conditions. Replace the battery promptly if the fob’s range decreases or it stops working to avoid inconvenience.

### Can I Use Any Battery For My Jeep Key Fob?

No, using the correct CR2032 battery is essential for proper function. Other batteries may not fit or work efficiently. Always use a high-quality, brand-name battery to ensure reliable performance and prevent damage to the key fob.
