## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Group 65 Battery For Diesel Trucks?



The Interstate MTX-65 and Odyssey ODX-AGM65 are top choices. Both offer strong CCA ratings and AGM technology for durability. They excel in starting power and long life, ideal for diesel trucks requiring reliable performance in harsh conditions.



### How Long Do Group 65 Agm Batteries Typically Last?



Group 65 AGM batteries usually last 3 to 5 years. Brands like ACDelco and Weize provide warranties up to 36 months. Proper maintenance and usage conditions affect lifespan, with AGM technology enhancing durability and resistance to vibration and temperature extremes.



### Are Agm Batteries Better For Diesel Trucks Than Regular Batteries?



Yes, AGM batteries provide superior performance and durability. They resist vibrations and extreme temperatures better than traditional batteries. AGM batteries also offer maintenance-free operation, faster recharging, and higher cold cranking amps, making them ideal for demanding diesel truck engines.



### What Is The Cold Cranking Amps (cca) Rating Needed For Group 65 Batteries?



Diesel trucks typically require Group 65 batteries with at least 700 to 850 CCA. Batteries like Optima RedTop and Mighty Max offer up to 850 CCA, ensuring reliable starts in cold weather and heavy-duty conditions common for diesel engines.



### Can I Use A Group 94 Battery Instead Of Group 65?



Group 94 batteries can fit some diesel trucks but check dimensions first. They often have higher capacity and CCA ratings. Models like ACDelco Gold 94RAGM provide strong power but must match your truck's battery tray and terminal requirements.



### How Do I Maintain My Group 65 Agm Battery For Diesel Trucks?



AGM batteries require minimal maintenance. Keep terminals clean and tight, avoid deep discharges, and store the battery charged. Regularly check voltage and use a smart charger if stored long-term. This ensures optimal performance and extends battery life.
