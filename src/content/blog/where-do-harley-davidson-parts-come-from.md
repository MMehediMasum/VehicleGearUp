### Factory Locations Worldwide

Harley Davidson’s reach extends beyond the United States, with factories strategically placed around the globe. These locations ensure the company can serve diverse markets efficiently and keep production agile.

* **United States:**Main production and assembly lines.
* **Brazil:**Focuses on motorcycles tailored for South American markets.

Each facility adapts to local demand while maintaining Harley’s high standards. This global network means parts are closer to customers, reducing wait times and shipping costs.

### Key Production Facilities In The Usa

The heart of Harley Davidson’s manufacturing lies in several key American plants known for quality and innovation.

* **York, Pennsylvania:**This is Harley’s largest manufacturing facility. It handles engine production and final motorcycle assembly. The plant is known for integrating new technology while preserving traditional craftsmanship.
* **Milwaukee, Wisconsin:**The birthplace of Harley Davidson still plays a vital role. Here, the company focuses on research, development, and prototyping new parts. It’s also home to the Harley-Davidson Museum, connecting history with modern production.
* **Kansas City, Missouri:**This plant produces parts like frames and other components that are crucial for motorcycle assembly. The facility emphasizes lean manufacturing to keep processes efficient.

## Role Of Oem And Aftermarket Parts

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-the-role-of-oem-and-aftermarket-parts-is-crucial-for-anyone-looking-to-maintain-or-customize-their-harley-davidson-these-parts-determine-not-only-the-bikes-performance-but-also-its-safety-and-longevity&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding the role of OEM and aftermarket parts is crucial for anyone looking to maintain or customize their Harley Davidson. These parts determine not only the bike's performance but also its safety and longevity. Knowing where your parts come from helps you make smarter choices that suit your riding style and budget.

### Differences Between Oem And Aftermarket

**OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)**parts are made by Harley Davidson or their authorized suppliers. They fit perfectly and maintain your bike’s original quality and warranty. These parts often cost more but guarantee compatibility and reliability.

**Aftermarket parts**are made by third-party companies and offer a wider variety of options. They can be cheaper and sometimes even improve on the original design with enhanced features. However, quality varies, and some parts may not fit as well or last as long.

Have you ever wondered if saving money on a cheaper part might cost you more in repairs later? That’s the trade-off you face between OEM and aftermarket.

### Common Aftermarket Suppliers

Several trusted aftermarket suppliers have built strong reputations for Harley Davidson parts. Companies like **Vance & Hines**specialize in exhaust systems that boost performance and sound. **Arlen Ness**offers custom parts known for their unique style and durability.

Other popular names include **Drag Specialties**and **J&P Cycles**, both providing a wide range of parts from brakes to lighting. These suppliers often offer parts that fit multiple Harley models, giving you more choices to tailor your bike.

How do you balance the appeal of custom parts with the need for reliability? Choosing the right supplier can make all the difference.
