### How To Choose A Clutch For Dodge Ram 2500 Diesel?



Choose a clutch compatible with your engine size and model year. Prioritize heavy-duty kits like ClutchMaxPRO for turbo diesel engines. Ensure it matches OEM part numbers for a perfect fit and durability in towing and hauling tasks.



### Can I Replace My Dodge Ram 2500 Clutch Myself?



Yes, if you have mechanical skills and proper tools. Follow manufacturer instructions closely. Some clutches, like the Koomaha master and slave cylinder assembly, require precision. Otherwise, professional installation ensures optimal performance and safety.



### What Are Signs Of A Failing Clutch In Dodge Ram 2500 Diesel?



Common signs include slipping gears, difficulty shifting, burning smell, and unusual noises. Reduced engine power and poor acceleration can also indicate clutch issues. Early detection helps avoid costly repairs and maintains towing efficiency.



### Are Electronic Fan Clutches Better For Dodge Ram 2500 Diesel?



Electronic fan clutches like OAW 12-CR3282 offer precise temperature control and fuel efficiency. They engage only when needed, reducing engine load and improving performance. Ideal for modern Cummins engines from 2005-2018 Ram 2500 and heavier models.



### What Oem Part Numbers Fit Dodge Ram 2500 Diesel Clutches?



Key OEM numbers include 55056990AA, 52028894AA, 52014729AC, and RL111444AB. Matching these ensures compatibility with Dodge Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 models from 1994 to 2018. Always verify your vehicle’s exact specs before purchasing.
