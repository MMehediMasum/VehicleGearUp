## Ford Transit Van Height Basics

The height of a Ford Transit van varies by model and roof style. Understanding these basics helps with parking and loading. It also assists in choosing the right van for your needs.

### Standard Height Models

Standard roof Ford Transit vans measure about 83 inches tall. This height fits most garages and parking structures. It provides enough headroom for many uses. These models balance interior space with easy access.

### Extended Roof Options

High roof models offer extra height, around 110 inches. This allows standing room inside the van. High roof vans suit cargo or camper conversions well. They add comfort for long trips or workdays.

### Height Variations By Wheelbase

Wheelbase length can affect overall van height slightly. Longer wheelbases sometimes raise the van a bit. This change is small but worth noting. It can impact clearance under bridges or ramps.
