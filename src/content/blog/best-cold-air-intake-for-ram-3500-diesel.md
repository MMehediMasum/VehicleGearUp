### How Does A Cold Air Intake Affect Fuel Mileage?

### Are Heat Shields Necessary In Cold Air Intake Systems?



Yes, heat shields reduce intake air temperature. Cooler air improves engine performance and prevents heat soak, especially in hot engine bays like the Ram 3500 Diesel.



### Can I Install A Cold Air Intake Myself On Ram 3500?
