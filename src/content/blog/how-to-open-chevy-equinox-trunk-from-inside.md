## Locate The Trunk Release Lever

Locating the trunk release lever inside your Chevy Equinox is the first step to easily access the trunk without stepping outside. Knowing exactly where to find this lever saves you time and frustration, especially in tight parking spots or bad weather. Let’s focus on how to quickly spot this lever and understand its appearance.

### Check Driver's Side Floor Area

Start by looking near the driver’s seat on the floor area. The trunk release lever is usually positioned close to where your left foot rests while driving.

It might be tucked just under the dashboard or near the door frame. If you don’t see it immediately, crouch down and scan the lower panel carefully—you might be surprised how discreet it is.

### Identify The Lever Design

The lever typically has a clear icon showing a car with an open trunk. It’s often made of black or dark plastic to blend in but stands out enough for quick recognition.

Some models have a pull handle, while others have a small button or switch. Pay attention to its shape and texture; a simple pull or push should release the trunk.

## Accessing The Trunk Via Rear Seats

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=folding-down-the-rear-seats-reveals-a-pass-through-to-the-trunk-space-this-allows-easy-access-to-items-without-leaving-the-vehicle&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Folding down the rear seats reveals a pass-through to the trunk space. This allows easy access to items without leaving the vehicle. Simply release the seat latch and push the seat forward to open the trunk from inside.

Accessing the trunk of your Chevy Equinox from the inside can be useful in various situations. You might need quick access to items stored in the trunk without exiting the vehicle. This method provides a practical solution, especially in tight parking spaces or during inclement weather.

### Fold Down Rear Seats

Start by folding down the rear seats. Locate the seat release levers at the top of the backrest. Pull these levers to release the locks. Push the seat forward until it lays flat. This creates a spacious opening to access the trunk area. Ensure the seats are securely flat to prevent them from moving.

### Reach Through To The Trunk

Once the seats are down, you can reach into the trunk. The flat seat position allows easy access to stored items. Retrieve what you need without hassle. Remember to secure the seats back in place after accessing the trunk. Simply lift the backrest and push it back until it clicks into place.



## Using Key Fob Or Interior Buttons

Using the key fob or interior buttons is the easiest way to open the Chevy Equinox trunk from inside. These controls give quick access without leaving the driver's seat. Knowing where to find these buttons helps you save time and effort.

### Locate The Interior Trunk Button

The interior trunk button usually sits near the driver's seat. Common places include:

* On the driver's door panel
* Near the dashboard, below the steering wheel
* On the center console

Press this button to release the trunk latch. It works only when the vehicle is in park or neutral. Check your owner’s manual for the exact location if unsure.

### Operate The Key Fob Trunk Release

The key fob has a dedicated trunk release button. Press and hold this button for a few seconds. The trunk will pop open automatically.

This function works even if the vehicle is locked. It provides quick access without using the interior controls. Replace the key fob battery if the trunk does not respond.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=sometimes-opening-the-chevy-equinox-trunk-from-inside-might-not-work-as-expected-small-problems-can-stop-the-lever-from-working&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Sometimes, opening the Chevy Equinox trunk from inside might not work as expected. Small problems can stop the lever from working. These issues can be easy to fix once identified. Understanding common troubles helps in quick solutions. This saves time and avoids frustration.

### When The Lever Doesn’t Work

The trunk release lever may fail due to a stuck or broken cable. Dirt or rust can block the mechanism. Check if the lever moves freely without resistance. A loose or damaged cable often causes the problem. Adjust or replace the cable if needed.

Sometimes, the latch itself may jam. Try pressing or pulling gently on the trunk lid while using the lever. This can free a stuck latch. Avoid forcing it too hard to prevent damage.

### Battery And Electrical Concerns

The trunk release system depends on the vehicle’s electrical power. A weak or dead battery can stop the trunk from opening. Check the battery charge and connections.

The fuse for the trunk release may blow. Inspect the fuse box and replace any blown fuses. Faulty wiring or a bad switch can also cause problems. Look for loose wires or corrosion around the trunk release button and connectors.




## Safety Tips When Opening The Trunk

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=opening-the-trunk-of-your-chevy-equinox-from-inside-requires-care-safety-should-be-your-top-priority&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Opening the trunk of your Chevy Equinox from inside requires care. Safety should be your top priority. Taking simple precautions can prevent injuries and damage.

Always be aware of your surroundings. Keep clear of moving parts and sharp edges. Make sure the trunk is fully open before reaching inside.

### Avoiding Accidental Closures

* Check the trunk latch before entering the trunk area.
* Do not lean on the trunk lid or support struts.
* Keep hands and fingers away from the closing mechanism.
* Make sure no objects block the trunk’s closing path.
* Use the interior release handle carefully to prevent sudden closure.

### Ensuring Proper Ventilation

* Open windows or doors for fresh air flow inside the vehicle.
* Avoid staying inside the trunk for long periods.
* Do not block air vents or ventilation openings.
* Check that the interior release handle is working before closing the trunk.
* Inform someone outside that you are inside the trunk for safety.
