## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Oil For Motorcycle Wet Clutches?



The best oil for wet clutches is a 10W-40 motorcycle oil with JASO MA or MA2 rating. Oils like Valvoline Full Synthetic 10W-40 or Lucas Oil 10W-40 Synthetic SAE protect clutch plates and ensure smooth engagement without slipping.



### Why Choose Synthetic Oil For Wet Clutch Motorcycles?



Synthetic oils offer better temperature stability and superior lubrication. They reduce clutch slippage and wear, improving performance. Products like TRIAX XTerra 4T 10W-40 Full Synthetic meet these needs, ensuring longer engine and clutch life in wet clutch motorcycles.



### Can Conventional Oils Work For Wet Clutches?



Yes, high-quality conventional oils like Lucas Oil High Performance SAE 10W-40 can work. However, synthetic or semi-synthetic oils provide better protection and smoother clutch operation, especially under extreme conditions and prolonged use.



### How Often Should I Change Oil In Wet Clutch Motorcycles?



Change oil every 3,000 to 5,000 miles, or as recommended by the manufacturer. Using premium oils like Castrol Power1 4T 10W-40 ensures clutch efficiency and engine protection between oil changes.



### What Does Jaso Ma2 Rating Mean For Motorcycle Oils?



JASO MA2 rating certifies oils suitable for wet clutch motorcycles. It ensures optimal friction properties to prevent clutch slippage. Oils like Lucas Oil 10793 Synthetic SAE 10W-40 meet this standard for reliable clutch and engine performance.
