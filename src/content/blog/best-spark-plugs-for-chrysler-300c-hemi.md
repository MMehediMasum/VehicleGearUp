### Are Iridium Spark Plugs Better For Chrysler 300c?



Yes, iridium spark plugs like NGK Iridium IX offer better durability and improved ignition. They provide enhanced fuel efficiency and smoother engine performance for Chrysler 300C models.

Spark plugs on a Chrysler 300C typically need replacement every 30,000 to 100,000 miles. It depends on the plug type and driving conditions. Iridium plugs last longer than copper ones.
