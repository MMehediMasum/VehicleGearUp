## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Oil Viscosity For 4-stroke Bike Engines?



For 4-stroke bike engines, 10W-40 viscosity is ideal. It offers excellent protection and performance in various temperatures. Most top oils like Valvoline and Honda Pro GN4 use this grade for durability and smooth engine operation.



### Can Synthetic Oils Improve 4-stroke Motorcycle Performance?



Yes, synthetic oils like Valvoline Full Synthetic 10W-40 improve engine efficiency. They reduce friction, enhance wear protection, and extend oil change intervals for better engine health and performance.



### How Often Should I Change 4-stroke Bike Engine Oil?



Change 4-stroke bike oil every 3,000 to 5,000 miles or as per manufacturer advice. Using premium oils like Lucas Synthetic SAE 10W-40 helps maintain engine cleanliness and performance between changes.



### Is Synthetic Blend Oil Good For 4-stroke Motorcycles?



Synthetic blend oils, such as Quicksilver 10W-40, offer a balance of protection and affordability. They provide better wear resistance than conventional oils while being cost-effective for regular riding.



### Are Honda Pro Gn4 Oils Suitable For All 4-stroke Bikes?



Honda Pro GN4 oils, like the 10W-40 variant, are formulated for Honda and compatible 4-stroke engines. They ensure optimal lubrication, reduce engine deposits, and meet OEM standards for reliable bike performance.



### What Makes Lucas Oil 10w-40 Ideal For Atv And Utv Engines?



Lucas Oil 10W-40 is designed for high-performance ATV and UTV engines. It offers superior thermal stability, reduces engine wear, and protects against sludge, ensuring long engine life under tough conditions.
