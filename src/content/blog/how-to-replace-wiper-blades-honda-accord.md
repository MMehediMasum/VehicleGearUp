**Your Honda Accord’s wiper blades play a crucial role in keeping your windshield clear and your driving safe, especially during rain or snow. But when was the last time you checked them?**

If your wipers are leaving streaks or making noise, it’s time for a change. Replacing your wiper blades yourself is easier than you think and can save you time and money. In this guide, you’ll learn exactly how to replace wiper blades on your Honda Accord quickly and correctly—so you can get back on the road with clear visibility and confidence.

Keep reading to find out the simple steps you can follow right now.




## Tools And Materials Needed

Before you start replacing the wiper blades on your Honda Accord, gathering the right tools and materials is crucial. Having everything ready ensures a smooth, hassle-free process without unnecessary interruptions. You’ll save time and avoid frustration by preparing ahead.

Here’s what you’ll typically need:

* New wiper blades compatible with your Honda Accord
* A flathead screwdriver (sometimes helpful for releasing clips)
* A clean cloth or rag to wipe the windshield and wiper arms
* Gloves to protect your hands and improve grip

Sometimes, the wiper arm might be stiff or stuck. In that case, a little WD-40 or lubricant can help loosen it up. But usually, the replacement is straightforward if you have the right blades and simple tools.

### Types Of Wiper Blades

Not all wiper blades are built the same. You’ll find three main types: traditional frame-style blades, beam blades, and hybrid blades. Each type has different benefits and fits differently on your car.

Frame-style blades have a metal skeleton and are generally the most common and affordable. Beam blades are sleek, with no external frame, offering better performance in snow and ice. Hybrid blades combine features of both for durability and smooth wiping.

Choosing the right type depends on your local weather and driving conditions. If you live where it snows frequently, beam blades might give you better visibility. What kind of weather challenges does your Honda Accord face?

### Choosing The Right Size

Size matters when it comes to wiper blades. Using the wrong size can lead to poor wiping performance and even damage to your windshield. Honda Accords typically use different sizes for driver and passenger sides, so make sure you check both.

You can find the exact sizes in your vehicle’s owner manual or by using online tools provided by blade manufacturers. For example, a 2020 Honda Accord often uses a 26-inch blade on the driver’s side and a 22-inch blade on the passenger’s side.

Getting the correct size ensures your new blades fit perfectly and clear your windshield effectively. Have you checked your current blade sizes lately? It’s a simple step that makes a big difference.




## Preparing Your Honda Accord

Preparing your Honda Accord properly is key to replacing the wiper blades safely and easily. Taking a moment to set up your car and the wiper arms helps avoid damage and ensures a smooth process. Clear space and a steady setup make the job faster and less frustrating.

### Park And Turn Off Engine

Choose a flat, stable area to park your Honda Accord. Make sure the engine is completely off. Remove the key from the ignition to prevent any accidental movement. This step keeps the wiper arms from moving while you work on them. It also protects you from any electrical issues.

### Lift The Wiper Arms

Gently lift each wiper arm away from the windshield. The arms should hold in a raised position by themselves. If they don’t, support them carefully to avoid snapping back. This position gives you full access to the blades and makes removal much easier. Handle the arms with care to prevent bending or damage.

## Removing Old Wiper Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lift-the-wiper-arm-away-from-the-windshield-gently-press-the-small-tab-to-release-the-old-blade-and-slide-it-off-carefully&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lift the wiper arm away from the windshield gently. Press the small tab to release the old blade and slide it off carefully. Avoid forcing it to prevent damage to the arm or windshield.

Removing old wiper blades from your Honda Accord may seem daunting, but it's a straightforward process that anyone can tackle. It's crucial to replace them regularly for optimal visibility, especially during heavy rain or snow. Let's make this task a breeze by breaking it down into simple steps.

### Locate The Release Tab

Begin by raising the wiper arm away from the windshield. This will give you better access to the wiper blade. Look closely near the connection point of the wiper blade and arm. You will find a small tab, often called the release tab. This tab is the key to removing the blade. Press it gently. You might hear a slight click, indicating the blade is unlocked. It's amazing how a simple press can make a big difference!

### Detach The Blade From The Arm

With the release tab pressed, slide the blade down towards the windshield. This motion will unhook the blade from the wiper arm. Be careful and gentle to avoid scratching your car's paint or damaging the windshield. Once unhooked, the blade will slide off easily. ## Installing New Wiper Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-new-wiper-blades-on-your-honda-accord-is-a-straightforward-task-that-can-greatly-improve-your-visibility-during-rainy-or-snowy-conditions-the-key-lies-in-correctly-aligning-and-securing-the-blade-to-ensure-it-performs-effectively-without-damaging-the-windshield-or-arm&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing new wiper blades on your Honda Accord is a straightforward task that can greatly improve your visibility during rainy or snowy conditions. The key lies in correctly aligning and securing the blade to ensure it performs effectively without damaging the windshield or arm. Let’s break down how to get this right.

### Aligning The Blade With The Arm

Start by holding the new wiper blade next to the arm to check the fit. Make sure the connector on the blade matches the shape of your Honda Accord’s wiper arm.

Position the blade so it is parallel to the arm, ensuring the rubber edge will contact the windshield evenly. This alignment prevents streaking and uneven wear.

Have you noticed how a misaligned blade can create more noise and reduce visibility? Taking a moment here saves frustration later.

### Securing The New Blade

Once aligned, attach the blade by sliding or snapping it into place on the arm’s hook or pin, depending on your model. You should hear a click or feel it lock firmly.

Give the blade a gentle tug to confirm it’s securely fastened. A loose blade can come off while driving and cause damage or accidents.

If your Honda Accord uses a different attachment style, consult the blade’s instructions to ensure a proper fit. Incorrect installation can reduce blade life and performance.

## Testing The New Wipers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=testing-the-new-wipers-is-a-crucial-final-step-after-replacement-it-ensures-the-blades-work-properly-and-clear-your-windshield-well&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Testing the new wipers is a crucial final step after replacement. It ensures the blades work properly and clear your windshield well. Proper testing helps avoid streaks, noise, and poor visibility during rain.

Handle the wiper arms carefully to prevent damage. Follow the correct process to check their function smoothly and safely.

### Lowering The Arms Gently

Hold the wiper arm firmly near the blade. Slowly lower it onto the windshield surface. Avoid dropping the arm quickly to prevent cracking the glass. Make sure the blade sits flat and contacts the glass evenly. Repeat for the other arm.

### Running The Wipers

Turn on the car’s ignition and activate the wipers. Watch how the new blades move across the windshield. Check for smooth motion without skipping or squeaking. Confirm the blades clear water and debris fully. Test with washer fluid to see the spray and wipe action. Stop the wipers and inspect for any damage or loose parts.

## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=proper-maintenance-keeps-your-honda-accords-wiper-blades-in-good-shape-it-ensures-clear-visibility-and-safer-driving&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Proper maintenance keeps your Honda Accord’s wiper blades in good shape. It ensures clear visibility and safer driving. Simple care routines extend the life of your wipers and improve performance.

### Cleaning Wiper Blades Regularly

Clean the wiper blades often to remove dirt and debris. Use a soft cloth with mild soap and water. Wipe the rubber edge gently to avoid damage. This helps prevent streaks and uneven wiping. Clean both blades and windshield for best results. Regular cleaning stops buildup that can wear out the blades faster.

### When To Replace Again

Replace your wiper blades every 6 to 12 months. Check them for cracks, tears, or stiffness. Poor wiping or squeaking sounds mean it is time for new blades. Replace them sooner in harsh weather or heavy use. Fresh blades ensure clear vision during rain or snow. Always choose the right size and type for your Accord.

Yes, replacing wiper blades on a Honda Accord is easy and can be done without tools. Lift the wiper arm, unclip the old blade, and attach the new one. Follow the specific blade’s instructions for a secure fit.

Silicone or hybrid wiper blades work best for Honda Accord. They offer better durability and smoother wiping. Silicone blades resist weather damage, while hybrid blades combine rubber’s flexibility with a sleek design. Choose based on your driving conditions.
