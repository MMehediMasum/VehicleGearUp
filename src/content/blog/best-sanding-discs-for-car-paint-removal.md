## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Grit Sanding Discs Work Best For Car Paint Removal?



Grits between 60 and 240 are ideal for removing car paint. Lower grits like 60 remove paint fast. Higher grits like 240 smooth the surface after stripping. Using varied grits ensures efficient and clean paint removal without damaging the metal.



### Can I Use Sanding Discs On Both Metal And Wood Surfaces?



Yes, many sanding discs are versatile for metal and wood. Products like WelderElite and Vantego strip paint and rust on both surfaces. Always check the disc specifications to ensure compatibility for your project material.



### How Do Stripping Discs Differ From Regular Sanding Discs?



Stripping discs are coarser and designed for paint, rust, and oxidation removal. Regular sanding discs focus on smoothing surfaces. Stripping discs often have abrasive materials like aluminum oxide or silicon carbide for heavy-duty tasks.



### Are Wet And Dry Sanding Discs Effective For Car Paint Removal?



Yes, wet and dry sanding discs provide flexibility in paint removal. Wet sanding reduces dust and heat, protecting the car paint layers. Dry sanding is faster but creates more dust. Choose based on your project needs and tools.



### What Angle Grinder Size Fits Most Sanding And Stripping Discs?



Most sanding and stripping discs fit 4 to 5-inch angle grinders. Common sizes include 4-1/2" and 5" discs. Ensure the disc’s arbor size matches your grinder’s spindle for secure attachment and safe operation.



### How Many Sanding Discs Should I Buy For A Car Paint Removal Project?



Buy at least 6 to 12 discs in varied grits for efficiency. Kits with 60 to 2000 grit offer versatility. More discs mean longer use and better finish control during paint removal and surface preparation.
