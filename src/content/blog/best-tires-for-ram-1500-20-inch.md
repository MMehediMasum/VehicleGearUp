## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Tire Sizes Fit Ram 1500 20 Inch Rims?



The common tire size for Ram 1500 20 inch rims is 275/55-20. This size offers good balance of performance, comfort, and load capacity. It fits perfectly with OE Wheels DG65 and DG62 styles, ensuring optimal safety and handling for your truck.



### Are Satin Black Or Chrome Rims Better For Ram 1500?



Satin black rims offer a sleek, modern look with less visible dirt. Chrome rims shine brightly but require more maintenance. Both styles fit Ram 1500 with 5x139. 7 bolt pattern and 20x9 size, so choose based on your preferred aesthetic and upkeep.



### Can I Use Oe Wheels Dg65 Rims On My Ram 1500?



Yes, OE Wheels DG65 20 inch rims fit Ram 1500 with a 5x139. 7 bolt pattern. They come in satin black, chrome, and gloss silver finishes. These rims are compatible with TerraMax RT and Dueler H/L Alenza tires for reliable performance.



### What Tires Offer Best Traction For Ram 1500 20 Inch Rims?



TerraMax RT tires provide excellent all-terrain traction and durability for Ram 1500. Dueler H/L Alenza tires offer smoother highway rides with good grip. Both tire types in 275/55-20 size match the OE Wheels DG series rims perfectly.



### Are Steel Rims Suitable For 20 Inch Ram 1500 Wheels?



Steel rims are strong and budget-friendly but heavier than alloy rims. They suit basic or work trucks and offer durability. For 20 inch wheels, OE Direct Replacement black steel rims fit 2013-2018 Ram 1500 models well and support standard tire sizes.



### How Do I Choose The Right Bolt Pattern For Ram 1500 Wheels?



Ram 1500 typically uses 5x139. 7 or 6x139. 7 bolt patterns. Check your truck’s specs to confirm. OE Wheels DG65 and DG62 rims come in 5x139. 7, while DG74 offers 6x139. 7. Matching bolt pattern ensures proper fit and safe wheel installation.
