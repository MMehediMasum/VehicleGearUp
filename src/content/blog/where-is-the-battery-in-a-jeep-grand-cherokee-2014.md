## Battery Location In 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee

Knowing the exact location of the battery in your 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee can save you a lot of time and frustration, especially during emergencies or routine maintenance. This SUV has a design that balances accessibility with protection, so the battery placement is not always where you might expect. Understanding where to find it helps you troubleshoot issues or jump-start your vehicle quickly.

### Under The Hood Placement

The primary battery in the 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee is located under the hood, which is the most common spot for car batteries. You’ll find it on the driver’s side near the front corner of the engine bay. This placement makes it easy to access for routine checks or replacements without needing special tools.

Look for a rectangular black box with two cables connected—this is your battery. Its location is designed to be convenient while also protected from excessive heat coming from the engine.

### Inside The Engine Bay

Inside the engine bay, the battery is secured firmly with a clamp and surrounded by a plastic cover to shield it from debris and moisture. This cover can be removed by loosening a few clips or screws, allowing you to inspect the battery terminals or clean corrosion if needed.

The battery terminals are marked with plus (+) and minus (-) signs to help you connect jumper cables correctly. Have you ever noticed how corrosion buildup on these terminals can cause starting problems? Regular checks here can prevent unexpected dead battery issues.

### Alternative Battery Locations

Unlike some vehicles that place the battery in the trunk or under the rear seats, the 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee sticks to the under-hood location for its main battery. However, it’s worth noting that some models might have an auxiliary battery or a battery disconnect switch located elsewhere for specific electrical systems.

If you’re unsure or if your Grand Cherokee has additional electrical accessories, check your owner’s manual or under the rear cargo area for any extra battery components. ## Accessing The Battery

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=accessing-the-battery-in-a-2014-jeep-grand-cherokee-is-straightforward-knowing-its-exact-location-helps-in-performing-maintenance-or-replacement&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Accessing the battery in a 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee is straightforward. Knowing its exact location helps in performing maintenance or replacement. This section guides you through opening the hood, removing the battery cover, and following safety steps.

### Opening The Hood

Start by locating the hood release lever inside the vehicle. It is usually found under the dashboard on the driver's side. Pull the lever to unlock the hood. Move to the front of the Jeep and find the safety latch beneath the hood. Lift the hood carefully and secure it with the prop rod to keep it open while you work.

### Removing Battery Cover

The battery sits on the driver's side of the engine bay. It has a plastic cover to protect it. To remove the cover, look for clips or screws holding it in place. Use a screwdriver if needed. Lift the cover gently and set it aside. This exposes the battery terminals and cables for access.

### Safety Precautions

Always wear gloves and safety glasses when handling the battery. Avoid touching both battery terminals at the same time to prevent shocks. Disconnect the negative terminal first to reduce short circuit risk. Keep metal tools away from the battery to avoid sparks. Work in a well-ventilated area and do not smoke near the battery.

The 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee uses an Absorbent Glass Mat (AGM) battery. AGM batteries are durable and provide stable power. They are designed for vehicles with high electrical demands.

### Voltage And Capacity

The Jeep Grand Cherokee's battery operates at 12 volts. It typically has a capacity of 700 to 850 cold cranking amps (CCA). This capacity ensures the vehicle starts smoothly in cold weather.

## Battery Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-battery-of-your-2014-jeep-grand-cherokee-ensures-reliable-starts-and-longer-battery-life-simple-care-steps-prevent-common-battery-problems-and-keep-your-vehicle-running-smoothly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the battery of your 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee ensures reliable starts and longer battery life. Simple care steps prevent common battery problems and keep your vehicle running smoothly. Focus on cleaning, checking charge, and knowing when to replace the battery.

### Cleaning Terminals

Dirty terminals cause poor electrical connection and starting issues. Use a wire brush or battery terminal cleaner to remove corrosion. Disconnect the negative cable first, then the positive cable. After cleaning, reconnect the cables, positive first, then negative. Apply a thin layer of petroleum jelly to prevent future corrosion.

### Checking Charge Levels

Regularly check the battery’s charge level to avoid unexpected failure. Use a multimeter to measure voltage; a fully charged battery reads about 12.6 volts. If the voltage drops below 12.4 volts, it needs charging. Keep the battery charged, especially during cold weather or long periods without driving.

### When To Replace The Battery

Batteries usually last 3 to 5 years. Signs of a weak battery include slow engine crank and dim lights. Frequent jump-starts or warning lights on the dashboard also indicate replacement time. Test the battery at a service center if unsure about its condition. Replace promptly to avoid being stranded.

  


## Troubleshooting Battery Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=battery-problems-can-stop-your-jeep-grand-cherokee-from-starting-knowing-how-to-spot-and-fix-these-issues-saves-time-and-stress&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Battery problems can stop your Jeep Grand Cherokee from starting. Knowing how to spot and fix these issues saves time and stress. Troubleshooting helps find the cause quickly and get you back on the road.

### Common Battery Problems

* Battery drains fast or won’t hold a charge.
* Corroded battery terminals causing poor connection.
* Loose or damaged battery cables.
* Old battery with reduced capacity.
* Electrical system drawing power when off.

### Jump-starting The Vehicle

Jump-starting can revive a dead battery. Park a working vehicle close to the Jeep. Connect jumper cables in this order:

* Red cable to positive (+) terminal on dead battery.
* Other end of red cable to positive (+) terminal on good battery.
* Black cable to negative (-) terminal on good battery.
* Other end of black cable to a metal ground on Jeep’s engine.

Start the working vehicle and let it run for a few minutes. Then try to start your Jeep. Remove cables in reverse order after starting.

### When To Seek Professional Help

* Battery won’t charge despite jump-starting.
* Repeated battery drain without clear cause.
* Visible battery damage or swelling.
* Electrical issues persist after battery check.
* Unfamiliar with handling car batteries safely.

Professionals have tools to test and replace batteries safely. They diagnose deeper electrical problems if needed.
