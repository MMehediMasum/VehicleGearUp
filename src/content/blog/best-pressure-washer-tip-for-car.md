## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Pressure Washer Tip For Car Detailing?



The 15-degree nozzle tip is ideal for car detailing. It offers a strong spray without damaging paint. It removes dirt efficiently and suits most pressure washers.



### How Do Foam Cannon Tips Improve Car Washing?



Foam cannon tips create thick, clinging foam that loosens dirt. This foam protects paint and enhances cleaning power. They are perfect for gentle, thorough car washes.



### Can I Use A Turbo Nozzle For Washing Cars?



Yes, turbo nozzles provide a rotating spray that removes tough grime quickly. Use with caution as the pressure is strong and can damage paint if too close.



### Which Nozzle Size Fits Most Snow Foam Lances?



A 1. 1 mm thread nozzle is commonly universal for snow foam lances. It ensures compatibility and optimal foam production for pressure washers up to 3000 PSI.



### What Is The Advantage Of Quick Connector Nozzle Tips?



Quick connectors allow easy and fast nozzle changes during car washing. They save time and improve convenience without needing extra tools or complicated setups.



### How Does A Dual Connector Foam Cannon Benefit Cleaning?



Dual connector foam cannons spray two foam streams simultaneously. This speeds up coverage on large surfaces like SUVs and trucks. It improves cleaning efficiency and foam distribution.



## Conclusion



Choosing the right pressure washer tip can make car cleaning easier and safer. Different tips offer various spray angles and pressures to suit your needs. For delicate car surfaces, use wider angles like 40 degrees to avoid damage. Foam cannon tips help apply soap evenly, making dirt removal gentle and effective.



Turbo nozzles provide strong, rotating sprays that clean tough grime quickly but need careful use. Quick connector tips save time by letting you switch nozzles fast. Always match the tip pressure to your car’s surface to protect the paint. Using the right tip improves cleaning results and keeps your car looking its best.



Remember, gentle care extends your car’s shine and finish. Choose wisely, and your pressure washer will be a helpful tool for every wash.
