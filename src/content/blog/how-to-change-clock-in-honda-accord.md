## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls in your Honda Accord is the first step to adjusting the time. The controls are usually found near the dashboard, close to the center console. Knowing exactly where to find these buttons or knobs saves time and effort.

The clock controls may look different depending on your Honda Accord model year. Some models use physical buttons, while others might use touchscreen settings. Identifying the type of control helps you change the clock quickly and easily.

### Check The Dashboard Area

Start by looking at the central dashboard. Most Honda Accords have the clock display integrated there. Look for buttons labeled “Clock,” “H,” or “M.” These stand for hour and minute adjustments.

### Identify Physical Buttons

Many models have small buttons near the radio or climate controls. They might be round or rectangular. Common labels include “Clock,” “Set,” or “Adjust.” Pressing these buttons allows you to enter the clock setting mode.

### Use The Touchscreen Interface

Newer Honda Accords feature a touchscreen display. Access the main menu and find the “Settings” option. Within settings, look for “Clock” or “Time.” This digital option lets you adjust the time without physical buttons.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual

If you cannot find the clock controls, your owner’s manual is a helpful resource. It provides clear images and instructions specific to your vehicle model. This helps you locate and operate the clock controls with ease.

## Set The Minutes

Setting the minutes on your Honda Accord’s clock might seem tricky at first, but it’s straightforward once you know the right buttons to press. The minutes often require a different approach than the hours, so paying attention here will save you time and frustration.

Think about how often you glance at your dashboard clock. Having the correct minutes displayed keeps you punctual and stress-free. Let’s focus on exactly how to adjust those minutes with ease.

### Locate The Minute Adjustment Button

Look closely at your Honda Accord’s dashboard or infotainment panel. You’ll find a small button labeled “Minute” or “MIN” near the clock display.

If your model has a touchscreen, this button might be virtual, so check the clock settings menu. Sometimes, the same button used to adjust hours doubles for minutes by pressing it differently.

### Press And Hold To Activate Minute Setting Mode

Press and hold the “Minute” button until the minutes start blinking on the display. This signals that you are now in the minute adjustment mode.

It’s a subtle change but crucial to notice. If you don’t see the blinking, you might be pressing the wrong button or your model requires a different sequence.

### Adjust The Minutes Using The Same Or A Different Button

Once the minutes blink, press the “Minute” button repeatedly to increase the minutes one by one.

In some Honda Accord models, you might need to use the “Clock” or “Set” button to toggle through numbers instead. Experiment gently to find what works without forcing any button.

### Finalize The Minute Setting

After setting the correct minute, press the “Set” or “Clock” button again to save your changes.

The blinking should stop, confirming the minutes are locked in. If the time resets or doesn’t save, try repeating the process or consult your owner’s manual for your specific year and trim.

### Tips To Avoid Common Mistakes

* Don’t hold buttons too long unless the instructions say so; a quick press might be enough.
* Be patient—some models have a delay between button presses.
* If your clock is digital and connected to GPS or radio signals, the manual setting might get overridden automatically.

## Save The New Time

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-setting-the-correct-time-on-your-honda-accord-saving-the-new-time-is-essential-this-step-ensures-the-clock-keeps-accurate-time-even-after-turning-off-the-engine&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After setting the correct time on your Honda Accord, saving the new time is essential. This step ensures the clock keeps accurate time even after turning off the engine. Without saving, the clock might reset or show the wrong time.

Saving the time is simple and quick. It locks your changes into the car’s system. This process helps avoid confusion and makes your drive smoother.

### Press The Clock Or Set Button

Locate the clock or set button on your dashboard. Press it to confirm the new time. Hold it for a few seconds until the display stops blinking. This action saves the time into the memory.

### Check The Display For Confirmation

After saving, look at the clock display. It should show the new time steadily without blinking. A steady display means the time is saved properly. If it blinks, repeat the saving step.

### Turn Off And On The Engine To Test

Turn off your Honda Accord’s engine. Wait a few seconds, then start it again. Check the clock to see if the new time remains. If correct, the time saved successfully. If not, try saving it again.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Changing the clock in a Honda Accord usually goes smoothly. Still, some common issues may cause trouble. These problems can make setting the time confusing or impossible. Understanding these issues helps fix the clock quickly and easily.

Many problems come from simple causes. Dead car batteries or electrical glitches can reset the clock. Other times, the buttons used to set the time might not work well. Dirt or wear can block proper button function.

Sometimes, the display screen does not show the time correctly. This could mean a connection problem or a need for a system reset. Knowing how to handle these issues keeps your clock accurate.

### Buttons Not Responding

Check if the buttons are stuck or dirty. Clean around the buttons with a soft cloth. Avoid using water or harsh chemicals. If buttons remain unresponsive, the internal switch may be faulty.

### Clock Resets After Engine Off

A weak or dead battery often causes this issue. Test the car battery voltage. Replace the battery if it is low. Also, check the fuse related to the clock or radio system.

### Display Not Showing Time

Inspect the display screen for cracks or damage. Reset the car’s electronics by disconnecting the battery for a few minutes. Reconnect and check if the clock works properly.

### Time Changes Incorrectly

Ensure you use the correct buttons to set hours and minutes. Follow the steps in the owner’s manual carefully. Software glitches might cause wrong time changes; a system update or dealer visit may help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Set The Clock In A Honda Accord?

To set the clock, turn on the ignition without starting the engine. Press and hold the clock button until the time blinks. Use the “H” button to adjust hours and the “M” button for minutes. Press the clock button again to save the time.

### Can I Change The Clock Using The Honda Accord Touchscreen?

Yes, newer Honda Accord models with touchscreen allow clock adjustments in the settings menu. Go to “Settings,” then “Clock,” and adjust hours and minutes. Confirm the changes and exit the menu. This method is quick and user-friendly.

### Why Won’t My Honda Accord Clock Change?

If the clock won’t change, the ignition might not be on or the clock button could be stuck. Check the fuse related to the clock system. Refer to your owner’s manual for troubleshooting steps if the problem persists.

### How To Reset The Honda Accord Clock After Battery Replacement?

After replacing the battery, the clock resets to 12:00. Turn on the ignition, press and hold the clock button until it blinks. Adjust hours and minutes using the “H” and “M” buttons. Press the clock button to save the new time.
