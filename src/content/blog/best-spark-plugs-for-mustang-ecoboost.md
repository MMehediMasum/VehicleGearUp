## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Spark Plugs For Mustang Ecoboost?



The NGK Iridium IX and A-Premium Iridium Platinum spark plugs are top choices for Mustang Ecoboost. They offer excellent performance, durability, and compatibility with 2. 3L engines from 2015-2021, ensuring improved ignition and fuel efficiency for Mustang Ecoboost owners.



### How Often Should Mustang Ecoboost Spark Plugs Be Replaced?



Spark plugs for Mustang Ecoboost should be replaced every 60,000 to 100,000 miles. Iridium and platinum plugs last longer. Regular replacement ensures optimal engine performance, fuel economy, and prevents misfires or ignition issues.



### Are Iridium Spark Plugs Better For Mustang Ecoboost?



Yes, iridium spark plugs provide better ignition, longer lifespan, and improved fuel efficiency. They resist wear and deliver consistent performance in Mustang Ecoboost engines, outperforming traditional copper plugs in durability and reliability.



### Can I Use Motorcraft Spark Plugs On Mustang Ecoboost?



Yes, Motorcraft spark plugs like SP548 and SP493 are genuine OEM options. They fit Mustang Ecoboost models and offer reliable performance with proper heat range and durability designed for Ford engines.



### What Ignition Coil Packs Fit Mustang Ecoboost?



UF670 ignition coil packs are compatible with Mustang Ecoboost 2. 3L engines. They improve spark delivery, reduce misfires, and enhance overall engine performance. These coil packs are often sold with matching spark plugs for convenience.



### Do Spark Plugs Affect Mustang Ecoboost Fuel Efficiency?



Yes, quality spark plugs improve combustion efficiency and fuel economy. Worn or faulty plugs cause incomplete combustion, reducing mileage. Upgrading to iridium or platinum plugs can enhance Mustang Ecoboost’s fuel efficiency noticeably.
