## Remote Start Features

The remote start feature on the Chevy Equinox adds comfort and convenience to your daily drives. It allows you to start the engine from a distance, warming or cooling the cabin before you get inside. This feature works through the key fob or a mobile app, depending on the model. It helps save time and makes driving in extreme weather easier.

Understanding the remote start features helps you use it safely and effectively. Here are the key benefits, model compatibility, and range limits to know.

### Key Benefits

* Prepares the vehicle’s temperature before driving
* Improves comfort during hot summers or cold winters
* Starts the engine without unlocking the doors
* Helps defrost windows for better visibility
* Can reduce wear on the engine by warming it up

### Compatibility With Models

The remote start feature is available on many Chevy Equinox trims. Most newer models from 2018 and later include this option. It often comes standard on higher trims or as part of an upgrade package. Some older or base models may not support remote start.

Check your owner’s manual or ask your dealer to confirm if your Equinox supports remote start. You may need to activate the feature or update your vehicle software.

### Range And Limitations

The remote start system works within a limited distance. Typically, the key fob has a range of up to 300 feet. The mobile app can operate from anywhere with internet access, but it depends on your phone’s connection and vehicle network.

Remote start will not work if the car is already running or the hood is open. It also won’t start if the check engine light is on. Safety features prevent starting if the vehicle is in gear or the doors are unlocked.

## Activating Remote Start

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=press-the-remote-start-button-twice-to-activate-the-engine-remotely-the-chevy-equinox-will-start-warming-up-or-cooling-down-before-you-enter&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Press the remote start button twice to activate the engine remotely. The Chevy Equinox will start, warming up or cooling down before you enter. This feature helps prepare your vehicle quickly and comfortably.

Activating Remote Start on your Chevy Equinox can be a helpful feature. It helps warm up the car in winter or cool it down in summer. You have three ways to activate it. Each method is simple and quick. Let's explore how you can use remote start.

### Using The Key Fob

The key fob is a small device you carry. It allows you to control your car remotely. To start the Equinox, press the lock button on your key fob. Then press and hold the remote start button. Your car will start, and the lights may flash. It's that easy.

### Using The Mychevrolet App

The MyChevrolet App is a handy tool for your smartphone. Download and install it from your app store. Open the app and sign in with your Chevrolet account. Find the remote start option on the dashboard. Tap it, and your Equinox will start remotely. You can also check fuel levels in the app.

### Starting With A Smartphone

Your smartphone can also start your Chevy Equinox. Download the MyChevrolet App on your phone. Ensure your phone has a stable internet connection. Log in to your account. Locate the remote start feature. Tap the start button, and your car will start. It's convenient and quick.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-the-remote-start-feature-on-your-chevy-equinox-can-save-time-and-frustration-some-problems-happen-more-often-than-others&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with the remote start feature on your Chevy Equinox can save time and frustration. Some problems happen more often than others. Knowing how to fix them helps you enjoy the convenience of remote start without delays.

This section covers three common issues: the remote start not activating, range problems, and error messages. Each problem has simple checks and solutions to get your system working again quickly.

### Remote Start Not Activating

Check the key fob battery first. A weak battery may stop the signal.

* Replace the battery if it is old or weak.
* Make sure the vehicle is in park and the doors are closed.
* Ensure the hood is fully closed; the system won’t start if it’s open.
* Look for any warning lights on the dashboard that may block remote start.

Try the remote start again after these checks. If it still won’t activate, consult your owner’s manual for specific reset steps.

### Range Problems

The remote start may not work if you are too far from your vehicle. Obstacles like buildings or trees reduce signal strength.

* Move closer to the vehicle and try again.
* Remove any objects blocking the key fob transmitter.
* Check for nearby electronic devices that can cause interference.

Regularly check the key fob battery to keep the signal strong. A fresh battery improves the range.

### Error Messages

Sometimes, the system shows error messages on the dashboard or key fob display. These messages give clues about the problem.

* Write down the exact error message.
* Look up the message in your vehicle’s owner’s manual.
* Common errors include low battery, maintenance reminders, or safety sensor alerts.
* Clear the issue mentioned before trying remote start again.

If the error message stays, contact your dealer for help. They can diagnose and fix complex problems.

## Safety Tips For Remote Start

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-the-remote-start-feature-on-your-chevy-equinox-can-make-your-driving-experience-more-comfortable-especially-during-extreme-weather-however-safety-should-always-be-a-priority-whenever-you-use-this-convenience&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using the remote start feature on your Chevy Equinox can make your driving experience more comfortable, especially during extreme weather. However, safety should always be a priority whenever you use this convenience. Below are essential safety tips to keep in mind to protect yourself and others while enjoying the benefits of remote start.

### Avoiding Carbon Monoxide Risks

Carbon monoxide is a deadly gas that can build up quickly in enclosed spaces. Never use remote start in a closed garage or any area without proper ventilation. Even if the garage door is open, it’s safer to avoid running the engine remotely to prevent dangerous fumes from accumulating.

Have you ever thought about how quickly carbon monoxide can become a threat? It’s invisible and odorless, making it hard to detect until symptoms appear. Always be cautious and prioritize well-ventilated spaces when using remote start.

### Parking In Safe Areas

Where you park your Chevy Equinox before starting it remotely matters. Choose spots away from heavy traffic or pedestrian paths to reduce risks. Avoid parking on steep slopes where the vehicle could accidentally roll after starting.

Think about the last time you parked—did you consider the safety of your surroundings? Taking a moment to choose a secure spot helps prevent accidents and gives you peace of mind as your vehicle warms up or cools down.

### Locking The Vehicle

Always ensure your Chevy Equinox is locked before activating the remote start. This prevents unauthorized access and protects your belongings. The remote start system is designed to keep the doors locked during operation, but double-checking is a smart habit.

Have you ever left a door unlocked by mistake? It happens to the best of us. Locking your vehicle before remote start keeps it secure and reduces the risk of theft or unwanted entry while your vehicle is unattended.
