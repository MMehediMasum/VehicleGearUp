## When The Oil Change Light Appears

The oil change light on your Honda Odyssey signals it is time for maintenance. This light appears to remind you that engine oil needs changing soon. Ignoring this alert can harm your engine and reduce vehicle performance. Understanding when this light turns on helps keep your car in good condition.

### Common Causes

* Oil life is low due to driving time or mileage
* Oil quality has degraded from use or contamination
* Oil sensor detects oil pressure issues
* System needs resetting after an oil change
* Dashboard indicator reacts to scheduled maintenance intervals

### Importance Of Resetting

Resetting the oil change light after service clears the reminder. It helps track the next oil change accurately. Without resetting, the light may stay on, causing confusion. Resetting confirms the car’s computer knows fresh oil is in use. This action supports proper engine care and timely maintenance.

## Resetting Oil Change Light On 2011-2017 Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-change-light-on-honda-odyssey-models-from-2011-to-2017-is-simple-this-light-reminds-drivers-to-change-the-oil-for-better-engine-health&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil change light on Honda Odyssey models from 2011 to 2017 is simple. This light reminds drivers to change the oil for better engine health. After an oil change, the light must be reset to turn off.

This process does not require special tools. Following clear steps helps avoid confusion. It takes just a few minutes and ensures accurate maintenance tracking.

### Step-by-step Process

* Turn the ignition to the ON position without starting the engine.
* Press the “Select/Reset” button on the dashboard until the oil life indicator appears.
* Press and hold the “Select/Reset” button for about 10 seconds.
* The oil life indicator will start blinking and then reset to 100%.
* Release the button and turn off the ignition.
* Start the engine to confirm the oil change light is off.

### Troubleshooting Tips

* Ensure the ignition is in the ON position but the engine is off.
* Hold the “Select/Reset” button firmly until the indicator blinks.
* Repeat the steps if the oil life does not reset on the first try.
* Check the owner’s manual for model-specific instructions.
* Visit a mechanic if the light stays on despite resetting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset Oil Change Light On Honda Odyssey?

To reset the oil change light, turn the ignition to ON without starting. Press the accelerator pedal fully three times within 10 seconds. The light will blink and then turn off, indicating a successful reset.

### Why Won't My Honda Odyssey Oil Light Reset?

If the oil light won't reset, ensure the ignition is ON and pedal pressed fully three times. Check your vehicle’s manual for specific model instructions. A software or sensor issue may require professional diagnosis.

### When Should I Reset The Oil Change Light?

Reset the oil change light after completing an oil change. This ensures accurate tracking of your next oil service interval. Resetting helps maintain your Honda Odyssey’s engine health and performance.

### Can I Reset Oil Change Light Without A Tool?

Yes, the Honda Odyssey allows manual reset without tools. Use the ignition switch and accelerator pedal method. Follow your owner’s manual instructions carefully for a successful reset.
