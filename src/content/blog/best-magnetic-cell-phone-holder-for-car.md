## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Magnetic Car Phone Holder Strong?



Magnetic car phone holders use powerful magnets, like 20+ strong magnets or 2400gf magnetic force. This ensures your phone stays secure, even on bumpy rides. Strong suction cups add extra grip to dashboards or vents, preventing slips and falls while driving.



### Are Magnetic Phone Holders Compatible With All Phones?



Most magnetic holders support iPhone models 12 to 16 Pro Max and many Android phones. Universal designs with metal plates allow compatibility with Samsung and other smartphones. Always check if your phone supports MagSafe or if a metal plate is needed for magnetic attachment.



### How Adjustable Are Magnetic Phone Holders For Cars?



Magnetic holders often offer 360° rotation and multi-angle adjustments. This allows you to position your phone for optimal viewing while driving. Adjustable mounts reduce glare and improve safety by keeping your hands free and your eyes on the road.



### Can Magnetic Mounts Damage My Phone Or Case?



Magnetic mounts are generally safe for phones and cases. They use strong but controlled magnets that do not interfere with phone functions. For extra protection, use a compatible case designed for magnetic mounts or place metal plates between the phone and the holder.



### How Easy Is Installation Of Magnetic Car Phone Holders?



Installation is quick and tool-free. Most holders use suction cups or clips to attach to the dashboard, vent, or windshield. They come with reusable adhesives or strong suction for secure placement, allowing easy removal or repositioning without damaging surfaces.



### What Features Improve Safety Using A Magnetic Phone Holder?



Features like 360° rotation, strong magnetic hold, and stable suction improve safety. They keep your phone visible without obstructing your view. Hands-free use reduces distractions and allows you to focus on driving, promoting a safer driving environment.
