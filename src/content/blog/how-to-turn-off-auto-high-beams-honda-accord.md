## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Sometimes, the auto high beam system in your Honda Accord may not work as expected. Troubleshooting common issues helps you fix problems without visiting a mechanic. This section covers frequent concerns and easy solutions.

Understanding these issues saves time and keeps your driving safe. Follow simple checks to ensure your auto high beams function correctly.

### Auto High Beam Won't Turn Off

If the auto high beams stay on constantly, start by checking the control switch. Make sure it is set to the off position.

Dirty or obstructed sensors can cause this issue. Clean the sensors around the headlights gently with a soft cloth.

Resetting the system often solves the problem. Turn off the car, wait a minute, then restart it.

Inspect the fuse related to the lighting system. Replace it if it is blown or damaged.

### Settings Reset After Restart

When settings reset after each restart, the system might not save your preferences. This can happen due to low battery voltage.

Check the car battery and replace it if it is weak. A stable power supply helps save settings.

Sometimes, the software needs updating. Visit a Honda dealer to get the latest system update.

Verify that the vehicle’s memory functions correctly. Faulty memory modules can cause setting loss.
