Stainless steel is ideal for Silverado exhausts. It resists rust, lasts longer, and maintains a polished look, as seen in Flowmaster and DNA Motoring kits.



### Do Aftermarket Exhausts Affect Fuel Efficiency On Silverado 1500?



Aftermarket exhausts like Flowmaster and Rough Country can improve fuel efficiency slightly by enhancing exhaust flow and engine performance, but gains vary by model and driving style.
