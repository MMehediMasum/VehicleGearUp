## Locate The Speedometer Settings

Locating the speedometer settings on your Jeep Grand Cherokee is the first step to customizing your driving experience. Knowing exactly where to find these settings saves time and avoids frustration. It also lets you tailor the display to your preferences, whether you want a digital readout or a classic look.

### Access The Instrument Cluster Menu

Start by turning on your Jeep's ignition without starting the engine. This powers up the instrument cluster and lets you interact with its menu.

Use the buttons on your steering wheel, typically on the left side, to navigate. Look for a button labeled “Menu” or an icon that resembles a list or gears. Press it to enter the instrument cluster menu.

Once inside, you will see different options related to vehicle settings. Take your time to scroll through them using the arrow buttons or toggle switch until you reach the speedometer settings.

### Identify The Display Options

Within the speedometer settings, you will find several display options to choose from. These may include:

* Digital or analog speed readout
* Units of measurement (miles per hour or kilometers per hour)
* Brightness and contrast adjustments

Choosing the right display can improve readability and reduce distractions while driving. For example, switching to a digital display can offer clearer numbers during night drives.

Have you noticed how small changes in your dashboard can make a big difference in your driving comfort? Spend a few moments experimenting with these options to find the setup that works best for you.




## Switch Between Metric And Imperial Units

Switching between metric and imperial units on your Jeep Grand Cherokee’s speedometer is a simple yet valuable feature. Whether you're traveling across borders or prefer a specific measurement system, adjusting the display to show either miles per hour (mph) or kilometers per hour (km/h) makes your driving experience smoother. Let’s look at how to make this change quickly and confidently.

### Select Miles Per Hour Or Kilometers Per Hour

To start, access your vehicle’s settings menu using the steering wheel controls or touchscreen interface. Look for the “Units” or “Display” option, which is usually found within the settings related to the instrument cluster or speedometer.

Once there, you’ll see options to choose between **miles per hour (mph)**and **kilometers per hour (km/h)**. Highlight your preferred unit and select it. The interface is designed to be user-friendly, so you shouldn’t have to navigate through complex menus.

### Confirm The Unit Change

After selecting your preferred unit, the system typically asks you to confirm the change. Make sure you confirm this to apply the new setting. If you skip this step, the speedometer might revert to the previous unit when you turn off the vehicle.

Once confirmed, glance at your speedometer to verify that the numbers now display in the unit you chose. This immediate feedback helps ensure you’re ready for the road without any confusion about your speed.

## Use Steering Wheel Controls

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-speedometer-display-on-a-jeep-grand-cherokee-is-simple-use-the-steering-wheel-controls&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the speedometer display on a Jeep Grand Cherokee is simple. Use the steering wheel controls. These controls allow you to switch between different display options quickly. The process is straightforward, making adjustments a breeze. Follow the steps below to navigate and save your settings with ease.

### Navigate Menus With Buttons

Find the buttons on the left side of your steering wheel. Use these buttons to access the main menu on your dashboard display. Look for the "Menu" button and press it. Once you are in the menu, use the arrow buttons. Navigate through the options until you find the "Settings" or "Display" menu. Select it by pressing the "OK" or "Enter" button. This will open up more options for you to explore.

### Save And Exit Settings

Once you select the desired display setting, confirm your choice. Press the "OK" or "Enter" button on the steering wheel. This action saves your new speedometer display settings. After saving, navigate back to the main menu using the arrow buttons. Exit the settings menu by pressing the "Menu" button again. Your new speedometer display is now active.

  


## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=sometimes-your-jeep-grand-cherokees-speedometer-display-might-not-respond-as-expected-when-you-try-to-change-its-settings-this-can-be-frustrating-especially-if-you-rely-on-accurate-information-while-driving&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Sometimes, your Jeep Grand Cherokee's speedometer display might not respond as expected when you try to change its settings. This can be frustrating, especially if you rely on accurate information while driving. Troubleshooting common issues can save you time and help keep your vehicle's digital display working smoothly.

### Resetting The Display Settings

If your speedometer display freezes or shows incorrect information, a reset can often fix the problem. You can reset the settings by turning the ignition off and then back on while holding down the display button on the dashboard. This simple action clears minor glitches without affecting other vehicle functions.

Another way to reset is through the vehicle’s menu system:

* Navigate to the Settings option on the dashboard screen.
* Select Display or Instrument Cluster settings.
* Choose the option to reset or restore defaults.

Have you noticed your display lag or freeze after using certain features? Resetting can restore normal operation quickly and often avoids the need for complicated repairs.

### When To Consult A Dealer

Some issues with the speedometer display require professional help, especially if resetting doesn’t work. If you encounter persistent errors, flickering screens, or complete display failure, it’s best to visit your Jeep dealer.

Dealers can run diagnostic tests to identify deeper electrical or software problems. Attempting repairs on your own in such cases may void warranties or cause further damage.

Think about when you last updated your vehicle’s software. Could outdated software be the root of your display troubles? Dealers often provide updates that fix bugs and improve performance, which you might miss if you only try basic troubleshooting.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Speedometer Units On A Jeep Grand Cherokee?

To change speedometer units, access the vehicle settings via the dashboard menu. Select “Display” or “Units,” then choose between mph or km/h. Confirm your selection to update the speedometer display instantly.

### Can I Customize The Speedometer Display Style On My Jeep?

Yes, you can customize the speedometer style. Use the Uconnect system or dashboard settings to select different display themes, colors, or layouts. This enhances your driving experience by personalizing the instrument cluster appearance.

### Is It Possible To Reset The Speedometer After Changes?

Yes, you can reset the speedometer display to default settings. Navigate to the settings menu and select “Reset” or “Factory Defaults. ” This will undo any customizations and restore the original display configuration.

### Do I Need Special Tools To Change The Speedometer Display?

No special tools are required. All adjustments are made through the vehicle’s infotainment system or dashboard controls. Simply follow the on-screen instructions to modify the speedometer display easily.
