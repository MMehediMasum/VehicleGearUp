## Locate The Clock Controls

Finding the clock controls on your 2023 Toyota Highlander is the first step to changing the time. These controls are easy to access but vary depending on the model's trim and features. Knowing exactly where to look saves time and avoids frustration.

The clock settings are usually integrated into the infotainment system or located near the dashboard. Some models may use physical buttons, while others rely on touchscreen menus. Understanding these options helps you quickly locate and adjust the clock.

### Check The Infotainment Screen

Most 2023 Toyota Highlanders have a touchscreen display. The clock settings often appear inside the main menu or settings menu. Look for icons labeled “Setup,” “Settings,” or “Clock.”

Tap these icons to open the clock adjustment options. You can then change the hour and minute manually or set automatic time sync.

### Look For Physical Buttons Near The Dashboard

Some Highlander models have physical buttons dedicated to clock settings. These buttons might be labeled “Clock,” “H,” and “M.”

They are usually near the radio or climate control panel. Pressing these buttons allows manual adjustment of the time without using the screen.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual

The owner’s manual provides detailed instructions on finding and using the clock controls. It includes images and explanations tailored to your model.

Keep the manual in your glove box for quick reference. It helps avoid confusion and ensures you adjust the time correctly.

## Using The Touchscreen Display

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-2023-toyota-highlander-features-a-modern-touchscreen-display-that-makes-adjusting-the-clock-simple-the-touchscreen-offers-an-intuitive-way-to-set-the-time-without-pressing-multiple-buttons&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The 2023 Toyota Highlander features a modern touchscreen display that makes adjusting the clock simple. The touchscreen offers an intuitive way to set the time without pressing multiple buttons. You can easily access, adjust, and save the clock settings directly on the display.

### Access Clock Settings

Start by turning on your Highlander's infotainment system. On the home screen, locate the “Settings” icon and tap it. Scroll through the menu until you find “Clock” or “Time Settings.” Select this option to open the clock settings menu.

### Adjust Hours And Minutes

In the clock settings, you will see options to change the hours and minutes. Use the plus (+) or minus (–) buttons on the touchscreen to adjust the hour first. After setting the hour, move to the minutes and adjust them the same way. The display updates in real time.

### Save Changes

Once the correct time appears on the screen, look for the “Save” or “OK” button. Tap it to confirm your new time settings. The display will exit the clock menu, and your changes will be saved automatically. Now, your Highlander’s clock shows the correct time.

## Using Physical Buttons

Press the physical buttons on the dashboard to adjust the clock settings. Use the “H” button to change hours and the “M” button to set minutes. Hold a button briefly to move the time quickly.

Changing the time on a Toyota Highlander 2023 is simple. This section explains using physical buttons to adjust the clock.

### Find Clock Adjustment Buttons

First, locate the clock adjustment buttons. These are usually near the dashboard or infotainment system. Look for buttons labeled "Clock," "H," and "M." These buttons help adjust the hour and minute settings. Ensure your car is in park mode for safety.

### Set The Time Manually

Press the "Clock" button to start. Then, use the "H" button to set the hour. Each press increases the hour by one. Watch the display change as you press. Next, press the "M" button to adjust the minutes. Each press increases the minutes by one. Double-check the time to ensure it's correct. When done, the system should automatically save the new time.



## Syncing With Gps Or Smartphone

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=syncing-your-2023-toyota-highlanders-clock-with-gps-or-a-smartphone-keeps-the-time-accurate-it-saves-you-the-hassle-of-setting-the-time-manually&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Syncing your 2023 Toyota Highlander’s clock with GPS or a smartphone keeps the time accurate. It saves you the hassle of setting the time manually. The system updates automatically based on your location and network time. This is useful during travel or daylight saving changes.

Using GPS or smartphone sync ensures the clock matches the official time. It also adjusts for time zone changes without needing your input. This feature helps maintain punctuality and avoids confusion during trips.

### Enable Automatic Time Update

Access the vehicle’s settings on the touchscreen display. Find the “Clock” or “Time” menu in the setup options. Look for an option labeled “Automatic Time Update” or similar. Turn this feature on to allow syncing with GPS or your phone.

Make sure your smartphone is connected via Bluetooth or USB. The system uses this connection to receive the correct time. GPS-based updates work when the vehicle has a clear sky view for satellite signals.

### Troubleshoot Sync Issues

Check if Bluetooth or USB connection is active and stable. Restart your phone and the vehicle’s infotainment system if syncing fails. Ensure the GPS signal is strong, especially in tunnels or parking garages.

Verify that the vehicle software is up to date. Outdated firmware can cause syncing problems. Resetting the clock settings to default can also help resolve errors.

* Disconnect and reconnect your smartphone.
* Disable and re-enable the automatic time update feature.
* Move to an open area for better GPS reception.

## Tips For Accurate Time Setting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-the-time-on-your-2023-toyota-highlander-accurately-is-more-than-just-a-convenienceit-keeps-you-on-schedule-and-helps-your-vehicles-systems-work-seamlessly-small-errors-in-time-settings-can-affect-navigation-reminders-and-even-voice-commands&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting the time on your 2023 Toyota Highlander accurately is more than just a convenience—it keeps you on schedule and helps your vehicle’s systems work seamlessly. Small errors in time settings can affect navigation, reminders, and even voice commands. Here are essential tips to ensure your clock is always spot on.

### Check For Daylight Saving Time

Make sure your clock automatically adjusts for Daylight Saving Time (DST). The Toyota Highlander’s system usually has an option to toggle DST on or off. If this isn’t set correctly, you might find your clock an hour ahead or behind, especially during the spring and fall transitions.

Think about the last time your clock was off by an hour—did you remember to check DST settings? It’s a quick fix that saves a lot of confusion.

* Go to the clock settings in the infotainment menu.
* Look for the Daylight Saving Time option and enable it if you live in an area that observes DST.
* If you travel frequently across time zones, consider disabling automatic DST to avoid repeated adjustments.

### Verify Time Zone Settings

Even if the clock is set to the right time, an incorrect time zone can throw everything off. The Highlander lets you select the correct time zone, and getting this right is critical for accuracy.

Ask yourself: Are you sure the time zone matches your current location? It’s easy to overlook this, especially after a road trip or move.

* Access the time zone settings through the vehicle’s system menu.
* Scroll through the list and select your correct zone to match your geographic location.
* Double-check after any cross-country trips to ensure the zone hasn’t reverted or changed.

Taking just a few minutes to confirm these settings can save you from missed appointments and confusing displays. Your Highlander’s clock will then truly become a reliable companion on every journey.






## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Clock On A 2023 Toyota Highlander?

To change the clock, press the "Menu" button on the touchscreen. Select "Setup," then "Clock," and adjust the time using the "+" or "–" buttons. Confirm by pressing "OK" or "Set. "

### Can I Set The Time Manually On Toyota Highlander 2023?

Yes, you can manually set the time via the infotainment system. Access "Setup" > "Clock," then use the controls to adjust hours and minutes.

### Does The 2023 Highlander Update Time Automatically?

The 2023 Highlander updates time automatically if connected to GPS or a smartphone via Bluetooth. Otherwise, manual adjustment is needed.

### How To Fix Incorrect Time After Daylight Saving Change?

Manually update the time in "Setup" > "Clock. " Adjust the hour forward or backward as needed to reflect daylight saving changes.
