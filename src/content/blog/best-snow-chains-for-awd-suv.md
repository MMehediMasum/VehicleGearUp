## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Snow Chains For Awd Suvs?



The best snow chains for AWD SUVs offer strong traction and easy installation. Models like SCC SZ143 and SCC Peerless Auto-Trac are top-rated for durability and performance in snowy conditions.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size Snow Chains For My Suv?



Measure your tire width and diameter accurately. Snow chains like SCITOO and KYX offer adjustable sizes to fit various tire widths from 195mm to 295mm for SUVs and pickups.



### Are Cable Tire Chains Better Than Traditional Chains?



Cable tire chains are lighter and easier to install. They provide excellent traction for AWD SUVs on icy roads, as seen in the SCC Z-575 and Security Chain Company models.



### Can I Install Snow Chains Without Professional Help?



Yes, many models like SCITOO and AUTO HIGH feature quick, easy installation steps. They come with tools and instructions suitable for beginners and emergency use.



### Are Universal Snow Chains Effective For All Suv Tires?



Universal chains like KYX and SCITOO fit a wide range of tire sizes. They offer reliable traction but always verify compatibility with your specific tire dimensions before purchase.



### What Maintenance Is Required For Snow Chains After Use?



After use, clean chains thoroughly to remove salt and dirt. Dry them completely and store in a dry bag like the AUTO HIGH storage pack to prevent rust and damage.
