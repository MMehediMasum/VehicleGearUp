## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Full Suspension Mountain Bike Better For Trails?



Full suspension bikes absorb shocks from rough terrain, offering smoother rides. They improve control, comfort, and reduce rider fatigue on trails.



### Which Bike Has The Best Suspension For The Price?



The SAVADECK Carbon Fiber Soft Tail offers excellent front and rear suspension. It balances quality and affordability for a smooth ride.



### How Important Is Wheel Size In Full Suspension Bikes?



Wheel size affects speed and maneuverability. 29-inch wheels like on the Schwinn Traxion roll over obstacles easier, while 27. 5-inch wheels offer better agility.



### Are Hydraulic Disc Brakes Better Than Mechanical Ones?



Hydraulic disc brakes, like on the Mongoose Salvo Comp, provide stronger, more consistent stopping power and require less maintenance than mechanical brakes.



### Can Electric Full Suspension Bikes Handle Rough Terrain?



Yes, electric full suspension bikes like the Ridstar Electric offer powerful motors and shock absorption. They are great for tough, off-road rides.



### What Gear Speeds Suit Beginner Riders Best?



Beginner riders benefit from 7 to 21-speed bikes. Models like Mongoose Impasse 1. 0 and Ecarpat 24 26 offer versatile gearing for various trail conditions.
