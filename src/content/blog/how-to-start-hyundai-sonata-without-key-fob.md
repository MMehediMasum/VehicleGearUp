## Locate The Key Slot

Locating the key slot on your Hyundai Sonata is the first crucial step when you need to start the car without the key fob. This slot is often hidden for security reasons, but knowing exactly where to find it can save you time and frustration. Let’s break down how to access the manual key and pinpoint the key slot on your door.

### Accessing The Manual Key

Your Hyundai Sonata’s key fob contains a hidden manual key inside it. This key is designed for emergencies like a dead fob battery or lost remote access. To get it out, look for a small button or latch on the fob; pressing or sliding this will release the mechanical key.

Keep this key handy in your wallet or glovebox. It’s a simple tool but essential when your electronic access fails. Have you ever found yourself locked out because the fob battery died unexpectedly? This manual key prevents that panic.

### Finding The Key Slot On The Door

The key slot is usually concealed to maintain the sleek look of your Sonata’s door handle. Typically, it hides behind a small cover near the driver’s side door handle. To reveal it, gently pry or slide off the cover—no need for force, just patience.

Once exposed, the slot looks like a traditional keyhole. Insert your manual key here to unlock the door manually. This simple action lets you open the door and start the engine using the key slot located on the ignition or push-to-start system, depending on the model year.

Have you checked your Sonata’s owner manual? It often has images showing the exact location of the key slot, which can be a real time-saver.
