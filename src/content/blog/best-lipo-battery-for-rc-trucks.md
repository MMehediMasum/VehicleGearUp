## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Voltage For Rc Truck Lipo Batteries?



Most RC trucks perform well with 7. 4V (2S) or 11. 1V (3S) LiPo batteries. Choose based on your truck's motor requirements and speed needs.



### How Does Battery Capacity Affect Rc Truck Runtime?



Higher capacity (mAh) means longer runtime. For RC trucks, 1800mAh to 6200mAh batteries provide a good balance between weight and usage time.



### Are Hard Case Lipo Batteries Better For Rc Trucks?



Yes, hard case batteries offer better protection and durability. They are ideal for rough terrains and high-impact RC truck activities.



### Which Connector Type Is Best For Rc Truck Batteries?



Common connectors include T, XT60, Deans, and EC5. Match the connector to your RC truck for optimal power transfer and compatibility.



### How Does Discharge Rate (c Rating) Impact Rc Truck Performance?



Higher C ratings deliver more current for better acceleration. For RC trucks, a 35C to 120C rating ensures efficient power without overheating.



### Can I Use 2s And 3s Lipo Batteries Interchangeably?



Only if your RC truck supports both voltages. Check the motor and ESC specifications to avoid damage or poor performance.
