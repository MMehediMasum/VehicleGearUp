### Check The Battery Orientation

Before you insert the new battery, look closely at the battery compartment for polarity markings. Usually, you will see a plus (+) and minus (-) sign indicating how the battery should be positioned.

Align the new battery so that the positive side matches the plus sign inside the compartment. Incorrect placement can prevent your key from working, so double-check this step.

### Gently Place The Battery Into The Slot

Handle the battery with clean hands or a cloth to avoid transferring oils or dirt. Slide the battery carefully into the slot, making sure it fits snugly without forcing it.

If you feel resistance, pause and verify the orientation again. The battery should sit flat and flush inside its holder.

### Secure The Battery Cover

After inserting the battery, replace the key’s cover by snapping or sliding it back into place. Ensure it locks securely to protect the battery from dust and moisture.

## Tips For Battery Maintenance

Keeping the Lexus RX350 key battery in good shape helps avoid sudden failures. Proper care extends battery life and keeps the key working smoothly. Simple habits protect the battery and save time and money on replacements.

### Store The Key Away From Extreme Temperatures

Heat and cold can damage the battery inside the key. Keep the key in a cool, dry place. Avoid leaving it in direct sunlight or freezing areas. This helps the battery last longer and keeps the key responsive.

### Avoid Using The Key Unnecessarily

Pressing buttons too often drains the battery faster. Use the key only when needed. Do not hold buttons for long periods. This reduces battery wear and extends its life.

### Clean The Key Contacts Gently

Dirt and dust can block battery contacts inside the key. Use a soft cloth or cotton swab to clean contacts carefully. Avoid using water or harsh chemicals. Clean contacts improve battery connection and key performance.

### Check Battery Status Regularly

Test the key battery once a month. Watch for slower response or weak signal. Replace the battery at the first sign of trouble. Early replacement prevents key failure and inconvenience.

### Use The Correct Battery Type

Always install the battery recommended by Lexus. Using the wrong type may damage the key or shorten battery life. Check the key manual or inside the key for the right battery model.
