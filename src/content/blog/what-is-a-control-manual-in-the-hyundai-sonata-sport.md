## Maintenance Tips

Maintaining your Hyundai Sonata Sport ensures it runs smoothly and lasts longer. The control manual offers clear instructions for basic upkeep. Simple care routines prevent costly repairs and keep your car safe.

Regular attention to key parts improves performance and fuel efficiency. Understanding common issues helps you handle problems quickly. The manual guides you through easy steps to keep your vehicle in top shape.

### Routine Checks

* Check engine oil level weekly using the dipstick.
* Inspect tire pressure and tread depth for safety.
* Examine brake fluid and coolant levels monthly.
* Test all lights and signals before driving.
* Clean air filters to improve engine breathing.
* Look for leaks under the vehicle regularly.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

* Engine won’t start: Check battery connections and charge.
* Strange noises: Identify source and consult manual guidelines.
* Warning lights: Refer to the manual for specific codes.
* Overheating: Ensure coolant is at proper level and radiator is clean.
* Poor fuel economy: Inspect spark plugs and tire pressure.
* Brake problems: Check fluid level and brake pad wear.
