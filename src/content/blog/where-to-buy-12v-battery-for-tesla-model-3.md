## Tesla Model 3 12v Battery Basics

The 12V battery in your Tesla Model 3 plays a crucial role in powering essential systems like lights, locks, and the touchscreen. Unlike the main high-voltage battery that drives the car, this smaller battery handles everyday electronics. Understanding its basics helps you know when and where to replace it, ensuring your Tesla stays reliable and ready to go.

### Battery Type And Specs

Your Tesla Model 3 uses a sealed lead-acid 12V battery, designed specifically for electric vehicles. It typically has a capacity of around 33 Ah and a voltage of 12 volts, which is standard for most automotive 12V batteries. The battery is compact and fits snugly under the hood or in the front trunk, depending on your Model 3 version.

Choosing the right replacement means matching these specs exactly. Using a battery with different voltage or size can cause issues with your car’s electrical system. You’ll also want to look for batteries that offer good cold-cranking amps (CCA) to handle starting demands in colder climates.

### Signs You Need A Replacement

How do you know when your 12V battery is failing? One clear sign is if your car struggles to start or the touchscreen and lights act up unexpectedly. You might also notice warning messages on your dashboard or hear clicking sounds when you try to unlock or start the car.

Another tip is to watch out for dimming headlights or inconsistent operation of electronic features. If your battery is more than three years old, it’s wise to get it tested regularly. Ignoring these signs can leave you stranded or cause more expensive electrical problems down the road.
