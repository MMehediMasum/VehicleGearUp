## Remote Start Basics

Understanding the basics of remote start helps you use your 2017 Honda Civic more efficiently. Remote start lets you start the engine without being inside the car. This feature is handy during cold or hot weather. It allows the car to warm up or cool down before you get in. Let’s explore what remote start does and why it benefits your daily drive.

### What Remote Start Does

Remote start turns on your Honda Civic’s engine from a distance. You press a button on your key fob or smartphone app. The engine starts running, and the climate system activates. This warms or cools the car before you enter. The car stays locked and secure while running. It also runs for a limited time and shuts off automatically if not used.

### Benefits Of Remote Starting Your Honda Civic

* Comfort: Get into a warm or cool car instantly.
* Convenience: Start the engine without walking to the car.
* Safety: The car stays locked while the engine runs.
* Fuel Efficiency: Modern engines warm up faster with remote start.
* Defrosting: Clear windows quickly in cold weather.
* Time-Saving: Ready to go when you are.

### Models With Factory Remote Start

Certain 2017 Honda Civic trims include factory remote start. Typically, EX, EX-T, EX-L, and Touring trims offer this feature. It comes with the key fob or HondaLink app. Check your owner's manual or key fob buttons for a remote start icon.

Factory remote start allows you to start the engine and climate control remotely. It works within a set range, usually up to 300 feet. This feature is reliable and integrates well with your vehicle's systems.

### Aftermarket Remote Start Options

If your Honda Civic lacks factory remote start, install an aftermarket system. Many brands offer kits designed for 2017 Civics. Choose one compatible with your vehicle's electronics and security system.

Professional installation is recommended. It ensures proper function and avoids damage. Aftermarket systems provide remote start with added flexibility and features, such as extended range or smartphone control.

## Using The Factory Remote Start

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-factory-remote-start-feature-in-the-2017-honda-civic-allows-you-to-start-your-car-from-a-distance-this-helps-warm-up-or-cool-down-the-vehicle-before-you-get-inside&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The factory remote start feature in the 2017 Honda Civic allows you to start your car from a distance. This helps warm up or cool down the vehicle before you get inside. Using the original remote makes the process simple and safe. No extra tools or apps are needed. Just follow the easy steps below to use this feature correctly.

### Locate The Remote Start Button

The remote start button is on your car’s key fob. It looks like a circular arrow or a car with a curved arrow. This button is usually separate from the lock and unlock buttons. Sometimes it is smaller and may need to be pressed twice or held down to activate. Make sure your key fob battery is working well for the best signal.

### Step-by-step Activation Process

* Press the lock button on the key fob to secure the doors.
* Within five seconds, press and hold the remote start button.
* Hold the button for about two seconds until the car’s lights flash.
* The engine will start, and the climate system will activate.
* The car will run for up to 10 minutes before shutting off automatically.
* To turn off the engine remotely, press the remote start button again.

Make sure your car is parked in a safe, open space before using remote start. The system will not start if the hood is open or the key is inside the vehicle. This keeps your Civic safe and prevents accidents.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=remote-starting-your-2017-honda-civic-adds-convenience-especially-on-chilly-mornings-or-hot-summer-days-but-what-happens-when-the-remote-start-doesnt-work-as-expected&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Remote starting your 2017 Honda Civic adds convenience, especially on chilly mornings or hot summer days. But what happens when the remote start doesn't work as expected? Troubleshooting these issues can save you time and frustration, helping you get back on the road quickly.

### Remote Start Not Activating

If pressing the remote start button doesn't activate your Honda Civic, start by checking the basics. Is your vehicle in “Park” and are all doors fully closed? The remote start system won't work if these conditions aren't met.

Battery health plays a big role. A weak car battery or a faulty connection can prevent remote start from activating. Try turning on your headlights—if they appear dim, your battery might need a charge or replacement.

Also, look at the temperature settings. Some Honda models disable remote start if the outside temperature is too low or too high, to protect the engine. Have you noticed this happening only during extreme weather?

### Key Fob Not Responding

Your key fob is the command center for remote start, so a non-responsive fob can halt your plans. First, check the key fob battery. A dead or weak battery is a common culprit and easy to fix.

Try re-syncing the key fob to your car. Sometimes, the connection between the fob and the vehicle gets lost. If you’re unsure how to do this, the owner's manual provides step-by-step instructions tailored for your Civic.

Interference from nearby electronics or strong radio signals can also block the fob’s communication. Have you tried using the remote start in a different location to rule out this issue?

## Safety Tips For Remote Starting

Remote starting your 2017 Honda Civic offers great convenience, especially on chilly mornings or hot afternoons. However, it’s essential to prioritize safety every time you use this feature. Understanding the key precautions can protect you, your passengers, and others around you.

### Avoiding Carbon Monoxide Risks

Carbon monoxide is a dangerous gas that can build up quickly if your car is running in an enclosed space like a garage. Always make sure your vehicle is parked outside or in a well-ventilated area before using the remote start.

Never remote start your Civic inside a closed garage, even if the garage door is open. The gas can linger and pose serious health risks. Think about your family’s safety—would you want to risk exposure without realizing it?

Consider installing a carbon monoxide detector near your garage entrance as an extra layer of protection. This simple step can alert you early if dangerous levels start to accumulate.

### Legal Considerations In Your Area

Remote starting laws vary depending on where you live, and some places have strict rules about idling vehicles. Check your local regulations before you use the remote start feature to avoid fines or legal trouble.

Some cities limit how long you can let your car idle, especially in residential areas. Knowing these rules helps you use remote start responsibly and avoid unnecessary penalties.

If you’re unsure, contact your local DMV or law enforcement agency. They can provide clear guidelines, making sure your convenience doesn’t come at the cost of breaking the law.
