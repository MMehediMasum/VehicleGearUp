## Locate The Wiper Controls

Finding the wiper controls is the first step to turning off the back windshield wiper on a Ford Escape. These controls are designed for easy reach and clear use. Knowing their location helps you quickly adjust the wipers during your drive.

The controls are usually placed where the driver can access them without distraction. They come in different forms, like switches or levers. Understanding where and how these controls work makes your driving safer and more comfortable.

### Dashboard And Steering Column Controls

The wiper controls are often near the steering wheel. Look around the steering column for a lever or stalk. This lever usually controls the front and rear wipers.

Some models have buttons on the dashboard instead. These buttons can be marked with a wiper icon. The dashboard controls are easy to spot and use while driving.

### Identifying The Rear Wiper Switch

The rear wiper switch is separate from the front wiper control. It might be part of the same lever or a different button. Look for an icon that shows a window with a wiper blade.

* The rear wiper switch often has settings like off, intermittent, and on.
* Turning it to off stops the rear wiper from moving.
* Be sure to check the markings to avoid confusion with the front wiper control.




## Turn Off The Rear Wiper

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-off-the-rear-windshield-wiper-on-a-ford-escape-is-simple-this-feature-helps-keep-your-back-window-clear-improving-your-view-while-driving&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning off the rear windshield wiper on a Ford Escape is simple. This feature helps keep your back window clear, improving your view while driving. Knowing how to switch it off quickly can save battery life and reduce noise.

There are two main ways to turn off the rear wiper: using the control stalk or the dashboard button. Both options are easy and fast to use.

### Using The Control Stalk

The control stalk is located on the right side of the steering wheel. It controls both front and rear wipers.

* Look for the small switch or ring on the stalk.
* Rotate the switch until the rear wiper stops moving.
* Ensure the stalk is in the “off” position for the rear wiper.

This method works well if you want quick access without looking away from the road.

### Using The Button On The Dashboard

Some Ford Escape models have a dedicated rear wiper button on the dashboard.

* Locate the button, often marked with a rear wiper icon.
* Press the button once to turn off the rear wiper.
* Pressing again may turn it back on if needed.

This option is useful if you prefer using buttons instead of stalk controls.

## Troubleshooting Wiper Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=dealing-with-windshield-wiper-issues-on-your-ford-escape-can-be-frustrating-especially-when-the-wiper-wont-turn-off-before-jumping-into-repairs-it-helps-to-understand-the-common-causes-behind-these-problems&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Dealing with windshield wiper issues on your Ford Escape can be frustrating, especially when the wiper won't turn off. Before jumping into repairs, it helps to understand the common causes behind these problems. Knowing what to check can save you time and avoid unnecessary expenses.

### Wiper Won't Turn Off

If your wiper keeps running even after you switch it off, it might be due to a stuck wiper switch or a malfunction in the control module. Sometimes, dirt or moisture can cause the switch to stick, so cleaning around the control stalk might fix the issue.

Another quick test is to turn the ignition off and back on. If the wiper stops, the problem might be in the vehicle’s electrical system rather than the switch itself. Have you noticed if this happens only after heavy rain or car washes? That might hint at water intrusion causing the fault.

### Electrical Or Fuse Problems

Electrical issues can cause the wiper system to behave unpredictably. Check the fuse box for any blown fuses related to the wipers. Your Ford Escape’s manual lists the exact fuse for the windshield wiper circuit; replacing a blown fuse might restore normal function.

If the fuse is intact, the problem might be a faulty relay or wiring issue. Look for corrosion or loose connections in the fuse box and wiring harnesses near the wiper motor. Have you ever had electrical components fail because of water damage? It’s more common than you think, especially in older vehicles.

Performing these basic checks can often pinpoint the problem before you seek professional help. Would you try these steps yourself or prefer a mechanic to handle the electrical side? Your comfort level with car repairs will guide your next move.

## Tips For Wiper Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ford-escapes-back-windshield-wiper-in-top-shape-doesnt-just-improve-visibility-it-also-extends-the-wipers-life-simple-maintenance-steps-can-prevent-annoying-streaks-and-sudden-failures-especially-when-you-need-your-wiper-the-most&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Ford Escape’s back windshield wiper in top shape doesn’t just improve visibility; it also extends the wiper’s life. Simple maintenance steps can prevent annoying streaks and sudden failures, especially when you need your wiper the most. Let’s look at some practical tips to help you maintain your wiper effectively.

### Regular Cleaning

Dirt and debris build-up on the wiper blade can cause scratches on your windshield and reduce wiping efficiency. Use a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water to wipe the rubber blade gently. Cleaning the blade once a month helps remove grime and keeps it flexible.

Don’t forget to check the wiper arm and the glass surface as well. Dust or residue on the glass can damage the blade over time. Have you noticed your wiper leaving streaks even after cleaning? It might be time to inspect both the blade and the glass closely.

### Replacing Wiper Blades

Wiper blades don’t last forever. Over time, they become brittle or torn, which affects your back windshield’s visibility. Most manufacturers recommend changing the blades every 6 to 12 months, but if you see signs like skipping or streaking, replace them sooner.

Choosing the right size and type for your Ford Escape is essential. Check your owner’s manual or ask at an auto parts store to find the perfect fit. Remember, installing new blades is usually straightforward and can save you a costly windshield repair down the road.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Turn Off The Rear Wiper On Ford Escape?

To turn off the rear wiper on Ford Escape, rotate the rear wiper control knob to the "Off" position. This stops the back windshield wiper from operating.

### Where Is The Rear Wiper Control On Ford Escape?

The rear wiper control is on the wiper stalk, usually on the right side of the steering wheel. Rotate or push it to control the rear wiper.

### Can I Disable The Back Wiper Permanently On Ford Escape?

No, Ford Escape doesn't support permanent disabling. You can only turn it off using the rear wiper control switch.

### What If The Rear Wiper Won’t Turn Off On Ford Escape?

If the rear wiper won’t turn off, check the control switch first. If it persists, a fuse or motor issue might need professional inspection.
