## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Cloth For Cleaning Car Windows?



Microfiber cloths like E-Cloth and VIKING are best for car windows. They clean streak-free and remove grime effectively without scratching.



### How Often Should I Wash Microfiber Window Cleaning Cloths?



Wash microfiber cloths after every 5-7 uses. Use mild detergent and avoid fabric softeners to maintain their cleaning power and longevity.



### Can Microfiber Cloths Clean Both Interior And Exterior Car Windows?



Yes, microfiber cloths like USANOOKS and Airlab work great on both interior and exterior windows. They remove dirt and fingerprints efficiently.



### Are Microfiber Cloths Reusable And Eco-friendly For Window Cleaning?



Yes, most microfiber cloths such as E-Cloth and Puomue are washable and reusable. This reduces waste and is eco-friendly.



### Do Microfiber Cloths Work Without Chemical Cleaners?



Yes, premium microfiber cloths like E-Cloth Glass Kits clean windows effectively with just water, reducing chemical use and streaks.



### Which Microfiber Cloth Size Is Ideal For Car Window Cleaning?



Sizes between 12x12 to 20x16 inches, like HOMEXCEL and Chemical Guys, offer good coverage and ease for car window cleaning.
