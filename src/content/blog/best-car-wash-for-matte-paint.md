## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Soap For Matte Car Paint?



The best soap for matte paint is pH-balanced and residue-free. Products like Chemical Guys CWS 995 and McKee's 37 Matte Finish Auto Wash are ideal. They clean gently without damaging the matte finish or gloss, preserving the paint's unique look and texture.



### Can I Use Foam Cannons With Matte Car Wash Soap?



Yes, many matte car wash soaps like Chemical Guys CWS 995 and graphene shampoos work perfectly with foam cannons. They create thick foam that lifts dirt safely without harming matte or satin finishes. Always use products labeled safe for matte paint when using foam guns.



### How Do Matte Car Wash Kits Protect Paint?



Matte car wash kits, such as McKee’s 37 and Dr. Beasley’s kits, combine soap, cleaners, and protectants. They clean gently, prevent water spots, and add UV protection. These kits maintain the matte finish by reducing gloss and preserving the paint’s texture and color.



### Are Matte Car Wash Soaps Environmentally Friendly?



Many matte car wash soaps like Dr. Beasley’s and Stjarnagloss are biodegradable and VOC-free. They are designed to be eco-friendly while protecting your car's finish. Choosing these soaps helps reduce harmful chemical runoff and supports sustainable car care practices.



### Can I Use Waterless Wash On Matte Paint?



Yes, waterless washes like Dr. Beasley’s Matte Waterless Wash are safe for matte finishes. They provide high lubrication to prevent scratches while cleaning dirt and dust. This option is convenient for quick touch-ups without risking damage to the matte surface.



### Does Matte Car Wash Soap Add Uv Protection?



Some matte car wash soaps, including McKee's 37 Matte Finish Auto Wash, add UV protection. This helps prevent fading and oxidation caused by sun exposure. Using these products regularly extends the life and appearance of your matte paint finish.
