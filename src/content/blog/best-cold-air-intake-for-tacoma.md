Cold air intakes help your Tacoma’s engine breathe better by bringing in cooler air. Cooler air is denser and contains more oxygen, which improves combustion. This leads to better horsepower, torque, and towing ability. Many systems also include heat shields and high-flow air filters to protect the engine from hot air and dirt.



Brands like Rough Country, Spectre, K&N, Niyako, EVIL ENERGY, and PHILTOP offer reliable kits for Tacoma models from 2005 to 2025.
