## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Exhaust System For Ram 1500?



The best exhaust system depends on your needs. Flowmaster and BORLA offer durable, performance-tuned options for Ram 1500. Choose based on sound preference, build material, and fitment year. Stainless steel systems provide longevity and aggressive sound.



### Are Flowmaster Exhausts Compatible With Ram 1500?



Yes, Flowmaster exhausts like the Super 10 and 40 Series fit Ram 1500 models from 2009 to 2018. They offer dual 3-inch pipes and aggressive sound. Flowmaster systems are known for durability and improved exhaust flow.



### What Benefits Does A Dual Exhaust System Offer?



Dual exhausts improve engine efficiency by reducing backpressure. They enhance horsepower and torque for Ram 1500 trucks. Additionally, dual tips like black chrome or slash chrome add a stylish look. They produce a deeper, more aggressive sound.



### Is Stainless Steel Better For Ram 1500 Exhausts?



Yes, stainless steel exhausts resist rust and corrosion, ideal for harsh conditions. Brands like BORLA and Rough Country use T-304 stainless steel for durability. This material extends exhaust system life and maintains performance over time.



### Can I Install A Ram 1500 Exhaust System Myself?



Many Ram 1500 exhaust kits offer direct fitment for easy installation. Kits like TOTALFLOW and YOLANDO come with bolt-on designs. Basic mechanical skills and tools are needed. Professional installation is recommended for best results and warranty.



### How Does A Cat-back Exhaust Improve Ram 1500 Performance?



A cat-back exhaust replaces the system from the catalytic converter back. It improves exhaust flow, boosting horsepower and fuel efficiency. Systems like Flowmaster Outlaw and BORLA provide aggressive sound and performance gains without engine modifications.
