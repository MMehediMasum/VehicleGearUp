## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Tie Down Straps For Moving Trucks?



The best tie down straps for moving trucks are heavy-duty ratchet straps with high break strength. Options like AUGO 2200 LB and XSTRAP 4400 LB provide secure cargo hold. Choose straps with safety locks and durable hooks for safe moving.



### How Strong Should Tie Down Straps Be For Moving?



Tie down straps should have a break strength of at least 1500 to 3000 lbs for moving trucks. Higher strength ensures secure fastening of heavy items like appliances and motorcycles. Always check the load capacity before use.



### Are Ratchet Straps Better Than Bungee Cords For Moving?



Yes, ratchet straps are better for moving because they offer stronger, more secure hold. Bungee cords stretch and may loosen, risking cargo safety. Ratchet straps with safety locks provide reliable tension control.



### Can Tie Down Straps Be Used For Motorcycles And Kayaks?



Absolutely. Many tie down straps like AUGO and XSTRAP are designed to secure motorcycles and kayaks during transport. Look for straps with soft loops and hooks to avoid damage to your gear.



### What Length Tie Down Straps Work Best For Moving Trucks?



Straps between 15 to 20 feet long work best for moving trucks. This length fits most cargo sizes and allows easy securing. The 15 ft AUGO and 20 ft XSTRAP straps are popular choices.



### How Do Safety Lock S Hooks Improve Tie Down Straps?



Safety lock S hooks prevent accidental strap release during transit. They keep the ratchet mechanism secure, enhancing cargo safety. Straps with these hooks, like AUGO’s set, reduce risk of loosening or detachment.
