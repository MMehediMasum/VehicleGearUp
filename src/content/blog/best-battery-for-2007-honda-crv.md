## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Battery For A 2007 Honda Crv?



The best battery for a 2007 Honda CRV is an AGM or maintenance-free battery. Group size 47 or 48 with 12V, 60-70Ah, and 680-760 CCA fits well. Brands like Mighty Max MM-H5 and UPLUS AGM batteries are reliable choices.



### Which Key Fob Battery Fits A 2007 Honda Crv?



The CR1616 key fob battery is the correct size for a 2007 Honda CRV. It is compatible with Honda models from 2003 to 2017. Packs usually come with 2 batteries and sometimes include pry tools for easy replacement.



### How Often Should I Replace My Honda Crv Key Fob Battery?



Honda CRV key fob batteries typically last 2 to 3 years. Replace them when the remote’s range decreases or buttons stop responding. Using CR1616 replacement batteries ensures consistent performance and avoids lockout issues.



### Can I Use A Cr1616 Battery For Other Honda Models?



Yes, CR1616 batteries fit many Honda models like Accord, Civic, Pilot, Odyssey, and Ridgeline. These batteries work for key fobs from 2003 to 2017. Always check the fitment guide before purchasing to ensure compatibility.



### What Are The Benefits Of Agm Batteries For Honda Crv?



AGM batteries offer better durability, faster charging, and longer life than traditional lead-acid batteries. They handle start-stop systems well and resist vibration. AGM batteries like UPLUS and Mighty Max improve reliability for your 2007 Honda CRV.
