**Are you selling your car in Illinois and wondering what to do with your license plate? You’re not alone.**

Many people get confused about whether to keep, transfer, or turn in their plates once the sale is done. Making the right move with your Illinois license plate can save you money, avoid legal trouble, and keep your records clean.

You’ll discover exactly what steps you need to take after selling your car in Illinois. Keep reading to make sure you handle your license plate the right way and protect yourself from unnecessary headaches.



## Remove License Plates

Removing your Illinois license plates after selling a car is a crucial step that many sellers overlook. It’s not just about taking off the plates but understanding the timing and what to do with specialized plates. Failing to remove or properly handle your plates can lead to unexpected complications or even legal issues.

### When To Take Off Plates

You should remove your license plates as soon as the sale is complete and ownership has officially transferred. Keeping plates on a car you no longer own can cause confusion or liability if the new owner gets into trouble. Illinois law requires sellers to take off their plates before handing over the vehicle.

Think about how quickly you want to complete the sale. If you remove the plates right after signing the paperwork, you avoid any mix-ups. Waiting too long could mean you’re still responsible for the car in the eyes of the law.

### Handling Personalized Plates

Personalized or vanity plates in Illinois have special rules. You can keep these plates and transfer them to your next vehicle, but you must notify the Illinois Secretary of State. This keeps your registration clean and allows you to maintain your unique plate identity.

If you don’t plan to use the personalized plates again, you can return them to the DMV to avoid any fees. Leaving personalized plates on a sold vehicle might cause you to be charged for renewals or violations you didn’t commit.

Have you checked if your plates are personalized before you sell? This simple step can save you from unnecessary hassle and expense down the line.

## Notify The Illinois Secretary Of State

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-selling-your-car-in-illinois-notifying-the-illinois-secretary-of-state-sos-is-a-key-step-you-cannot-skip-this-notification-protects-you-from-liability-and-updates-the-state-records-to-reflect-the-change-in-ownership&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After selling your car in Illinois, notifying the Illinois Secretary of State (SOS) is a key step you cannot skip. This notification protects you from liability and updates the state records to reflect the change in ownership. It ensures you are no longer responsible for anything related to the vehicle, like tickets or accidents.

### How To Report The Sale

You can report the sale of your vehicle to the Illinois Secretary of State in several ways, making it convenient no matter your schedule. The easiest method is online through the SOS website, where you enter the buyer’s information and details about the sale. This immediate update helps prevent any future confusion or issues.

If you prefer, you can also report the sale by mail or in person at a local SOS office. Just make sure to fill out the Vehicle Sold Notice form accurately. Have you ever wondered what happens if you skip this step? Not reporting could leave you liable for events involving the car after the sale.

### Required Documents

* **Vehicle Sold Notice (Form VSD 190):**This is the official form used to notify the SOS of the sale.
* **Vehicle Title:**You’ll need to sign over the title to the buyer; keep a copy for your records.
* **Bill of Sale:**While not mandatory, it’s a good idea to have one as proof of the transaction.

Remember, the Vehicle Sold Notice form should be submitted within 10 days of the sale. Missing this deadline can cause complications, such as receiving fines or tickets meant for the new owner. Keeping these documents organized and submitting the notice promptly simplifies your car-selling process and protects your interests.

## Transfer Or Surrender Plates

Illinois requires you to transfer or surrender your license plates when selling a car. You can keep the plates for your next vehicle or return them to the DMV. This helps avoid fines and keeps your records updated.

When selling a car in Illinois, handling your license plates is crucial. You must decide whether to transfer them to another vehicle or surrender them to the state. Each option has specific steps and requirements. Understanding these can ensure a smooth process.

### Transferring Plates To Another Vehicle

Transferring plates is a practical choice. It lets you keep the same plates on a new car. First, ensure your registration is current. Next, visit a local Secretary of State facility. Bring your old vehicle's registration and proof of purchase for the new car. Pay the transfer fee. Complete the necessary forms. The process is straightforward. Keeping your existing plates saves time and money.

### Surrendering Plates To The State

Surrendering plates might be necessary. Especially if you don't plan to transfer them. To do this, take your plates to a local Secretary of State office. Inform them that you are surrendering your plates. You may need to fill out a form. This ensures your plates are officially removed from your record. It prevents any future liability. This step is essential if you're not transferring them. It ensures the state knows you no longer own the vehicle.



## Cancel Or Transfer Vehicle Registration

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=when-you-sell-your-car-in-illinois-deciding-whether-to-cancel-or-transfer-the-vehicle-registration-is-crucial-this-choice-affects-your-legal-responsibilities-and-can-save-you-from-unnecessary-fees-or-penalties&w=424&h=324&c=7)

When you sell your car in Illinois, deciding whether to cancel or transfer the vehicle registration is crucial. This choice affects your legal responsibilities and can save you from unnecessary fees or penalties. Understanding the proper steps helps you manage the process smoothly and avoid future hassles.

### Steps For Registration Cancellation

If you choose to cancel your vehicle registration after selling your car, you’ll need to notify the Illinois Secretary of State. This action officially ends your legal obligation for that vehicle’s registration and prevents any future charges or liabilities.

* Remove your license plates from the vehicle before handing it over to the buyer.
* Visit an Illinois Secretary of State facility or use their online system to submit a cancellation request.
* Provide your vehicle’s plate number and other identification details as required.
* Keep a copy of the cancellation confirmation for your records.

Canceling registration is a straightforward way to close your account with the state, especially if you don’t plan to transfer plates or own another vehicle soon. Have you checked if you’re eligible for any partial refund on your registration fees after cancellation?

### Transferring Registration To Buyer

Transferring your vehicle registration to the buyer means they take over the responsibility for the car’s registration and taxes. This option often simplifies the process for both parties but requires some cooperation and proper documentation.

* Remove your license plates from the vehicle; in Illinois, plates usually stay with the seller.
* Ensure the buyer obtains a new set of plates and completes the registration process in their name.
* Complete and sign the title transfer section on the vehicle title document.
* Submit a Notice of Sale to the Illinois Secretary of State to inform them of the ownership change.

Have you ever wondered what happens if you don’t submit a Notice of Sale? Without it, you might still be held liable for tickets or toll violations. Protect yourself by promptly reporting the sale and confirming the buyer registers the vehicle properly.

  


## Handle Vehicle Title

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handling-the-vehicle-title-is-a-crucial-part-of-selling-a-car-in-illinois-the-title-proves-who-owns-the-car-and-must-be-legally-transferred-to-the-buyer&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handling the vehicle title is a crucial part of selling a car in Illinois. The title proves who owns the car and must be legally transferred to the buyer. Properly managing this process protects both the seller and buyer from future problems. It is important to follow Illinois state rules carefully.

### Signing Over The Title

Sign the title in the designated area on the back. Include the date of sale and the buyer's name exactly as shown on the title. Both the seller and buyer must sign the title to make it valid. If the car has a lien, ensure it is fully paid and the lienholder releases their claim. Keep a copy of the signed title for your records.

### Notifying The Buyer

Notify the buyer about the title transfer process. Explain that they must take the signed title to the Illinois Secretary of State office. The buyer needs to submit the title with a completed application for a new title and registration. Inform the buyer about any fees or taxes due during this process. This step helps the buyer register the vehicle in their name quickly and legally.

## Insurance Considerations

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=when-you-sell-a-car-in-illinois-handling-your-insurance-correctly-is-key-insurance-protects-you-from-financial-loss-due-to-accidents-or-damage&w=424&h=324&c=7)

When you sell a car in Illinois, handling your insurance correctly is key. Insurance protects you from financial loss due to accidents or damage. Once the car is sold, the insurance tied to it may no longer be needed. Knowing the right steps can save money and avoid legal trouble.

### When To Cancel Insurance

Cancel your insurance on the day the car is sold. Keeping insurance on a car you no longer own can cause extra charges. Also, it may cause issues if a claim is made for that vehicle. Contact your insurance provider quickly to stop coverage.

Sometimes, you may want to keep insurance if you plan to buy another car soon. In that case, ask your insurer about options to transfer or pause coverage. Make sure to confirm the end date of your current policy.

### Informing Your Insurer

Tell your insurance company right after the sale. Provide details like the buyer’s name, sale date, and new license plate if known. This helps update their records and avoids future problems.

Ask for a confirmation that your policy has been updated or canceled. Keep this confirmation for your records. It proves you are no longer responsible for the vehicle’s insurance.

## Avoid Liability After Sale

Selling your car in Illinois means more than just handing over the keys. Avoiding liability after the sale is crucial to protect yourself from any future legal or financial troubles linked to the vehicle. Taking the right steps immediately after the sale can save you from unexpected fines or claims.

### Filing A Notice Of Sale

Once you sell your car, filing a Notice of Sale with the Illinois Secretary of State is essential. This document officially informs the state that you are no longer responsible for the vehicle. Without this notice, tickets or violations could still be sent to you, even though the car is no longer yours.

Filing is simple and can be done online, by mail, or in person. It’s a quick process that takes only a few minutes but offers you peace of mind. Have you considered how quickly this step could free you from future headaches?

### Keeping Records

After the sale, keep a copy of the bill of sale and any signed documents. These records prove the transfer of ownership and protect you if any disputes arise. Store them safely, either digitally or physically, for at least a year.

Think about how often paperwork disappears when you need it most. Keeping organized records is your best defense against unexpected claims. It’s a small effort that could save you a lot of trouble later.






## Frequently Asked Questions

### What To Do With Illinois License Plate After Selling A Car?

You must remove the license plates before handing over the vehicle. Keep the plates or transfer them to another vehicle. Notify the Illinois Secretary of State about the sale and plate removal. This prevents liability for any future incidents involving the sold car.

### Can I Transfer My Illinois License Plate To A New Car?

Yes, you can transfer your Illinois license plate to your new vehicle. Visit the Secretary of State office with proof of ownership. Pay any applicable fees and update your vehicle registration. This saves money and maintains your plate number.

### How To Notify Illinois Dmv After Selling A Car?

Submit a Notice of Sale form to the Illinois Secretary of State. This can be done online or by mail. Include buyer’s information, sale date, and your vehicle details. It legally releases you from liability for the sold car.

### Do I Need To Surrender Illinois License Plates After Selling?

You are not required to surrender plates immediately. However, you must remove them from the sold vehicle. You can either keep, transfer, or return them to the DMV. Returning plates can help avoid future fees or penalties.
