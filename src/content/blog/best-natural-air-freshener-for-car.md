## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Natural Scents Are Best For Car Air Fresheners?



Natural scents like teakwood, vanilla, sandalwood, and eucalyptus are popular. They provide a fresh, calming aroma without harsh chemicals, enhancing your driving experience naturally.



### How Long Do Natural Car Air Fresheners Last?



Most natural car air fresheners last between 45 to 180 days. Duration depends on the product type and exposure to airflow and heat inside the car.



### Are Natural Car Air Fresheners Safe For Pets And Kids?



Yes, natural air fresheners use essential oils and non-toxic ingredients. They are generally safe for pets and kids but avoid direct contact with sensitive individuals.



### How Do Bamboo Charcoal Air Fresheners Work?



Bamboo charcoal absorbs odors and moisture naturally. It purifies air without added fragrances, making it ideal for scent-free odor elimination inside cars.



### Can I Use Essential Oil Diffusers In My Car?



Yes, essential oil diffusers designed for cars provide long-lasting fragrance. They are compact, easy to use, and offer customizable scent options for a pleasant drive.



### What Makes Wooden Car Air Fresheners Effective?



Wooden air fresheners absorb essential oils and release scent slowly. They are durable, eco-friendly, and add a stylish touch while keeping your car smelling fresh.
