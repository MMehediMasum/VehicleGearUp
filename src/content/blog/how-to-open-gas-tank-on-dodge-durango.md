## Locate The Gas Tank Release

To open the gas tank on a Dodge Durango, the first task is to locate the gas tank release. This release mechanism allows access to the fuel door without needing to use force or tools. Knowing where to find it makes refueling quick and easy.

The gas tank release is designed to be within easy reach of the driver. It usually comes in two common forms: a physical lever inside the vehicle or a button on the key fob. Both options provide convenience and security for accessing the fuel tank.

### Inside The Driver’s Side Door

Most Dodge Durangos have a gas tank release lever inside the driver’s side door area. It is often located near the floor or on the lower side panel. The lever is usually marked with a fuel pump icon for easy identification.

Pulling this lever gently will pop open the fuel door. This method does not require any special tools or keys. It helps keep the fuel door secure while driving.

### Using The Key Fob

Some Dodge Durango models include a gas tank release button on the key fob. This button allows remote opening of the fuel door. It adds an extra layer of convenience, especially in bad weather or tight spaces.

Press and hold the fuel door button on the key fob for a few seconds. The fuel door will then unlock automatically. This feature helps drivers access the gas tank without leaving the vehicle.

## Step-by-step Opening Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=open-the-drivers-door-and-locate-the-fuel-door-release-lever-near-the-floor-pull-the-lever-to-release-the-gas-tank-cover&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Open the driver’s door and locate the fuel door release lever near the floor. Pull the lever to release the gas tank cover. Walk to the fuel door, press it gently to open, then remove the gas cap by turning it counterclockwise.

Opening the gas tank on your Dodge Durango might seem straightforward, but sometimes, the simplest tasks can leave you scratching your head. Whether you're a new owner or simply haven't had to refuel in a while, knowing the correct procedure is crucial. Let's break down the process step by step, ensuring you can get back on the road quickly and efficiently.

### Unlocking The Fuel Door

Begin by ensuring your vehicle is unlocked. Many models automatically secure the fuel door when the car is locked. Once unlocked, locate the small square panel on the driver's side of your Dodge Durango. A gentle press on the edge of the fuel door will pop it open. It's a design that prioritizes both safety and convenience.

### Opening The Fuel Cap

With the fuel door open, you’ll see the gas cap. Turn it counterclockwise to unscrew. It’s designed to come off easily without much force, so if you find it stuck, double-check for any debris or dirt that might be causing resistance. This step ensures you’re ready to refuel without any hitches.

### Closing The Fuel Door Securely

After refueling, ensure you tighten the gas cap by turning it clockwise until you hear a click. This click is a security measure to prevent fuel evaporation and maintain system pressure. Then, gently push the fuel door until it snaps back into place. A securely closed fuel door not only keeps your fuel safe but also maintains the sleek appearance of your Dodge Durango. ## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=facing-trouble-opening-the-gas-tank-on-your-dodge-durango-can-be-frustrating-especially-when-youre-in-a-hurry-understanding-the-common-issues-that-cause-these-problems-can-save-you-time-and-stress&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Facing trouble opening the gas tank on your Dodge Durango can be frustrating, especially when you're in a hurry. Understanding the common issues that cause these problems can save you time and stress. Let’s look at some frequent obstacles and how to tackle them effectively.

### Fuel Door Won't Open

If your fuel door refuses to open, the issue might be as simple as the release mechanism sticking. Dirt, ice, or debris can cause the latch to jam, especially in colder months. Try gently pressing around the door or using a little lubricant on the latch to loosen it up.

Have you checked if the interior release lever or button is functioning properly? Sometimes the cable connecting the lever to the fuel door can become loose or broken. Testing the lever for resistance can help you pinpoint if the problem lies inside the vehicle.

### Key Fob Malfunction

Your Dodge Durango’s key fob controls many convenience features, including unlocking the fuel door on some models. If the fuel door won’t open remotely, the fob’s battery might be weak or dead. Swapping in a fresh battery often restores full functionality.

Occasionally, the key fob can lose synchronization with your vehicle. Pressing the lock and unlock buttons simultaneously for a few seconds can reset the connection. If you notice other remote features acting up, this quick fix might save you a trip to the dealer.

### Mechanical Blockages

Sometimes, the fuel door is physically blocked by something stuck in the hinge or surrounding area. Leaves, mud, or even small objects can jam the door shut. Carefully inspecting the edges and cleaning out any debris can help free the door.

If you suspect a mechanical failure inside the door mechanism, gently applying pressure while trying to open it might help. Avoid forcing it open, as this can cause damage. In tough cases, consulting a professional might be the safest option to prevent costly repairs.

## Maintenance Tips For The Fuel Door

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-fuel-door-on-your-dodge-durango-keeps-it-working-smoothly-and-looking-good-simple-care-prevents-common-problems-like-sticking-or-rust&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the fuel door on your Dodge Durango keeps it working smoothly and looking good. Simple care prevents common problems like sticking or rust. Regular attention makes opening and closing the fuel door easy every time you refuel. Here are some key tips to help you care for your fuel door properly.

### Cleaning And Lubrication

Clean the fuel door area often to remove dirt and grime. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the paint or plastic.

Lubricate the hinges and latch with a light spray lubricant. This stops stiffness and prevents squeaking. Wipe off any extra oil to keep the surface clean.

### Preventing Rust And Damage

Check the fuel door edges and hinge for signs of rust or corrosion. Rust can cause the door to stick or not close properly. Use rust remover or a rust-proof spray to protect metal parts.

Avoid slamming the fuel door shut. Close it gently to prevent dents or damage. Fix any small dents or scratches quickly to stop rust from forming.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Open The Gas Tank On A Dodge Durango?

To open the gas tank on a Dodge Durango, locate the fuel door release lever inside the vehicle. It’s usually near the driver’s seat on the floor or side panel. Pull the lever, and the fuel door will pop open for refueling.

### Where Is The Gas Tank Release Lever In Dodge Durango?

The gas tank release lever in a Dodge Durango is typically found on the driver’s side floor near the seat or on the lower dashboard. Check your owner's manual if you can’t locate it, as placement may vary slightly by model year.

### Can I Open The Dodge Durango Gas Tank Manually?

Yes, you can open the Dodge Durango gas tank manually by gently prying open the fuel door if the release lever fails. Use caution to avoid damaging the door or paint, and consider checking for mechanical issues afterward.

### Is The Gas Tank Lock Key Necessary For Dodge Durango?

Most Dodge Durangos do not require a separate gas tank lock key. The fuel door unlocks automatically with the central locking system when you unlock the vehicle, making refueling convenient and secure.
