## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Car Wax For Black Plastic Trim?



Meguiar's Ultimate Black Plastic Restorer and Turtle Wax Graphene Acrylic Trim Restorer are top choices. They restore color and provide UV protection. These products revive faded trim and offer long-lasting results. Choose based on your preference for spray or wipe-on application.



### How Long Does Black Plastic Trim Wax Last?



Most high-quality trim waxes last between 3 to 6 months. For example, ExoForma Black Trim Restorer lasts over 6 months. Ceramic coatings like CERAKOTE® can protect trim for over 200 washes. Longevity depends on product type and exposure to weather.



### Can Car Wax Protect Black Plastic Trim From Uv Damage?



Yes, many car waxes like Meguiar's and Turtle Wax include UV protection. This prevents fading and cracking caused by sun exposure. Regular application helps maintain the trim’s deep black finish and extends the lifespan of the plastic parts.



### How Do I Apply Wax On Black Plastic Trim?



Clean the trim thoroughly before applying wax. Use a microfiber cloth or applicator pad to spread the product evenly. Allow it to dry or cure as instructed. Buff gently to a shine for the best finish and protection.



### Will Car Wax Hide Scratches On Black Plastic Trim?



Some products like ExoForma and Turtle Wax fill minor scratches and scuff marks. They enhance the trim’s appearance but may not completely hide deep damage. For heavy scratches, consider professional repair alongside waxing for best results.



### Is Ceramic Coating Better Than Traditional Wax For Trim?



Ceramic coatings like CERAKOTE® provide longer-lasting protection than traditional wax. They resist washing off and offer superior durability. Wax products are easier to apply but require more frequent reapplication. Choose based on desired maintenance level and durability needs.
