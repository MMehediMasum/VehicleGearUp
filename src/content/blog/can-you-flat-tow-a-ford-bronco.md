## Flat Towing Basics

Understanding the basics of flat towing is essential if you’re considering towing your Ford Bronco. It’s not just about hooking up your vehicle and hitting the road. Knowing what flat towing involves helps you avoid costly mistakes and keeps your Bronco safe during transit.

### What Is Flat Towing

Flat towing means pulling your vehicle with all four wheels on the ground. Unlike other towing methods, your Bronco remains fully supported by its tires throughout the journey.

This method requires special equipment, like a tow bar and base plates attached to the Bronco’s frame. It’s also crucial to ensure your Bronco is compatible with flat towing to prevent damage to the drivetrain.

Have you checked if your Bronco’s transmission or transfer case needs to be in a specific mode before towing? This step is often overlooked but can save you from expensive repairs.

### Benefits Of Flat Towing A Bronco

Flat towing offers several advantages for Bronco owners. One major benefit is convenience—you can tow your vehicle behind an RV or truck without needing a trailer.

This reduces the overall weight and length of your setup, making it easier to maneuver and park. Plus, you avoid the hassle of loading and unloading your Bronco onto a trailer.

* **Cost savings:**No need to rent or buy a trailer.
* **Fuel efficiency:**Less wind resistance compared to towing a trailer.
* **Quick setup:**Hooking up a tow bar is faster than loading onto a trailer.

Still wondering if your Bronco is a good candidate for flat towing? It helps to review the owner’s manual or consult a professional to understand any specific requirements or restrictions.

## Step-by-step Flat Towing Setup

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-ford-bronco-for-flat-towing-requires-careful-attention-to-detail-each-step-ensures-safety-and-prevents-damage-to-your-vehicles-drivetrain&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your Ford Bronco for flat towing requires careful attention to detail. Each step ensures safety and prevents damage to your vehicle's drivetrain. If you want to tow your Bronco smoothly behind your RV, understanding these steps is crucial.

### Connecting Tow Bars And Safety Chains

Start by attaching the tow bar securely to your Bronco’s front frame. Make sure the tow bar is rated for your Bronco’s weight for a safe connection.

Next, hook up the safety chains. Cross the chains under the tow bar to form an “X” shape—this prevents the tow bar from hitting the ground if it disconnects.

* Check that both the tow bar and safety chains are tight but not overly strained.
* Verify the connections by gently pulling forward with the towing vehicle.
* Test the lighting connections to ensure brake lights and turn signals work.

Have you ever skipped the safety chain check and regretted it later? Don’t risk it. These chains are your backup if the main connection fails.

### Setting The Bronco’s Transmission And Transfer Case

Before towing, put your Bronco’s transmission in neutral. This lets the wheels roll freely without damaging internal parts.

If your Bronco has a transfer case, shift it to the neutral position as well. This disengages the drivetrain and prevents wear.

| Model Year | Transmission Setting | Transfer Case Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2021 and newer | Neutral (N) | Neutral (N) or as per owner’s manual |
| Older models | Neutral (N) | Neutral or 2WD (check manual) |

Did you know that towing without setting these correctly can cause thousands in repair costs? Take the time to double-check. Your Bronco will thank you with smooth towing and no unexpected breakdowns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can You Flat Tow A Ford Bronco Safely?

Yes, you can flat tow a Ford Bronco safely if it is a 4WD model with the transfer case in neutral. Always check the owner's manual for specific instructions and follow manufacturer guidelines to avoid damage.

### Does Flat Towing Damage A Ford Bronco’s Transmission?

Flat towing a Ford Bronco with the transfer case in neutral prevents transmission damage. Incorrect towing can harm the drivetrain, so it’s crucial to follow the manual’s recommendations precisely.

### What Equipment Is Needed To Flat Tow A Ford Bronco?

To flat tow a Ford Bronco, you need a tow bar, safety chains, a base plate, and a supplemental braking system. Make sure all equipment is compatible with your Bronco model for safe towing.

### Can All Ford Bronco Models Be Flat Towed?

Not all Ford Bronco models can be flat towed. Only certain 4WD versions with a manual or neutral transfer case are suitable. Check the specific model year and owner’s manual for flat towing eligibility.
