**Is your Honda Accord’s TPMS light flashing and you’re not sure how to turn it off? That little warning can be annoying and distracting, especially when you’re confident your tires are fine.**

But ignoring it isn’t the answer either—it’s important to know what that light means and how to reset it safely. In this guide, you’ll discover simple, step-by-step instructions to switch off your TPMS light quickly and get back to driving with peace of mind.

## Causes Of Tpms Light Activation

The TPMS (Tire Pressure Monitoring System) light on your Honda Accord can turn on for several reasons. Understanding why it activates helps you address the issue quickly and avoid unnecessary trips to the mechanic. Let’s look at the common causes that trigger the TPMS light and what you can do about them.

### Low Tire Pressure

Low tire pressure is the most frequent reason for the TPMS light to come on. Tires lose air over time, especially if you haven’t checked them recently. Driving with underinflated tires can reduce fuel efficiency and increase wear, so it’s important to check your tire pressure with a gauge and inflate them to the recommended levels in your owner’s manual.

### Faulty Tpms Sensor

Sometimes the issue isn’t your tires but the TPMS sensor itself. These sensors can fail due to battery depletion or damage from road debris. If you notice the light stays on even after correcting tire pressure, a sensor malfunction might be the cause, and a professional diagnostic can confirm this.

### Tire Replacement Or Rotation

Have you recently replaced or rotated your tires? Changing the tire position or installing new tires can confuse the TPMS system. The sensors need to be reprogrammed or reset to recognize the new tire positions. Forgetting this step often triggers the warning light.

### Extreme Temperature Changes

Rapid temperature drops can cause tire pressure to fall quickly, activating the TPMS light. Cold weather contracts the air inside your tires, sometimes enough to trigger a warning. Checking your tires more frequently during seasonal shifts can prevent surprise alerts and keep you safe on the road.

## Reset Tpms Light On Honda Accord

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-tpms-tire-pressure-monitoring-system-light-on-a-honda-accord-warns-about-low-tire-pressure-sometimes-after-fixing-tire-pressure-the-light-stays-on&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The TPMS (Tire Pressure Monitoring System) light on a Honda Accord warns about low tire pressure. Sometimes, after fixing tire pressure, the light stays on. This means the system needs a reset. Resetting the TPMS light helps the car recognize the correct tire pressure again. Here are easy ways to reset the TPMS light on your Honda Accord.

### Using The Tpms Reset Button

Locate the TPMS reset button under the dashboard or near the steering wheel. Turn the ignition to the “ON” position without starting the engine. Press and hold the reset button until the TPMS light blinks three times. Release the button and wait for the light to turn off. This resets the sensor and clears the warning.

### Resetting Through Vehicle Menu

Turn on your Honda Accord and access the menu on the dashboard display. Use the controls to find the “Vehicle Settings” or “TPMS” option. Select “TPMS Calibration” or “Reset TPMS.” Follow the prompts to confirm the reset. The system will update and turn off the TPMS light after calibration.

### Driving To Complete Reset

Sometimes, the TPMS light turns off after driving for a few minutes. Drive your Honda Accord at 50 mph or higher for about 10 minutes. The sensors will update the tire pressure status automatically. This method works after adjusting tire pressure or changing tires. The light should turn off once the system completes the reset.

## Troubleshooting Persistent Tpms Light

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-tpms-light-in-your-honda-accord-alerts-you-to-tire-pressure-issues-sometimes-this-light-stays-on-even-after-fixing-tire-pressure&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The TPMS light in your Honda Accord alerts you to tire pressure issues. Sometimes, this light stays on even after fixing tire pressure. A persistent TPMS light means the system may have other problems. Troubleshooting these issues helps keep your vehicle safe and running smoothly.

### Inspecting Tpms Sensors

TPMS sensors monitor tire pressure and send signals to your car. Dirt, damage, or battery failure can stop sensors from working. Check each sensor for visible damage or corrosion. Clean sensors gently to remove dirt. Use a TPMS tool to check if sensors send signals. Faulty sensors cause the light to stay on.

### Replacing Dead Sensors

TPMS sensors have batteries that last 5 to 10 years. Dead batteries cause sensors to fail. Replace sensors if the battery is dead or if they do not respond. Buy sensors compatible with your Honda Accord model. Replace sensors in a tire shop or use a DIY kit. After replacement, reset the TPMS system to clear the light.

### Consulting A Professional Mechanic

Persistent TPMS light may need expert help. Mechanics have tools to diagnose sensor or system faults. They can fix wiring issues or sensor problems. A professional service ensures the system works correctly. Visit a certified mechanic to avoid future TPMS troubles and drive safely.

### Seasonal Tire Pressure Checks

Cold weather can lower tire pressure quickly. Warm weather may increase tire pressure. Check tire pressure at least once a month. Use a reliable tire pressure gauge for accuracy. Inflate tires to the pressure listed on the door sticker. Avoid overinflating or underinflating tires.

### Awareness Of Tpms Alerts

Do not ignore the TPMS warning light. It signals low tire pressure or sensor problems. Stop and check tires as soon as the light comes on. Fix issues promptly to avoid damage or accidents. Reset the TPMS system after repairs or tire changes. This keeps the warning light from returning unnecessarily.

  



## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Tpms Light On Honda Accord?

To reset the TPMS light, ensure all tires are properly inflated. Then, turn the ignition to ON without starting. Press and hold the TPMS reset button until the light blinks three times. Release the button and wait for the light to turn off.

### What Causes The Tpms Light To Stay On In Honda Accord?

The TPMS light stays on due to low tire pressure, a faulty sensor, or a dead battery in the sensor. It can also stay on after tire rotation if the system is not reset properly. Check tire pressure and sensor condition first.

### Can I Turn Off The Tpms Light Without Fixing Tires?

Turning off the TPMS light without fixing tires is not recommended. The light warns of unsafe tire pressure, which affects safety and fuel efficiency. Always inspect and correct tire pressure before resetting or turning off the light.

### Where Is The Tpms Reset Button In Honda Accord?

The TPMS reset button is usually located under the steering wheel or near the glove box. Consult your Honda Accord owner’s manual for the exact location. The button allows you to reset the tire pressure monitoring system after inflating tires.

Turning off the TPMS light on your Honda Accord is simple. Check your tire pressure first. Inflate tires to the recommended level. Use the reset button or drive for a few minutes. The light should go off soon. Keep your tires properly maintained to avoid this warning.
