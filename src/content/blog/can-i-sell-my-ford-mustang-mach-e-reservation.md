## Selling Your Mach E Reservation

Selling your Ford Mustang Mach E reservation can be a smart move. Some buyers may want a faster way to own this electric vehicle. Others may no longer need their spot in line. Understanding the process helps avoid mistakes. This section explains key points about selling your reservation.

### Reservation Transfer Rules

Ford allows reservation transfers under specific conditions. The original reserver must agree to transfer. Both parties usually need to contact Ford directly. Ford may require proof of identity and a formal request. Transfers happen before the vehicle order is finalized. After ordering, reservation changes become more complex. Always check with Ford for the latest rules.

### Ford’s Policy On Reservation Sales

Ford does not officially support selling reservations for profit. The reservation is meant for the original buyer. Ford may cancel reservations if they detect unauthorized sales. Some owners choose to transfer for free or at cost. Be cautious; Ford’s policy may change over time. Keeping the transfer transparent is important to avoid issues.

### Legal Considerations

Local laws may affect your ability to sell a reservation. Some places treat reservations like property. Others may restrict transferring or selling them. Writing a clear agreement protects both buyer and seller. Consult legal advice if unsure about rules in your area. Misunderstandings can lead to disputes or loss of reservation. Stay informed to make safe decisions.

Before you list your reservation for sale, verify its current status with Ford. Is your reservation active, or has it been modified or canceled? This information affects the value and transferability of the reservation.
