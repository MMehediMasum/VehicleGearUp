**Is your Honda Odyssey’s maintenance light on and you’re not sure how to turn it off? You’re not alone.**

That little warning can be confusing and even a bit stressful. But don’t worry—resetting the maintenance light is easier than you think. In this guide, you’ll learn simple, step-by-step instructions to reset the light quickly and safely. Keep reading, and you’ll have your dashboard looking clean and ready in no time.




## Locate The Maintenance Light

Finding the maintenance light on your Honda Odyssey is the first step to resetting it. This light gives important information about your vehicle's service needs. Knowing where to spot it helps you take care of your car promptly.

### Dashboard Indicators

The maintenance light is part of the dashboard indicators. It usually looks like a small wrench or a message saying "Maintenance Due." You can find it on the instrument cluster, near the speedometer and fuel gauge. The light is easy to see once you know what to look for.

### When The Light Appears

The maintenance light turns on after a set number of miles or months. This signals that your Odyssey needs an oil change or other routine check. The light may blink or stay steady. It stays on until you reset it after service.

## Prepare Your Vehicle

Preparing your Honda Odyssey before resetting the maintenance light is important. This ensures safety and makes the process smooth. Take a moment to get your vehicle ready. Follow simple steps to set up the car properly.

### Turn Off The Engine

Make sure the engine is completely off. This step prevents any electrical issues. Avoid starting the reset process with the engine running. A fully off engine is necessary for the system to reset correctly.

### Ensure The Vehicle Is In Park

Check that your Odyssey is in the Park position. This is important for safety and proper system function. The reset cannot be done if the vehicle is in Drive or Neutral. Confirming Park keeps the vehicle stable during the reset.

## Reset Using The Instrument Panel

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turn-the-ignition-to-the-on-position-without-starting-the-engine-use-the-instrument-panel-buttons-to-navigate-to-the-maintenance-reset-option-and-hold-the-reset-button-until-the-light-turns-off&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turn the ignition to the ON position without starting the engine. Use the instrument panel buttons to navigate to the maintenance reset option and hold the reset button until the light turns off. This clears the maintenance reminder on your Honda Odyssey.

Resetting the maintenance light on your Honda Odyssey can seem challenging. But with the right steps, it's quite simple. Using the instrument panel, you can reset the maintenance light in a few minutes. This method involves a few easy actions. Follow these instructions to ensure your vehicle is in top condition.

### Press The Odometer Button

Locate the odometer button on your dashboard. It's typically near the speedometer. Press and hold this button. This step is crucial for resetting the light.

### Turn The Ignition On

Insert your key into the ignition. Turn it to the "On" position. Do not start the engine. Keep the odometer button pressed while doing this.

### Hold The Button Until The Light Blinks

Continue holding the odometer button. Wait until the maintenance light blinks. This may take a few seconds. Once it blinks, release the button. Your maintenance light should now be reset.



## Reset Via Multi-information Display

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-multi-information-display-on-your-honda-odyssey-provides-a-simple-way-to-reset-the-maintenance-light-this-method-does-not-require-any-special-tools&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The multi-information display on your Honda Odyssey provides a simple way to reset the maintenance light. This method does not require any special tools. It uses the vehicle’s built-in menu system to clear the reminder after service.

Using the display helps keep track of your vehicle’s maintenance easily. Follow these steps to reset the light quickly and safely.

### Access The Settings Menu

Turn on the ignition without starting the engine. Locate the “Settings” button on the steering wheel or dashboard controls. Press the button to open the settings menu on the display screen. Wait for the menu options to load fully.

### Navigate To Maintenance Info

Use the arrow buttons to scroll through the menu options. Look for “Maintenance Info” or a similar label. Select this option by pressing the enter or OK button. The screen will show details about oil life and other maintenance reminders.

### Select Reset Option

Find the “Reset” option within the maintenance info menu. Highlight it using the arrows. Press enter or OK to confirm the reset. The display will show a message confirming the reset is complete. Turn off the ignition and then start the vehicle to check if the light is off.

  


## Using Obd-ii Scanner For Reset

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-an-obd-ii-scanner-to-reset-the-maintenance-light-on-your-honda-odyssey-is-a-straightforward-and-efficient-method-this-device-connects-to-your-vehicles-onboard-computer-allowing-you-to-access-and-clear-maintenance-codes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using an OBD-II scanner to reset the maintenance light on your Honda Odyssey is a straightforward and efficient method. This device connects to your vehicle’s onboard computer, allowing you to access and clear maintenance codes. It saves time and ensures that the reset is done correctly without guesswork.

### Connect The Scanner

Locate the OBD-II port under the dashboard on the driver’s side. Plug the scanner’s connector firmly into this port. Make sure your car is in the “On” position, but the engine doesn’t need to be running.

Once connected, power on the scanner. It will communicate with your vehicle’s computer and display a menu. Take a moment to read the scanner’s instructions to navigate through its options smoothly.

### Erase Maintenance Codes

On the scanner’s menu, find the option that says “Read Codes” or “Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC).” This lets you see any existing maintenance or error codes stored in your Odyssey’s system.

After reviewing the codes, select the “Erase Codes” or “Clear Codes” option. Confirm your choice when prompted. This action will reset the maintenance light and clear the codes from your vehicle’s memory.

Have you noticed how quickly the light disappears after clearing the codes? It’s a satisfying confirmation that the reset worked. Just remember, if the light comes back on, it might indicate a real issue that needs attention.

## Tips To Avoid Maintenance Light Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-honda-odysseys-maintenance-light-from-coming-on-unexpectedly-saves-you-time-and-stress-the-key-is-not-just-resetting-the-light-but-preventing-unnecessary-triggers-in-the-first-place&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Honda Odyssey’s maintenance light from coming on unexpectedly saves you time and stress. The key is not just resetting the light but preventing unnecessary triggers in the first place. With a few smart habits, you can avoid false alerts and keep your vehicle running smoothly.

### Regular Servicing

Scheduling regular service checks is the best way to stay ahead of maintenance light issues. Your Odyssey needs oil changes, filter replacements, and brake inspections on time to avoid warning signals. Missing these routine tasks often causes the light to come on prematurely.

Think about how skipping a simple oil change once led to that annoying maintenance light in your own car. Regular visits to the dealer or trusted mechanic can keep your vehicle’s sensors happy and accurate. Plus, a well-maintained car means fewer surprises on the road.

### Check For Software Updates

Your Honda Odyssey’s onboard computer controls the maintenance light, and it relies on updated software to function correctly. Automakers release updates that fix bugs or improve system accuracy, preventing false maintenance warnings.

Have you ever wondered why your maintenance light pops up even after servicing? It might be outdated software confusing the sensors. Checking with your dealer or using an OBD-II scanner app can help ensure your car’s software is current and running smoothly.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Maintenance Light On Honda Odyssey?

To reset the maintenance light, turn the ignition to ON without starting. Press and hold the Select/Reset button until the light blinks and turns off. This process clears the maintenance reminder on the dashboard.

### When Should I Reset The Maintenance Light On Odyssey?

Reset the light right after completing scheduled maintenance like oil changes or inspections. It ensures accurate tracking of the next service interval and keeps your vehicle’s reminders up to date.

### Can I Reset The Maintenance Light Without Tools?

Yes, resetting the maintenance light on a Honda Odyssey requires no special tools. It only needs the ignition key and the dashboard buttons, making it a simple DIY task for owners.

### Why Is The Maintenance Light Still On After Reset?

If the light stays on, maintenance may not have been completed properly. Check if the oil level and filter were changed. If issues persist, a professional diagnostic might be necessary.
