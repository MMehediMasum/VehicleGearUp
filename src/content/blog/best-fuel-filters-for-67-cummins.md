## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Fuel Filters Fit 6.7 Cummins Dodge Ram Models?



Mopar OEM and compatible fuel filters fit 2013-2024 Dodge Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 with 6. 7L Cummins engines. Key part numbers include 68157291AA, 68436631AA, and 68197867AA, ensuring perfect fit and reliable filtration for these trucks.



### How Often Should I Replace My 6.7 Cummins Fuel Filter?



Replace the fuel filter every 15,000 to 20,000 miles or as recommended by Dodge. Regular changes prevent clogging, maintain fuel flow, and protect the engine from contaminants, ensuring optimal diesel engine performance and longevity.



### Are Mopar Genuine Oem Filters Better For 6.7 Cummins?



Yes, Mopar Genuine OEM filters offer precise fit and filtration quality. They meet factory standards, ensuring dependable fuel and water separation. Using OEM parts helps maintain warranty coverage and prolongs engine life with consistent, high-quality protection.



### Can Aftermarket Filters Replace Oem Fuel Filters On 6.7 Cummins?



Aftermarket filters like Wix and Doc’s Diesel provide effective alternatives with compatible fit for 6. 7L Cummins. Ensure the part numbers match OEM references for proper filtration and fuel system protection. Choose reputable brands to avoid performance or warranty issues.



### What Are Signs Of A Clogged 6.7 Cummins Fuel Filter?



Symptoms include reduced engine power, rough idling, hard starts, and increased fuel consumption. A clogged filter restricts fuel flow, causing poor engine performance. Prompt replacement restores normal operation and prevents fuel system damage.



### Do Fuel/water Separators Improve 6.7 Cummins Diesel Engine Life?



Yes, fuel/water separators remove water and contaminants from diesel fuel, preventing corrosion and injector damage. Using quality separators extends engine life, enhances fuel efficiency, and protects critical components in the 6. 7L Cummins diesel system.
