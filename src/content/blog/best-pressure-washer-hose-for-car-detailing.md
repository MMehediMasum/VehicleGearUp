## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Pressure Washer Hose For Car Detailing?



The best hose offers durability, flexibility, and high-pressure resistance. Models like Hydro Jet and KIMISS suit car detailing perfectly. They ensure efficient water flow without leaks, making cleaning easier and protecting your car’s paint.



### How Long Should A Pressure Washer Hose Be For Cars?



A hose between 6 to 8 meters (20 to 26 feet) is ideal. It provides enough reach around the vehicle without tangling. Products like KIMISS 8M and Akozon 6M offer great length for easy maneuverability during car washes.



### Can I Use Garden Hoses With Pressure Washers?



No, regular garden hoses can't handle high pressure. Pressure washer hoses like RYOBI or MTM Hydro are designed for high-pressure water flow. Using garden hoses can cause leaks and damage the washer or car surface.



### What Pressure Rating Is Suitable For Car Detailing Hoses?



A hose rated for 3000 to 4000 PSI is perfect for car detailing. It handles strong water pressure without bursting. The MTM Hydro Kobrajet, with 4000 PSI, is a reliable choice for thorough cleaning.



### Are Rubber Or Plastic Hoses Better For Pressure Washers?



Rubber hoses, like Akozon’s, offer better durability and flexibility. Plastic hoses, such as RYOBI’s transparent model, are lightweight but less durable. Choose based on your need for longevity or ease of use in car detailing.



### How Do I Maintain My Pressure Washer Hose?



Regularly check for leaks, cracks, and kinks. Store the hose coiled in a dry place away from sunlight. Clean the nozzles and fittings after use to ensure long-lasting performance and safety during car detailing.
