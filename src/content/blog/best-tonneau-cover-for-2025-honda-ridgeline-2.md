Soft covers are lightweight and easy to install. Hard covers offer more protection and are theft-resistant. Choose based on your preference for convenience or security.



### How Do I Choose A Tonneau Cover For My Ridgeline Bed Size?



Ensure the cover matches your Ridgeline’s 5-foot 4-inch bed size. Most compatible covers like North Mountain and KUIPERAUTO fit the 64-inch bed perfectly.



### Are Tonneau Covers Waterproof For The 2025 Honda Ridgeline?



Many covers like XTWEEX and MOSTPLUS are waterproof. They protect your cargo from rain and snow, keeping your truck bed dry.



### Can I Install A Tonneau Cover Myself On The Ridgeline?



Yes, most covers like Tyger Auto and Xcover come with easy-to-follow instructions. They require minimal tools and can be installed in under an hour.



### Do Tonneau Covers Improve My Honda Ridgeline’s Fuel Efficiency?



Yes, tonneau covers reduce wind drag. This can improve fuel efficiency by a few miles per gallon on highways. Hard covers often offer better aerodynamics.
