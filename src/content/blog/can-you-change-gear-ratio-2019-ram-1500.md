## Gear Ratio Basics

Understanding gear ratios is key to knowing how your 2019 Ram 1500 performs. Gear ratios impact power delivery, fuel efficiency, and towing ability. This section breaks down the basics to help you make informed decisions about changing gear ratios.

### What Gear Ratio Means

The gear ratio is the number of turns the driveshaft makes to turn the wheels once. A ratio of 3.55 means the driveshaft turns 3.55 times for one wheel turn. This ratio is set by the ring and pinion gears in the differential.

Lower ratios like 3.21 mean fewer driveshaft turns per wheel turn. Higher ratios like 4.10 mean more driveshaft turns per wheel turn. The gear ratio affects engine speed and torque at the wheels.

### How Gear Ratios Affect Performance

Gear ratios change how your truck uses power. A higher ratio gives more torque at the wheels. This helps with towing and hauling heavy loads. It also improves acceleration but can lower fuel economy.

A lower gear ratio reduces engine RPMs at highway speeds. This saves fuel and lowers noise. It can make the truck feel less powerful when starting or climbing hills.

Choosing the right gear ratio depends on your driving needs. Consider towing weight, driving style, and fuel goals before changing ratios.

## Steps To Change Gear Ratio

Changing the gear ratio on your 2019 Ram 1500 can significantly affect its performance and fuel efficiency. The process requires careful attention to detail, but it’s manageable if you have the right tools and a bit of patience. Understanding each step will help you avoid costly mistakes and get the most out of your truck’s drivetrain.

### Tools And Equipment Needed

Before you start, gather essential tools to make the job smoother. You’ll need a torque wrench, a gear marking compound, a dial indicator, and a set of sockets and wrenches.

Don’t forget safety gear like gloves and eye protection. Having a service manual specific to your Ram 1500 will guide you through torque specs and clearances.

### Removing The Differential Cover

Begin by placing your truck on a stable lift or jack stands to access the rear axle safely. Drain the differential fluid by removing the drain plug or loosening the cover bolts.

Remove the bolts holding the differential cover, then gently pry it off. Expect some fluid to spill, so keep a drain pan handy.

### Replacing Ring And Pinion Gears

Once the cover is off, remove the bearing caps and carefully extract the carrier assembly. Mark the bearing caps to ensure they go back the same way.

Press out the old ring and pinion gears using a hydraulic press or take it to a professional if you lack this tool. Install the new gears, making sure to apply gear marking compound to check the mesh pattern.

### Reassembling And Testing

Reinstall the carrier with new shims or adjusters to achieve the correct backlash as specified in your manual. Tighten the bearing caps to the recommended torque.

Replace the differential cover with a new gasket or sealant and refill with the correct gear oil. Before hitting the road, test drive your truck to listen for unusual noises and check for smooth gear engagement.

## Testing And Maintenance Tips

Testing and maintaining the gear ratio on a 2019 Ram 1500 is vital for smooth performance. Proper care ensures your truck runs efficiently and avoids costly repairs. Focus on breaking in new gears correctly and keeping regular inspections to extend gear life.

### Breaking In New Gears

New gears need a break-in period to settle properly. Drive gently during the first 500 miles. Avoid heavy loads and sudden acceleration. This helps the gear teeth mesh smoothly and reduces wear. Listen for unusual noises that may show improper installation. Check fluid levels often to keep gears lubricated and cool.

### Regular Inspection And Servicing

Inspect gears and differential regularly for signs of wear or damage. Look for metal shavings in the gear oil, which indicate wear. Change gear oil as recommended in your truck’s manual. Use the correct oil type for your gear ratio. Tighten any loose bolts and check seals for leaks. A well-maintained gear system improves vehicle safety and performance.
