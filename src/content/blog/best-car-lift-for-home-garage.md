### How Much Weight Can These Home Garage Car Lifts Support?



Most home garage car lifts support between 700 kg to 3 tons. For example, VEVOR hydraulic lifts hold up to 3 tons safely. Always check the lift’s maximum load capacity before use to avoid accidents or damage.



### Are Portable Car Lifts Suitable For Suv And Trucks?



Yes, portable car lifts like the 2-ton scissor jack can handle SUVs. For heavier trucks, opt for lifts with higher weight capacity, such as 4-ton farm jacks. Ensure the lift matches your vehicle’s weight and size for safe operation.



### What Safety Features Do These Car Lifts Offer?



These car lifts include safety locks, adjustable handles, and sturdy bases. Hydraulic lifts often have pistons with pressure control. Some models have air cushions to prevent slipping. Always follow manufacturer guidelines for safe use.



### Can I Use These Lifts For Motorcycles And Atvs?



Yes, specific lifts like the VEVOR hydraulic motorcycle lift support up to 700 kg. These lifts offer adjustable height and wheels for easy movement. They are ideal for garage repairs on bikes and ATVs.
