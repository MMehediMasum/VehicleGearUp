## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Car Shampoo Ideal For Ceramic Coatings?



A car shampoo ideal for ceramic coatings is pH-neutral and gentle. It preserves the coating while removing dirt and grime. These shampoos enhance hydrophobic properties and do not strip protective layers. They maintain shine and extend the coating’s lifespan effectively.



### How Does Ph-neutral Shampoo Benefit Ceramic-coated Cars?



PH-neutral shampoos protect ceramic coatings by preventing damage from harsh acids or alkalis. They clean effectively without dulling or stripping the coating. This balance ensures safe washing and long-lasting protection, preserving the car’s glossy finish and hydrophobic features.



### Can I Use Foam Cannons With Ceramic Car Shampoos?



Yes, many ceramic car shampoos are designed for foam cannons. They create thick foam that lifts dirt gently. This reduces the risk of scratching and maximizes cleaning efficiency. Products like Adam's Polishes and CERAKOTE work well with foam applications.



### Do Ceramic Car Shampoos Add Extra Protection?



Some ceramic shampoos add hydrophobic protection and enhance coatings. They boost water beading and improve shine. Products like Meguiar’s Hybrid Ceramic Wash & Wax combine cleaning with added protection, making maintenance easier and preserving the coating’s durability.



### How Often Should I Wash A Ceramic-coated Car?



Washing a ceramic-coated car every 1-2 weeks is ideal. Regular washing removes contaminants without damaging the coating. Using a dedicated ceramic shampoo ensures safe cleaning and maintains the coating’s performance and appearance over time.
