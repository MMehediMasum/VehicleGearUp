### How Does A Dual-plane Manifold Benefit Chevy 350 Engines?



Dual-plane manifolds, like JGhyperX 2701, provide better low to mid-range torque. They improve drivability and fuel efficiency. This design suits street and mild performance Chevy 350 engines perfectly, delivering smooth power across various RPM ranges.

Single-plane intake manifolds, such as JGhyperX 2925, work best for high RPM and racing applications. They optimize top-end horsepower but may reduce low-end torque. Choose single-plane if you seek maximum power in Chevy 350 engines above 3000 RPM.

Yes, aluminum manifolds like JGhyperX and SMMS offer lightweight durability and improved heat dissipation. This leads to better performance and longevity in Chevy 350 engines. Aluminum also helps maintain cooler air charge for more power.

### What Rpm Range Suits The Edelbrock 2701 Performer Manifold?



The Edelbrock 2701 Performer manifold is best for 1200 to 5500 RPM range. It excels in street and light performance builds. It balances low-end torque with good mid-range power, ideal for most Chevy 350 applications.
