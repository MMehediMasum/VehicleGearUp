Yes, Valvoline Full Synthetic High Mileage 5W-20 is suitable for Honda Civic 1. 5 Turbo engines. It provides excellent wear protection and helps extend engine life, especially for higher mileage vehicles.



### How Often Should I Change Oil With The Factory Racing Parts Kit?



With the Factory Racing Parts Deluxe Oil Change Kit, change oil every 5,000 to 7,500 miles. Regular changes maintain engine health and improve performance for Honda Civic 1. 5 Turbo models.

Honda Pro GN4 10W30 is ideal for certain Civic models, but 0W-20 is preferred for the 1. 5 Turbo. Always check your owner's manual for the exact oil recommendation.



### What Benefits Does Mobil 1 Turbo Diesel 5w-40 Offer Civic Engines?

### Are Synthetic Oils Better For Honda Civic 1.5 Turbo Engines?



Yes, synthetic oils like Valvoline and Factory Racing Parts 0W-20 improve engine efficiency. They offer better protection, reduce wear, and support turbocharged engine performance in Honda Civic 1. 5 Turbo models.
