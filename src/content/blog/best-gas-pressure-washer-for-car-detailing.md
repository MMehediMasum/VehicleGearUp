## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Psi And Gpm Are Ideal For Car Detailing?



For car detailing, 2100 to 3500 PSI and 1. 2 to 2. 4 GPM work best. This range offers enough pressure to remove dirt without damaging paint. Higher PSI models like 4200 PSI are better for heavy-duty cleaning, but lower PSI is safer for delicate surfaces.



### Are Gas Pressure Washers Better Than Electric For Cars?



Gas pressure washers provide more power and mobility without cords. They are ideal for large vehicles and tough grime. Electric washers are quieter, lighter, and easier for small tasks. Choose gas if you want higher PSI and portability for detailed car cleaning.



### How Do Quick-connect Nozzles Improve Cleaning?



Quick-connect nozzles let you switch spray patterns easily, saving time. Different nozzles adjust pressure and spray angle for specific cleaning needs. This flexibility helps target tough stains or delicate areas on cars, improving cleaning efficiency and protecting surfaces.



### Is A Soap Tank Necessary For Car Pressure Washers?



Yes, a soap tank helps apply detergent evenly for better cleaning. It makes removing grease and grime easier, enhancing results. Models with built-in soap tanks save time and effort during car detailing, providing professional-quality washes.



### How Long Is The Average Hose Length For These Washers?



Most gas pressure washers come with a 25-foot hose, ideal for mobility. This length offers enough reach around vehicles without moving the machine. Longer hoses increase convenience when cleaning driveways or larger vehicles.



### Are These Pressure Washers Epa And Carb Compliant?



Several models like the 4200 PSI and 3500 PSI washers comply with EPA and CARB standards. Compliance ensures lower emissions and environmentally friendly operation. This is important for legal use in certain states and for reducing pollution.
