**Is your Nissan Altima’s cabin air feeling stuffy or filled with dust? Changing the cabin air filter might be the simple fix you need.**

You might think this task is complicated or expensive, but it’s actually something you can do yourself quickly and easily. In this guide, you’ll learn exactly how to change your cabin air filter step-by-step. By the end, you’ll breathe fresher air every time you drive—and save money on costly repairs.

Ready to improve your car’s air quality and comfort? Let’s get started!



## Tools Needed

Changing the cabin air filter in a Nissan Altima is a simple task. Having the right tools makes the process faster and easier. Below is a list of tools you will need for this job. Most tools are common and easy to find.

### Replacement Cabin Air Filter

Choose the correct cabin air filter model for your Nissan Altima. Check the owner's manual or ask at an auto parts store. A proper fit ensures good air quality inside your car.

### Screwdriver Set

You may need a Phillips or flathead screwdriver to remove the glove box or filter cover. A small set with different sizes works best for various screws.

### Socket Wrench

A socket wrench might be required for some bolts. Usually, a 7mm or 8mm socket fits most Nissan Altima parts related to the filter housing.

### Flashlight

A flashlight helps you see inside dark spaces behind the glove box. Proper lighting reduces mistakes and speeds up the process.

### Gloves

Wear gloves to keep your hands clean. They also protect your skin from dust and allergens inside the filter area.




## Locate The Cabin Air Filter

Knowing where to find the cabin air filter in your Nissan Altima is the first step to improving the air quality inside your car. It’s not hidden under the hood but rather in a spot that’s easy to access with a little guidance. Finding it quickly saves you time and effort, making the filter change less of a chore.

### Check The Owner’s Manual

Your Nissan Altima’s owner’s manual is the best place to start. It usually has clear diagrams and specific locations for the cabin air filter. If you don’t have a physical copy, you can often find a PDF version online, which makes it easy to search for “cabin air filter” directly.

### Look Behind The Glove Compartment

Most Nissan Altimas have the cabin air filter located behind the glove compartment. You’ll need to open the glove box and gently push in the sides to release the stops. This allows the glove compartment to drop down or come out, revealing the filter housing.

### Identify The Filter Housing Cover

Once the glove box is out of the way, look for a rectangular plastic cover secured by clips or screws. This cover protects the cabin air filter. Removing it carefully without breaking the clips is essential, as they can be delicate and might need replacement if damaged.

### Notice The Filter Orientation

Before removing the filter, take note of how it sits in the housing. The airflow direction is usually marked on the filter with arrows. This ensures you install the new filter correctly, keeping your car’s ventilation system working efficiently.

## Access The Filter Compartment

Accessing the cabin air filter compartment in a Nissan Altima is simple. It requires removing parts of the glove box. This step is crucial to reach the filter and replace it. Follow the instructions carefully to avoid damage to the glove box or filter compartment.

### Open The Glove Box

Start by opening the glove box fully. Pull the handle gently to avoid breaking it. Clear out any items inside. This provides space and better access to the stops and filter compartment behind it.

### Remove The Glove Box Stops

Locate the stops on each side of the glove box. These stops hold the glove box in place and prevent it from falling out. Push or squeeze the sides inward to release the stops. Slowly lower the glove box to gain full access to the filter compartment behind it.

## Remove The Old Filter

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=removing-the-old-cabin-air-filter-in-your-nissan-altima-is-a-simple-task-it-helps-keep-the-air-inside-your-car-clean-and-fresh&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Removing the old cabin air filter in your Nissan Altima is a simple task. It helps keep the air inside your car clean and fresh. The filter traps dust, pollen, and other particles. Over time, it gets dirty and needs replacement. Removing the old filter prepares the way for a new one.

### Locate The Cabin Air Filter Compartment

The cabin air filter is usually behind the glove box. Open the glove box fully to find the access panel. Some models have clips or small screws securing the panel. Check carefully to avoid breaking any parts.

### Open The Filter Housing

Remove the screws or unclip the panel gently. Pull the cover away to reveal the filter inside. The filter sits in a small tray or slot. Be ready to catch the filter as it might fall out.

### Take Out The Old Filter

Slide the filter out carefully to avoid spreading dust. Notice how the filter is positioned. This helps when putting the new one back. Dispose of the old filter properly. It may be dirty and full of debris.

## Clean The Filter Housing

Clean the filter housing to remove any dust or debris before inserting the new cabin air filter. Use a soft cloth to wipe the interior surfaces gently. This ensures better airflow and performance in your Nissan Altima.

Cleaning the filter housing in your Nissan Altima is crucial. It ensures optimal air quality inside the vehicle. Dirt and debris can accumulate over time, affecting the performance. A clean filter housing allows for efficient airflow, improving comfort. Follow these steps to clean the filter housing effectively.

### Inspect The Filter Housing

Begin by locating the filter housing under the dashboard. Carefully remove the cover to access the housing. Check for visible dirt or debris inside. This step helps identify the level of cleaning needed.

### Remove Loose Debris

Use a soft brush or cloth to sweep out loose particles. Be gentle to avoid damaging any components. This step clears away surface dirt, preparing the housing for deeper cleaning.

### Wipe Down The Interior

Dampen a microfiber cloth with a mild cleaning solution. Wipe the interior surfaces of the housing thoroughly. This removes any remaining dust or grime, leaving the housing spotless.

### Dry The Housing Completely

After cleaning, ensure the housing is completely dry. Use a dry cloth to remove any moisture. Moisture can lead to mold growth, affecting air quality inside the car.

### Reinstall The Filter

Place the new or cleaned filter back into the housing. Ensure it fits snugly and securely. A properly installed filter ensures maximum efficiency and performance.

## Install The New Filter

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-the-new-cabin-air-filter-in-your-nissan-altima-is-a-simple-task-it-ensures-clean-air-flows-inside-your-car&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing the new cabin air filter in your Nissan Altima is a simple task. It ensures clean air flows inside your car. Proper installation keeps dust and allergens out. Follow these tips to place the filter correctly for best results.

### Check Filter Orientation

Look for arrows on the filter frame. These arrows show the airflow direction. The arrow must point toward the car's interior. Double-check this before inserting the filter. Incorrect orientation reduces filter efficiency.

### Insert The Filter Properly

Slide the filter gently into the slot. Make sure it fits snugly without bending. Align edges with the filter housing to avoid gaps. A tight fit prevents air from bypassing the filter. Close the cover securely after installing the filter.

  


## Reassemble The Glove Box

Reassembling the glove box after changing the cabin air filter in your Nissan Altima is a straightforward task, but it requires attention to detail. Putting everything back correctly ensures your glove box functions smoothly and keeps the filter securely in place. Let’s break down the process to make it easy for you.

### Align The Glove Box Door

Start by positioning the glove box door so it lines up with the hinges and the opening frame. Check that the sides fit evenly and nothing is caught or misaligned. A properly aligned door prevents rattling noises while driving and protects the new cabin air filter from dust.

### Reconnect The Damper Arm

The damper arm controls how the glove box opens and closes. Gently reattach it to its hook or pin on the glove box door. If this part is not secured properly, the door might drop open too quickly or slam shut, which can be annoying and even cause damage over time.

### Secure The Glove Box With Screws

Use the screws you removed earlier to fasten the glove box back in place. Tighten them evenly but avoid over-tightening, which can strip the plastic threads. Missing screws or loose fittings can cause the glove box to wobble or fall open unexpectedly.

### Test The Glove Box Operation

Open and close the glove box several times to check for smooth movement. Listen for any unusual sounds or resistance. If you notice anything off, double-check the alignment and damper arm connection before finishing up.




## Test The Airflow

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=testing-the-airflow-after-changing-the-cabin-air-filter-in-your-nissan-altima-confirms-the-filters-effectiveness-this-step-ensures-clean-air-circulates-inside-the-vehicle&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Testing the airflow after changing the cabin air filter in your Nissan Altima confirms the filter's effectiveness. This step ensures clean air circulates inside the vehicle. Proper airflow improves comfort and helps maintain the HVAC system.

Testing is simple and requires no special tools. You can feel the difference in air quality and strength quickly. A clean filter allows stronger airflow and reduces dust inside the car.

### Turn On The Air Conditioning Or Heater

Start your car and switch on the air conditioning or heater. Set the fan speed to the highest level. This makes it easier to notice any airflow changes.

### Check Airflow From Vents

Place your hand near the dashboard vents. Feel the air pressure and temperature. Air should blow steadily and strongly without obstruction.

### Listen For Strange Noises

Pay attention to sounds coming from vents. Clean airflow usually sounds smooth. Rattling or whistling may indicate filter problems or loose parts.

### Compare To Previous Performance

Think about how the airflow felt before changing the filter. The new filter should improve air quality and strength. Weak airflow may mean the filter is installed incorrectly or damaged.

### Repeat If Needed

If airflow feels weak, check the filter installation again. Remove and reinstall it properly. Retest the airflow to confirm improvement.

## Maintenance Tips

Keeping your Nissan Altima’s cabin air filter in good shape is key to enjoying clean air inside your car. Regular maintenance doesn’t just improve air quality; it also supports the overall efficiency of your vehicle’s HVAC system. Here are some practical tips to help you maintain your cabin air filter effectively.

### Checking The Filter Regularly

How often do you peek at your cabin air filter? It’s a good idea to check it every 12,000 to 15,000 miles or at least once a year. Dirt and debris build up faster if you drive in dusty or urban areas, so adjust your schedule accordingly.

When inspecting, look for signs like visible dirt, leaves, or a musty smell inside your car. If the filter looks clogged or damaged, it’s time for a replacement.

### Choosing The Right Replacement Filter

Not all cabin air filters are created equal. Some offer extra protection against pollen, dust, and even odors. Think about your driving environment and allergies when picking a filter.

OEM filters are designed specifically for your Nissan Altima and usually fit perfectly. However, high-quality aftermarket options can offer enhanced filtration at a lower price.

### Keeping The Filter Area Clean

When you remove the old filter, don’t forget to clean the surrounding area. Dust and debris can settle in the filter housing, reducing airflow and efficiency.

Use a vacuum or a damp cloth to wipe the compartment before installing the new filter. This simple step can make a noticeable difference in air quality.

### Signs Your Filter Needs Immediate Attention

* Windows fog up more frequently and take longer to clear.
* You notice a persistent unpleasant odor inside the cabin.
* The air coming from vents feels weaker than usual.

Ignoring these warning signs can lead to discomfort and strain on your vehicle’s ventilation system. Have you noticed any of these symptoms in your Altima?




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Often Should I Change Nissan Altima Cabin Air Filter?

Change the cabin air filter every 15,000 to 20,000 miles. Replace it sooner if you drive in dusty areas. Regular changes ensure clean air inside your car and improve HVAC system performance.

### Where Is The Cabin Air Filter Located In Nissan Altima?

The cabin air filter is behind the glove box in most Nissan Altima models. Open the glove box, remove the stops, and gently pull it out to access the filter compartment.

### What Tools Do I Need To Change The Cabin Air Filter?

No special tools are required to change the cabin air filter. Usually, you only need your hands to open the glove box and remove the old filter. Some models might need a screwdriver.

### Can A Dirty Cabin Air Filter Affect Car Performance?

Yes, a dirty cabin air filter reduces airflow inside the car. It can cause bad odors and strain the HVAC system, leading to inefficient heating and cooling.
