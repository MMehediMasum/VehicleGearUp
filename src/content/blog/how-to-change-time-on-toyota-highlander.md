**Is your Toyota Highlander showing the wrong time? It can be frustrating when your clock is off, especially if you rely on it every day.**

Luckily, changing the time on your Highlander is easier than you might think. In this guide, you’ll find simple, step-by-step instructions to get your clock set correctly in no time. Keep reading, and you’ll never have to guess the time while driving again.



## Locate The Clock Controls

To change the time on your Toyota Highlander, the first task is to find the clock controls. These controls let you adjust the hours and minutes easily. The location of these controls depends on your vehicle’s model year and the type of display it uses.

Most Toyota Highlanders have two common ways to set the clock: physical buttons on the dashboard or a touchscreen interface. Knowing where to look saves time and effort.

### Dashboard Button Placement

In many Highlander models, clock settings are adjusted using buttons near the dashboard. These buttons are usually labeled with "Clock," "H," and "M."

* The "Clock" button activates the time-setting mode.
* "H" changes the hour.
* "M" changes the minutes.

Look for a small panel around the radio or climate controls. Sometimes, these buttons are grouped together or close to the audio system.

### Using The Touchscreen Interface

Newer Highlander models often use a touchscreen to control many settings, including the clock. To find the clock settings:

* Tap the "Setup" or "Settings" icon on the main screen.
* Find the "Clock" or "Time" option in the menu.
* Adjust the hour and minute by tapping the arrows or numbers.

The touchscreen method is simple and user-friendly. It provides clear visual feedback while you make changes.

## Adjusting Time Manually

Adjusting the time manually on a Toyota Highlander is simple and quick. This skill helps keep your clock accurate, which is useful for daily schedules and trips. You can change the time either by using physical buttons on the dashboard or through the vehicle’s menu settings. Both ways are user-friendly and require no special tools.

### Using Physical Buttons

Many Toyota Highlander models have buttons near the clock display. These buttons allow you to set the time directly.

* Locate the “Clock” or “Time” button on the dashboard.
* Press and hold the button until the time starts blinking.
* Use the “Hour” and “Minute” buttons to adjust the time.
* Press the “Clock” button again to save the changes.

This method is fast and works well if you prefer tactile controls.

### Navigating The Menu Settings

The Toyota Highlander also lets you adjust time through the infotainment system. This option is common in newer models.

* Turn on the vehicle and access the main menu on the touchscreen.
* Find and select the “Setup” or “Settings” option.
* Look for “Clock” or “Time” settings within the menu.
* Use the touchscreen to change the hour and minutes.
* Confirm and save the new time before exiting the menu.

This method offers a clear interface and precise control over the clock settings.

  


## Setting Time With Bluetooth

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-the-time-on-your-toyota-highlander-through-bluetooth-is-a-modern-and-convenient-way-to-keep-your-clock-accurate-without-manual-adjustments-by-syncing-your-smartphone-with-your-vehicle-you-can-ensure-the-time-updates-automatically-reflecting-changes-like-daylight-saving-or-travel-across-time-zones&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting the time on your Toyota Highlander through Bluetooth is a modern and convenient way to keep your clock accurate without manual adjustments. By syncing your smartphone with your vehicle, you can ensure the time updates automatically, reflecting changes like daylight saving or travel across time zones.

### Connecting Your Phone

Start by pairing your phone with the Highlander’s Bluetooth system. Go to your vehicle’s infotainment screen and access the Bluetooth settings.

On your phone, turn on Bluetooth and look for the Highlander’s system name. Once you see it, tap to connect and confirm any pairing codes if prompted.

Having your phone connected is essential not just for calls and music, but also for syncing time automatically. If your phone isn’t connected, the system won’t pull the time data.

### Syncing Time Automatically

After connecting, the Highlander’s system will often sync the time using your phone’s clock. This feature saves you the hassle of manually setting the time, especially when crossing time zones.

Check your vehicle settings to enable automatic time sync if it isn’t active by default. You might find this option under “Clock” or “Date & Time” in the infotainment menu.

Keep in mind, if your phone’s time is incorrect, the vehicle’s clock will also show the wrong time. Make sure your phone’s time settings are set to automatic and accurate.




## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-time-on-your-toyota-highlander-should-be-straightforward-but-sometimes-you-might-encounter-issues-that-prevent-the-clock-from-updating-correctly-understanding-common-problems-can-save-you-frustration-and-time&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the time on your Toyota Highlander should be straightforward, but sometimes you might encounter issues that prevent the clock from updating correctly. Understanding common problems can save you frustration and time. Let’s look at some typical glitches and how you can fix them yourself.

### Clock Not Responding

If your Highlander's clock doesn’t respond when you try to change the time, it’s often due to a system glitch or a touchscreen calibration issue. First, try turning your vehicle off and on again to reset the system. If the clock still won’t budge, check if the infotainment system needs a software update—Toyota dealerships can help with this.

Another quick fix is to ensure you’re following the correct procedure for your model year. Sometimes the buttons or touchscreen controls are slightly different. Have you reviewed the owner’s manual recently? It might reveal a step you missed.

### Time Reset After Restart

Does your clock reset every time you turn off the engine? This usually points to a battery or electrical issue. The most common cause is a weak or failing car battery that can't hold settings when the vehicle is off.

Try checking the battery voltage or visit a mechanic to test it. Also, loose wiring or a faulty fuse connected to the clock or infotainment system can cause this problem. Have you noticed any other electrical oddities in your Highlander? That could be a clue.

Making sure your battery is in good condition and connections are tight can keep your time settings stable. Don’t ignore these signs—small electrical problems can lead to bigger issues down the road.
