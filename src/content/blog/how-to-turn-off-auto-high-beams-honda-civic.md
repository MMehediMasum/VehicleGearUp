### When Auto High Beams Won't Turn Off

The auto high beams may stay on even when you want them off. This could happen due to dirty sensors blocking light detection. Clean the sensor area gently with a soft cloth.

Check the headlight switch position. Make sure it is not stuck or set incorrectly. Sometimes, the system needs the car to be fully stopped before turning off the high beams.

Look for system warnings on the dashboard. These alerts can show if there is a fault with the lighting system. Resetting the car by turning it off and on may clear temporary errors.

### Resetting The System

Resetting the auto high beam system can fix electronic glitches. Follow these steps:

* Turn off the engine and remove the key.
* Wait about 5 minutes to allow the system to reset.
* Restart the car and test the auto high beams.

If the system still does not work, disconnect the car battery for a few minutes. Reconnect it and check the functionality again. This process resets the car’s electronic control units.

Refer to the owner’s manual for specific reset instructions. Some Honda Civics may require additional steps or diagnostic tools for a full reset.




## Safety Tips When Using High Beams

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-high-beams-can-improve-night-driving-safety-by-increasing-visibility-still-improper-use-can-blind-other-drivers-and-cause-accidents&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using high beams can improve night driving safety by increasing visibility. Still, improper use can blind other drivers and cause accidents. Follow these simple safety tips to use high beams responsibly and avoid risks on the road.

### Understanding When To Use High Beams

Use high beams only on dark roads without streetlights. Switch to low beams when approaching other vehicles or driving behind them. High beams can cause glare and reduce others' visibility.

### Check Surroundings Before Turning On High Beams

Look for oncoming cars and pedestrians. Avoid using high beams in fog, rain, or snow, as light reflects and reduces your view. Adjust your lights to fit road and weather conditions.

### Dim High Beams For Oncoming Traffic

Always dim your high beams at least 500 feet before meeting another vehicle. This action helps prevent temporary blindness and keeps all drivers safe. Watch for other cars’ lights to know when to switch.

### Use High Beams To Spot Hazards Early

High beams help spot animals, people, and obstacles sooner. Use them to prepare for potential dangers. Be ready to slow down or stop when needed to avoid accidents.

### Keep Your Headlights Clean And Properly Aligned

Dirty or misaligned headlights reduce light effectiveness. Clean your headlights regularly. Have them checked and aligned by a professional to ensure the best performance.

  


## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Disable Auto High Beams On Honda Civic?

To disable auto high beams, locate the headlight control stalk. Twist the stalk to turn off the auto high beam function. Refer to your owner’s manual for specific model instructions.

### Where Is The Auto High Beam Switch On Honda Civic?

The auto high beam switch is usually on the left side stalk near the steering wheel. It may be a button or a twist control.

### Can I Manually Control High Beams On Honda Civic?

Yes, you can manually control high beams by pushing or pulling the headlight stalk. This overrides the auto high beam system.

### Does Turning Off Auto High Beams Affect Other Lights?

No, turning off auto high beams only disables automatic switching. Your regular headlights and low beams will function normally.
