## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Car Battery For Honda Accord?



The UPLUS BCI Group 48 AGM battery is ideal for Honda Accord. It offers 760 CCA, 70Ah capacity, and maintenance-free use. It ensures reliable starting power and long-lasting performance for your vehicle.



### How Long Do Agm Batteries Last In Honda Accords?



AGM batteries typically last 3 to 5 years in Honda Accords. Their sealed design resists vibration and extreme weather, enhancing lifespan and reliability. Proper maintenance also extends battery life significantly.



### Can I Use A Group 47 Battery For Honda Accord?



Group 47 batteries fit some Honda Accord models but check your vehicle’s manual. They offer slightly lower capacity and CCA than Group 48 but can be a suitable alternative for specific trims.



### Are Maintenance-free Batteries Better For Honda Accord?



Yes, maintenance-free AGM batteries like the UPLUS and Weize models are better. They don’t require water refills, resist leaks, and provide consistent power, making them convenient and reliable for Honda Accord owners.



### What Is The Importance Of Cca In Honda Accord Batteries?



Cold Cranking Amps (CCA) measure battery's starting power in cold weather. Higher CCA like 760 ensures the Honda Accord starts smoothly in winter and harsh conditions, preventing starting issues.



### Do Key Fob Battery Replacements Affect Honda Accord Remote Function?



Yes, replacing key fob batteries like CR2032 or CR1616 ensures your Honda Accord’s remote works properly. Weak batteries cause signal loss, so timely replacement maintains remote performance and convenience.
