Sway bars help control side-to-side movement caused by wind, turns, or uneven roads. For Class C RVs, strong and reliable sway bars like Hellwig’s heat-treated chromoly bars offer durability and better handling. Weight distribution hitches with sway control also balance the load and reduce sway effectively.



Products like the Camco Eaz-Lift provide easy on/off friction control for constant sway reduction. Other options include anti-sway hitch ball mounts and replacement pins to keep your system working well. Using the right sway bar system ensures safer towing and a smoother ride, giving you confidence while driving your RV.
