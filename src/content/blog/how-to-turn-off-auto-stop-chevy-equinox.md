**If you own a Chevy Equinox, you’ve probably noticed the Auto Stop feature kicking in every time you stop at a light. While this can save fuel, it’s not always the most convenient, especially if you find it interrupting your drive.**

You might be wondering how to turn off Auto Stop and take back control of your ride. You’ll discover simple, step-by-step instructions to disable this feature quickly and easily. Keep reading to learn how to make your driving experience smoother and more comfortable.



## Auto Stop Feature In Chevy Equinox

The Auto Stop feature in the Chevy Equinox helps reduce fuel use and emissions. It automatically stops the engine when the vehicle is idle. This happens, for example, at traffic lights or in heavy traffic. The engine restarts quickly when you release the brake pedal. This feature aims to make driving more eco-friendly and efficient.

### Purpose Of Auto Stop

The main purpose of Auto Stop is to save fuel by turning off the engine. It lowers fuel consumption during short stops. It also reduces harmful exhaust emissions. Auto Stop supports cleaner air and helps meet environmental rules. It works best in city driving with frequent stops.

### Benefits And Drawbacks

* **Benefits:**Saves fuel and cuts emissions. Reduces engine wear during stops. Helps drivers save money on gas.
* **Drawbacks:**Can feel strange at first. Some drivers find it annoying in stop-and-go traffic. May cause slight delays when restarting the engine.

## Disabling Auto Stop Temporarily

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=disabling-the-auto-stop-feature-on-your-chevy-equinox-can-make-your-driving-experience-smoother-especially-in-stop-and-go-traffic-or-when-you-prefer-full-control-over-your-engine-temporarily-turning-off-auto-stop-is-simple-and-doesnt-require-digging-through-complicated-menus&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Disabling the Auto Stop feature on your Chevy Equinox can make your driving experience smoother, especially in stop-and-go traffic or when you prefer full control over your engine. Temporarily turning off Auto Stop is simple and doesn’t require digging through complicated menus. It gives you the freedom to decide when you want the engine to stay running without permanently changing your vehicle’s settings.

### Using The Auto Stop Button

Right on your dashboard, you’ll find a dedicated Auto Stop button. Pressing this button disables the Auto Stop system for the current drive cycle. It’s a quick way to keep your engine running without the automatic stops at red lights or traffic jams.

Keep in mind, this button doesn’t permanently turn off Auto Stop. It only suspends the feature until you turn off the car. This is perfect if you’re driving in conditions where frequent engine restarts feel annoying or you just want a smoother ride without interruptions.

### When The Setting Resets

Here’s something many drivers don’t realize: the Auto Stop system reactivates every time you start your Equinox. The temporary disable you set with the button resets automatically. This means you’ll need to press the Auto Stop button again if you want to disable it on your next drive.

Think about whether you prefer manually controlling this feature each time or if it might be worth exploring permanent settings options. How often do you find yourself disabling Auto Stop? Your answer could help you decide the best approach for your driving style.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Disable Auto Stop On A Chevy Equinox?

To disable Auto Stop, press the Auto Stop/Start button located on the dashboard. This temporarily turns off the feature for your current drive.

### Can Auto Stop Be Permanently Turned Off In Chevy Equinox?

No, the Auto Stop feature resets each time you start your vehicle. You must disable it manually every drive if desired.

### Why Would I Want To Turn Off Auto Stop In My Equinox?

Some drivers turn off Auto Stop for smoother acceleration and to avoid engine restart delays in traffic.

### Does Turning Off Auto Stop Affect Fuel Efficiency?

Yes, disabling Auto Stop can reduce fuel savings since the engine runs continuously instead of shutting off at stops.
