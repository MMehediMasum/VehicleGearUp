### Can I Use Safe Pro Fire Stop For Home Fires?

### How Long Does The Fire Extinguisher Fluid Last?



Fire extinguisher fluid typically lasts several years if unused. Check the manufacturer’s expiration date on the label. Proper storage in a cool, dry place extends fluid effectiveness. Regular inspections ensure the extinguisher is ready when needed for car or home fires.



### What Types Of Fires Do These Extinguishers Handle?



These fire extinguishers primarily target Class A, B, and C fires. They are effective against flammable liquids, paper, wood, and electrical fires. However, they are not suitable for large or chemical fires. Always verify the extinguisher's rating before use.
