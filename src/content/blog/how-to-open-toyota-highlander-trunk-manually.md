## Locate The Trunk Release

Knowing how to locate the trunk release on your Toyota Highlander can save you time and frustration, especially if the automatic opening system isn’t working. The release controls are designed to be within easy reach, but they vary depending on whether you’re inside or outside the vehicle. Understanding where each release option is will help you open the trunk manually without hassle.

### Find The Interior Release Lever

Look near the driver’s seat for a small lever or button dedicated to the trunk. It’s often located on the lower left side of the dashboard or near the floor by the driver’s seat. If you’ve ever had groceries to unload quickly, you’ll appreciate how this simple lever can instantly open the trunk without stepping out.

Not all Highlanders have the same setup, so check your owner’s manual if you don’t spot it right away. Have you ever tried pulling it and wondered why nothing happened? Sometimes, the lever requires a firm pull or press to activate, so don’t hesitate to give it a good tug.

### Check The Key Fob Buttons

Your key fob likely has a dedicated button for the trunk or tailgate. Press and hold this button for a couple of seconds to manually pop open the trunk. This method is especially handy when your hands are full or when you’re standing a short distance away from your vehicle.

Keep in mind that if the key fob battery is weak or dead, this function might not work. Have you tested your key fob lately? Replacing the battery can often restore full functionality quickly and without needing a trip to the dealer.

### Identify The Exterior Handle

The exterior handle for the trunk is usually integrated just above the license plate or under the rear Toyota emblem. On some models, you might find a small button next to the handle that you press to release the trunk manually. This option is your go-to if the electronic release isn’t working or if your key fob is out of battery.

When you find the handle, make sure to press firmly or lift the handle gently after pressing the button. If the trunk feels stuck, check if the vehicle is locked, as some models require the car to be unlocked first. Have you noticed how sometimes the trunk doesn’t open immediately? Patience and a little extra pressure can do the trick.




## Use The Interior Release Lever

The Toyota Highlander trunk can be opened manually using the interior release lever. This lever provides a simple way to access the trunk without needing the key or remote. It is especially helpful during power failures or emergencies.

Using the interior release lever requires knowing its exact location and how to operate it correctly. This method is quick and does not need any special tools.

### Access The Lever Position

The interior release lever is located inside the vehicle, near the driver’s seat. Usually, it is found on the lower side of the driver’s seat or near the floor on the driver’s side. Check the side panel or under the seat for a small handle or lever marked with a trunk icon.

The lever is easy to reach without stretching or moving too much. Keep the vehicle on and ensure it is parked safely before attempting to use the lever.

### Pull The Lever To Unlock

Grasp the interior release lever firmly. Pull it in the direction indicated by the trunk icon or handle shape. You will feel a click or hear a sound signaling the trunk has unlocked.

After pulling the lever, move to the rear of the vehicle. Lift the trunk door gently to open it fully. The manual release lever bypasses electronic locks, allowing easy trunk access.

  


## Open With The Key Fob

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=opening-the-toyota-highlander-trunk-using-the-key-fob-is-quick-and-easy-the-key-fob-sends-a-signal-to-release-the-trunk-latch-remotely&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Opening the Toyota Highlander trunk using the key fob is quick and easy. The key fob sends a signal to release the trunk latch remotely. This feature lets you open the trunk without using the physical key. It is handy when your hands are full or you are close to the vehicle.

### Press The Trunk Button

Locate the trunk button on your Toyota Highlander key fob. It usually has a small icon of a car with an open trunk. Press and hold this button for about two seconds. The trunk will unlock and pop open slightly. Walk to the back of the vehicle and lift the trunk lid fully.

### Troubleshoot Key Fob Issues

If the trunk does not open, check the key fob battery first. A weak battery can prevent the signal from reaching the car. Replace the battery if it is old or dead. Also, ensure you are within range of the vehicle, usually about 20 feet. If problems persist, try pressing the trunk button multiple times. You may also try using the physical key as a backup. For persistent issues, visit a Toyota dealer for further help.

## Tips For Smooth Operation

Ensuring smooth operation of your Toyota Highlander trunk is essential for convenience and safety. Simple care routines can prevent issues and keep the manual trunk opening working well. Regular attention to key parts avoids unexpected problems.

### Regular Maintenance Checks

Check the trunk latch for dirt and debris often. Clean it with a soft cloth and mild cleaner. Lubricate the latch mechanism every few months using a silicone spray. Inspect the trunk seal for cracks or damage. Replace it if you notice wear to keep water out. Test the manual release handle regularly to ensure it moves freely. Address any stiffness or resistance immediately. These small checks help maintain smooth trunk function.

### Battery Replacement Advice

The trunk’s electronic components depend on a healthy battery. Check battery health during routine vehicle service. A weak battery can cause trunk release failures. Replace the battery before it fully drains. Use the correct battery type for your Highlander model. Dispose of old batteries properly. Keep battery terminals clean and free from corrosion. This helps maintain reliable power for trunk operation.
