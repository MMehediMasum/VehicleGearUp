## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Battery Size For Ram 1500 Trucks?



The ideal battery size for Ram 1500 is Group 94R or H7. These sizes fit perfectly and offer optimal power. Batteries like UPLUS AGM-L70-M and ACDelco 94RAGM are excellent choices for this size.



### How Many Cold Cranking Amps (cca) Do Ram 1500 Batteries Need?



Ram 1500 trucks typically require batteries with at least 700-850 CCA. Higher CCA ensures reliable starts in cold weather. Batteries like OPTIMA RedTop 75/25 and MARXON 94R offer 800-880 CCA, making them great for cold climates.



### Are Agm Batteries Better For Ram 1500 Trucks?



Yes, AGM batteries provide better durability and maintenance-free use. They resist vibration and recharge faster than conventional batteries. Many top options like Interstate MTX-94R and ACDelco Gold 49AGM are AGM, making them ideal for Ram 1500.



### How Long Do Premium Agm Batteries Last In Ram 1500?



Premium AGM batteries last around 3 to 5 years under normal conditions. Brands like UPLUS and MARXON offer warranties of up to 36 months, ensuring reliability and peace of mind for Ram 1500 owners.



### Can I Use Dual Terminal Batteries In My Ram 1500?



Yes, dual terminal batteries like OPTIMA RedTop 75/25 fit Ram 1500 trucks well. They offer versatile mounting options and make installation easier. These batteries support both SAE and GM terminal types.



### What Maintenance Is Needed For Ram 1500 Truck Batteries?



Maintenance-free AGM batteries require minimal care. Regularly check terminals for corrosion and keep them clean. Ensure tight connections and avoid deep discharges to maximize battery life in your Ram 1500.
