## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Car Battery Size For Honda Cr-v?



The ideal battery size for Honda CR-V is Group 47 or H5. These batteries offer 12V, 60Ah capacity, and around 680 CCA. AGM batteries like Mighty Max or Weize Platinum are highly recommended for longevity and performance.



### How To Replace The Key Fob Battery For Honda Cr-v?



Honda CR-V key fobs commonly use CR2032 or CR1616 batteries. Simply open the fob case with a small tool, replace the old battery with a new one, and close it. Always check the fitment guide to ensure compatibility.



### What Is The Difference Between Agm And Standard Batteries?



AGM batteries are sealed, maintenance-free, and have better vibration resistance. They provide higher cold cranking amps (CCA) and longer lifespan. Standard flooded batteries need regular maintenance and are less durable under extreme conditions.



### How Long Do Honda Cr-v Batteries Typically Last?



A quality AGM battery in a Honda CR-V lasts around 3 to 5 years. Proper maintenance and driving habits can extend battery life. Regularly check battery health to avoid unexpected failures.



### Can I Use A Mighty Max Battery For My Honda Cr-v?



Yes, Mighty Max MM-H5 and MM-G35 AGM batteries fit Honda CR-V models. They provide 12V, 60Ah or 55Ah, with strong 650-680 CCA power. These batteries are reliable replacements and offer good performance and durability.
