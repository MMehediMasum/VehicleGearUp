## Off-road And Towing Capabilities

The Ford Explorer ST Line stands out with its impressive off-road and towing capabilities, making it a versatile choice for adventure seekers and practical drivers alike. Whether you're navigating rough trails or hauling heavy loads, this SUV offers features designed to boost your confidence and control. Let’s dive into what makes its terrain handling and towing performance so remarkable.

### Terrain Management

The Explorer ST Line comes equipped with a Terrain Management System that adjusts the vehicle's settings based on the surface you're driving on. You can switch between modes like Normal, Mud/Ruts, Sand, and Snow, each optimizing traction and stability. This means you don’t have to worry about losing grip or control when the road gets tough.

Imagine driving through a muddy trail after a rainstorm. The system adapts instantly to keep you moving forward without getting stuck. This feature also changes how the engine, transmission, and brakes work together, giving you smoother handling in tricky conditions.

### Towing Performance

If towing is a priority for you, the Explorer ST Line delivers strong performance with its robust engine and towing package. It can tow up to 5,600 pounds, enough for boats, trailers, or campers. This capability makes weekend trips and hauling heavy gear much easier.

What’s more, the trailer sway control helps keep your load steady, reducing the risk of accidents. Integrated brake controllers and a clear rearview camera work together to make hitching and maneuvering safer and more precise. Have you ever struggled with trailer sway on a windy day? The Explorer’s system can give you peace of mind in those moments.
