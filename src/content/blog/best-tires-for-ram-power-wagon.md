Yes, ReadyLIFT 66-1113 supports tires up to 35" on 2013-2018 Ram 2500/3500 4WD. Other kits may vary, so check specs for tire size compatibility before installation.



### Are There Recommended Wheel Upgrades For Ram Power Wagon?



Factory replacement 17x8" matte black alloy wheels and OE 22" chrome rims are popular options. Both enhance style and accommodate larger tires, improving off-road capability.



### Do Husky Liners Mud Guards Fit Ram Power Wagon?



No, Husky Liners front and rear mud guards for 2019-2024 Ram 2500/3500 exclude Power Wagon models. Power Wagon owners should seek specialized guards designed for their truck.



### How Do Tpms Sensors Benefit Ram Power Wagon Tires?



TPMS sensors monitor tire pressure in real-time, enhancing safety and fuel efficiency. The 68249197AA sensor fits Ram 1500/2500 Power Wagon models from 2014-2015, ensuring accurate pressure alerts.



### What Suspension Kits Exclude Power Wagon Packages?



ReadyLIFT 66-19180 and 46-19120 leveling kits exclude snowprep, ambulance, and Power Wagon packages. Check product details to ensure compatibility with your Ram model before buying.
