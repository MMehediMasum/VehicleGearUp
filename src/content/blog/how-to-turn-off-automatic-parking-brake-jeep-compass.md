## Locate The Parking Brake Controls

Locating the parking brake controls in your Jeep Compass is the first step to turning off the automatic parking brake. Knowing exactly where these controls are can save you time and avoid frustration. It also ensures you handle the system safely and correctly.

### Identify The Parking Brake Button

Look around the center console area near the gear shift. The parking brake button is usually a small switch or lever labeled with a "P" symbol or the words "Brake" or "Parking." Sometimes it’s a simple button you pull up or push down to engage or release the brake.

If you don’t see a traditional lever, your Jeep Compass might have an electronic parking brake system. This button often glows or has a light indicator showing if the brake is active.

### Check The User Manual

Your Jeep’s user manual is the best source for precise information on parking brake controls. It provides clear diagrams and instructions tailored to your model year and trim. If you’ve misplaced your manual, many manufacturers offer digital copies online that you can access quickly.

Have you ever skipped reading the manual and ended up guessing your way through controls? Checking the manual first avoids unnecessary trial and error and ensures you won’t accidentally engage the wrong feature.

## Manual Parking Brake Operation

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-jeep-compass-is-equipped-with-a-convenient-automatic-parking-brake-system-yet-there-are-times-when-manual-operation-might-be-preferable&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The Jeep Compass is equipped with a convenient automatic parking brake system. Yet, there are times when manual operation might be preferable. Understanding how to engage and release the parking brake manually can be useful. This guide will help you manage the parking brake with ease.

### Engaging The Brake Manually

Engaging the manual parking brake in your Jeep Compass is straightforward. Follow these simple steps:

* Locate the parking brake lever between the front seats.
* Firmly pull the lever upward until you hear a click.
* Ensure the lever is fully engaged to prevent vehicle movement.

These steps secure your Jeep, keeping it in place. This is especially handy on slopes or inclines. Always double-check that the brake is engaged before leaving your vehicle.

### Releasing The Brake

To release the parking brake manually, follow these steps:

* Return to the driver's seat and press the brake pedal.
* Firmly hold the parking brake lever.
* Lift the lever slightly while pressing the release button.
* Gently lower the lever to its original position.

Releasing the brake correctly ensures your Jeep can move freely. Always ensure the brake is fully released before driving. This prevents unnecessary wear and tear on the brake system.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-problems-with-the-automatic-parking-brake-in-your-jeep-compass-can-save-time-and-stress-some-issues-may-prevent-the-brake-from-turning-off-as-expected&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common problems with the automatic parking brake in your Jeep Compass can save time and stress. Some issues may prevent the brake from turning off as expected. Knowing how to identify and fix these problems helps keep your vehicle safe and running smoothly.

Electrical glitches or sensor errors often cause the brake to stay engaged. Dirt or debris around the brake components may also create problems. Checking the system carefully can pinpoint the exact cause before taking action.

### When The Brake Won't Turn Off

The parking brake might stay on due to low battery voltage. A weak battery can prevent the brake release signal from working. Test the battery and replace it if needed.

Another cause is a faulty brake switch. The switch might not send the correct signal to release the brake. Inspect the switch and wiring for damage or loose connections.

Sometimes, the brake motor or actuator fails. It may get stuck or lose power. Listen for unusual sounds when trying to release the brake. Replace faulty parts as required.

### Resetting The System

Resetting the parking brake system can clear minor errors. Turn the ignition off and on to restart the vehicle’s computer. This simple step may fix electronic glitches.

Perform a manual release of the parking brake. Hold the brake release button while pressing the brake pedal. This combination can reset the brake system.

Check the owner’s manual for a specific reset procedure. Some Jeep Compass models need a special sequence to reset the parking brake module.

Another key moment is during vehicle maintenance or towing. Engaging the manual brake ensures the car stays stationary, protecting both the vehicle and those around it. Keep your Jeep’s brake components clean and free from debris, especially if you drive in muddy or snowy conditions. Moisture and dirt can cause corrosion and reduce brake effectiveness over time.
