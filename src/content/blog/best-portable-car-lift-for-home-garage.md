## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Capacity For A Portable Car Lift?



The best capacity depends on your vehicle weight. For home garages, 5,000 to 7,000 lbs capacity lifts cover most cars and trucks safely. Choose a lift with enough weight support to handle your vehicle without strain or risk.



### How High Can Portable Car Lifts Raise Vehicles?



Most portable car lifts raise vehicles between 25. 6" and 28". This height range allows easy access for repairs and maintenance under the car. Adjustable height features provide flexibility for different tasks and vehicle sizes.



### Are Portable Car Lifts Safe For Home Garage Use?



Yes, portable car lifts with safety locks and heavy-duty steel frames are safe. Look for models with certified weight capacity and stable design to ensure secure lifting during repairs or storage.



### Can I Use Portable Car Lifts For Suvs And Trucks?



Absolutely. Many portable lifts support up to 7,000 lbs, suitable for SUVs and light trucks. Adjustable platforms and extension plates help fit various vehicle types, providing versatile garage lifting solutions.



### What Power Source Do Portable Car Lifts Require?



Most portable car lifts operate on standard 110V or 120V power units. This makes them convenient for home garages without special electrical needs. Always check power compatibility before purchase.



### How Portable Are These Car Lifts For Garage Use?



These lifts are designed to be compact and lightweight for easy movement. They often feature wheels or carry handles, allowing users to store or reposition them conveniently in tight garage spaces.
