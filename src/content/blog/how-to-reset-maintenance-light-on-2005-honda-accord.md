## Locate The Maintenance Light

Before resetting the maintenance light on your 2005 Honda Accord, you need to find where the light appears. This light shows up on the dashboard. It alerts you when it is time for regular car maintenance. Knowing its location helps you reset it correctly. The maintenance light usually looks like an engine or wrench symbol.

It stays on after the car completes a set number of miles. The light reminds you to check your oil and other important parts. Finding the light is the first step to turning it off after service.

### Dashboard Display Area

The maintenance light appears in the main dashboard area. This is the panel behind the steering wheel. It shows all important driving information. Look for a small icon shaped like a wrench or the word "Maintenance." The light can glow in orange or yellow.

### Instrument Cluster

The light is part of the instrument cluster. This cluster includes the speedometer, fuel gauge, and other indicators. It is often near the center of the dashboard. Focus your eyes on the middle or left side of the cluster. The maintenance light is small but clear.

### During Ignition Start-up

The maintenance light turns on when you start the car. It stays lit briefly as the system checks all indicators. If the light stays on after a few seconds, it means maintenance is due. Watch the dashboard carefully as you turn the key to the "On" position. This helps confirm the light’s location.

### Press And Hold The Select/reset Button

Start by locating the Select/Reset button on your dashboard. It's usually found near the speedometer or on the steering wheel, depending on your model. Press and hold this button firmly. This initial step is crucial, as it prepares the system to accept the reset command.

### Turn Ignition To On Position

While holding the button, turn your car key to the 'On' position. This means turning the ignition without starting the engine. You’ll notice all the dashboard lights illuminating—this is normal. Keep holding the Select/Reset button throughout this step.

### Wait For Light To Flash

Patience is key here. Continue to hold the button and watch for the maintenance light to begin flashing. This indicates the system is processing your reset command. It usually takes about 10 seconds, but it can feel like a mini eternity when you're anxious to get it done.

### Release The Button

Once the maintenance light flashes and then disappears, it's time to release the button. This final step confirms that the reset has been successful. Your dashboard should now be free of the maintenance light, signaling that your car is ready for the road ahead. Resetting this light not only gives you peace of mind but also keeps your vehicle running smoothly. ### What If The Maintenance Light Won’t Reset On My Honda Accord?
