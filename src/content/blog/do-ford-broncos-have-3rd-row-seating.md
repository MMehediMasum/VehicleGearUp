### Factory Options

The current Ford Bronco models do not come with a factory-installed third row. Ford has designed the Bronco primarily as a two-row SUV, focusing on off-road capability and rugged style. This means if you need to fit more than five passengers, the standard Bronco may not meet your needs right away.

Ford does offer larger SUVs like the Ford Explorer or Expedition if third-row seating is a must. However, for the Bronco itself, there is no official third-row option as part of the factory lineup. This can be a dealbreaker if your family or group regularly requires more seats.

### Aftermarket Solutions

If you already own a Bronco and want to add a third row, aftermarket companies provide some creative seating solutions. These options range from custom bench seats to modular seating kits designed to fit the Bronco's interior.

Keep in mind, installing aftermarket third-row seats can affect your vehicle’s safety, warranty, and cargo space. Always check with professionals and ensure any modifications comply with local vehicle regulations. Have you thought about how much extra space you really need, and whether the added seats will fit your lifestyle?

Many Bronco owners find that aftermarket seats work well for occasional extra passengers but may not be practical for daily use. If your Bronco is mainly for adventure trips with friends, adding a third row might be worth the trade-off in cargo room.
