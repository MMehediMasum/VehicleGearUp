## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make Oil Filled Radiators Ideal For Large Rooms?



Oil filled radiators provide consistent, long-lasting heat that spreads evenly across large spaces. They operate quietly and maintain warmth even after being turned off. Features like remote control, digital thermostats, and safety protections enhance user convenience and safety, making them perfect for large rooms.



### How Energy Efficient Are Oil Filled Radiator Heaters?



Oil filled radiators consume less electricity compared to traditional heaters. Their oil retains heat, reducing the need for constant power. Models with programmable timers and adjustable thermostats further optimize energy use, lowering electricity bills while keeping large rooms comfortably warm.



### Are Oil Filled Radiators Safe For Homes With Children?



Yes, many oil filled radiators include child lock features and tip-over protection. These safety measures prevent accidental burns and shut off the heater if tipped. Overheat protection also ensures safe operation, making these heaters a reliable choice for homes with children.



### Can I Control Oil Filled Radiators Remotely?



Most modern oil filled radiators come with remote controls. This allows you to adjust temperature, switch modes, and set timers from across the room. Remote control adds convenience, especially in large rooms or for users with mobility challenges.



### How Quiet Are Oil Filled Radiator Heaters During Operation?



Oil filled radiators operate almost silently since they use convection heating without fans. This makes them ideal for bedrooms, offices, or quiet spaces. Their quiet performance ensures warmth without disrupting sleep or concentration.



### What Size Oil Filled Radiator Is Best For Large Rooms?



For large rooms, choose oil filled radiators with at least 1500W power and 7 to 8 fins. This ensures efficient heat distribution. Models with adjustable thermostats and multiple heat settings offer better temperature control for bigger spaces.
