**Are you dreaming of owning a Harley Davidson but worried about the price? You’re not alone.**

Many riders want the iconic Harley experience without emptying their wallet. So, what is the cheapest Harley Davidson motorcycle you can get without sacrificing quality and style? You’ll discover the most affordable Harley models that still deliver the thrill and freedom you crave.

Keep reading to find out which bike fits your budget and gets you on the road faster than you think.




## Harley Davidson Entry Models

Harley Davidson offers entry models designed for new riders and budget-conscious buyers. These motorcycles balance affordability with the classic Harley style and performance. They provide an accessible way to join the Harley community without a large initial investment.

Entry models feature manageable engine sizes and simple controls. They deliver enough power for city rides and short trips. Their design keeps the iconic Harley look while offering comfort and ease of handling.

### Affordable Options In The Lineup

Harley Davidson entry models often include these bikes:

* Street 500
* Street 750
* Iron 883

These models come with lower price tags compared to premium Harley bikes. They still maintain quality and Harley’s signature style. Ideal for riders who want the brand without a high cost.

### Engine Sizes And Performance

Entry models usually have engines between 500cc and 900cc. This range offers enough power for beginners and everyday riding. The Street 500 has a 494cc engine. The Iron 883 features an 883cc engine.

These engines provide smooth acceleration. They are easy to control and maintain. Perfect for urban riding and weekend cruising.

### Design Features And Styles

Harley Davidson entry bikes have simple and classic designs. They include features like:

* Low seat height for easy reach
* Minimalist bodywork
* Blacked-out finishes
* Comfortable riding posture

These design elements create a timeless look. They appeal to new riders who want a stylish yet practical motorcycle.




## Price Comparison Of Popular Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=finding-the-cheapest-harley-davidson-motorcycle-means-looking-closely-at-various-popular-models-and-their-price-tags-prices-vary-not-only-between-models-but-also-depend-on-the-year-condition-and-features&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Finding the cheapest Harley Davidson motorcycle means looking closely at various popular models and their price tags. Prices vary not only between models but also depend on the year, condition, and features. Comparing these can help you decide which bike fits your budget without sacrificing the Harley experience.

### Street 500 Vs Street 750

The Harley Street 500 and Street 750 are often the first choices for new riders. The Street 500 usually comes at a lower price point, making it the most affordable new Harley on the market.

Here’s a quick comparison:

| Model | Engine | Starting Price (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street 500 | 494cc | $6,799 |
| Street 750 | 749cc | $7,499 |

The Street 750 offers more power but at about $700 more. If you’re budget-conscious but still want a Harley, the Street 500 might be your best bet. Do you want a bike that’s easier on the wallet or one with more punch?

### Iron 883 And Other Sportsters

The Iron 883 is another popular choice within the Sportster family, known for its classic look and reliable performance. It generally costs more than the Street series but offers a bigger engine and a tougher image.

Prices for the Iron 883 typically start around $10,000 new, which can feel steep compared to the Street models. However, it holds value well and has a strong fan base. If style and engine size matter more to you than the initial price, the Iron 883 is worth considering.

### Used Vs New Prices

Buying used can dramatically lower your entry cost into owning a Harley. Many used Street 500 and Iron 883 models sell for 20-40% less than their new counterparts depending on condition and mileage.

* Used Street 500 models often fall between $4,000 and $6,000.
* Used Iron 883 bikes typically range from $7,000 to $9,000.

Are you comfortable with a bike that has some miles on it if it means saving thousands? Inspecting used bikes carefully can get you a nearly new Harley at a fraction of the price. Knowing where to look and what to check can protect your investment.

## Factors Affecting Motorcycle Costs

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=when-considering-the-cheapest-harley-davidson-motorcycle-several-factors-can-impact-costs-price-isnt-just-about-the-sticker-value&w=424&h=324&c=7)

When considering the cheapest Harley Davidson motorcycle, several factors can impact costs. Price isn't just about the sticker value. There are hidden costs that influence the overall expenditure. Understanding these elements helps in making an informed decision.

### Customization And Accessories

Customizing a Harley can significantly change its cost. Adding personal touches can enhance your bike's look but increases expenses. Accessories like saddlebags, custom seats, or chrome finishes add to the price. Each addition means extra spending. Consider what features are essential versus optional.

### Maintenance And Insurance

Regular maintenance ensures your motorcycle runs smoothly. This includes oil changes, brake checks, and tire replacements. These services cost money over time. Insurance is another cost to consider. Premiums vary based on factors like location and driving record. Comparing insurance options can lead to savings.

### Resale Value

Resale value impacts the long-term cost of owning a Harley. A well-maintained motorcycle retains its value better. Popular models may sell for more when resold. This can offset initial purchase costs. Researching which models hold value better is wise.




## Where To Find Budget Harley Bikes

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=finding-a-budget-friendly-harley-davidson-motorcycle-may-seem-challenging-but-there-are-smart-places-to-look-that-can-help-you-land-a-great-deal-whether-youre-a-first-time-buyer-or-a-seasoned-rider-seeking-affordability-knowing-where-to-search-is-key-to-saving-money-without-sacrificing-quality&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Finding a budget-friendly Harley Davidson motorcycle may seem challenging, but there are smart places to look that can help you land a great deal. Whether you're a first-time buyer or a seasoned rider seeking affordability, knowing where to search is key to saving money without sacrificing quality.

Here are some practical options where you can find Harley bikes that fit your budget and riding style.

### Authorized Dealers And Promotions

Authorized Harley Davidson dealers often have seasonal promotions or clearance sales on certain models. Visiting a dealership can give you access to certified pre-owned bikes, which come with warranties and thorough inspections.

Don’t hesitate to ask about financing options or trade-in deals that can reduce your upfront cost. Sometimes, dealers offer special pricing on last year’s models, which can save you a significant amount.

### Online Marketplaces

Websites like Cycle Trader, eBay Motors, and Craigslist provide a wide range of Harley motorcycles at different price points. You can filter searches by location, price, and bike condition to find the best deals near you.

When browsing online, look closely at photos and descriptions, and always request a history report if possible. Connecting directly with sellers allows you to negotiate price and arrange test rides to ensure the bike meets your expectations.

### Local Auctions And Private Sellers

Local motorcycle auctions can be treasure troves for budget Harley bikes. These events sometimes feature repossessed or lightly used motorcycles sold below market value.

Private sellers might also offer better prices than dealers since they don’t have overhead costs. Meeting sellers in person gives you the chance to inspect the bike thoroughly and ask detailed questions about its maintenance and history.

  


## Tips To Save On Your Harley Purchase

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=saving-money-on-a-harley-davidson-motorcycle-takes-smart-planning-many-buyers-want-the-best-price-without-losing-quality&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Saving money on a Harley Davidson motorcycle takes smart planning. Many buyers want the best price without losing quality. Simple tips can help reduce the cost of your new bike.

These strategies focus on timing, trade value, and choosing the right purchase program. Each step helps cut expenses while still getting a great Harley.

### Seasonal Discounts And Financing

Dealers offer discounts during certain times of the year. End of the year and holiday sales often have lower prices. Watch for special offers on popular models.

Financing through Harley Davidson can also save money. Low-interest loans or payment plans make buying easier. Check different financing options before deciding.

### Trade-ins And Negotiation

Trading your old motorcycle can reduce the price of your new Harley. Dealers often give credit for trade-ins, lowering your total cost. Prepare your current bike to get the best value.

Negotiation is key. Always ask for a better deal. Small discounts add up and can save hundreds of dollars.

### Certified Pre-owned Programs

Certified Pre-Owned Harleys offer quality and savings. These bikes undergo inspection and come with warranties. They cost less than new bikes but still perform well.

Buying certified pre-owned means less risk and more value. Consider this option if you want a Harley without the new bike price.
