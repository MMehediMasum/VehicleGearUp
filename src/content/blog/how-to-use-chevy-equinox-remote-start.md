## Remote Start Features

The remote start feature on the Chevy Equinox is designed to add convenience and comfort to your driving experience. It allows you to start your vehicle from a distance, warming it up or cooling it down before you even step inside. This simple function can save you time and make your daily routine smoother.

### Key Benefits

Remote start offers several practical advantages that enhance your daily drive. You can warm up your engine on cold mornings, which helps improve performance and reduces wear. On hot days, it lets you cool down the interior by activating the air conditioning before you enter.

Beyond comfort, remote start also adds a layer of safety. By starting the engine remotely, you ensure your vehicle is ready to go without rushing. It’s especially helpful when you're in a hurry or carrying groceries and don’t want to fumble with keys.

* Heats or cools your vehicle before entry

## Activating Remote Start

Knowing how to activate this feature properly ensures you can make the most of it without any frustration.

### Using The Key Fob

To activate remote start using your key fob, make sure you are within a reasonable range of your vehicle, usually about 200 feet. Press the **lock button**once to secure the doors.

Then, quickly press and hold the **remote start button**—usually marked with a circular arrow—until the vehicle lights flash or the engine starts. This process takes just a few seconds, so be ready to hold the button firmly.

If your Equinox doesn’t start, check that all doors and the hood are securely closed. The system won’t activate if it detects any issues, protecting your vehicle and yourself.

### Using The Mychevrolet App

The MyChevrolet app offers another way to start your Equinox remotely, even if you’re not close to the vehicle. After downloading and logging in, select the **Remote Start**option on the app’s dashboard.

Before pressing start, verify your vehicle's status shown in the app, like fuel level and whether the doors are locked. This helps you avoid surprises when you reach your car.

Once you tap the remote start button, the app will send the command to your Equinox. You’ll get a notification when the engine starts, so you know your vehicle is ready without needing to go outside.

## Stopping Remote Start

Knowing how to stop the remote start feature on your Chevy Equinox is just as important as starting it. There are a couple of ways to turn off the engine remotely, giving you full control over your vehicle even from a distance. This can come in handy if you change your mind or need to conserve fuel.

### Manual Engine Shutdown

You can manually stop the engine by pressing the remote start button on your key fob again. Usually, holding down the remote start button for a few seconds will shut off the engine immediately.

Try this if you’re nearby and want to quickly cancel the remote start without getting into the vehicle. It’s straightforward and fast—just press and hold until the engine stops.

### Automatic Engine Shutdown

Your Chevy Equinox is designed to automatically shut down the engine after a set period, usually around 10 to 15 minutes. This safety feature prevents the car from running endlessly and wasting fuel if you forget to turn it off.

Have you ever left your car running remotely and then got distracted? This automatic stop saves you from worrying about that. It’s a built-in safeguard that ensures your vehicle won’t stay on indefinitely.

  


## Troubleshooting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=remote-start-systems-can-be-incredibly-convenient-but-they-sometimes-run-into-hiccups-knowing-how-to-troubleshoot-your-chevy-equinox-remote-start-can-save-you-time-and-frustration&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Remote start systems can be incredibly convenient, but they sometimes run into hiccups. Knowing how to troubleshoot your Chevy Equinox remote start can save you time and frustration. Let’s look at common problems you might face and simple ways to reset the system to get it working smoothly again.

### Common Issues

One common problem is the remote start not activating the engine. This can happen if the key fob battery is low or dead. Try replacing the battery first to see if that solves the issue.

Another frequent issue is the system not responding due to the vehicle’s security settings. If the doors aren’t fully closed or the hood is open, the remote start won’t work. Double-check these before assuming the system is faulty.

Sometimes, extreme weather conditions affect the remote start function. Cold temperatures can cause the engine to resist starting remotely. Have you noticed your remote start failing mostly on colder days? That’s a clue to inspect the battery and starter health.

### Resetting The System

If your remote start stops working properly, resetting the system might help. Start by locking and unlocking the doors using the key fob. This simple action can reset the car’s electronics and restore functionality.

Another way to reset is by disconnecting the vehicle battery for a few minutes. This forces the car’s computer to reboot. Be careful when handling the battery and make sure you have any necessary radio codes ready before disconnecting.

After resetting, test the remote start again. If it still doesn’t work, consider checking your owner’s manual for specific reset instructions or contacting a dealer. Sometimes a software update or professional diagnosis is needed to fix deeper issues.




## Safety Precautions

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-the-chevy-equinox-remote-start-feature-adds-convenience-especially-on-cold-mornings-or-hot-afternoons-however-its-important-to-keep-safety-at-the-forefront-whenever-you-use-this-technology&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using the Chevy Equinox remote start feature adds convenience, especially on cold mornings or hot afternoons. However, it's important to keep safety at the forefront whenever you use this technology. Ignoring safety precautions can lead to dangerous situations for you and those around you.

### Proper Use Guidelines

Always make sure your vehicle is parked in a well-ventilated area before activating remote start. Running the engine in an enclosed space like a garage can cause harmful carbon monoxide buildup. Have you ever thought about how quickly fumes can accumulate without proper airflow?

Never leave your vehicle unattended with the doors unlocked after remote starting. This practice prevents unauthorized access and protects your belongings. Remember to check if your Equinox’s remote start system automatically locks the doors or if you need to do it manually.

Keep children and pets away from the vehicle during remote start. The engine running could tempt them to approach or even enter the vehicle, creating a safety risk. Make sure you’re aware of your surroundings before you press the remote start button.

### Legal Considerations

Some states and localities have laws against idling vehicles unattended or restrictions on remote start use. Are you aware of the rules in your area? Ignoring these laws can lead to fines or other penalties.

Check your state’s regulations regarding engine idling times and locations. For example, some places limit idling to just a few minutes to reduce pollution. Following these rules helps you avoid legal trouble and contributes to cleaner air.

Your vehicle’s manual often outlines legal advice related to remote start use. Take a moment to review it thoroughly. It’s a small step that can save you from unexpected issues on the road.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Activate Chevy Equinox Remote Start?

To activate remote start, press the lock button on your key fob twice. Hold the remote start button for 3 seconds. The engine will start, and the vehicle will run for 10 minutes before shutting off automatically.

### Can I Use Remote Start In Cold Weather?

Yes, remote start is ideal for cold weather. It warms the engine and cabin before you enter. This feature helps defrost windows and improves comfort when you begin driving.

### How Far Is The Remote Start Range?

The remote start range varies but typically works within 300 feet. Obstacles like walls or other vehicles can reduce the effective distance. Ensure your key fob battery is charged for best performance.

### Is Remote Start Safe For My Chevy Equinox?

Yes, remote start has built-in safety features. The system won’t start if the vehicle is in gear. It also disables if the hood is open or the brake pedal is pressed.
