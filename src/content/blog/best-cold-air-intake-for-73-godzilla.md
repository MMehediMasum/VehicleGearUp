### How Does An Oiled Filter Improve 7.3l Diesel Intake?



Oiled filters trap more dirt and debris than dry filters. They enhance airflow while protecting the engine. This improves performance and extends engine life for the 7. 3L Powerstroke diesel.

### Are Aftermarket Cold Air Intakes Reliable For Daily Driving?
