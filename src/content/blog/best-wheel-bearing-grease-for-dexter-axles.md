## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Grease For Dexter Axle Wheel Bearings?



The best grease for Dexter axle wheel bearings is high-quality lithium or synthetic grease. It offers excellent water resistance, heat tolerance, and long-lasting lubrication, protecting bearings from wear and corrosion. Always choose grease compatible with Dexter axle specifications for optimal performance.



### How Do Trailer Axle Dust Caps Protect Wheel Bearings?



Trailer axle dust caps prevent dirt, water, and debris from entering the wheel bearings. This protection reduces contamination and corrosion risks, extending bearing life. Properly sealed dust caps maintain grease integrity and ensure smooth trailer axle operation.



### Can I Use Universal Grease Seals On Dexter Axles?



Yes, universal grease seals can fit Dexter axles if dimensions match. Ensure seals have compatible inner and outer diameters and spindle size. Using correct seals prevents grease leakage and protects bearings effectively.



### Why Choose Pre-greased Trailer Hub Kits For Dexter Axles?



Pre-greased trailer hub kits save time and effort during installation. They come with factory-applied grease, ensuring optimal lubrication and protection. These kits reduce maintenance and improve bearing lifespan on Dexter axles.



### How Often Should I Replace Grease Seals And Dust Caps?



Replace grease seals and dust caps during routine maintenance or if damaged. Signs include grease leakage, rust, or debris inside hubs. Regular replacement ensures bearings stay lubricated and free from contamination on Dexter axles.
