### Operating Instructions

* Park your car in the designated vacuum area. Make sure to leave enough space on both sides for easy access.
* Turn off your engine and ensure your car is in park. Safety first!
* Locate the vacuum handle and hose. They are usually positioned conveniently for both driver and passenger sides.
* Press the power button or lever to start the vacuum. Listen for the sound to confirm it's working.

Once you've got the vacuum running, you're ready to tackle the dirt and debris. Remember, it's all about making each move count.

### Tips For Effective Cleaning

* Begin with the seats and work your way down to the floor mats. This top-down approach prevents dirt from falling onto already cleaned areas.
* Use the crevice tool attachment for tight spots like between seats and under pedals. This ensures you reach every nook and cranny.
* Give special attention to high-traffic areas such as door panels and console spaces. These spots often collect the most grime.
* Empty personal items from your car before vacuuming. This allows you to clean uninterrupted and ensures nothing gets lost.
