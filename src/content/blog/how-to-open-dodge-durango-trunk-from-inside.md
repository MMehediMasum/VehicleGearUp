The trunk release is typically found near the rear seats. It is easy to spot once you know where to look. The lever or button is made visible and reachable for passengers inside the vehicle.

### Check The Rear Seat Area

Start by examining the back seat of your Dodge Durango. Look closely at the bottom edge or side panels. Some models have the release near the seat cushions or on the center console between seats.

Fold the rear seats down if you can. This action might reveal hidden release mechanisms. Also, check any small compartments or panels that could hide the release lever.

### Identify The Release Lever Or Button

The trunk release usually looks like a small lever or button. It might have a symbol of a car with an open trunk. The lever is often made of plastic and colored for easy spotting.

## Manual Release Options

### Accessing The Trunk With Tools

Some Dodge Durango models include a manual trunk release lever or cable inside the rear seat area. If not visible, tools like a flathead screwdriver can help access hidden compartments or panels. These tools allow you to reach the trunk latch mechanism.

Follow these steps to access the trunk manually:

* Fold down the rear seats to reach the trunk area from inside.
* Locate the small access panel or cover near the trunk latch.
* Use a flathead screwdriver to gently pry open the panel.
* Find the manual release lever or cable inside.
* Pull the lever or cable firmly to open the trunk.

Be careful when using tools to avoid damaging interior parts. Keep tools handy during road trips for quick access if needed.

### When To Use Manual Methods

Manual release is useful when the electronic trunk release fails. Dead car battery or electrical issues often disable power trunk opening. In such cases, manual methods provide a simple backup.

Other situations include:

* Trunk lock malfunction or jammed latch
* Lost or broken key fob
* Emergency situations requiring quick trunk access

Using manual release ensures you maintain control over your vehicle. It prevents being locked out of the trunk and keeps your belongings safe.
