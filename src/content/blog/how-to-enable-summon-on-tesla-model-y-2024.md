Summon needs a strong connection between your phone and car. Weak Bluetooth or Wi-Fi signals cause delays or failures. Check these points:

* Restart your phone and Tesla app if connection drops.
* Verify your Tesla app has permission to use Bluetooth and location services.
* Update your phone’s operating system and Tesla app for best compatibility.

### Feature Not Responding

Sometimes, Summon does not respond to commands. This may happen due to safety limits or settings. Try these fixes:

* Try closing and reopening the Tesla app to refresh commands.
