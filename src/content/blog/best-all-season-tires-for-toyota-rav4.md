## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best All-season Tires For Toyota Rav4?



The best all-season tires for Toyota RAV4 include Fullway PC369, Firestone All Season, and Forceum Penta. These tires offer excellent traction, durability, and performance for year-round driving on various road conditions. They fit the standard 225/65R17 size for RAV4 models.



### How Do Fullway Pc369 Tires Perform On Wet Roads?



Fullway PC369 tires provide reliable wet traction with their advanced tread design. They feature a UTQG rating of 380AA, ensuring good grip and safety on rainy or slippery roads. Their 4-ply construction enhances stability and durability for SUV driving needs.



### Are Forceum Penta Tires Suitable For Heavy Loads?



Yes, Forceum Penta tires have a Load Range XL and a load index of 106H. This means they support heavier weights, making them ideal for trucks and SUVs like the Toyota RAV4. They also offer excellent tread wear with a UTQG rating of 400AA.



### What Size Tires Fit Toyota Rav4 2015 And Newer?



Most Toyota RAV4 models from 2015 onward use 225/65R17 tires. Options like Firestone All Season, Sailun Atrezzo SH408, and Starfire Solarus AS fit this size perfectly. These tires balance comfort, performance, and all-season capability for RAV4 drivers.



### How Long Do All-season Tires Typically Last?



All-season tires like Accelera Phi and Fullway HP108 usually last between 40,000 and 60,000 miles. Their UTQG ratings, such as 400AA or 380AA, indicate good treadwear and traction. Proper maintenance and driving habits also extend tire lifespan.



### Are All-season Tires Noisy On Highways?



Most tires listed, including SURETRAC COMFORTRIDE and STARFIRE Solarus AS, are designed for quiet highway rides. Their tread patterns reduce road noise and vibration. This makes them comfortable for long drives in all weather conditions on your Toyota RAV4.
