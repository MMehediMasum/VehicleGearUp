## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Glue For Rc Car Tires?



The best glue for RC car tires should be strong, quick-drying, and waterproof. Products like Viomis Black Rubber Glue and Pro-Line Racing Pro-Bond offer durable bonding and heat resistance, ideal for tire repair and edge bonding on RC tires.



### How Do I Apply Rc Tire Glue Properly?



Clean the tire surface before applying glue. Use a thin, even layer of adhesive like TEAM LOSI RACING Tire Glue. Press the tire and rim firmly together and allow full curing time for a strong bond and long-lasting tire performance.



### Can Rubber Glue Withstand Heat And Water Exposure?



Yes, many rubber glues like Bottrony Instant Super Glue and Bob Smith Industries BSI-130 are heat-resistant and waterproof. These adhesives maintain strong bonding even under harsh conditions, making them ideal for RC tires and automotive parts.



### Which Glue Is Best For Diy Rc Tire Repairs?



Viomis 30g Black Rubber Glue and Y'allCanDiy Super Glue are perfect for DIY repairs. They dry quickly, bond rubber edges well, and are versatile for crafts, seals, and tire maintenance on RC vehicles.



### Is There A Difference Between Ca Glue And Rubber Adhesive For Rc Tires?



Yes, CA glue (cyanoacrylate) like TEAM LOSI RACING Tire Glue cures faster but can be brittle. Rubber adhesives like ClenyMagi Rubber Glue offer more flexibility and durability, making them better for high-stress tire repairs.
