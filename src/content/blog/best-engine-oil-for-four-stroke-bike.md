## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Synthetic Oil For Four-stroke Bikes?



Valvoline 4-Stroke Motorcycle Full Synthetic 10W-40 is excellent. It offers superior engine protection and smooth performance. It resists wear and deposits, ensuring long engine life and optimal power delivery for four-stroke bikes.



### Can I Use 10w-40 Oil For All Four-stroke Engines?



Yes, 10W-40 oil like Lucas Oil Synthetic SAE 10W-40 suits most four-stroke engines. It balances viscosity for cold starts and high temperatures, providing consistent lubrication and engine protection.



### How Often Should I Change Four-stroke Bike Oil?



Typically, change oil every 3,000 to 5,000 miles or as per manufacturer. Using quality oils like Honda Pro GN4 10W40 improves engine health between changes, reducing wear and maintaining performance.



### Is Synthetic Oil Better Than Conventional For Four-stroke Bikes?



Yes, synthetic oils like Valvoline Powersport Full Synthetic offer better wear protection and cleaner engines. They resist breakdown and perform well in extreme temperatures, enhancing engine longevity compared to conventional oils.



### Can I Use Small Engine Oil In Four-stroke Motorcycles?



Small engine oils such as STP Premium Small Engine 4 Cycle Oil are designed for lawn equipment. It’s best to use motorcycle-specific oils for four-stroke bikes to ensure proper additives and performance.



### What Oil Viscosity Is Ideal For Four-stroke Motorcycle Engines?



10W-30 and 10W-40 viscosities are commonly ideal, like Honda Pro GN4 10W30 or Kawasaki 10W40. These grades provide balanced flow at startup and high temperature, protecting engine parts effectively.
