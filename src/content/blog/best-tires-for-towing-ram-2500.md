### Can I Use These Airbags On Both 2wd And 4wd Ram 2500?



Yes, most air suspension kits such as FLOVENTIS and TORQUE fit both 2WD and 4WD Ram 2500 models. This versatility makes them suitable for various driving conditions and towing needs.
