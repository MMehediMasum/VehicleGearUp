## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Cold Air Intake For 5.7 Hemi Ram 1500?



The Rough Country Cold Air Intake series is highly recommended for the 5. 7 Hemi Ram 1500. It offers improved airflow, durability, and fits models from 2009 to 2024. These intakes enhance horsepower and torque while maintaining OEM quality and ease of installation.



### How Does A Cold Air Intake Improve Ram 1500 Performance?



A cold air intake increases airflow to the engine, providing cooler, denser air. This improves combustion efficiency, resulting in better horsepower and torque. It also enhances throttle response and fuel economy for the 5. 7 Hemi Ram 1500.



### Are Rough Country Cold Air Intakes Compatible With All Ram 1500 Models?



Rough Country intakes fit most 5. 7L Ram 1500 models from 2009 to 2024, including Classic versions. Always verify your truck’s model year and engine type to ensure compatibility before purchasing.



### Does Installing A Cold Air Intake Affect Vehicle Warranty?



Installing a cold air intake generally does not void your Ram 1500’s warranty. However, if damage occurs related to the intake, warranty coverage may be affected. It’s best to check with your dealer beforehand.



### What Maintenance Is Required For A Cold Air Intake System?



Cold air intakes require periodic cleaning of the air filter to maintain performance. Use manufacturer-recommended cleaning kits and follow instructions for best results. Proper maintenance ensures longevity and consistent engine efficiency.



### Can I Install A Cold Air Intake Myself On A Ram 1500?



Yes, most cold air intake kits for the 5. 7 Hemi Ram 1500 come with instructions and necessary hardware. Basic mechanical skills and common tools are sufficient for a DIY installation. Always follow safety guidelines.
