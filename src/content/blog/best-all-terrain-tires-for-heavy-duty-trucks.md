## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes These All-terrain Tires Ideal For Heavy-duty Trucks?



These tires feature 6-ply or higher construction for durability. They resist punctures and handle rough terrain like mud, sand, and trails. Heavy-duty load ratings ensure safe performance under truck weight and harsh conditions, making them reliable for demanding off-road use.



### How Do Tubeless Tires Benefit Atv And Utv Users?



Tubeless tires reduce air leakage and improve puncture resistance. They are easier to repair and maintain in remote areas. This design enhances safety by minimizing sudden air loss, providing better traction and control on uneven, rocky, or muddy surfaces.



### Are These Tires Suitable For Different Terrains Like Mud And Sand?



Yes, these tires have aggressive tread patterns optimized for mud, sand, and rocky trails. They provide excellent traction and self-cleaning capabilities. Their reinforced sidewalls prevent damage, ensuring consistent performance across various off-road environments.



### What Size Options Are Available For These All-terrain Tires?



Sizes include 25x8-12, 25x10-12, and 22x10-10 for ATV/UTV applications, plus LT285/70/17 for trucks and SUVs. Multiple size options ensure compatibility with different vehicle models and wheel configurations.



### Can These Tires Handle Heavy Loads And Rough Conditions?



Absolutely, most tires have a 6-ply or 10-ply rating with high load ranges. This design supports heavy vehicles and rough off-road terrain without compromising durability or safety.



### How Do Offset And Pcd Specifications Affect Tire Fitment?



Proper offset and PCD (Pitch Circle Diameter) ensure wheels mount securely and align correctly. The sets listed have offsets like 3. 5+2. 5 and PCDs such as 4x136 or 4x156. Matching these prevents fitment issues and maintains vehicle handling.
