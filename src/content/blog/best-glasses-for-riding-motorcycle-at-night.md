## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Night Vision Motorcycle Glasses?



Night vision glasses reduce glare and enhance visibility during night rides. They improve contrast and protect your eyes from UV rays. This leads to safer and more comfortable motorcycle riding at night.



### How Do Yellow Tinted Lenses Help Motorcycle Riders At Night?



Yellow lenses filter out blue light and glare from oncoming headlights. They increase contrast and depth perception, making it easier to see road details. This reduces eye strain during night rides.



### Are Polarized Lenses Necessary For Night Motorcycle Riding?



Polarized lenses reduce reflections and glare from wet roads and headlights. While useful, not all night riding glasses are polarized. Choose based on your riding conditions and personal comfort.



### Can I Wear Night Vision Glasses Over My Prescription Glasses?



Yes, some models like URUMQI fit comfortably over prescription glasses. They offer anti-glare and UV protection without compromising your vision. Check for size and fit before purchasing.



### Which Material Is Best For Lightweight Night Riding Glasses?



TR90 frames are lightweight, flexible, and durable. They provide comfort during long rides and resist damage from impacts. Many night vision glasses use TR90 for enhanced rider comfort.



### Do Night Driving Glasses Protect Against Uv Rays?



Most quality night vision glasses include UV400 protection. This blocks harmful UVA and UVB rays even during low light conditions. Protecting eyes from UV damage is essential for all riders.
