## Requirements For Remote Start

Remote starting your 2017 Honda Civic requires specific conditions. Not every model supports this feature. The key fob must have remote start functions. The battery and signal strength also play a role. Understanding these points helps avoid frustration.

### Compatible Honda Civic Models

Only certain 2017 Honda Civics come with remote start capability. Usually, higher trims like EX, EX-L, and Touring include it. Base models often lack this feature. Check your owner’s manual or dealer to confirm compatibility.

### Key Fob Features

Your key fob must support remote start functions. Look for a button with a circular arrow or "remote start" icon. Basic key fobs may not have this option. A smart key fob usually includes remote start buttons.

### Battery And Signal Range

The key fob’s battery must be strong. Weak batteries can prevent remote start from working. Replace the battery regularly for best results. Signal range varies but is typically about 100-200 feet. Obstacles like walls or cars can reduce the signal distance.

## Preparing Your Vehicle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparing-your-2017-honda-civic-for-remote-start-is-crucial-for-a-smooth-and-safe-experience-you-want-to-make-sure-the-car-is-in-the-right-condition-before-you-send-the-signal-to-start-the-engine&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparing your 2017 Honda Civic for remote start is crucial for a smooth and safe experience. You want to make sure the car is in the right condition before you send the signal to start the engine. Skipping these preparation steps can lead to the remote start not working or, worse, safety hazards.

### Ensure Doors And Hood Are Closed

Before you attempt a remote start, double-check that all doors and the hood are fully closed. The vehicle’s system is designed to prevent remote starting if it detects any open entry points. This safety feature protects your Civic and prevents the engine from starting unintentionally.

Have you ever tried to remote start your car only to find it won’t start because a door was slightly ajar? A quick walk-around can save you that hassle. It’s a simple step that ensures your car is secure and ready.

### Check Transmission Position

Your Honda Civic must be in the 'Park' position for the remote start to activate. The system won’t engage if the transmission is in 'Drive' or 'Neutral'. This is a critical safety measure to prevent the car from moving unexpectedly.

Think about this: If your car could start remotely while in gear, what could go wrong? Always verify the gear position on your dashboard before using remote start.

### Activate Security Settings

Make sure your Civic’s security system is fully active. Some models require the alarm or immobilizer to be armed before remote start will function. If your security settings aren’t properly enabled, the remote start feature might be disabled.

Take a moment to review your vehicle’s manual on security settings or check your key fob indicators. Setting these correctly helps you avoid frustration and keeps your car protected.

## Using The Remote Start Function

### Locate The Remote Start Button

The first step in using the remote start function is identifying the right button on your key fob. Look for a circular arrow symbol, which represents the remote start. It's often located alongside other buttons like lock and unlock.

Make sure your fob is within range of your vehicle. Typically, this means being no more than 100 feet away. Check your surroundings to ensure nothing is obstructing the signal.

### Step-by-step Activation

* Press the lock button on your key fob once. This ensures your vehicle is secure before starting the engine.
* Immediately press and hold the remote start button (circular arrow) for a few seconds. You'll notice the lights on your Civic flash, indicating the start sequence is initiated.
* Release the button once the engine starts. You should hear the engine roar to life, signaling a successful start.

If it doesn’t work on the first try, ensure your doors are locked and try again. Timing is key—pressing the buttons in quick succession usually does the trick.

### Confirming Engine Start

Once the engine is running, the parking lights will remain on. This visual cue confirms your car is ready to go.

Head to your car within the programmed time limit, usually 10 minutes, to avoid the engine shutting off automatically. The system is designed this way to conserve fuel and prevent emissions.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-your-2017-honda-civics-remote-start-can-save-you-time-and-frustration-many-problems-are-simple-to-fix-once-you-know-what-to-look-for&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with your 2017 Honda Civic's remote start can save you time and frustration. Many problems are simple to fix once you know what to look for. Let’s walk through some frequent challenges and how you can address them effectively.

### Remote Not Responding

If your remote doesn’t respond, the first thing to check is the battery. A weak or dead battery often causes unresponsiveness. Try replacing it with a fresh one and see if the remote starts working again.

Also, confirm you are within the proper range. The remote start system typically works only within a limited distance. If you’re too far from your car, the signal won’t reach.

Sometimes, interference from other electronic devices or strong radio signals can block communication. Try moving to a different location and test the remote again. Have you noticed any places where it works better?

### Engine Fails To Start

Your remote may signal the car, but the engine doesn’t start. This can happen if the car is not in 'Park' or the hood is open. Always check these safety features before attempting remote start.

Another reason could be a faulty key fob or security system issue. The immobilizer might be preventing the engine from turning over. If you suspect this, a quick reset by locking and unlocking the car manually can help.

Is your fuel level too low? Some vehicles disable remote start when fuel is critically low to prevent damage. Checking the fuel gauge might reveal the problem.

### Battery Replacement Tips

Replacing the battery in your remote is simple but requires attention. Use the exact battery type recommended in your owner’s manual, usually a CR2032 lithium coin cell.

Before opening the remote, clean your hands to avoid transferring dirt or moisture inside. Use a small flat screwdriver or coin to gently pry open the case without damaging the seals.

After replacing the battery, test the remote immediately to ensure the new battery works. If it still doesn’t respond, check for corrosion or damaged contacts inside the remote. Keeping a spare battery handy can save you from unexpected failures.

## Safety Tips For Remote Starting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=remote-starting-your-2017-honda-civic-is-convenient-and-saves-time-safety-must-come-first-to-avoid-accidents-or-damage&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Remote starting your 2017 Honda Civic is convenient and saves time. Safety must come first to avoid accidents or damage. Follow these safety tips to ensure your remote start works smoothly and securely.

### Avoid Enclosed Spaces

Never use remote start inside a garage or enclosed area. Exhaust fumes can build up quickly and cause harm. Always start the car outdoors or in open spaces to keep air fresh.

### Monitor Vehicle Status

Keep an eye on your car’s condition after remote starting. Check if the engine is running smoothly and the doors are locked. Watch for warning lights or unusual sounds that need attention.

### Emergency Shut-off Procedures

Know how to stop the engine remotely in an emergency. Most key fobs have a button to turn off the car immediately. Practice this to react quickly if any problem arises during remote start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Remote Start A 2017 Honda Civic?

To remote start a 2017 Honda Civic, press the lock button on your key fob twice, then press and hold the remote start button until the engine starts. This activates the remote start feature, allowing the car to run without being inside.

### Is Remote Start Available On All 2017 Honda Civic Models?

No, remote start is only available on select trims of the 2017 Honda Civic, typically EX and higher models. Check your owner’s manual or vehicle specifications to confirm if your model supports remote start.

### Can I Use Remote Start Without Unlocking The Doors?

Yes, remote start allows you to start the engine without unlocking the doors. The vehicle remains locked for security until you physically unlock it or enter with the key fob.

### How Far Can I Remote Start My 2017 Honda Civic?

The remote start range depends on the key fob and environment, usually around 200 to 300 feet. Obstacles like walls or interference can reduce this range, so use it within a reasonable distance for best results.
