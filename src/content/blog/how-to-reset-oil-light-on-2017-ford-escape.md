**You just changed the oil in your 2017 Ford Escape, but that stubborn oil light on your dashboard won’t turn off. It’s frustrating, right?**

If you don’t reset the oil light, it can keep bothering you every time you start your car. The good news is, resetting it is easier than you think—and you can do it yourself in just a few simple steps.

Keep reading, and you’ll learn exactly how to reset the oil light on your 2017 Ford Escape so you can drive with confidence and peace of mind.




## Locate The Oil Reset Button

Locating the oil reset button on your 2017 Ford Escape is the first step to successfully resetting the oil light. This button is essential because it lets your vehicle know the oil change has been completed, helping the system track maintenance accurately.

Finding the button might seem tricky at first, but it’s usually straightforward once you know where to look. The oil reset button isn’t a standalone button labeled “oil reset” but part of the trip or information display controls.

### Check The Steering Wheel Controls

Many Ford Escape models have the oil reset function integrated into the steering wheel controls. Look for arrow buttons or an “OK” button on the right side of your steering wheel. These buttons help you navigate the dashboard menu where you can reset the oil light.

### Inspect The Dashboard Display Panel

Your dashboard display panel is where most reset functions happen. Turn your ignition to the “On” position without starting the engine, then use the steering wheel buttons to scroll through the menu. Look for “Settings” or “Vehicle Information” where the oil life or maintenance section appears.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual

If you’re unsure where the reset button is, your owner’s manual is the best guide. It provides specific instructions and diagrams showing the exact location and how to use the controls. This saves time and prevents any guesswork.

### Use The Trip Reset Button If Needed

Some 2017 Ford Escape models use the trip reset button located near the instrument cluster as the oil reset button. Pressing and holding this button while the ignition is on often triggers the reset. If this works, it’s a quick and easy way to reset the oil light without navigating menus.




## Turn On The Ignition

Turning on the ignition is the first and crucial step to reset the oil light on your 2017 Ford Escape. This step wakes up your car's electrical system without starting the engine, allowing you to access the dashboard settings.

Have you ever wondered why the oil light stays on even after an oil change? Often, it’s because the system needs a manual reset, which starts by simply turning the ignition on without firing up the engine.

### Insert The Key Or Press The Start Button

If your Escape uses a traditional key, insert it into the ignition and turn it to the “On” position. This position is just before the engine starts.

For models with a push-button start, press the button once without pressing the brake pedal. This action powers the dashboard but keeps the engine off.

### Wait For The Dashboard Lights To Activate

Once the ignition is on, watch your dashboard closely. All the warning lights, including the oil light, will illuminate briefly as the system runs its checks.

This moment is important because it lets your car’s computer system prepare for the reset process.

### Do Not Start The Engine Yet

It might be tempting to start the engine immediately, but hold off for now. Starting the engine before resetting can interrupt the process.

Keep the ignition on long enough to ensure the car’s systems are fully awake—usually around 10 seconds.

## Reset The Oil Life

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-life-on-your-2017-ford-escape-keeps-the-maintenance-system-accurate-it-helps-the-car-track-oil-changes-correctly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil life on your 2017 Ford Escape keeps the maintenance system accurate. It helps the car track oil changes correctly. This process clears the old data and starts fresh. This way, the oil life monitor shows the right time for the next oil change.

### Press And Hold The Reset Button

Start by turning the ignition to the "On" position. Do not start the engine. Locate the reset button on the instrument panel. Press and hold this button. Keep holding it until the oil life indicator starts flashing or resets to 100%. This usually takes about 10 seconds.

### Confirm The Reset

Release the reset button once the oil life resets. Check the display to confirm it shows 100%. Turn off the ignition. Turn the car back on to verify the reset holds. The oil life monitor should now read full, signaling a successful reset.

  


## Verify The Oil Light Reset

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=verifying-the-oil-light-reset-on-a-2017-ford-escape-is-crucial-after-performing-maintenance-this-step-confirms-the-oil-change-reminder-system-is-properly-updated&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Verifying the oil light reset on a 2017 Ford Escape is crucial after performing maintenance. This step confirms the oil change reminder system is properly updated. It prevents unnecessary alerts and ensures the vehicle monitors oil life accurately.

Check the dashboard display to see if the oil light is off. If the light remains on, the reset may not have been completed correctly. Follow these checks to confirm the reset status.

### Check The Instrument Cluster

Turn the ignition to the "On" position without starting the engine. Look at the instrument cluster for the oil light. The oil light should stay off if the reset was successful.

### Use The Info Button On The Steering Wheel

Press the information button repeatedly until the oil life percentage appears. A reading of 100% indicates a successful reset. If the percentage is lower, the reset might still be pending.

### Start The Engine And Observe

Start the engine and watch the dashboard for the oil light. It should illuminate briefly and then turn off. Continuous illumination means the reset did not complete.

### Drive A Short Distance

Sometimes the system needs a short drive to update. Drive the vehicle for about 5 minutes. Check the oil light again to ensure it stays off after driving.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual

Refer to the Ford Escape owner’s manual for specific instructions. Manuals often provide troubleshooting tips if the oil light does not reset. Follow any additional guidance provided.




## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-light-on-a-2017-ford-escape-is-usually-simple-sometimes-issues-arise-that-need-extra-care&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil light on a 2017 Ford Escape is usually simple. Sometimes, issues arise that need extra care. Troubleshooting these problems helps avoid confusion and keeps your car running well. Below are common problems and ways to fix them.

### Oil Light Stays On

The oil light may stay on even after an oil change. This can signal a sensor problem or low oil pressure. Check the oil level with the dipstick first. Add oil if it is low.

If oil levels are fine, the sensor might be faulty. Dirty sensors or wiring issues can cause false warnings. Clean the sensor area and inspect the wires for damage. Replace the sensor if needed.

Another cause is a clogged oil filter. A blocked filter can reduce oil flow and trigger the light. Changing the filter often solves this issue.

### Reset Button Not Responding

Sometimes, the reset button does not work. This may happen due to a weak battery or software glitches. Try restarting the car and attempting the reset again.

Check the dashboard controls carefully. The reset button might require holding down for a few seconds. Follow the exact timing to reset the oil light.

If the button still won’t respond, inspect the fuse box. A blown fuse can stop the reset function. Replace any blown fuses with the correct type.

Software updates or system resets at a mechanic can fix persistent problems. Your vehicle’s manual provides specific reset instructions too.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Oil Light On A 2017 Ford Escape?

To reset the oil light, turn ignition to ON without starting. Press the brake and gas pedals simultaneously for 10 seconds. The oil light will blink and then turn off, indicating a successful reset.

### Why Won’t My 2017 Ford Escape Oil Light Reset?

The oil light may not reset if the oil change isn’t done. Ensure the oil level is correct and the engine is off before resetting. Follow the proper reset procedure to clear the light.

### How Often Should I Reset The Oil Light On Ford Escape?

Reset the oil light after every oil change, typically every 5,000 to 7,500 miles. This helps maintain accurate maintenance reminders and keeps your engine running smoothly.

### Can I Reset Oil Light Without A Scanner Tool?

Yes, you can reset the oil light manually using the pedal and ignition method. No special tools are required for a 2017 Ford Escape reset process.
