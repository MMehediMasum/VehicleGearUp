## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Jack Type For Tesla Model 3?



The best jack type for Tesla Model 3 is a 2. 5 to 3-ton scissor jack. It offers stable lifting and fits Tesla’s low ground clearance. Many kits come with Tesla-specific jack pads to protect the battery and frame during lifting.



### Can I Use A Universal Car Jack For Tesla Model 3?



Yes, you can use a universal car jack for Tesla Model 3 if it supports 2. 5 tons or more. Tesla-specific jack pads are recommended to avoid damage. Many universal kits include these pads along with lug wrenches and ratchet tools.



### How Do Tesla Jack Pads Protect My Model 3?



Tesla jack pads distribute weight evenly and prevent damage to the battery and frame. They fit perfectly into designated Tesla lift points. Using these pads with a scissor jack ensures safe lifting and protects your car from scratches or dents.



### What Tools Come With Tesla Model 3 Jack Kits?



Tesla Model 3 jack kits typically include a scissor jack, two jack pads, a lug wrench, and ratchet wrenches. Some kits also feature socket drill adapters and storage bags for convenience. These tools simplify tire changing and roadside repairs.



### Are Scissor Jacks Safe For Tesla Model 3 Lifting?



Scissor jacks rated 2. 5 to 3 tons are safe for lifting Tesla Model 3 when used with jack pads. They provide stable support and proper height clearance. Always follow Tesla’s lifting guidelines to ensure safety during maintenance or tire changes.
