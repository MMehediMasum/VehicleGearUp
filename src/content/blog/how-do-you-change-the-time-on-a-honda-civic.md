**Changing the time on your Honda Civic might seem tricky at first, but it’s actually a quick and simple task. If you’ve ever found yourself staring at the clock, wondering how to adjust it, you’re not alone.**

Getting the correct time displayed can make a big difference in your daily drive, helping you stay punctual and stress-free. In this guide, you’ll discover easy steps to set your Honda Civic’s clock right, so you never have to guess the time while on the road.

## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls on your Honda Civic is the first step to adjusting the time accurately. Knowing where these controls are can save you time and frustration, especially if you’ve just changed time zones or switched to daylight saving time.

Many drivers overlook how simple it is to find the clock settings once you know where to look. Have you ever spent more time fiddling with buttons than actually driving? Let’s make sure that doesn’t happen.

### Check The Dashboard Display

The clock is usually displayed prominently on the dashboard, often near the center console or above the infotainment screen. Look closely at your dashboard—does the time appear on a small digital screen or integrated into a larger display?

In some Honda Civic models, the time shows up on the touchscreen infotainment system. In others, it’s a dedicated digital clock with its own small screen. Your dashboard’s layout can hint at how to find the clock controls.

### Identify Clock Buttons

Once you spot the clock display, check for buttons labeled “Clock,” “H,” “M,” or symbols like a small clock icon nearby. These buttons usually allow you to set hours and minutes manually.

Some models use a combination of buttons or a menu within the infotainment system to adjust the clock. If you see buttons like “Settings” or “Menu,” pressing these might reveal clock adjustment options.

Have you noticed any unlabeled buttons close to the clock display? Sometimes, a quick press or holding down a button activates the clock setting mode, which can be a handy shortcut.




## Turn On The Ignition

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-on-the-ignition-is-the-first-action-to-change-the-time-on-your-honda-civic-without-power-the-clock-settings-remain-locked&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning on the ignition is the first action to change the time on your Honda Civic. Without power, the clock settings remain locked. Activating the ignition supplies electricity to the car's systems, including the clock. This step prepares your car to accept new time adjustments.

### Set The Car To Accessory Mode

Turn the key in the ignition to the accessory position. This mode powers the radio and clock but keeps the engine off. Look for a click or a light on the dashboard to confirm. Accessory mode is perfect for adjusting settings without running the engine.

### Ensure Power To The Clock

Check that the clock display is lit up. If the clock is dark, the car might not be in the correct ignition mode. Without power, time adjustments will not save. Make sure the ignition is at least in accessory mode to keep the clock active during changes.

## Adjust The Time Settings

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-time-settings-on-your-honda-civic-is-a-straightforward-task-that-ensures-your-clock-always-shows-the-correct-time-whether-youve-experienced-a-time-change-or-need-to-reset-after-battery-replacement-knowing-how-to-tweak-these-settings-helps-you-avoid-confusion-during-your-drives&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the time settings on your Honda Civic is a straightforward task that ensures your clock always shows the correct time. Whether you’ve experienced a time change or need to reset after battery replacement, knowing how to tweak these settings helps you avoid confusion during your drives. Let’s break down the simple steps to get your clock accurately set.

### Press The Clock Or Setup Button

Start by locating the **Clock**or **Setup**button on your dashboard or infotainment system. This button is usually near the radio controls or touchscreen interface. Pressing it will activate the clock adjustment mode, allowing you to make changes.

Sometimes, you might need to hold the button for a few seconds until the time starts blinking. This blinking indicates the clock is ready for changes. If you don’t see the option immediately, try checking your owner’s manual as button placement can vary by model year.

### Use The Hour And Minute Buttons

Once in clock adjustment mode, look for the buttons labeled **Hour**and **Minute**. These let you change the time one unit at a time. Press the *Hour*button repeatedly until the display shows the correct hour.

Similarly, press the *Minute*button until you reach the correct minute. Some models might use a dial or touchscreen arrows instead, but the process remains the same. It’s easy to get carried away, so pause occasionally to check if the time displayed matches your desired setting.

### Set Am Or Pm If Applicable

Depending on your Honda Civic’s clock system, you may need to specify whether the time is AM or PM. This is crucial for avoiding confusion, especially if your clock uses a 12-hour format. Look for an indicator or a button that toggles between AM and PM.

If your display doesn’t show AM or PM, it might use a 24-hour format instead. In that case, just make sure the hour is set correctly in military time. Adjusting this correctly ensures your clock aligns with your daily schedule without surprises.




## Save The New Time

Changing the time on your Honda Civic is a straightforward process, but ensuring that the new time is saved correctly is crucial to avoid repeating the task. Once you've adjusted the time, your next step is to make sure the changes are stored. Saving the new time correctly means you can confidently look at your dashboard without second-guessing the clock.

### Confirm The Changes

After setting the new time, it's essential to confirm your adjustments. Double-check the display to ensure the hours and minutes reflect the desired setting. This simple check prevents any small errors that might lead to big confusions later on.

Think about how easy it is to overlook a minor mistake, especially when you’re in a rush. Taking a moment to verify ensures that your time settings are accurate. This simple habit can save you from future frustrations, like arriving late to an appointment.

### Exit The Clock Settings Menu

Once you've confirmed the new time, it's time to exit the clock settings menu. This step locks in your changes and returns your display to its regular state. Look for an "Exit" or "Back" button, which is usually the same button you used to enter the settings.

Don't forget to check if your changes reflect on the main screen after exiting. If the new time displays correctly, you've successfully updated your Honda Civic's clock. It’s a satisfying feeling knowing your car's time is set accurately, ready to guide you through your day.

  


## Tips For Different Honda Civic Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-time-on-your-honda-civic-varies-depending-on-the-model-year-and-the-type-of-system-it-uses-understanding-these-differences-can-save-you-time-and-frustration&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the time on your Honda Civic varies depending on the model year and the type of system it uses. Understanding these differences can save you time and frustration. Here’s how to handle the clock settings across different Civic generations and systems.

### Older Model Variations

Older Honda Civics usually have manual knobs or physical buttons for setting the clock. You’ll often find a small button labeled “Clock” or “H” and “M” near the radio or dashboard. Press and hold the “Clock” button until the time starts blinking, then use the “H” button to change hours and the “M” button to adjust minutes.

Sometimes, the process can be a bit tricky because some models require you to press and hold the button for a few seconds before the time blinks. If your car has a separate stereo system, the clock settings might be controlled directly through the radio interface. Have you ever struggled to find the right button only to realize it was hidden behind the radio display?

### Newer Model Touchscreen Systems

Newer Honda Civics come equipped with touchscreen displays, which change the way you interact with the clock settings. Instead of physical buttons, you’ll navigate through the settings menu on the touchscreen. Typically, you tap the “Settings” icon, then select “System,” followed by “Clock” or “Time & Date.”

Once there, you can manually adjust the hours and minutes or switch to automatic time sync if your model supports GPS or network time updates. This feature keeps your clock accurate without any manual input. Have you checked if your Civic’s clock updates automatically after daylight saving changes?

### Using The Infotainment System

The infotainment system integrates audio, navigation, and vehicle settings, including the clock. Accessing the time settings here might differ slightly depending on your Civic’s year and model. Usually, you’ll find the clock settings under the “Setup” or “Settings” menu, then “Clock” or “Time.”

Some infotainment systems allow you to set the time zone and daylight saving rules, which is handy if you travel frequently. It’s worth exploring these options to ensure your clock stays accurate without constant manual adjustments. Did you know some infotainment systems even let you display the time in different formats like 12-hour or 24-hour?

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-time-on-a-honda-civic-is-usually-simple-but-sometimes-issues-happen&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the time on a Honda Civic is usually simple. But sometimes, issues happen. Troubleshooting helps fix common problems quickly. Here are some frequent issues and easy tips to solve them.

### Clock Not Responding

The clock may not change when you try to adjust it. This can happen if the buttons are sticky or the system is frozen. Try pressing each button firmly. If nothing works, restart the car’s audio system by turning it off and on. Sometimes, a system reset clears small glitches.

### Time Reset After Battery Change

When you replace the car battery, the clock often resets to 12:00. This is normal. You need to set the time again manually. Keep the owner's manual handy. It shows the exact steps for your model. Avoid disconnecting the battery unless necessary to prevent this reset.

### When To Consult The Manual

Each Honda Civic model has slight differences. The manual has detailed instructions and diagrams. If the clock still won’t set or the system acts strange, check the manual. It also explains warning lights and error codes related to the clock. Use it as a trusted guide for any tricky problems.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Clock On A Honda Civic?

To change the clock, press and hold the clock or "H" button until the hour blinks. Use "H" for hours and "M" for minutes. Release the button once the correct time is set.

### Can I Update The Time On My Honda Civic Touchscreen?

Yes, access the settings menu on your Civic's touchscreen. Select "Clock," then adjust hours and minutes. Confirm the changes to update the time easily.

### Why Won’t My Honda Civic Clock Change?

If the clock doesn't change, the battery may be low or disconnected. Check for blown fuses or reset the system by turning off the car and restarting it.

### How To Sync Honda Civic Clock With Gps Time?

In newer models, go to the settings menu, select "Clock," then choose "Automatic" or "Sync with GPS. " This keeps your clock accurate using satellite time data.
