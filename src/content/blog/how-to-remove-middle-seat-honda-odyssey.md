## Tools Needed

A socket wrench set is essential to remove bolts holding the seat. Different bolt sizes may be used, so a set with multiple sockets is best. It helps loosen and tighten bolts quickly.

A flathead screwdriver helps pry up clips or covers hiding bolts. It also assists in removing trim pieces without damage. Use it carefully to avoid scratching surfaces.

Some Honda Odyssey models use Torx screws. A Torx driver fits these special screws. Check the screw type before starting to ensure you have the correct size.

Wear work gloves to protect your hands from sharp edges. Gloves improve grip on tools and parts. They keep your hands clean and safe during the removal process.

A flashlight or work light helps you see under the seats. Good lighting makes it easier to find bolts and connectors. It reduces mistakes and speeds up the work.

## Remove The Seat Back

### Identify Mounting Points

Look closely at the seat back. Find the points where it attaches to the seat base. These are usually bolts or clips. Check both sides and bottom of the seat back. Use a flashlight if needed to see clearly.

* Locate bolts near the hinges or brackets.
* Spot any plastic covers hiding fasteners.
* Note if clips or hooks hold the seat back in place.

### Detach The Seat Back

Start by removing any covers over the mounting points. Use a flathead screwdriver carefully to pry covers off. Use a wrench or socket set to loosen bolts. Turn bolts counterclockwise until they come free.

If clips hold the seat back, press or pull them gently. Avoid forcing the seat back off. It should come away smoothly once all mounts are undone.

Have a helper support the seat back while detaching. This prevents it from falling or scratching the interior. Lift the seat back straight up or away from the base. Set it aside safely to avoid damage.
