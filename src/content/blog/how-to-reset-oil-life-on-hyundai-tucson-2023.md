## Select Oil Life Reset Option

After completing your oil change, resetting the oil life indicator is essential. Selecting the oil life reset option clears the old data. Choosing the correct reset option is simple. The display guides you through the steps clearly. This ensures the oil life monitor starts fresh and provides reliable maintenance reminders.

### Selecting The Reset Option On The Dashboard

Turn the ignition to the "On" position without starting the engine. Use the steering wheel controls to navigate the menu. Find the “User Settings” or “Vehicle Settings” section. Scroll to the “Oil Life” or “Oil Change” option. Press the confirm button to select it.

### Confirming The Oil Life Reset

Once the oil life option is selected, a prompt will appear. This asks if you want to reset the oil life. Choose “Yes” or “Reset” to confirm. The display will show 100% oil life after reset. This confirms the reset was successful.

### Using The Touchscreen Display

Some Hyundai Tucson models have a touchscreen interface. Access the main menu from the home screen. Tap on “Settings,” then “Maintenance.” Look for the “Oil Life Reset” option. Tap it and confirm to reset the oil life meter.

### Resetting Oil Life Via Instrument Cluster

Press the trip button on the instrument cluster until the oil life display appears. Hold the trip button for a few seconds. The display will start flashing or show a reset prompt. Release and press the button again to reset the oil life.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Troubleshooting common issues while resetting the oil life on your Hyundai Tucson 2023 can save you time and prevent unnecessary trips to the mechanic. Sometimes, the reset process might not work as expected, or the oil change indicator may still be on even after resetting. Understanding why these problems occur helps you fix them quickly and keep your vehicle's maintenance on track.

### Reset Not Working

If the oil life reset doesn't seem to work, first check if you followed the reset steps correctly. Make sure the ignition is in the "On" position but the engine is off. Sometimes, missing this detail stops the system from registering the reset.

Another cause could be a software glitch in the vehicle's onboard computer. Try turning the car off completely, waiting a few minutes, and then attempting the reset again. If that fails, disconnecting the battery for a short period can sometimes clear minor electronic issues.

Remember, some Hyundai Tucson models require you to press and hold the reset button for several seconds. If you let go too soon, the reset won't trigger. Double-check your owner's manual to confirm the exact procedure for your model.

### Indicator Still On

After resetting, if the oil change indicator remains on, it might mean the system hasn’t registered the reset command. Sometimes, the vehicle needs to be driven for a short distance to update the display.

Check the oil level and quality yourself. If the oil is low or dirty, the sensor might keep the indicator lit as a warning. Addressing the actual oil condition often clears the alert once the reset is performed.

If the indicator persists despite a successful reset and good oil condition, there could be a sensor malfunction. In such cases, scanning the car’s computer for error codes at a service center can identify the problem.
