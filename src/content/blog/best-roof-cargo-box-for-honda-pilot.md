The SportRack Vista XL offers spacious rear-opening access and fits Honda Pilot perfectly. It’s durable, weatherproof, and easy to install. This box maximizes storage without compromising vehicle aerodynamics.



### Can I Use A Soft Roof Bag Without A Roof Rack?



Yes, models like the MeeFar and Sailnovo roof bags fit cars with or without racks. They include anti-slip mats, reinforced straps, and door hooks for secure attachment.



### How Much Cargo Space Do These Roof Boxes Provide?



Cargo capacity ranges from 11 to 21 cubic feet depending on the model. For example, the Auto Dynasty hard shell box offers 11 cubic feet, while the SportRack Vista XL provides 21 cubic feet.



### Are These Roof Cargo Boxes Waterproof?



Most options, including the MIDABAO and FIVKLEMNZ bags, feature waterproof materials and sealed zippers. This ensures your belongings stay dry in rain or snow.



### How Secure Are These Roof Cargo Boxes?



Many models come with lockable features and reinforced straps. The DNA MOTORING and SportRack boxes include security keys and luggage locks for added protection.



### Is Installation Difficult On A Honda Pilot?



Installation is generally tool-free and straightforward. Soft bags use straps and hooks, while hard shells like the Auto Dynasty model require mounting on roof rails, which Honda Pilot supports.
