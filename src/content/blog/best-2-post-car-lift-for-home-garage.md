## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Weight Capacity Of A 2 Post Car Lift?



Most 2 post car lifts for home garages support between 7,000 to 12,000 lbs. Choose a lift matching your vehicle’s weight for safety and efficiency.



### Are 2 Post Lifts Suitable For Home Garage Use?



Yes, 2 post lifts are ideal for home garages. They save space, provide easy vehicle access, and handle most personal vehicles and light trucks.



### How Does Single Point Lock Release Work On These Lifts?



Single point lock release allows unlocking both posts simultaneously. It enhances safety and convenience during vehicle lifting and lowering operations.



### What Power Requirements Do These 2 Post Lifts Need?



Most 2 post lifts require a 220V power supply with a motor ranging from 3. 0 to 4. 0 HP. Verify your garage's electrical setup before installation.



### Can 2 Post Lifts Handle Trucks And Suvs?



Yes, models with 10,000 to 12,000 lbs capacity can safely lift trucks and SUVs. Always confirm the lift’s rated capacity matches your vehicle’s weight.



### What Are The Benefits Of A Clearfloor 2 Post Lift?



Clearfloor lifts keep the floor space unobstructed, allowing easier movement and storage. They are ideal for tight home garages and professional workshops.
