Syncing the clock on your Mazda CX-5 with GPS ensures your time is always accurate. The system uses satellite signals to set the correct time automatically. This feature saves you the hassle of manual adjustments.

### Enable Automatic Time Update

Start by turning on the automatic time update feature in your vehicle’s settings. This option allows the car to receive time data directly from GPS satellites. Follow these steps:

* Access the main menu on your Mazda’s infotainment screen.
* Go to the “Settings” or “Clock” section.
* Find the “Automatic Time Update” or “Sync with GPS” option.
* Turn the feature ON to enable GPS time syncing.

Once enabled, your Mazda will update the clock automatically. It will adjust the time during startup or when GPS signal is available.

### Check For Time Zone Accuracy

Even with automatic updates, verify the time zone is correct. Incorrect settings can cause the clock to show wrong time. Use this checklist:

* Open the “Clock” or “Time Settings” menu.
* Locate the “Time Zone” or “Region” option.
* Confirm the selected time zone matches your current location.
* Adjust manually if the time zone is incorrect.

Correct time zone settings help the GPS sync function keep your clock precise. This is important when crossing time zones during travel.

## Manual Clock Adjustment Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-clock-manually-in-your-mazda-cx-5-is-simple-once-you-know-the-right-steps-manual-clock-adjustment-ensures-your-time-is-accurate-without-relying-on-automatic-updates&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the clock manually in your Mazda CX-5 is simple once you know the right steps. Manual clock adjustment ensures your time is accurate without relying on automatic updates. This helps keep your schedule on track during trips or daily drives.

Knowing how to reset the clock after a battery replacement or adjust for daylight saving time saves time and hassle. These tips make the process quick and easy.

### Resetting After Battery Replacement

After replacing your Mazda CX-5 battery, the clock often resets to 12:00. To fix this, use the following steps:

* Turn on the ignition without starting the engine.
* Press the “Clock” button on the dashboard.
* Use the “Hour” and “Minute” buttons to set the correct time.
* Hold the buttons briefly to speed up the adjustment.
* Release the buttons once the correct time appears.
* Turn off the ignition to save the new time setting.

### Adjusting For Daylight Saving Time

Daylight saving time requires setting the clock one hour forward or backward. Follow these easy steps:

* Turn on your Mazda CX-5’s ignition.
* Press the “Clock” button to enter time setting mode.
* Adjust the hour using the “Hour” button only.
* Keep the minutes unchanged unless needed.
* Confirm the time by waiting a few seconds or turning off the ignition.

## Troubleshooting Clock Issues

Clock issues in a Mazda CX-5 can be frustrating. Sometimes the clock does not update as expected. Other times, the system might freeze or show the wrong time. Troubleshooting these problems helps keep your car’s clock accurate.

Understanding common causes can save time and effort. Problems can stem from software glitches or user errors. Simple steps often fix most clock issues without professional help.

### Clock Not Changing

The clock may not change due to several reasons. Check if you are following the correct procedure to set the time. Sometimes the buttons on the infotainment screen may not respond properly.

* Ensure the car is in the “On” or “Accessory” mode.
* Try setting the time through the settings menu carefully.
* Look for any screen freezes or lag during the process.
* If the clock still does not update, the software may need a reset.

### Infotainment System Reset

Resetting the infotainment system can resolve many clock problems. This action clears temporary errors and refreshes the system.

* Turn off the engine and open the driver’s door.
* Press and hold the power button on the infotainment screen for about 10 seconds.
* The screen will turn off and restart automatically.
* After reboot, try setting the clock again.

If issues persist after the reset, check for system updates or consult your Mazda dealer.
