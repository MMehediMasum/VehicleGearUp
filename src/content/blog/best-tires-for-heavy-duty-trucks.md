## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Tires For Heavy Duty Trucks?



The best tires for heavy duty trucks offer durability, load capacity, and traction. Options include BFGoodrich HD-Terrain, Firestone Transforce HT3, and Goodyear Wrangler Workhorse. These tires handle heavy loads and rough terrains efficiently, ensuring safety and long tire life.



### How To Choose Heavy Duty Truck Tires By Load Range?



Choose tires with Load Range G or higher for heavy duty trucks. Load Range indicates tire strength and ply rating. For example, Transeagle ST Radial and Zeemax trailer tires have Load Range G, suitable for heavy loads and highway use.



### Are All-terrain Tires Good For Heavy Duty Trucks?



Yes, all-terrain tires like Cooper Evolution M/T and Americus Rugged MT offer excellent traction on various surfaces. They balance durability and off-road performance, making them ideal for trucks used both on highways and rough terrains.



### What Is The Importance Of Tire Ply Rating For Trucks?



Tire ply rating shows the number of layers in the tire, affecting strength and load capacity. Heavy duty truck tires often have 10 to 14 plies, like Zeemax and Americus tires, providing better durability and resistance to punctures under heavy loads.



### Can Trailer Tires Handle Heavy Duty Truck Loads?



Heavy duty trailer tires such as Transeagle ST Radial and Zeemax True Highway are designed for trailers, not trucks. They offer high load capacity and stability but are optimized for trailer use, not for primary truck traction or steering.



### How Does Speed Rating Affect Heavy Duty Truck Tires?



Speed rating indicates the maximum safe speed for a tire. Heavy duty tires like Zeemax have ratings like K (68 mph), balancing durability and safety. Choose tires with speed ratings that match your truck’s operational speeds for optimal performance and safety.
