**A car that burns oil needs special care to avoid damage and costly repairs. Choosing the right oil helps reduce oil consumption and protect the engine.**



Cars burning oil suffer from worn piston rings or valve seals. Using high mileage oil or oil additives can help seal leaks and improve compression. Products like Lucas Oil High Mileage Oil Stabilizer and Rislone High Mileage Compression Repair are designed to reduce oil burning.



Synthetic blends, such as Castrol GTX High Mileage 10W-40 and Mobil 1 Extended Performance, also protect engine parts better than regular oils. Oil additives like Liqui Moly Oil Smoke Stop and Bar’s Leaks High Mileage Engine Repair help stop smoke and leaks. Proper oil choice keeps your engine running longer and lowers smoke and oil use. This guide covers the best oils and treatments for cars that burn oil.
