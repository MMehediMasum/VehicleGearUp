## Requirements For Full Self Driving

Activating Full Self Driving (FSD) on a Tesla Model 3 needs certain conditions. Knowing these requirements helps ensure your car supports FSD features. This section explains key factors to check before activating FSD.

### Tesla Model 3 Compatibility

Not all Tesla Model 3 versions support Full Self Driving. Your car must have the necessary hardware installed. Tesla started including FSD hardware in Model 3 vehicles made after mid-2017.

* Hardware 3.0 or newer is required for FSD.
* Older models may need a hardware upgrade.
* Check your vehicle’s specifications in the Tesla app or on the Tesla website.

### Software Version Needed

Your Tesla must run the right software to use Full Self Driving. Tesla frequently updates its software to improve FSD functions.

* Ensure your car’s software version is the latest available.
* Updates happen over-the-air, so connect your car to Wi-Fi.
* FSD features may not work correctly on outdated software.

### Subscription Vs One-time Purchase

Tesla offers Full Self Driving as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Each option has distinct benefits based on how long you plan to use FSD.

| Option | Description | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Step-by-step Activation Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=activating-full-self-driving-on-your-tesla-model-3-requires-following-a-clear-and-simple-process-this-ensures-your-car-is-ready-to-use-advanced-driving-features-safely&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Activating Full Self Driving on your Tesla Model 3 requires following a clear and simple process. This ensures your car is ready to use advanced driving features safely. The activation happens through the Tesla interface and involves a few key steps. Each step is easy to follow and takes just a few minutes.

### Accessing The Tesla Menu

Start by sitting inside your Tesla Model 3. Turn on the touchscreen display. Locate the main menu icon, usually at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to open the settings menu. Here, you will find various options related to your car’s features and software.

### Selecting Full Self Driving Option

Within the settings menu, find the “Autopilot” section. Tap on it to view available driving assistance features. Look for the “Full Self Driving” option. Select this to begin the activation process. The screen will show details about what the feature includes and how it works.

### Confirming Payment Or Subscription

After selecting Full Self Driving, the system will ask for payment confirmation. You can choose to buy it outright or subscribe monthly. Enter your payment details if needed. Confirm the purchase to proceed. Tesla will verify the payment before enabling the feature on your car.

### Downloading And Installing Software

Once payment is confirmed, the Tesla system will download necessary software updates. This may take several minutes depending on your internet connection. Keep the car powered on and connected to Wi-Fi. After download, the software installs automatically. Your Model 3 will notify you when the installation is complete and ready to use.

## Calibrating Autopilot Sensors

Calibrating the autopilot sensors on your Tesla Model 3 is crucial. It ensures the car's Full Self Driving system functions optimally. This process is straightforward but requires patience and attention. Proper calibration helps the vehicle understand its surroundings better. It also enhances driving accuracy and safety on the road.

### What Calibration Involves

Calibration involves aligning the car's sensors with its environment. The sensors include cameras, radar, and ultrasonic sensors. They work together to detect objects, lanes, and traffic signals. This alignment process ensures all components communicate effectively. It also helps the system make precise driving decisions.

### How To Calibrate After Activation

After activating Full Self Driving, find a safe, open area. Start driving at a moderate speed, around 20 to 30 mph. Maintain this speed for about 30 minutes. During this time, the car will automatically calibrate its sensors. Keep an eye on the dashboard for any calibration notifications. Once complete, the system will notify you.

### Troubleshooting Calibration Issues

If calibration issues occur, try a few troubleshooting steps. First, ensure the sensors are clean and unobstructed. Dirt or debris can affect sensor accuracy. Restart the vehicle if the problem persists. Go for another drive at a steady speed. If issues continue, contact Tesla support for assistance.

## Troubleshooting Common Problems

Troubleshooting common problems during the activation of Full Self Driving (FSD) on your Tesla Model 3 can save you time and frustration. Many owners face similar issues, but most can be resolved with simple steps. Understanding these common hurdles helps you stay calm and get your FSD up and running smoothly.

### Activation Errors

Activation errors often occur when the system fails to verify your purchase or subscription. Check that your Tesla account is linked properly to your vehicle and that payment has been processed. Sometimes, logging out and back into the Tesla app refreshes the connection and clears the error.

Another cause could be a mismatch between your vehicle’s software version and the FSD package requirements. Make sure your car is updated to the latest software version before trying activation again. Have you checked the Tesla forums? Other owners often share specific error codes and fixes that match your situation.

### Software Glitches

Software glitches can disrupt FSD activation or performance. These glitches might cause unexpected freezes or reset the activation screen. Restarting your vehicle’s touchscreen by holding down both scroll wheels on the steering wheel usually helps clear minor software hiccups.

If the problem persists, try a full vehicle reboot by pressing the brake and holding down the two scroll wheels simultaneously until the screen turns black and then restarts. Keep an eye on Tesla’s software update notes; sometimes, new releases fix known FSD bugs.

### Contacting Tesla Support

If you’ve exhausted basic troubleshooting, Tesla Support is your next step. When contacting them, provide clear details: your vehicle’s VIN, software version, and the exact error messages. This information speeds up diagnosis and resolution.

Use the Tesla app to schedule a service appointment or chat with a support agent. Remember, persistence pays off—don’t hesitate to follow up if the issue isn’t resolved quickly. Have you documented your troubleshooting attempts? Sharing these details can help Tesla pinpoint the problem faster.
