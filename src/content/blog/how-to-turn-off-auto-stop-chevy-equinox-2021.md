## Auto Stop Feature In Chevy Equinox

The Auto Stop feature in the Chevy Equinox 2021 is designed to improve fuel efficiency and reduce emissions by automatically shutting off the engine when the vehicle is at a complete stop. This technology activates during traffic lights or heavy traffic, restarting the engine instantly when you press the accelerator. Understanding how this system works helps you decide when and how to turn it off if needed.

### Purpose And Benefits

The main purpose of the Auto Stop feature is to save fuel and lower harmful emissions. By stopping the engine during idle moments, your Equinox uses less gas, which can add up to significant savings over time.

Besides saving fuel, this feature also reduces wear on certain engine components since the engine isn’t running constantly. Many drivers notice quieter rides in stop-and-go traffic because the engine isn’t roaring while the car is stopped.

### Common User Concerns

Some drivers find the Auto Stop feature annoying, especially if they frequently drive in city traffic with lots of stops. The repeated stopping and starting of the engine can feel intrusive or unsettling for some.

Others worry about potential delays when restarting, which might affect smooth driving in busy intersections. You might ask yourself, does this feature actually cause more stress than it saves fuel?

If you prefer full control over your vehicle’s engine, knowing how to turn off Auto Stop gives you the flexibility to drive the way you want. Have you tried disabling it during your daily commute to see if it improves your driving comfort?

## Step-by-step Guide To Disable Auto Stop

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=disabling-the-auto-stop-feature-on-your-chevy-equinox-2021-can-improve-your-driving-experience-some-drivers-prefer-to-turn-off-this-feature-for-smoother-starts-and-stops&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Disabling the Auto Stop feature on your Chevy Equinox 2021 can improve your driving experience. Some drivers prefer to turn off this feature for smoother starts and stops. This guide explains simple ways to disable Auto Stop quickly.

Follow clear instructions to turn off Auto Stop using the vehicle’s controls. Both physical buttons and menu settings allow easy adjustment. Choose the method that works best for you.

### Turning Off Via Physical Button

Locate the Auto Stop button on your dashboard. It often shows an “A” with a circular arrow around it. Press this button once to disable the feature for the current drive.

The Auto Stop icon will light up or change color, indicating it is off. Keep in mind, this setting resets when you turn off the engine. You must press the button again each time you start your car.

### Adjusting Settings Through The Menu

Access the vehicle’s main menu using the infotainment system. Navigate to the “Vehicle Settings” or “Driver Assistance” section. Find the Auto Stop or Auto Start/Stop option within the menu.

Select the option and switch it to “Off” or “Disabled.” This setting may keep Auto Stop turned off until you change it again. Use the touchscreen or control knob to confirm your choice.

## When Auto Stop Resets Automatically

Many drivers of the 2021 Chevy Equinox experience the Auto Stop feature. It's designed to enhance fuel efficiency by shutting off the engine at stops. Yet, it can restart unexpectedly. This happens each time you restart your vehicle, as the system resets itself. Understanding why it resets can help manage expectations.

### Understanding Temporary Disabling

You can temporarily disable the Auto Stop feature. Press the Auto Stop button located on the dashboard. This action will deactivate the feature until the engine stops again. It's useful for short-term needs.

### Settings Persistence Limits

Unfortunately, the settings don't persist after restarting the vehicle. Each time you turn off and on your Equinox, the Auto Stop resets. This means manual deactivation is required each time you drive. It remains a consistent experience among many users.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Disable Auto Stop On Chevy Equinox 2021?

To disable Auto Stop, press the Auto Stop button located on the center console. The system will stay off until you restart the vehicle.

No, Auto Stop resets each time you start your Equinox. You must disable it manually every drive.
