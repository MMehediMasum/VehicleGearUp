## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Tires For Jeep Compass Trailhawk?



The best tires for Jeep Compass Trailhawk offer excellent traction and durability. Look for all-terrain tires with strong sidewalls. Brands like BFGoodrich and Goodyear provide reliable options for off-road and on-road use. Proper tire size and tread design improve performance and safety.



### How Do Wheel Spacers Affect Jeep Compass Trailhawk Tires?



Wheel spacers increase the track width, improving stability and handling. They allow for larger tires and better clearance. Ensure spacers match the 5x110 bolt pattern and 65. 1mm hub size for Jeep Compass Trailhawk. Proper installation avoids vibration and safety issues.



### Can I Use A 1.5" Lift Kit With Jeep Compass Trailhawk Tires?



A 1. 5" lift kit enhances ground clearance and allows bigger tires. However, some lift kits exclude Trailhawk models, so check compatibility carefully. Proper lift kits maintain suspension geometry and tire clearance, improving off-road capability without compromising ride comfort.



### How Do Tpms Sensors Benefit Jeep Compass Trailhawk Tires?



TPMS sensors monitor tire pressure in real-time, ensuring safety and fuel efficiency. For Jeep Compass Trailhawk, use 433MHz sensors compatible with 2017-2018 models. They alert drivers to low pressure, preventing tire damage and improving overall vehicle performance.



### What Size Wheel Spacers Fit Jeep Compass Trailhawk Rims?



For Jeep Compass Trailhawk, 1 to 2-inch wheel spacers with 5x110 bolt pattern and 65. 1mm hub bore fit perfectly. Using 12x1. 25 studs ensures secure mounting. Correct spacer size improves wheel fitment and tire clearance for better driving dynamics.



### Are Aftermarket Mirrors Compatible With Jeep Compass Trailhawk?



Yes, aftermarket mirrors designed for Jeep Compass Trailhawk (2017-2021) fit well. Options include paintable and non-dimming models for the passenger side. Quality mirrors enhance visibility and match OEM specifications for seamless replacement and improved aesthetics.
