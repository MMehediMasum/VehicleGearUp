## Spare Tire Options For Jeep Renegade

The Jeep Renegade offers several spare tire options for drivers. Each option suits different needs and preferences. Understanding these choices helps you prepare for tire troubles on the road.

### Factory-installed Spare Tires

Some Jeep Renegade models come with a full-size spare tire. This tire matches the other four and fits the vehicle perfectly. It allows easy replacement without worrying about speed limits or distance. Full-size spares take more space but offer maximum safety and convenience.

### Tire Repair Kits

Many newer Renegade models include a tire repair kit instead of a spare. The kit contains sealant and an air compressor. It helps fix small punctures quickly and temporarily. This option saves weight and space inside the vehicle. It is useful for minor tire damage but not for large holes or blowouts.

### Run-flat Tires

Run-flat tires are another option for some Jeep Renegade trims. These tires allow driving even after a puncture. They can go up to 50 miles at a reduced speed without air pressure. This feature reduces the need for a spare tire. It provides peace of mind but may result in a harsher ride.




## Models And Year Variations

The Jeep Renegade's spare tire availability varies across different models and years. It is important to understand these differences to know what to expect when owning or buying a Renegade.

Jeep has made changes to the spare tire options over time. These changes depend on the model year and trim level. Let’s explore how the spare tire situation has evolved in the Renegade lineup.

### Early Model Years

In the early model years, Jeep Renegade usually came with a full-size spare tire. This spare was mounted on the back door. It was easy to access and replaced a flat tire quickly.

These models prioritized convenience and off-road readiness. The full-size spare matched the tires on the vehicle. This helped maintain performance and safety.

### Recent Model Years

Recent Jeep Renegade models often do not include a full-size spare tire. Some have a compact or temporary spare. Others rely on tire repair kits instead.

This change reduces vehicle weight and saves space. It also improves fuel efficiency. However, it can affect convenience during a flat tire situation.

### Differences Between Trims

Spare tire availability can differ among trims. Higher trims may offer a full-size spare as an option. Base trims often come with a tire repair kit only.

* Sport trim: Usually no full-size spare, uses repair kit.
* Latitude trim: Sometimes includes a compact spare tire.
* Trailhawk trim: More likely to have a full-size spare.

Trim choice impacts the spare tire setup. It is crucial to check specific trim details before purchase.

## Locating The Spare Tire

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-where-your-jeep-renegades-spare-tire-is-located-can-save-you-time-and-stress-during-an-unexpected-flat-tire-situation-the-location-varies-depending-on-the-model-and-year-so-its-essential-to-check-your-specific-vehicle&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing where your Jeep Renegade’s spare tire is located can save you time and stress during an unexpected flat tire situation. The location varies depending on the model and year, so it’s essential to check your specific vehicle. Let’s look at the most common places you might find the spare tire on a Jeep Renegade.

### Under The Cargo Area

Many Jeep Renegade models store the spare tire underneath the cargo floor inside the rear storage area. You can lift the carpet or cargo mat to reveal a compartment where the tire is secured.

This location keeps the tire protected from the elements and easy to access without leaving the vehicle. Have you ever struggled to find your spare only to realize it was hidden right under your feet?

### Mounted On The Rear Door

Some Renegade trims come with the spare tire mounted on the outside rear door. This placement gives the vehicle a rugged look and frees up interior space.

However, accessing the tire here means opening the rear door and possibly lifting the heavy wheel into position. If you choose this model, it’s good to practice removing and reinstalling the tire before an emergency occurs.

### Inside The Vehicle

Less common but still possible, certain Renegade versions keep the spare inside the cabin, often under the rear seats or behind panels.

This protects the tire from theft and weather damage but may reduce interior storage space. Do you prioritize easy access or extra cargo room? Knowing this can help you understand the trade-offs with different spare tire locations.




## Replacing And Maintaining The Spare

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-jeep-renegade-may-come-with-a-full-size-or-compact-spare-tire-depending-on-the-model-regularly-checking-and-maintaining-the-spare-ensures-its-ready-for-use-during-emergencies&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A Jeep Renegade may come with a full-size or compact spare tire, depending on the model. Regularly checking and maintaining the spare ensures it’s ready for use during emergencies. Replace the spare tire if it shows signs of wear or damage for safe driving.

Replacing and maintaining the spare tire in your Jeep Renegade might not be the most glamorous task, but it’s crucial for your safety. Imagine being stuck on a desolate road with a flat tire and no spare. It’s a situation no one wants to find themselves in. Ensuring your spare tire is in good condition can save you from unexpected headaches and keep your adventures on track.

### Checking Tire Pressure

Regularly checking the tire pressure of your spare is a simple yet often overlooked task. A flat spare is as useless as not having one at all. Use a reliable tire pressure gauge to check the spare every month. Keeping it at the recommended pressure ensures that it’s ready to go when you need it.

### When To Replace The Spare

Even if you rarely use your spare, it doesn't mean it lasts forever. Tires age, and rubber degrades over time. Consider replacing the spare if it's over six years old, even if it looks fine. Would you trust your safety to an old, brittle tire during an emergency?

### Tools Needed For Replacement

Having the right tools can make replacing your spare tire a breeze. Ensure you have a lug wrench, jack, and the Jeep Renegade’s owner manual handy. A portable air compressor and a flashlight are also helpful additions to your toolkit. With the right tools, you can handle a tire change efficiently and confidently. Maintaining a spare tire might not seem urgent until you're in a pinch. Why not take a few minutes today to check it? It’s a small effort that can provide peace of mind on your next journey.

## Alternatives To Spare Tires

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=not-all-jeep-renegade-models-come-with-a-spare-tire-this-creates-a-need-for-other-ways-to-handle-flat-tires&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Not all Jeep Renegade models come with a spare tire. This creates a need for other ways to handle flat tires. Alternatives to spare tires offer convenience and safety for drivers. These options can help you stay on the road without carrying a full-size spare. They also save space and reduce weight inside the vehicle.

### Roadside Assistance Options

Many drivers rely on roadside assistance services. These services send professionals to fix flat tires or tow your vehicle. Some plans include tire repair or replacement. Roadside help provides peace of mind during emergencies. You only need a phone call to get help quickly.

### Tire Sealants And Inflators

Tire sealants are liquids that plug small holes inside tires. They stop air from leaking out. Inflators add air to the tire after sealing it. This combo can temporarily fix minor punctures. Sealants work best on small punctures caused by nails or screws. Keep them in your car for quick fixes.

### Portable Air Compressors

Portable air compressors help maintain correct tire pressure. They plug into your vehicle’s power outlet. Use them to inflate a tire slowly and safely. These devices work well with sealants or to top off tires after repair. They are compact and easy to store in the Jeep Renegade.

Adding a spare tire to a Jeep Renegade affects both the vehicle's weight and storage space. These factors can influence driving performance and how much cargo the vehicle can hold. Understanding these impacts helps owners decide if carrying a spare tire fits their needs.

### Weight Considerations

A full-size spare tire adds significant weight to the Jeep Renegade. This extra weight can slightly reduce fuel efficiency. It may also affect acceleration and braking performance. Lighter weight improves handling and ride comfort. Removing the spare tire can lower the vehicle’s overall weight by about 30 to 40 pounds.

### Cargo Space Implications

A spare tire takes up valuable space in the cargo area. It limits how much luggage or gear can fit inside the vehicle. Some Jeep Renegade models store the spare tire underneath the rear, saving interior space. Others place it inside the trunk, reducing storage room. Choosing a tire repair kit instead can free up more cargo space.

  


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Jeep Renegade Come With A Spare Tire?

Most Jeep Renegade models do not include a full spare tire. Instead, they come with a tire repair kit or a compact temporary spare, depending on the trim and year. Always check your specific model’s features for confirmation.

### Where Is The Spare Tire Located In Jeep Renegade?

If equipped, the spare tire in a Jeep Renegade is usually located under the rear cargo area floor. Some models may have it mounted on the rear exterior or stored underneath the vehicle, but this varies by year and trim.

### Can I Buy A Spare Tire For Jeep Renegade?

Yes, you can purchase a compatible spare tire for your Jeep Renegade. Many dealerships and tire retailers offer OEM or aftermarket spare tires. Verify the correct size and type to ensure proper fit and performance.

### Does Jeep Renegade Use A Full-size Or Compact Spare?

Jeep Renegade typically uses a compact “donut” spare tire rather than a full-size spare. The compact spare is smaller, lighter, and designed for temporary use until a full repair or replacement is done.
