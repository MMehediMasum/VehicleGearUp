Yes, Genuine Toyota TRD wheels, such as the 17-inch Matte Black set, fit 4Runner Limited models. They improve aesthetics and durability. These wheels also maintain factory specifications, ensuring perfect compatibility and performance for Toyota 4Runner Limited owners.



### How Do Tpms Sensors Benefit Toyota 4runner Tires?



TPMS sensors like Marsflex and PMV-107J monitor tire pressure continuously. They alert drivers about underinflation, enhancing safety and fuel efficiency. Properly functioning sensors maintain optimal tire condition and prolong tire life on Toyota 4Runner Limited vehicles.



### Can I Upgrade My 4runner Limited With Aftermarket Rock Sliders?

### What Wheel Size Is Recommended For Toyota 4runner Limited Tires?



Toyota 4Runner Limited typically uses 17-inch wheels. Options like TRD PRO wheels and RockTrix RT107 rims fit this size perfectly. Sticking to 17-inch wheels ensures tire fitment, performance, and factory ride quality are maintained.



### Are Rocktrix Rt107 Wheels Suitable For Off-road 4runner Tires?



Yes, RockTrix RT107 wheels are designed for off-road use. They offer durability and a 17x9 size with 6-lug compatibility. These wheels enhance traction and stability for Toyota 4Runner Limited tires in rugged terrains.
