**Choosing the best spark plugs for an SBC 350 with headers improves engine performance and reliability. Proper spark plugs and wires help the engine run smoothly under high heat from headers.**



Small Block Chevy (SBC) 350 engines need spark plugs that handle higher temperatures caused by headers. Headers increase exhaust flow but also heat around spark plugs and wires. Using the right spark plugs and heat-resistant wires prevents misfires and engine damage.



Products like ACCEL 8198 Performance U-Groove plugs and NGK V Power plugs offer good heat resistance and durability. Heat shield sleeves and special wire sets from JEGS and Moroso protect wires from heat damage. These parts fit well under headers and maintain strong spark signals. Choosing quality plugs and wires ensures your SBC 350 runs clean, powerful, and reliable.
