## Why Neutral Matters

Putting your Honda Pilot in neutral is important in several situations, especially when the battery is dead. Neutral allows the wheels to move freely without the engine running. This can help with towing, pushing, or moving the car safely. Understanding why neutral matters can prevent damage and keep you safe during emergencies.

### Situations Requiring Neutral

* Towing the vehicle to a repair shop
* Moving the car manually in a parking lot
* Jump-starting the battery without damaging transmission
* Performing maintenance or repairs that need the car to roll

### Risks Of Not Using Neutral

* Damage to the transmission system
* Difficulty in moving or towing the vehicle
* Unnecessary wear on engine and drivetrain parts
* Potential safety hazards during emergency situations

## Locating The Shift Lock Release

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-shift-lock-release-is-usually-near-the-gear-shift-lever-pressing-it-allows-you-to-move-the-honda-pilot-into-neutral-even-with-a-dead-battery&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The shift lock release is usually near the gear shift lever. Pressing it allows you to move the Honda Pilot into neutral even with a dead battery. Look for a small cover or slot to access this release.

Locating the shift lock release is crucial when your Honda Pilot's battery dies. This process allows you to move the gearshift to neutral without battery power. Understanding the shift lock mechanism helps in such situations.

### Shift Lock Purpose

The shift lock keeps the vehicle secure when parked. It prevents the gear from shifting accidentally. This safety feature locks the gearshift in place. Without power, you need to bypass it to move the gear.

### Finding The Release Slot

Locate the shift lock release slot near the gearshift. Usually, a small cover hides it. Remove this cover using a flat tool. Insert a key or screwdriver into the slot. Press down gently while moving the gearshift. This action allows shifting to neutral.

## Step-by-step Neutral Shift

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=shifting-a-honda-pilot-into-neutral-with-a-dead-battery-can-be-tricky-the-usual-electronic-controls-might-not-work&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Shifting a Honda Pilot into neutral with a dead battery can be tricky. The usual electronic controls might not work. You need a manual way to move the gear lever. Follow clear steps to do this safely and easily. This guide shows how to prepare the vehicle, use the shift lock release, and move the gear lever to neutral.

### Preparing The Vehicle

Start by ensuring the parking brake is fully engaged. This keeps the vehicle from rolling unexpectedly. Turn the ignition key to the "On" position if possible. If the battery is dead, this might not work. Make sure the brake pedal is pressed firmly. This step is essential for the shift lock release to work.

### Using The Shift Lock Release

Locate the shift lock release slot near the gear lever. It usually has a small cover that can be removed. Use a flat tool, like a screwdriver or key, to open the cover. Insert the tool gently into the slot. Press down to unlock the gear shift. This bypasses the electronic lock caused by the dead battery.

### Moving The Gear Lever

With the shift lock release pressed, hold the brake pedal down. Move the gear lever from "Park" to "Neutral" carefully. Do not force the lever, as this may cause damage. Once in neutral, release the shift lock release. The vehicle can now roll if the parking brake is off. Keep the brake engaged until ready to move the vehicle.

  


## Alternative Methods

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=alternative-methods-help-move-your-honda-pilot-when-the-battery-dies-these-options-do-not-need-the-usual-key-turn-to-shift-gears&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Alternative methods help move your Honda Pilot when the battery dies. These options do not need the usual key turn to shift gears. They provide practical ways to put the vehicle in neutral and get it to a safe spot or repair shop.

### Jump-starting The Battery

Jump-starting brings power back to the dead battery. Use jumper cables and another car with a good battery. Connect the cables carefully to the correct terminals.

* Attach the red cable to the positive terminal of the dead battery.
* Connect the other red cable end to the positive terminal of the good battery.
* Attach the black cable to the negative terminal of the good battery.
* Connect the other black cable end to an unpainted metal surface on the dead car.

Start the car with the good battery. Let it run for a few minutes. Try shifting the Honda Pilot into neutral now. This method restores enough power to move the gear shift.

### Using A Tow Dolly

A tow dolly lifts the front wheels off the ground. This lets you tow the Honda Pilot without starting the engine. It works well for front-wheel-drive models.

* Securely attach the tow dolly to the towing vehicle.
* Drive the Honda Pilot onto the dolly so front wheels rest on it.
* Lock the wheels in place using straps or chains.
* Check all connections before towing.

This method avoids shifting the gear and protects the transmission. It helps move the vehicle safely when the battery is dead and cannot be jump-started.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Shift Honda Pilot To Neutral With Dead Battery?

To shift your Honda Pilot to neutral with a dead battery, locate the shift lock release slot near the gear lever. Insert a key or screwdriver to unlock and move the shifter to neutral manually. This bypasses the electrical lock system safely.

### Why Can't I Put Honda Pilot In Neutral With Dead Battery?

The Honda Pilot’s shift lock system requires battery power to disengage. Without power, the shifter locks to prevent unintended movement. Using the shift lock release slot or jump-starting the battery helps bypass this lock and allows shifting into neutral.

### Can I Tow Honda Pilot With A Dead Battery In Neutral?

Yes, you can tow a Honda Pilot with a dead battery if it is shifted into neutral. Use the shift lock release to put the vehicle in neutral, ensuring safe towing without damaging the transmission or drivetrain.

### Is It Safe To Use Shift Lock Release On Honda Pilot?

Yes, using the shift lock release on your Honda Pilot is safe when done correctly. It is designed for emergency use to manually shift the car. Always follow the owner’s manual instructions to avoid any damage.
