## Ford Explorer Seating Options

The Ford Explorer offers flexible seating options to suit various needs. It balances passenger comfort with cargo space. Families and groups find it practical for trips and daily drives.

### Standard Seating Configuration

The base Ford Explorer seats five people comfortably. It includes two front bucket seats and a rear bench seat. The second row has ample legroom for adults. The interior design focuses on comfort and ease of access.

### Third Row Availability

Most Ford Explorer models come with a third row. This adds seating for two or three extra passengers. The third row folds down to increase cargo space. It is suitable for children and shorter trips for adults. This feature makes the Explorer ideal for larger families.
