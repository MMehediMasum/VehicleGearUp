The u-Box Full Width Front Bumper and Hooke Road Front Bumper are perfect for 2014-2024 5th Gen 4Runners. Both offer winch plates and mounts for added utility and protection during off-road adventures.



### How Do Old Man Emu Lift Kits Differ By Generation?



OME lift kits vary by generation for precise fit and suspension tuning. For 3rd Gen, use OME4R-3GEN; 4th Gen fits OME4R-4GEN kits. Each supports different weight loads to optimize driving performance and durability.



### Are Winch Plates Included On These Bumpers?



Yes, both the u-Box and Hooke Road bumpers come with integrated winch plates. This feature allows easy winch installation for recovery and off-road readiness, enhancing vehicle versatility.



### Can I Install These Lift Kits Myself?



Installation requires mechanical skills and tools. While possible for experienced DIYers, professional installation is recommended to ensure safety and proper suspension alignment. This avoids damage and maintains warranty coverage.
