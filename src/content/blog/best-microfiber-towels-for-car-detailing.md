## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Microfiber Towels Ideal For Car Detailing?



Microfiber towels have ultra-fine fibers that trap dirt and dust effectively. They are soft, absorbent, and prevent scratches. These towels provide streak-free cleaning and polishing, making them perfect for car detailing tasks like washing, drying, and buffing.



### How Often Can Usanooks Microfiber Towels Be Washed?



USANOOKS microfiber towels can withstand up to 1200 washes without losing effectiveness. This durability makes them a cost-effective choice for long-term car care and cleaning needs.



### Are Large Microfiber Towels Better For Drying Cars?



Yes, large towels like KKV's 35"x24" size cover more surface area. They absorb water quickly, reducing drying time and minimizing water spots on cars and trucks.



### Can Microfiber Towels Cause Scratches On Car Paint?



High-quality microfiber towels, like Edgeless or Chemical Guys, are designed to be scratch-free. Their soft texture and lint-free weave prevent damage to delicate car surfaces during cleaning.



### What Is The Advantage Of Dual-sided Microfiber Towels?



Dual-sided towels, such as MR. SIGA's, offer versatility for different tasks. One side can be for washing and the other for drying or buffing, enhancing convenience and cleaning efficiency.



### Are Microfiber Towels Suitable For Other Cleaning Tasks?



Yes, microfiber towels like AIDEA and FIXSMITH are versatile. They work well for dusting, kitchen cleaning, window washing, and pet drying, besides car detailing.
