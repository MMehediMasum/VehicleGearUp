### How Does An Oiled Filter Improve Intake Performance?



Oiled filters trap more dirt and particles while maintaining airflow. They are washable and reusable, reducing replacement costs and keeping your engine cleaner longer.



### Which Cold Air Intake Fits 1999.5-2003 Ford Super Duty Trucks?

### Can Installing A Cold Air Intake Increase Fuel Efficiency?



Yes, by improving airflow, cold air intakes help your engine burn fuel more efficiently. This leads to better mileage and reduced fuel consumption over time.



### Is It Difficult To Install A 7.3 Powerstroke Cold Air Intake?



Most kits come with detailed instructions and fit directly onto your truck. Basic mechanical skills and common tools are usually enough for installation in under an hour.



### What Maintenance Is Required For A 7.3 Powerstroke Cold Air Intake?



Regular cleaning and re-oiling of the filter keep performance optimal. Check the intake pipe for debris and secure fittings to avoid leaks or damage.
