Dual plane manifolds improve low to mid-range torque and throttle response. They split airflow into two separate paths, enhancing fuel atomization. This results in smoother idle and better drivability for Vortec engines, especially in street and towing applications.

### Are Single Plane Manifolds Good For Vortec Heads?



Single plane manifolds suit high RPM builds, offering better top-end power above 5000 RPM. The Vortec single plane high rise manifold supports 3000-7500+ RPM. They are ideal for racing or performance-focused Vortec SBC engines.



### Which Intake Manifold Fits Ls Cathedral Port Heads?



LS LS1, LS2, LS3, and LS6 cathedral port heads use specific 102mm intake manifolds. These manifolds fit Chevy 4. 8L to 6. 0L engines. They optimize airflow and improve horsepower across the RPM range for LS-series Vortec engines.



### What Rpm Range Is Ideal For Vortec Dual Plane Manifolds?



Vortec dual plane intake manifolds typically perform best between 1500 and 6500 RPM. This range provides optimal torque, throttle response, and fuel efficiency. They are perfect for street use, towing, and daily driving with Vortec heads.
