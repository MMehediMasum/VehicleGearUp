### Where To Look Inside Your Vehicle

The hood release lever is typically found on the driver's side, just beneath the dashboard. It’s often tucked near the left kick panel or under the steering wheel column. You might spot a small handle with a hood icon, making it easier to identify.

### How The Hood Release Lever Feels And Looks

The lever is usually made of sturdy plastic and designed to pull easily. It may be black or gray, blending with the interior but still distinct enough to notice. If you’re unsure, gently feel around the lower dash area for a small handle that pulls toward you.

## Close The Hood Safely

Closing the hood of your 2017 Ford Escape safely is essential for your safety and the vehicle’s protection. A securely closed hood prevents it from flying open while driving. It also keeps dirt and moisture out of the engine compartment.

Take a moment to lower the hood gently. Avoid dropping it, as this may damage the latch or the hood itself. Ensuring the hood clicks into place confirms it is locked securely.

### Lower The Hood Slowly

Hold the hood with both hands. Lower it gently until it is a few inches above the latch. Avoid slamming it down to prevent damage.

### Check The Latch Position

Press down firmly on the center of the hood. Listen for a clear click sound. This means the latch has caught and locked the hood.

### Test The Hood

Try to lift the hood slightly. If it stays in place, it is locked properly. If it moves, repeat the closing process carefully.

### Inspect The Hood Seal

Look around the edges of the hood. Ensure the rubber seal sits evenly to keep out water and dirt. A good seal helps maintain engine health.
