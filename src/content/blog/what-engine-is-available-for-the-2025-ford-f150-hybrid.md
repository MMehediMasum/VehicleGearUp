## 2025 Ford F150 Hybrid Engine Specs

The 2025 Ford F150 Hybrid offers a powerful and efficient engine designed for tough work and daily driving. This hybrid setup blends traditional power with electric assistance. It balances strength with better fuel savings than a standard engine.

Below, the key engine specifications of the 2025 Ford F150 Hybrid highlight what makes it stand out.

### Engine Type And Configuration

The F150 Hybrid uses a 3.5-liter V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electric motor. This combination forms a full hybrid powertrain. The engine features twin turbochargers to boost performance. The electric motor helps improve fuel economy and adds extra torque. The hybrid system uses a 1.5 kWh lithium-ion battery pack.

### Horsepower And Torque

The total output of the hybrid powertrain reaches 430 horsepower. Torque delivers a strong 570 lb-ft, perfect for towing and hauling. The electric motor provides instant torque for quick acceleration. This setup ensures smooth power delivery and strong performance on demand.

### Fuel Efficiency Ratings

The 2025 Ford F150 Hybrid achieves an estimated 25 MPG in the city. Highway driving offers about 26 MPG, making it more efficient than many trucks. The hybrid system also allows for some short trips on electric power alone. Overall, this truck reduces fuel costs and emissions while maintaining power.
