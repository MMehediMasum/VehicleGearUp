### How Does The Doona Car Seat & Stroller Benefit Parents?



Doona combines car seat and stroller in one. It’s compact, easy to fold, and perfect for quick travel with twins.



### Are Tandem Double Strollers Better Than Side-by-side For Twins?



Tandem strollers are narrower and easier to navigate through tight spaces. Side-by-side offers more room but can be bulkier.



### What Safety Features Should I Look For In A Twin Stroller?



Look for 5-point harnesses, sturdy frames, reliable brakes, and oversized canopies. Comfort and secure seating are essential for infant safety.



### Can Baby Trend Combos Support Toddlers As Well As Infants?



Yes, many Baby Trend combos include toddler seats or convertible options. They grow with your child, offering long-term use for twins.
