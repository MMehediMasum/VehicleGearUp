## Locate The Windshield Washer Reservoir

Locating the windshield washer reservoir in your Tesla Model Y is the first step to keeping your windshield clear and your driving safe. This part holds the fluid you spray to clean your windshield, so knowing exactly where it is can save you time and frustration. Let's walk through the simple process of finding it.

### Open The Front Trunk

Unlike traditional cars, the Tesla Model Y has its windshield washer reservoir located in the front trunk, often called the "frunk."

Start by unlocking the front trunk using the touchscreen inside your car or the Tesla app on your phone.

Lift the frunk lid carefully—it’s lightweight and opens upwards, giving you clear access to the area where the reservoir sits.

### Identify The Washer Fluid Cap

Once the frunk is open, look for a translucent plastic container with a blue or black cap. This is the windshield washer reservoir.

The cap typically features an icon of a windshield with water spray, making it easy to spot even if you’re new to the Model Y.

Make sure you don’t confuse it with other fluid reservoirs, such as the coolant or brake fluid, which are located nearby but have different cap designs and labels.




## Choose The Right Washer Fluid

Choosing the right windshield washer fluid is key for your Tesla Model Y’s visibility and safety. The correct fluid cleans the windshield without damaging the wiper system. Some fluids protect against freezing, while others remove tough grime. Picking the right type improves cleaning power and protects your car parts.

### Types Of Washer Fluids

Windshield washer fluids come in several types. Each serves a different purpose for cleaning and protection.

* **Standard Fluid:**Good for everyday dirt and dust. It works well in mild weather.
* **Bug and Tar Remover:**Stronger formula to remove stubborn bugs and sticky substances.
* **Waterless Fluid:**Concentrated and easy to store. Often used in emergency kits.
* **De-icing Fluid:**Contains chemicals to melt ice and frost quickly.

### Consider Seasonal Options

Seasonal changes affect windshield washer needs. Choose fluid based on the weather conditions you face.

* **Winter:**Use fluid with antifreeze to stop freezing in cold temperatures.
* **Summer:**Pick a fluid that removes bugs, dust, and pollen effectively.
* **All-Season:**Balanced formula for moderate climates with mixed weather.

Using the right seasonal fluid ensures clear vision and protects the Tesla Model Y’s washer system.

## Test The Windshield Washer System

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=testing-the-windshield-washer-system-in-your-tesla-model-y-ensures-clear-visibility-during-drives-it-confirms-the-washer-fluid-sprays-correctly-and-reaches-the-windshield&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Testing the windshield washer system in your Tesla Model Y ensures clear visibility during drives. It confirms the washer fluid sprays correctly and reaches the windshield. Checking this system helps avoid surprises on the road and keeps your view clean.

### Activate The Washer From Inside

Start by sitting in the driver's seat. Locate the washer control on the steering column stalk. Pull or press the control to activate the washer fluid spray. Listen for the pump noise and watch the windshield. You should see fluid spraying evenly across the glass.

If nothing happens, check the fluid level or inspect the fuse. Repeat activation to verify consistent operation.

### Ensure Proper Spray Coverage

Observe the spray pattern closely. It should cover the windshield evenly without missing spots. The fluid must reach the edges to clear dirt and debris fully. If spray is weak or uneven, clean the nozzles with a pin or soft cloth.

Test again after cleaning. Proper coverage means safer driving in rain or dust.
