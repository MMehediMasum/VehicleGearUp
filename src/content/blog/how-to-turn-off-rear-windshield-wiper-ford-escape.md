## Locate The Rear Wiper Control

Locating the rear windshield wiper control on your Ford Escape is the first step to turning it off. Knowing exactly where this control is can save you time and prevent frustration, especially during unexpected weather changes.

Different model years and trims might place these controls in varying spots, so recognizing the common locations will help you quickly find the right switch. Let’s explore the two primary places Ford usually places the rear wiper controls.

### Dashboard Controls

Some Ford Escape models have the rear wiper control on the dashboard. It is often a small dial or button labeled with a rear wiper icon.

Check near the climate controls or to the left of the steering wheel. The control might offer settings like “Off,” “Intermittent,” and “On.”

Have you noticed how sometimes a quick glance at the dashboard reveals multiple buttons? Spotting the right one becomes easier once you know what to look for—usually a symbol resembling a window with a wiper blade.

### Steering Column Lever

In many Ford Escapes, the rear wiper control is integrated into the lever attached to the steering column, usually the same lever that operates the front wipers and turn signals.

Look for a rotating end or a small switch on this lever. Rotating it can turn the rear wiper off or set it to intermittent wiping.

Have you ever fumbled with the lever, only to realize the rear wiper control is right there? It’s designed for easy access, so once you find it, turning off the rear wiper is just a twist or flick away.

## Turn Off Rear Wiper Manually

Turning off the rear windshield wiper on your Ford Escape manually is straightforward once you know where to look. Sometimes the rear wiper keeps running longer than you need it to, especially after a quick rain shower. Knowing how to control it yourself saves you from unnecessary noise and wear on the wiper blade.

### Using The Wiper Switch

The simplest way to turn off the rear wiper is by using the wiper control switch located on the right side of your steering wheel. This switch usually has several positions, including off, intermittent, and continuous wipe.

* Locate the rear wiper switch—it's often a small knob or lever on the wiper control stalk.
* Turn the knob or push the lever to the “OFF” position to stop the rear wiper immediately.
* If the rear wiper is stuck or still running, try turning the ignition off and back on to reset the system.

Have you ever been annoyed by your rear wiper running when you don’t want it? Taking a moment to find this switch can save you from that hassle.

### Adjusting The Intermittent Setting

Your Ford Escape may have an intermittent setting for the rear wiper, designed to wipe the window at set intervals. This feature is handy during light rain but can be turned off manually if you want full control.

* Check the rear wiper control for an intermittent position, often marked with a small clock symbol or “INT.”
* Adjust the setting by turning the knob or moving the lever to “OFF” to stop intermittent wiping.
* Some models allow you to customize the delay between wipes—experiment with this to suit your driving conditions.

Have you noticed how sometimes the intermittent setting is too frequent or too slow? Adjusting it yourself makes your drive more comfortable and less distracting.

  


## Check The Wiper Status

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=checking-the-status-of-your-ford-escapes-rear-windshield-wiper-is-essential-a-functioning-wiper-ensures-clear-visibility-during-adverse-weather&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Checking the status of your Ford Escape's rear windshield wiper is essential. A functioning wiper ensures clear visibility during adverse weather. Sometimes, the wiper may seem off, but it's not. Let's explore how to confirm its status and troubleshoot persistent issues.

### Confirming The Wiper Is Off

First, ensure the wiper switch is in the "off" position. Locate the wiper control stalk on the steering column. This stalk controls both front and rear wipers. Turn the switch to the "off" position. Listen for any noise indicating the wiper is still active. A quiet wiper confirms it's off.

Next, visually check the rear windshield. Look for any movement of the wiper blade. A stationary blade indicates the wiper is off. If the wiper moves, you need further action. Ensure your vehicle's ignition is off. Sometimes, the wiper resets when the ignition is off.

### Troubleshooting Persistent Wiper Operation

If the rear wiper continues to operate, inspect the wiper motor. A faulty motor can cause persistent operation. Listen for unusual sounds from the motor area. Strange noises could mean the motor is malfunctioning. Consider seeking professional help if the issue persists.

Inspect the wiper relay as another step. A stuck relay may cause the wiper to operate continuously. Consult your vehicle's manual to locate the relay. Replace it if necessary. Ensure the new relay matches the original specifications for compatibility.




## Reset Rear Wiper Settings

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-rear-windshield-wiper-settings-on-your-ford-escape-can-help-fix-any-unwanted-behavior-or-glitches-sometimes-the-wiper-might-keep-running-or-refuse-to-turn-off-due-to-software-hiccups-or-accidental-setting-changes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the rear windshield wiper settings on your Ford Escape can help fix any unwanted behavior or glitches. Sometimes, the wiper might keep running or refuse to turn off due to software hiccups or accidental setting changes. Resetting these settings gives you a fresh start and puts control back in your hands.

### Using Vehicle Settings Menu

You can adjust or reset the rear wiper settings directly through your Ford Escape’s vehicle settings menu. Start by turning on your ignition and accessing the touchscreen display.

Navigate to the “Settings” or “Vehicle” option, then look for “Wipers” or “Wiper Settings.” Here, you can customize how the rear wiper operates or reset it to default.

Have you noticed how easy it is to accidentally change settings when exploring menus? This simple step can save you from unwanted wiper activity without needing any tools.

### Performing A System Reset

If adjusting settings doesn’t work, performing a system reset may be necessary. This process refreshes the electronic control unit that manages the wipers.

To do this, turn off your vehicle and open the driver’s door. Leave it open for about 10 minutes to allow the system to power down completely.

Afterward, close the door and restart your Ford Escape. This “soft reset” often clears minor electronic glitches causing the rear wiper to malfunction. Have you tried this simple fix before getting frustrated with your wipers?

## Preventing Rear Wiper Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ford-escapes-rear-windshield-wiper-in-good-shape-isnt-just-about-convenienceit-helps-maintain-clear-visibility-and-ensures-your-safety-on-the-road-preventing-common-rear-wiper-issues-can-save-you-time-money-and-frustration-down-the-line&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A little attention and care can make a big difference in how well your rear wiper performs, especially when you need it most.

### Regular Maintenance Tips

Check the rear wiper blade regularly for cracks, stiffness, or missing rubber segments. These signs mean it’s time to replace the blade, which is often cheaper and easier than fixing a damaged wiper arm or motor.

Keep the wiper arm clean and free from dirt or debris. Dirt buildup can cause the wiper to skip or scratch the glass, reducing effectiveness.

* Wipe the blade with a damp cloth every few weeks to remove dirt and grime.
* Clear any leaves or twigs stuck around the wiper base to prevent jams.
* Test the wiper occasionally by activating it and observing its motion—smooth, consistent wipes mean it’s working well.

Have you noticed your rear wiper moving slower or making unusual noises? That’s a sign to give it some TLC before it stops working completely.

### When To Visit A Mechanic

If your rear wiper doesn’t turn off, moves erratically, or fails to respond to controls, it’s time to seek professional help. These symptoms often indicate electrical issues or motor problems beyond simple maintenance.

Don’t wait for the problem to worsen—ignoring faulty wipers can compromise your rear visibility, especially in bad weather. A mechanic can diagnose issues like blown fuses, faulty wiring, or a worn-out motor and fix them properly.

Think about how much you rely on your rear wiper during rain or snow. Is it worth risking your safety by delaying repairs?




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Turn Off The Rear Windshield Wiper On A Ford Escape?

To turn off the rear windshield wiper on a Ford Escape, rotate the rear wiper control knob to the "OFF" position. This knob is usually located on the right side of the steering wheel or on the wiper stalk.

### Where Is The Rear Wiper Control On Ford Escape?

The rear wiper control on a Ford Escape is typically found on the right-hand stalk behind the steering wheel. It may be a twist knob or a switch labeled specifically for the rear wiper.

### Can I Disable The Rear Wiper Without Affecting Front Wipers?

Yes, the rear wiper on a Ford Escape operates independently. You can turn it off or on without impacting the front windshield wipers by using the rear wiper control knob.

### What If The Rear Wiper Won’t Turn Off On My Ford Escape?

If the rear wiper won’t turn off, check the control knob for damage or debris. If the issue persists, it may require professional inspection for electrical or motor faults.
