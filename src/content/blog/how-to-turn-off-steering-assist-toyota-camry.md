## What Is Steering Assist

Steering Assist is a feature in the Toyota Camry designed to make driving easier and safer. It helps you steer the car by providing gentle corrective inputs, especially during lane keeping or avoiding unintentional drifting. Understanding what Steering Assist does can help you decide when you might want to turn it off.

### How Steering Assist Works

Steering Assist uses sensors and cameras to monitor your position on the road. If it detects that you are drifting out of your lane without signaling, it gently nudges the steering wheel to keep you centered. This support can reduce the chance of accidents caused by distracted or tired driving.

### When Steering Assist Activates

The system activates mainly on highways or well-marked roads where lane markings are clear. It works best at speeds above 40 mph, helping you stay safe during long drives. But if the road is poorly marked or in tight city traffic, it may not function as intended.

### Why You Might Want To Turn It Off

You might find Steering Assist annoying if it feels like it’s fighting your steering inputs. Some drivers prefer full control, especially in tricky conditions like winding roads or heavy traffic. It’s important to know you can disable it temporarily if it interferes with your driving style.

### How Steering Assist Affects Your Driving Experience

Many drivers appreciate the extra help on long trips, as it reduces fatigue and improves focus. However, it’s easy to become over-reliant on the system, which could dull your steering skills. Staying aware of how much assistance you want is key to staying confident behind the wheel.
