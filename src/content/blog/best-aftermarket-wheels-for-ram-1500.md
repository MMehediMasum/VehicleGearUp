## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Lug Nuts Fit Ram 1500 Aftermarket Wheels?



Lug nuts with 9/16-18 or M14x1. 5 thread sizes fit most Ram 1500 aftermarket wheels. Always check your wheel specs before buying.



### Are Wheel Hub Assemblies Necessary For Ram 1500 Upgrades?



Yes, quality wheel hub assemblies ensure smooth rotation and safety. Compatible models include 2012-2018 Ram 1500 and 2019 Ram 1500 Classic.



### Can I Use Lug Nuts From Chevy Or Ford On Ram 1500?



Yes, many M14x1. 5 lug nuts fit Chevy, Ford, and Ram 1500 wheels. Confirm thread size and seat type before purchase.



### What Types Of Lug Nuts Are Best For Ram 1500 Wheels?



Closed-end spline and bulge acorn lug nuts are popular for Ram 1500 aftermarket wheels. They offer durability and a secure fit.



### How Do I Choose The Right Wheel Rim Size For Ram 1500?



Common aftermarket rim sizes for Ram 1500 range from 20x9 inches with a 5x139. 7 bolt pattern. Match size to tire and suspension.



### Do Aftermarket Wheels Affect Ram 1500's Abs Sensors?



Compatible hub assemblies support ABS sensors on Ram 1500. Ensure parts specify ABS compatibility to maintain safety features.
