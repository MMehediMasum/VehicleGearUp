## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls in your Honda Civic is the first step to reset the time. The controls are usually easy to find but can vary by model year. Knowing where to look saves time and avoids frustration.

The clock controls are often integrated into the dashboard or the infotainment system. Some models have physical buttons near the radio or display screen. Others use touchscreens with menu options for clock settings.

Understanding the type of controls your Honda Civic has helps in adjusting the clock quickly. The location and design depend on the car’s model and year. Here are common places to check for clock controls.

### Dashboard Buttons Near The Radio

Many Honda Civics have small buttons labeled “Clock,” “H,” “M,” or similar near the radio. These buttons adjust the hours and minutes. Press the “Clock” or “Setup” button to access the clock settings.

### Infotainment Touchscreen Menu

Newer Civic models often use a touchscreen for clock settings. Tap the “Settings” or “Setup” icon on the screen. Look for a “Clock” or “Time” option within the menu. Use on-screen prompts to change the time.

### Steering Wheel Controls

Some models allow clock adjustments through steering wheel buttons. Check for a “Menu” or “Settings” button on the wheel. Navigate through the options to find the clock settings. Use arrow keys to adjust the time.

### User Manual Reference

The owner’s manual provides exact details about clock controls. It shows the location and usage instructions. Keep the manual handy for clear guidance, especially if controls differ by model year.




## Resetting The Clock Manually

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-clock-manually-in-a-honda-civic-is-simple-and-quick-it-helps-keep-your-time-accurate-without-relying-on-external-devices&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the clock manually in a Honda Civic is simple and quick. It helps keep your time accurate without relying on external devices. You can adjust the clock using the dashboard buttons directly. This section explains how to do it step by step.

### Using The Dashboard Buttons

Locate the clock or time button on your dashboard. Press and hold this button until the time display starts blinking. This means the clock is ready for adjustment. Some models use a “Clock” or “H” and “M” button for setting hours and minutes. Use these buttons to control the time easily.

### Adjusting Hours And Minutes

* Press the button marked “H” to change the hour.
* Press the button marked “M” to change the minutes.
* Each press moves the time forward by one unit.
* Hold the button for faster changes.
* Stop pressing when the correct time shows.

Once the time is set, press the clock button again to save it. The display will stop blinking. Your Honda Civic’s clock now shows the correct time.

## Resetting The Clock Via Infotainment System

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=access-the-honda-civics-infotainment-system-to-adjust-the-clock-easily-use-the-settings-menu-to-find-and-set-the-correct-time-quickly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Access the Honda Civic's infotainment system to adjust the clock easily. Use the settings menu to find and set the correct time quickly. This process helps keep your dashboard clock accurate and clear.

Resetting the clock in a Honda Civic using the infotainment system is simple. This method allows you to adjust the time quickly. The process involves accessing the car's settings through the touchscreen. Follow these easy steps to ensure your clock displays the correct time.

### Accessing The Settings Menu

Begin by turning on your Honda Civic's ignition. Make sure the infotainment system is active. Look for the "Settings" icon on the touchscreen display. Tap on it to access the settings menu. This menu contains various options for system adjustments.

### Navigating To Clock Settings

Once in the settings menu, find the "Clock" or "Time" option. This option might be under a "System" or "General" category. Tap on it to enter the clock settings. You will now see options related to time adjustments.

### Setting The Correct Time

In the clock settings, locate the hour and minute adjustment options. Tap the '+' or '-' buttons to change the time. Ensure the AM or PM setting is correct. Once satisfied with the time, save your changes. Your Honda Civic's clock is now updated.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-your-honda-civics-clock-can-save-time-and-frustration-some-problems-occur-due-to-simple-reasons-like-power-interruptions-or-user-errors&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with your Honda Civic's clock can save time and frustration. Some problems occur due to simple reasons like power interruptions or user errors. Understanding these issues helps you fix the clock quickly and keep your schedule accurate.

### Clock Not Responding

The clock may not respond due to a system glitch or faulty buttons. Try these steps:

* Turn the ignition off and on again to reset the system.
* Press and hold the clock button for a few seconds.
* Check if the buttons are stuck or dirty; clean gently if needed.
* Inspect the fuse related to the clock or radio system.

If the clock still does not respond, the issue might require professional help or a deeper electrical check.

### Time Reset After Battery Change

The clock often resets after changing the car battery. This happens because the power source is interrupted. Fix the time by:

* Turn on the ignition without starting the engine.
* Use the clock or setup buttons to enter the time-setting mode.
* Adjust hours and minutes using the appropriate buttons.
* Save the settings and turn off the ignition to confirm.

Keep a note of your clock settings before battery replacement to reset faster. Some Honda Civic models may lose other saved data too, like radio presets.




## Tips To Keep Your Clock Accurate

Keeping your Honda Civic's clock accurate is more than just a convenience—it's about staying on schedule and avoiding those small moments of frustration when you realize your time is off. Small steps can make a big difference in ensuring your clock reflects the correct time throughout your drives. Let’s dive into some practical tips that will help you maintain precise time on your dashboard.

### Regular Time Checks

Make it a habit to glance at your car’s clock whenever you start your engine. This quick check takes just seconds but helps you spot any time drift early.

If you notice the clock is off by a few minutes, reset it immediately. This prevents small errors from piling up over days or weeks.

Try syncing your clock with a reliable time source, like your watch or a wall clock you trust. Keeping this routine can save you from running late or arriving too early.

### Syncing With Gps Or Phone

Many newer Honda Civics can sync their clocks automatically with your phone or GPS system. This feature ensures the time adjusts itself when you cross time zones or during daylight saving changes.

Check your car’s manual to see if your model supports this syncing option. If it does, connect your phone via Bluetooth or USB and enable time sync in the settings.

Think about how often your phone updates its time. If your phone’s clock is accurate, syncing it with your car can eliminate the need for manual adjustments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Clock In My Honda Civic?

To reset the clock, press and hold the "Clock" button until the time blinks. Use the "H" and "M" buttons to set hours and minutes. Press "Clock" again to save the new time.

### Can I Reset The Honda Civic Clock Without The Manual?

Yes, you can reset the clock using dashboard buttons. Locate the "Clock," "H," and "M" buttons. Follow button instructions to adjust time quickly without needing the manual.

### Why Won’t My Honda Civic Clock Reset Properly?

If the clock won’t reset, check the car’s battery and fuse. A low battery or blown fuse can disrupt clock settings. Reset after fixing these issues.

### Does Resetting The Clock Affect Other Car Settings?

No, resetting the clock only changes the time display. Other car settings like radio presets and system preferences remain unaffected during the clock reset.
