## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Vinyl Type Is Best For Car Decals?



Cast vinyl is best for car decals. It offers durability, flexibility, and resists fading. It conforms well to curves and lasts years outdoors.



### How To Choose Vinyl Color For Car Decals?



Choose vinyl color based on your vehicle’s color and design contrast. Glossy and metallic finishes add visual appeal and visibility.



### Are Custom Vinyl Decals Weather-resistant?



Yes, quality vinyl decals are UV and water-resistant. They withstand sun, rain, and car washes without peeling or fading.



### Can I Apply Vinyl Decals On Any Car Surface?



Vinyl decals stick best on smooth, clean surfaces like glass and painted metal. Avoid textured or dirty areas for best adhesion.



### How To Install Vinyl Decals Without Bubbles?



Use an air release vinyl with a squeegee. Apply slowly, smoothing out bubbles as you go for a flawless finish.
