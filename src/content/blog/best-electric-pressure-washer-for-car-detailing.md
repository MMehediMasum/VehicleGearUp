## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Psi Is Ideal For Car Detailing Pressure Washers?



For car detailing, 1900 to 2100 PSI is ideal. It cleans effectively without damaging paint. Higher PSI models are better for tough grime but require careful use to avoid scratches.



### How Important Is Gpm In Electric Pressure Washers?



GPM (Gallons Per Minute) measures water flow. Higher GPM means faster cleaning. For cars, 1. 2 to 3. 6 GPM balances power and water use for efficient washing.



### Do Foam Cannons Improve Car Cleaning Results?



Yes, foam cannons apply soap evenly and loosen dirt. They enhance cleaning by reducing scrubbing time and minimizing paint scratches during washing.



### Are Electric Pressure Washers Easy To Maneuver During Car Washing?



Models with lockable wheels and telescoping handles offer easy maneuverability. Lightweight and compact designs improve user comfort and control when detailing cars.



### Can I Use Electric Pressure Washers For Surfaces Other Than Cars?



Absolutely. Most models work well on fences, patios, driveways, and garden tools. Adjustable nozzles allow safe cleaning on multiple surfaces without damage.



### What Safety Features Should I Look For In Pressure Washers?



Look for anti-tipping designs, overload protection, and quick-connect nozzles. These features ensure safe operation and prevent damage to the washer and your vehicle.
