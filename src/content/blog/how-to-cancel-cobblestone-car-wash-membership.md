### Refunds And Final Billing

Refund policies vary depending on how you paid and the timing of your cancellation. Some memberships offer prorated refunds for unused days, while others don’t provide any refund at all. You should ask Cobblestone Car Wash directly about their policy.

After cancellation, expect a final bill that sums up any remaining charges. This could include fees for early cancellation, unpaid services, or taxes. Make sure to review this carefully to avoid surprises.
