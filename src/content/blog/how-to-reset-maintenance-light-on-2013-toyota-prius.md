That little warning can be confusing and even a bit frustrating. But don’t worry—resetting the maintenance light is easier than you think. ## Locate The Maintenance Light

Finding the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Prius is simple. This light helps you know when your car needs a checkup or service. Knowing where it is on the dashboard makes it easier to reset after maintenance. Start by understanding the dashboard indicators.

### Identify The Dashboard Indicators

The dashboard has many lights and symbols. Each shows different information about your car's condition. The maintenance light usually looks like a small wrench or says "MAINT REQD." It is often amber or orange. This light appears on the main instrument panel behind the steering wheel. Pay attention to other warning lights too, but the maintenance light is specific to scheduled services.

### Understand The Maintenance Required Light

The maintenance required light signals that your Prius needs routine service. This could be an oil change, tire rotation, or other scheduled check. The light turns on after a certain number of miles driven since the last reset. It does not mean there is an emergency, but that it is time for regular care. Recognizing this light helps you keep your car running smoothly and avoid bigger problems.




## Prepare The Vehicle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparing-your-2013-toyota-prius-is-the-first-step-to-reset-the-maintenance-light-proper-preparation-ensures-the-reset-process-goes-smoothly&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparing your 2013 Toyota Prius is the first step to reset the maintenance light. Proper preparation ensures the reset process goes smoothly. It also avoids errors or safety issues. Follow these simple steps to get your vehicle ready.

### Turn Off The Engine

Make sure the engine is completely off before starting. Do not just switch to accessory mode. Turn off the engine and remove your foot from the brake. This step helps the vehicle’s system reset correctly.

### Ensure The Vehicle Is In Park

Check that the gear shift is set to Park (P). The reset will not work if the car is in any other gear. Confirm the parking brake is engaged for extra safety. This keeps the car stable during the reset process.

## Reset Procedure Using Odometer Button

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=press-and-hold-the-odometer-button-while-turning-the-ignition-to-the-on-position-keep-holding-the-button-until-the-maintenance-light-blinks-and-turns-off&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Press and hold the odometer button while turning the ignition to the "On" position. Keep holding the button until the maintenance light blinks and turns off. This simple process resets the maintenance light on a 2013 Toyota Prius.

Resetting the maintenance light on a 2013 Toyota Prius can seem daunting, but with the right steps, it's a breeze. Using the odometer button is one of the simplest ways to reset this light. You don’t need special tools or a mechanic to do it. Just a little patience and following the steps below can save you both time and money.

### Turn The Ignition To On Without Starting

Begin by sitting comfortably in the driver’s seat and ensuring your vehicle is parked. Insert the key and turn the ignition to the “On” position, but be careful not to start the engine. You should see the dashboard lights illuminate, indicating you’re ready for the next step.

### Press And Hold The Odometer Button

Locate the odometer button on your dashboard. It’s usually to the side of the speedometer. Press and hold this button. Keep an eye on the odometer display; it should show the current mileage or trip reading. Holding this button is crucial for resetting the maintenance light.

### Wait For The Light To Flash And Turn Off

While holding the odometer button, you’ll notice the maintenance light begin to flash. This is a good sign—it means the reset is in progress. Continue holding the button until the light turns off completely. Once it’s off, release the button. Now your maintenance light should be reset, giving you peace of mind on the road. Isn’t it satisfying to tackle these small car issues on your own? With just a few steps, you've maintained your Prius like a pro!

## Alternative Reset Method

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=if-the-traditional-way-of-resetting-the-maintenance-light-on-your-2013-toyota-prius-feels-a-bit-tricky-theres-a-handy-alternative-you-can-try-this-method-uses-the-cars-multi-information-display-which-many-drivers-overlook&w=424&h=324&c=7)

If the traditional way of resetting the maintenance light on your 2013 Toyota Prius feels a bit tricky, there's a handy alternative you can try. This method uses the car’s Multi-Information Display, which many drivers overlook. It’s a straightforward approach that puts control right at your fingertips, making it easy to keep your Prius maintenance alerts accurate.

### Using The Multi-information Display

The Multi-Information Display (MID) shows important vehicle info, including fuel economy and maintenance reminders. You can use it to reset the maintenance light without any tools or special equipment.

Here’s what you need to do:

* Turn the ignition switch to the “ON” position without starting the engine.
* Locate the MID controls on the steering wheel or dashboard.
* Navigate through the menu until you find the “Settings” or “Vehicle Settings” option.
* Select “Maintenance” or “Maintenance Reset” from the list.

This method gives you direct access to reset options, making the process simple and quick. Have you ever noticed how much easier it is to interact with your car’s digital menus once you get familiar with them?

### Accessing The Maintenance Reset Menu

Once you reach the Maintenance Reset menu, the next steps are clear and easy to follow.

* Highlight the “Reset” option using the arrow buttons.
* Press the “Enter” or “OK” button to confirm your choice.
* Wait a few seconds until the system confirms the maintenance light has been reset.
* Turn off the ignition and then start the engine to check if the light is gone.

This approach avoids the guesswork and doesn't require you to disconnect your battery or fiddle with fuses. It’s a neat trick I learned from a fellow Prius owner, and it saved me a lot of time during my last oil change. Wouldn’t it be great if more cars made maintenance reminders this user-friendly?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Maintenance Light On A 2013 Prius?

To reset the maintenance light, turn the ignition to "ON" without starting the engine. Press and hold the trip meter reset button until the light blinks and turns off. This process clears the maintenance reminder on your 2013 Toyota Prius.

### What Tools Are Needed To Reset The Maintenance Light?

No special tools are required to reset the maintenance light on a 2013 Prius. You only need access to the ignition and the trip meter reset button on the dashboard. The reset is a simple manual process.

### How Often Should I Reset The Maintenance Light?

Reset the maintenance light after each scheduled service or oil change. This keeps your Prius’s maintenance reminders accurate and helps track vehicle servicing intervals properly.

### Will Resetting The Maintenance Light Fix My Prius’s Issues?

No, resetting the maintenance light only clears the reminder. It does not fix any mechanical or maintenance issues. Always perform necessary servicing before resetting the light.
