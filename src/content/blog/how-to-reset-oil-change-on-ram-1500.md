## When To Reset The Oil Change Light

Knowing when to reset the oil change light on your Ram 1500 is crucial for keeping your truck in top shape. This light is more than just a reminder—it’s a signal that your vehicle needs attention to stay running smoothly. Ignoring it or resetting it too soon can lead to problems that affect performance and safety.

### Signs Your Ram 1500 Needs An Oil Reset

Your Ram 1500’s oil change light will usually come on after a set mileage or time interval, depending on your driving habits. Here are some clear signs you should reset the oil change light:

* **The oil has been changed recently:**After completing an oil change, the light should be reset to start tracking the new oil life.
* **The light remains on despite a recent oil change:**This means the system has not been reset and will continue to alert you unnecessarily.
* **You’ve reached the manufacturer’s recommended mileage or time interval:**Typically, this is around every 7,500 to 10,000 miles or 6 months.

Have you ever wondered why the light stays on even after you’ve changed the oil? Resetting it manually tells your truck’s computer that maintenance is done, avoiding confusion.

### Why Resetting The Oil Change Light Matters

Resetting the oil change light after an oil change isn’t just a routine task—it directly impacts your vehicle’s health. If the light is not reset, your Ram 1500 may continue to alert you unnecessarily, which can lead to ignoring the warning when it's genuinely needed.

Additionally, resetting helps maintain accurate tracking of oil life, so you get timely reminders based on actual usage. This prevents premature oil changes that waste money or delayed changes that risk engine damage.

Imagine driving without these alerts—would you remember exactly when to change your oil? The light acts as a practical guide, and resetting it keeps this system reliable.

## Resetting Oil Change On Ram 1500 Models

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-oil-change-on-ram-1500-models&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting Oil Change on Ram 1500 Models

Regular oil changes keep your Ram 1500 running smoothly. After an oil change, resetting the oil change indicator is important. It helps track maintenance schedules. Here's how to reset the oil change indicator on different Ram 1500 models.

### Reset For 2010-2018 Models

Follow these simple steps for older Ram 1500 models:

* Insert the key and turn it to the "On" position. Don't start the engine.
* Press the gas pedal slowly three times within 10 seconds.
* Turn the ignition off.
* Start the engine to check if the reset was successful.

### Reset For 2019 And Newer Models

Resetting the oil change indicator on newer models is easy:

* Press the start button twice without pressing the brake. This puts the truck in "Run" mode.
* Use the controls on the steering wheel to navigate to the "Vehicle Info" section.
* Select "Oil Life" and hold the "OK" button until it resets to 100%.
* Turn off the ignition and start the engine to confirm the reset.

## Step-by-step Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-change-light-on-your-ram-1500-is-simple-it-helps-keep-track-of-your-trucks-maintenance&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil change light on your Ram 1500 is simple. It helps keep track of your truck’s maintenance. Follow these clear steps to reset the oil life monitor quickly. No tools needed. Just use your truck’s dashboard controls.

### Accessing The Oil Life Monitor

Start by turning the ignition to the “On” position. Do not start the engine. Use the steering wheel buttons to navigate the menu. Look for the “Vehicle Info” or “Oil Life” option. This screen shows the current oil life percentage. It tells you when the next oil change is due.

### Navigating The Menu

Use the arrow buttons on the steering wheel to scroll through the menu. Find the “Oil Life” or “Oil Life Reset” option. Press the “OK” or “Select” button to choose it. The screen will ask if you want to reset the oil life. Use the arrow buttons to highlight “Yes.” Press “OK” to confirm.

### Completing The Reset

After confirmation, the oil life percentage will reset to 100%. Turn off the ignition and start the engine to check. The oil change light should be off. If not, repeat the steps carefully. Resetting ensures your truck tracks oil changes accurately.

### Oil Change Light Not Resetting

The oil change light may stay on after resetting. This happens if the reset procedure is incomplete or done incorrectly.

* Ensure the ignition is in the "On" position without starting the engine.
* Follow the exact reset steps in the owner’s manual.
* Check if the vehicle battery is low; weak power can affect the system.
* Try disconnecting the battery for a few minutes, then reconnect it.
* If the light persists, inspect the oil sensor for faults or dirt.

Ignoring this light can cause confusion about oil change needs. Proper reset ensures your truck alerts you at the right time.

### Dashboard Display Problems

The dashboard may not show reset information clearly. This issue can confuse drivers and affect maintenance tracking.

* Check the dashboard brightness settings and adjust them.

* Inspect the dashboard for any physical damage or loose connections.
* Perform a full vehicle system scan to detect electronic errors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Oil Change Light On Ram 1500?

To reset the oil change light, turn the ignition to ON without starting. Press the accelerator pedal fully three times within 10 seconds. The light will flash and then turn off, indicating the reset is complete.

### When Should I Reset Oil Change Light On Ram 1500?

Reset the oil change light immediately after completing an oil change. This keeps the vehicle’s maintenance system accurate and ensures timely reminders for the next oil change.

### Can I Reset Oil Change Light Without A Scan Tool?

Yes, you can reset the oil change light manually using the ignition and accelerator pedal method. No scan tool is necessary for most Ram 1500 models.

### What If Oil Change Light Doesn’t Reset On Ram 1500?

If the light won’t reset, double-check the reset steps and oil change completion. If it persists, consult a mechanic or use a diagnostic scan tool to clear the error.
