## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Grease Is Best For Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Lucas Oil 10005 Red 'N' Tacky Grease is ideal for trailer wheel bearings. It offers excellent adhesion and high-temperature resistance, ensuring long-lasting protection and smooth bearing operation.



### Is Marine Grease Suitable For Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Yes, marine-grade greases like STAR BRITE and Lucas Oil 10682 provide excellent water resistance. They protect trailer wheel bearings from moisture and corrosion, making them perfect for boat trailers.



### How Often Should I Grease Trailer Wheel Bearings?



Trailer wheel bearings should be greased at least once a year or every 12,000 miles. Frequent inspections and re-greasing ensure smooth performance and prevent premature bearing failure.



### Can High-temperature Grease Improve Bearing Life?



High-temperature greases like Timken GR224TUB and AtomLube Ultra Heavy Duty Red Grease withstand heat well. They reduce wear and extend trailer bearing life under heavy loads and heat.



### What Makes Lithium Grease Good For Trailer Bearings?



Lithium greases, such as the Wheel Bearing Grease Red and Tacky, provide excellent water resistance, oxidation stability, and mechanical protection, ensuring reliable lubrication under harsh trailer operating conditions.



### Are Grease Cartridges Or Tubs Better For Trailers?



Grease cartridges like Timken GR224C are convenient for grease guns and reduce mess. Tubs offer larger quantities but require manual application. Choose based on your maintenance preferences.
