## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Spark Plugs Are Best For Mercury 2 Stroke Outboards?



Iridium spark plugs like BDFHYK 4495 offer excellent performance and durability. They fit many Mercury 2 Stroke models and improve ignition reliability and fuel efficiency.



### How Often Should I Replace Spark Plugs On Mercury 2 Stroke?



Replace spark plugs every 100 hours or annually. Regular replacement ensures optimal engine performance and prevents misfires or starting issues.



### Are Iridium Spark Plugs Better For Mercury Outboards?



Yes, iridium spark plugs provide longer life, better ignition, and improved fuel efficiency compared to standard plugs. They withstand high heat and corrosion well.



### Can I Use Universal Spark Plugs On Mercury 2 Stroke Engines?



Universal plugs like BDFHYK 4495 are compatible with many Mercury 2 Stroke outboards. Always check model compatibility to ensure proper fit and performance.



### What Signs Indicate Spark Plug Failure In Mercury Outboards?



Engine misfires, hard starts, rough idle, and decreased power often signal spark plug issues. Inspect and replace spark plugs if these symptoms occur.



### Do Spark Plug Wire Sets Improve Mercury Outboard Performance?
