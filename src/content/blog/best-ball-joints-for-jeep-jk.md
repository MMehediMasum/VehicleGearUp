Ball joints connect the control arms to the steering knuckles. They allow your wheels to move up and down and turn left or right. Over time, these parts wear out and cause poor handling or noise. Choosing durable, heavy-duty ball joints designed for Jeep JK models is important.



Products like Rough Country Heavy Duty, TeraFlex, and MOOG offer reliable fit and performance. Many kits fit Dana 30 and Dana 44 axles, common on Jeep Wranglers from 2007 to 2018.
