## Choosing The Right Wipers

Choosing the right windshield wipers for your Ford Fusion is crucial for clear visibility and safe driving, especially during bad weather. Not every wiper fits every model, so paying attention to details will save you time and hassle. Let’s break down how to pick the perfect wipers that match your car’s needs.

### Check Your Ford Fusion Model Year

Your Ford Fusion’s model year directly influences the type and size of wipers you need. Different years can have variations in windshield size and wiper arm design. If you don’t check this, you might end up with blades that don’t fit properly or leave streaks.

Look inside your owner’s manual or use online tools by entering your Fusion’s year and trim. This step ensures you buy blades designed specifically for your car’s windshield shape and mounting system. Have you ever bought wipers only to find out they were the wrong size? Avoid that frustration by confirming your model year first.

### Select The Correct Wiper Size

Wiper size is not one-size-fits-all, even within the same car model. Your Ford Fusion usually requires two different sizes for the driver and passenger sides. Installing the wrong length can cause poor contact with the glass or even damage your windshield.

To find the right sizes, check the manual or measure the existing blades. Sizes often range between 18 to 26 inches for Fusions, but it’s best to be exact. If you’re unsure, local auto parts stores often have size charts or staff who can help you pick the right blades.

### Consider Wiper Blade Types

Wiper blades come in three main types: traditional frame-style, beam-style, and hybrid. Each offers different benefits depending on your driving conditions and budget.

* **Frame-style blades**have a metal framework supporting the rubber blade. They’re affordable and common but can wear unevenly over time.
* **Beam-style blades**are more modern, with a flexible design that hugs the windshield better for improved wiping in rain and snow.
* **Hybrid blades**combine the benefits of both, offering durability and smooth performance.

Think about your local weather—if you face heavy rain or snow, beam or hybrid blades might serve you better. What kind of wipers have you found work best during storms? Your choice here can make a big difference in visibility and safety.




## Preparing For Installation

Preparing for installation is the first key to a smooth windshield wiper replacement on your Ford Fusion. Taking a few simple steps before starting saves time and avoids damage. Proper preparation ensures safety and efficiency.

### Gather Necessary Tools

Collect all tools before you begin. Usually, you need:

* New windshield wipers compatible with Ford Fusion
* Flathead screwdriver or small pry tool
* Clean cloth or gloves to protect the glass and hands

Having these ready makes the process quick and easy.

### Park On A Flat Surface

Choose a flat, stable place to park your car. This prevents movement during installation. Turn off the engine and set the parking brake for extra safety. Avoid sloped or busy areas.

### Lift The Wiper Arms

Gently lift each wiper arm away from the windshield. They should stay upright without support. Be careful not to snap them back onto the glass. This step gives clear access to the wiper blades.

  


## Removing Old Wiper Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lift-the-wiper-arm-away-from-the-windshield-carefully-press-the-small-tab-on-the-wiper-blade-to-release-and-slide-it-off&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lift the wiper arm away from the windshield carefully. Press the small tab on the wiper blade to release and slide it off.

Removing old wiper blades from your Ford Fusion is a simple task. It requires some care to avoid damaging the wiper arm or windshield. Knowing the right steps makes the process smooth. Let's dive into the steps involved in removing the old wiper blades.

### Locate The Release Tab

First, find the release tab on the wiper blade. It's usually a small button or latch. This tab holds the blade to the arm. Pressing it allows you to detach the blade. Make sure your car is turned off for safety.

### Detach The Blade From The Arm

With the release tab located, press it firmly. While holding the tab, slide the wiper blade downward. This action should release the blade from the wiper arm. Be gentle to avoid breaking the tab.

### Handle Wipers Carefully

Handling wipers with care is crucial. Avoid letting the wiper arm snap back onto the windshield. It can cause cracks or scratches. Gently lower the arm onto a cloth or towel after removing the blade. This protects both the arm and the windshield.



## Installing New Wiper Blades

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-new-wiper-blades-on-your-ford-fusion-keeps-your-windshield-clear-and-your-driving-safe-this-process-is-simple-and-requires-no-special-tools&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing new wiper blades on your Ford Fusion keeps your windshield clear and your driving safe. This process is simple and requires no special tools. Follow the steps carefully to fit the blades correctly. Proper installation ensures effective wiping and longer blade life.

### Align The New Blade With The Arm

Hold the new wiper blade close to the wiper arm. Make sure the blade's connector matches the arm's hook shape. Gently align the blade so it fits into the arm’s hook. Avoid forcing the blade to prevent damage.

### Secure The Blade Into Place

Push the blade into the arm’s hook until it clicks. The click sound confirms the blade is locked in place. Double-check by gently pulling the blade. It should stay firmly attached to the arm.

### Check For Proper Attachment

Lower the wiper arm slowly onto the windshield. Make sure the blade lies flat and straight against the glass. Test the wipers by turning them on briefly. Look for smooth movement and no gaps during wiping.

## Testing The New Wipers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=testing-the-new-windshield-wipers-ensures-they-work-correctly-and-safely-this-step-helps-catch-any-installation-issues-early&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Testing the new windshield wipers ensures they work correctly and safely. This step helps catch any installation issues early. Proper testing guarantees clear vision during rain or snow.

### Lower The Wiper Arms

Gently lower the wiper arms back onto the windshield. Avoid letting them snap down to prevent damage. Position them so the blades sit flat on the glass.

### Activate The Wipers

Turn on the wipers using the control inside your Ford Fusion. Observe their movement across the windshield. Check the different speed settings to confirm smooth operation.

### Inspect For Smooth Operation

Watch the blades carefully as they wipe the glass. They should move without skipping or leaving streaks. Listen for unusual noises like squeaking or chattering.

If the wipers do not perform well, adjust or reinstall them. Proper function keeps your driving safe in bad weather.




## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ford-fusions-windshield-wipers-in-top-shape-is-easier-than-you-think-proper-maintenance-not-only-extends-the-life-of-the-blades-but-also-ensures-clear-visibility-during-those-unexpected-rain-showers&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Ford Fusion’s windshield wipers in top shape is easier than you think. Proper maintenance not only extends the life of the blades but also ensures clear visibility during those unexpected rain showers. Let’s look at some practical tips to help you maintain your wipers effectively.

### Clean Wiper Blades Regularly

Have you noticed streaks or smudges after your wipers run? Dirt and grime build up on the rubber blades, reducing their effectiveness. Use a soft cloth soaked in warm soapy water to gently wipe the blades every few weeks.

This simple habit removes debris and prevents scratches on your windshield. Don’t forget to also clean the windshield surface—it works hand-in-hand with your blades for a clear view.

### Replace Blades Every 6-12 Months

Even if your wipers look fine, the rubber can harden or crack over time. Most manufacturers recommend changing blades every 6 to 12 months. Have you checked the condition of your blades recently?

Replacing them on schedule keeps the wiping smooth and prevents damage to your glass. Consider keeping a spare set in your car for quick swaps after harsh weather.

### Avoid Using Wipers On Dry Windshield

Running your wipers on a dry windshield can cause unnecessary wear and tear. The blades drag across the glass without lubrication, which can lead to scratching and faster blade deterioration.

Instead, use washer fluid to wet the windshield first. This protects your blades and improves their cleaning power. Have you noticed how much smoother your wipers move with a bit of fluid?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Remove Old Windshield Wipers On Ford Fusion?

Lift the wiper arm away from the windshield. Press the release tab on the wiper blade. Slide the blade down to remove it. Handle carefully to avoid damaging the wiper arm.

### What Size Windshield Wipers Fit Ford Fusion?

Ford Fusion typically uses 24-inch wipers on the driver's side and 20-inch on the passenger side. Always check your owner's manual or measure your current blades to confirm sizes before purchase.

### How To Install New Windshield Wipers On Ford Fusion?

Align the new blade’s hook with the wiper arm. Slide it into the arm until it clicks securely. Gently lower the arm back to the windshield. Test the wipers to ensure proper installation.

### Can I Install Windshield Wipers On Ford Fusion Myself?

Yes, installing windshield wipers on a Ford Fusion is simple. It requires no special tools and takes only a few minutes. Follow the correct steps to avoid damage and ensure a proper fit.
