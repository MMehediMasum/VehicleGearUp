## Remote Start Basics

Understanding the basics of remote start is essential before you try to use this feature on your Volvo XC90. Remote start lets you power up your vehicle’s engine from a distance using the key fob. It’s a simple yet powerful convenience that can change how you prepare for your drives.

### What Is Remote Start?

Remote start is a function that allows you to start your Volvo XC90's engine without physically being inside the car. Using the key fob, you can activate the engine from a short distance, usually up to a few hundred feet away. This feature is especially useful in extreme weather, giving your car time to warm up or cool down before you get in.

Instead of rushing to get inside and start the engine manually, remote start gives you a head start. It powers the engine, heater, or air conditioner, depending on what you need. You don’t have to worry about carrying extra tools or gadgets—just your key fob.

### Benefits Of Remote Starting Your Volvo Xc90

Remote starting your Volvo XC90 brings several practical benefits you might not have considered. One of the biggest advantages is comfort. Imagine stepping into a warm car on a freezing winter morning without having to scrape ice off the windshield.

Another benefit is safety. By starting your car remotely, you ensure it’s running and the interior is at a comfortable temperature before you enter. This reduces the risk of slipping on ice or rushing in a hurry.

Remote start also saves time. Instead of waiting inside the vehicle for it to reach a good temperature, you can start it while finishing your morning routine or packing the last things. Have you ever thought about how much smoother your mornings could be with this small change?

## Step-by-step Remote Start Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-remote-start-feature-in-your-volvo-xc90-allows-you-to-warm-up-or-cool-down-the-car-before-you-get-inside-this-process-saves-time-and-adds-comfort-especially-in-extreme-weather&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The remote start feature in your Volvo XC90 allows you to warm up or cool down the car before you get inside. This process saves time and adds comfort, especially in extreme weather. Follow the simple steps below to use the remote start with your key fob.

### Locate The Lock And Remote Start Buttons

First, find your key fob. Look for the lock button, usually marked with a closed padlock icon. The remote start button is often a circular arrow or a small car symbol. These two buttons are essential for the remote start process.

### Press Sequence For Remote Start

* Press the lock button once to secure the vehicle.
* Within three seconds, press and hold the remote start button.
* Keep holding the remote start button for about three seconds.

This sequence sends a signal to the car to start the engine remotely.

### Confirming Engine Activation

After pressing the buttons, watch for the vehicle’s lights to flash. The horn may sound briefly, showing the engine has started. Check the key fob display for confirmation if available. Your Volvo XC90 is now running and ready for you.

## Troubleshooting Remote Start

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-the-remote-start-feature-on-your-volvo-xc90-can-save-you-time-and-frustration-sometimes-the-remote-start-might-not-work-as-expected-due-to-simple-issues-that-you-can-easily-fix&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting the remote start feature on your Volvo XC90 can save you time and frustration. Sometimes, the remote start might not work as expected due to simple issues that you can easily fix. Understanding common problems helps you get your vehicle running remotely without unnecessary trips to the dealer.

### Common Issues And Fixes

One frequent problem is a weak or dead key fob battery. If your remote start isn’t responding, try replacing the battery first. It’s a quick fix that often resolves the issue.

Another issue could be that your vehicle’s doors or hood are not fully closed. The remote start system won’t activate if it detects any open entry points. Double-check all doors, the trunk, and the hood before using the remote start.

Sometimes, the car's battery voltage might be too low for the system to engage. If you notice dim lights or slow electronics, the remote start might be disabled to protect the battery. Charging or replacing your car battery can restore functionality.

### When Remote Start Doesn’t Activate

If pressing the remote start button does nothing, check if your Volvo XC90 is in “Park.” The remote start won’t work if the transmission isn’t in the correct position. This is a common oversight that’s easy to fix.

Another reason could be the vehicle’s security system being triggered. If the alarm is active, the remote start will be disabled to prevent theft. Make sure your alarm is disarmed before attempting to start remotely.

Sometimes, the remote start feature might be disabled in your car’s settings or require a software update. If you’ve tried everything and still face issues, visiting your Volvo dealer for a system check is a good idea.

Have you ever been stuck in cold weather wishing your XC90 would start from inside your home? Identifying these issues beforehand can turn that wish into reality quickly and smoothly.

## Safety Tips For Remote Starting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=remote-starting-your-volvo-xc90-with-the-key-fob-is-convenient-especially-on-chilly-mornings-or-hot-afternoons-however-safety-should-always-be-your-priority&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Remote starting your Volvo XC90 with the key fob is convenient, especially on chilly mornings or hot afternoons. However, safety should always be your priority. Understanding how to prevent accidents and maintain proper air quality inside your vehicle is crucial when using this feature.

### Avoiding Accidental Starts

Accidental remote starts can be more than just a nuisance—they can pose safety risks. Make sure your key fob is stored securely, away from children or pets who might press buttons unintentionally.

Some Volvo models allow you to disable the remote start feature temporarily. Check your owner’s manual to see if this option is available and consider using it when you don’t need remote start.

Have you ever pressed a button on your key fob by mistake? Taking a moment to confirm that your car is parked safely and locked before using remote start can prevent potential mishaps.

### Ensuring Proper Ventilation

Starting your Volvo remotely means the engine will run while the vehicle is stationary. This makes ventilation inside the car essential.

Never remote start your XC90 in an enclosed space like a closed garage. Exhaust fumes can build up quickly, creating dangerous conditions.

Always park in an open, well-ventilated area before using remote start. This keeps fresh air flowing and protects you and your family from harmful gases.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Remote Start My Volvo Xc90 With The Key Fob?

To remote start your Volvo XC90, press the lock button on the key fob twice. Then, press and hold the remote start button until the engine starts. The car will run for a preset time before shutting off automatically.

### Is Remote Start Available On All Volvo Xc90 Models?

Remote start is available on most recent Volvo XC90 models but may vary by year and trim. Check your owner’s manual or contact your dealer to confirm if your specific model supports remote start with the key fob.

### Can I Use The Remote Start Feature In Cold Weather?

Yes, the remote start feature is designed to preheat or precool your Volvo XC90. This makes it convenient to warm up the engine and cabin before you enter, especially during cold or hot weather conditions.

### Does Remote Start Work If My Volvo Xc90 Is In Gear?

No, the remote start system will not activate if the vehicle is in gear. For safety, the car must be in park with the doors closed and locked before remote start can be used.
