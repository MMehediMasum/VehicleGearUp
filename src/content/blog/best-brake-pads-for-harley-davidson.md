## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Brake Pads Fit Harley Davidson Touring Models 2008-2022?



ECCPP and Zinger brake pads fit Harley Davidson Touring models from 2008 to 2022. They offer front and rear kits designed for models like Electra Glide, Road King, and Street Glide. These pads ensure optimal braking performance and durability for long rides.



### Are Sintered Brake Pads Better For Harley Davidson Bikes?



Yes, sintered brake pads provide superior heat resistance and longer lifespan. They enhance stopping power and reduce brake fade on Harley Davidson models. Sintered pads are ideal for heavy-duty use and consistent performance in various riding conditions.



### Can I Use Ceramic Brake Pads On Harley Davidson Touring Bikes?



Heavy-duty ceramic brake pads are compatible with Harley Davidson Touring models from 2000 to 2007. They offer quieter braking and less dust compared to metallic pads. Ceramic pads deliver smooth braking and protect your rotors from excessive wear.



### How Many Brake Pad Sets Are Needed For Harley Davidson?



Typically, three sets are recommended: two front and one rear. This setup covers both front and rear brakes for most Harley Davidson models. It ensures balanced braking and safer stopping on different terrains and riding styles.



### What Brands Offer Complete Brake Pad Kits For Harley Davidson?



Brands like ECCPP, Zinger, Cyleto, and EBC Brakes provide complete brake pad kits. These kits include front and rear pads tailored for Harley Davidson models. They ensure high quality, fitment accuracy, and improved braking performance.
