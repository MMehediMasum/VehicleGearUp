## Locating The Fog Lights

Locating the fog lights on your 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee is the first step before you can change them. Knowing exactly where these lights sit helps you work faster and with confidence. It also reduces the chance of damaging any surrounding parts during the process.

### Where Are The Fog Lights Positioned?

The fog lights are positioned low on the front bumper, near the edges of the vehicle. This placement helps them cut through fog and low-visibility conditions more effectively. You’ll see them mounted just below the headlights, integrated into the bumper design.

### Access Points For Reaching The Fog Lights

You can access the fog lights either from underneath the front bumper or by opening the hood. Some models allow easier reach from the wheel well area by turning the steering wheel outward. Have a flashlight handy to spot the exact location and mounting screws.

### Identifying The Fog Light Housing And Connectors

The fog light housing is usually a round or rectangular casing holding the bulb. Look behind the housing for the electrical connector—this powers the light. Carefully unplug this connector before attempting to remove the bulb or housing to avoid damage.




## Removing The Old Fog Lights

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=removing-the-old-fog-lights-on-your-2018-jeep-grand-cherokee-requires-careful-steps-safety-and-proper-handling-are-important&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Removing the old fog lights on your 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee requires careful steps. Safety and proper handling are important. This section guides you through disconnecting power, accessing parts, and removing the old fog lights safely.

### Disconnecting The Battery

Start by opening the hood of your Jeep. Locate the battery and use a wrench to loosen the negative terminal. Disconnect the negative cable to cut power. This step prevents electrical shorts or shocks during work. Keep the cable away from the battery terminal.

### Accessing The Fog Light Assembly

Move to the front bumper area. Remove any plastic covers blocking the fog light. You might need to turn the wheel for better access. Use a flat screwdriver or trim tool to carefully pry off clips. Avoid breaking the plastic parts. Once clear, you can see the fog light assembly.

### Unplugging The Fog Light Connector

Locate the wiring harness connected to the fog light. Press the tab on the connector to release it. Gently pull the connector away from the fog light. Do not pull on the wires themselves. This step disconnects the light from the Jeep’s electrical system.

### Removing Mounting Screws And Clips

Use a screwdriver or socket wrench to remove screws holding the fog light. Check for any plastic clips and remove them carefully. Keep all screws and clips safe for reinstallation. After all fasteners are removed, pull the fog light out of the assembly. Handle the light carefully to avoid damage.

## Installing The New Fog Lights

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=removing-the-old-fog-lights-on-a-2018-jeep-grand-cherokee-is-simple-and-quick-carefully-connect-the-new-lights-to-the-wiring-and-secure-them-in-place&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Removing the old fog lights on a 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee is simple and quick. Carefully connect the new lights to the wiring and secure them in place. Test the lights to ensure they work properly before finishing.

Installing new fog lights on your 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee is a rewarding task. Proper installation ensures safe driving in foggy conditions. Follow these steps to replace your fog lights with ease.

### Positioning The New Fog Light

First, align the new fog light with the mounting bracket. Make sure the light is facing the right direction. Check that the holes for screws are visible. This ensures a secure fit.

### Securing With Screws And Clips

Next, use screws to attach the fog light to the bracket. Tighten them with a screwdriver. Don't over-tighten, as this may damage the light. Use clips to hold the light in place. Ensure they click into position.

### Connecting The Wiring Harness

Finally, connect the wiring harness to the fog light. Match the connectors and push them together. Listen for a click to confirm a secure connection. Test the fog light by turning it on. It should illuminate without flickering. If not, recheck the connections.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Dealing with fog light problems on your 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee can be frustrating, especially when visibility matters the most. Identifying the root cause quickly saves you time and ensures safety on the road. Let’s walk through some common issues and how to fix them effectively.

### Fog Lights Not Turning On

If your fog lights refuse to turn on, start by checking the basics. Verify the fuse linked to the fog lights in your vehicle’s fuse box; a blown fuse is a common culprit and easy to replace.

Next, inspect the fog light bulbs for any signs of damage or burnout. Sometimes, simply swapping out the bulb restores full functionality. Don’t overlook the fog light switch itself—faulty wiring or a broken switch can interrupt power flow.

Have you ever traced a wiring issue only to find a loose connector behind the bumper? These hidden connections often cause unexpected failures, so give them a gentle tug to ensure they’re secure.

### Flickering Or Dim Lights

Flickering or dim fog lights usually point to electrical problems. Start by checking the battery voltage; low voltage can cause inconsistent lighting performance.

Corrosion on the wiring terminals reduces current flow and leads to dimming. Cleaning these connections with a wire brush or contact cleaner often resolves the issue.

Also, examine the ground wires. A poor ground connection is a sneaky cause of flickering lights. Tighten any loose grounds and replace damaged wires if needed to keep your fog lights shining steady.

### Alignment Adjustments

Proper fog light alignment is key for effective illumination without blinding other drivers. After replacing bulbs or after any impact, check if your fog lights point slightly downward and to the sides.

Use a flat surface and a wall to test alignment: park about 25 feet away, turn on your fog lights, and mark where the beams hit. Adjust the screws on the fog light housing to align the beams within the recommended height and width.

Have you noticed your fog lights scattering light unevenly? Misalignment not only reduces visibility but also wastes power. Taking a few minutes to adjust them can make a big difference on foggy nights.
