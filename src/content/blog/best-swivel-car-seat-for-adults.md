Memory foam cushions conform to body shape, providing excellent comfort and pressure relief. They reduce discomfort during long drives and help prevent pressure sores. Many swivel seat cushions, like Sojoy iGelComfort, combine memory foam with gel for added support and cooling effects.

### Can Swivel Car Seats Assist Pregnant Women?



Absolutely, swivel car seats ease the process of getting in and out of vehicles. They reduce twisting and strain on the belly and hips. Cushions like Ehucon’s and Vive provide comfortable support, making them practical aids for pregnant women during travel.
