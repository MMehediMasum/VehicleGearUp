## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Tire Size Is Best For Towing A 5th Wheel?



The best tire size for towing a 5th wheel varies by trailer. Common sizes include 5. 70x8 and 4. 80-8. Choose tires like Antego or Kenda with Load Range C for optimal durability and load capacity.



### How Do Load Ranges Affect 5th Wheel Towing Tires?



Load ranges indicate tire strength. Load Range C tires, like those from Antego, offer 6 ply rating. This ensures they can handle heavy towing loads safely and prevent blowouts on long trips.



### Are Galvanized Wheels Better For Trailer Towing?



Yes, galvanized wheels resist rust and corrosion better than painted ones. They last longer in wet or salty conditions, making them ideal for 5th wheel trailers exposed to outdoor elements.



### What Are The Benefits Of 6 Ply Trailer Tires?



6 ply tires provide extra strength and durability. They handle heavier loads and rough road conditions better. Tires like Antego’s 6 ply, Load Range C, improve towing safety and tire lifespan.



### Can I Use Flat-free Tires On A 5th Wheel Trailer?



Flat-free tires, like Tow Tuff’s 16" dolly tires, reduce flat risks. However, they may not absorb shocks as well as pneumatic tires. Use them mainly for trailer dollies or stabilizers, not main towing tires.
