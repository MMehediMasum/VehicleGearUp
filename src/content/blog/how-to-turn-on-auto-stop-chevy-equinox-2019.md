## Auto Stop Feature In Chevy Equinox

The Auto Stop feature in the 2019 Chevy Equinox helps save fuel and reduce emissions. It works by turning off the engine when the vehicle is stopped. This feature restarts the engine quickly when you release the brake pedal. It makes driving in traffic or at stoplights more efficient.

### What Auto Stop Does

Auto Stop turns off the engine automatically during stops. It activates only when the vehicle is fully stopped. The system shuts off the engine to save fuel. Once you lift your foot from the brake, the engine restarts instantly. This helps reduce idle time and lowers fuel consumption.

### Benefits Of Using Auto Stop

* Improves fuel efficiency by reducing engine idle time.
* Decreases harmful emissions, making the car eco-friendly.
* Reduces engine wear from constant idling.
* Helps save money on fuel over time.
* Works smoothly without driver effort.

The Auto Stop button sits on the dashboard, near the driver. Look to the left side of the steering wheel. It is often placed alongside other control buttons. This spot lets you reach it without stretching or distraction.

Sometimes the button is close to the headlight controls or near the climate control panel. It is usually within your natural hand movement range. The exact position might vary slightly by trim level, but it stays in the driver’s main control area.

### Icon And Label Details

The button shows a clear icon to identify it. You will see a circular arrow around the letter "A". This symbol means "Auto Stop" or "Auto Start-Stop". The button may also have the word "AUTO" or "STOP" below or beside the icon.

## Activating Auto Stop Mode

Activate Auto Stop Mode on your 2019 Chevy Equinox by pressing the Auto Stop button on the dashboard. This feature helps save fuel by stopping the engine when the vehicle is idle. Easy to use and improves efficiency during city driving.

Activating the Auto Stop Mode in your 2019 Chevy Equinox can save fuel. This feature turns off your engine when the vehicle is idle. It restarts automatically when you release the brake. Let's explore how to activate this mode.

### Step-by-step Activation

To activate Auto Stop, follow these steps: 1. Start your Chevy Equinox and ensure it's in a safe area. 2. Shift the gear to 'Drive' and gently press the brake pedal. 3. Keep the vehicle stationary for a few moments. 4. The engine will turn off automatically, indicating activation.

### Indicator Lights To Watch

Certain dashboard lights show Auto Stop status. Look for the green "A" icon. This light means the feature is active. If the light flashes, the system is paused. Keep an eye on these indicators for a smooth experience.

Did you know you can adjust how sensitive the Auto Stop system is to your driving conditions? This means you can control when the engine shuts off during stops, making it smoother or more aggressive depending on your preference.

Within the same settings menu, look for “Auto Stop Sensitivity” or a similar option. You’ll often find sliders or presets like “Low,” “Medium,” and “High.”

Setting it to “Low” might delay the engine stop, useful in heavy traffic or quick stop-and-go situations. “High” sensitivity will turn off the engine more frequently, helping save more fuel but requiring you to be comfortable with frequent restarts.

  


## Troubleshooting Auto Stop Issues

Experiencing issues with the Auto Stop feature on your 2019 Chevy Equinox can be frustrating, especially when you rely on it to save fuel during city drives. Troubleshooting these problems yourself can save time and avoid unnecessary trips to the dealer. Let’s dive into some common hiccups and practical fixes that might get your Auto Stop working smoothly again.

### Common Problems And Fixes

One frequent issue is the Auto Stop not activating when you expect it to. This often happens if the battery charge is low or the engine hasn’t reached the ideal temperature. Checking your battery health and making sure the engine warms up fully can resolve this quickly.

Another common problem is the feature shutting off unexpectedly. This could be due to climate control settings; if you have the air conditioning or heater set to max, the system may disable Auto Stop to maintain comfort. Try adjusting these settings to see if that helps.

Sometimes, dashboard warnings or alerts related to the Auto Stop system might appear. These can indicate sensor malfunctions or software glitches. Resetting the system by turning off the engine, waiting a few minutes, and restarting can clear minor errors.

* Ensure the vehicle is on a flat surface; steep inclines can prevent Auto Stop activation.
* Keep your seatbelt fastened and doors closed; safety sensors can block Auto Stop if these are open or unlatched.
* Check for any stored trouble codes using an OBD-II scanner for a clearer diagnosis.

### When To Visit A Dealer

If you’ve tried the basic fixes and the Auto Stop feature still misbehaves, it might be time to get professional help. Persistent warning lights or error codes often require dealer diagnostics. These specialists have access to manufacturer-specific tools that can pinpoint deeper electrical or software issues.

Also, if you notice unusual noises or performance drops when Auto Stop engages, avoid delaying a service visit. Ignoring these signs could lead to more costly repairs later. Have you noticed any patterns in when the system fails? Sharing this information with your dealer can speed up the troubleshooting process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Activate Auto Stop On Chevy Equinox 2019?

To activate Auto Stop, press the Auto Stop button located on the dashboard. Ensure the vehicle is in Drive and at a complete stop for Auto Stop to engage automatically.

### Can I Manually Turn Off Auto Stop On Chevy Equinox?

Yes, you can manually disable Auto Stop by pressing the Auto Stop button. The system will remain off until reactivated by pressing the button again or restarting the vehicle.

### What Conditions Must Be Met For Auto Stop To Work?

Auto Stop activates when the engine is warm, battery charge is sufficient, and the vehicle is completely stopped with the brake pedal pressed.
