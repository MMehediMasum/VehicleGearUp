## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Wipe-on Clear Coat For Cars?



The best wipe-on clear coat offers smooth application, durability, and high gloss finish. Products like Dupli-Color Clear Exact-Match and CERAKOTE Platinum Ceramic Sealant provide excellent protection and shine for various car surfaces.



### How To Apply Dupli-color Wipe-on Clear Coat Properly?



Clean and dry the surface before application. Use a soft cloth to evenly wipe the Dupli-Color clear coat. Allow it to dry fully between coats for a smooth, protective finish that resists scratches and UV damage.



### Can Wipe-on Clear Coats Protect Against Uv Damage?



Yes, quality wipe-on clear coats like Sherwin Williams Finish 1 and CERAKOTE Platinum include UV blockers. These protect your car’s paint from fading and oxidation caused by sun exposure, extending your vehicle’s exterior life.



### Are Wipe-on Clear Coats Compatible With Touch-up Paints?



Many wipe-on clear coats, such as Toyota Genuine Clear Coat Pen and Dupli-Color Touch-Up Spray, work well over touch-up paints. They seal and enhance gloss, providing a seamless, durable finish after minor paint repairs.



### How Long Does A Wipe-on Clear Coat Last On Cars?



Wipe-on clear coats typically last 1-3 years depending on conditions and maintenance. High-quality products like Speedokote 2K Acrylic Urethane offer longer-lasting protection with proper care and regular cleaning.



### Is It Safe To Use Aerosol Wipe-on Clear Coats On Cars?



Yes, aerosol clear coats like Spray Max USC 2K High Gloss offer easy, even application. They provide durable, high-gloss finishes and are safe when used in well-ventilated areas with proper surface prep.
