## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Good Front And Rear Dash Cam?



A good dash cam has high resolution, wide-angle lenses, night vision, GPS, and parking mode. These features ensure clear footage, even in low light and while parked, enhancing security and evidence collection.



### How Important Is 4k Video Quality In Dash Cams?



4K video provides sharper, detailed footage, making it easier to identify license plates and faces. It improves overall video clarity compared to 1080p, which is beneficial for legal or insurance purposes.



### Do Dash Cams With Built-in Wifi Offer Better Convenience?



Yes, built-in WiFi allows easy video transfer and live viewing via smartphone apps. This feature speeds up downloading footage without removing the memory card, enhancing user convenience.



### What Is The Benefit Of Dual Or Multi-channel Dash Cams?



Dual or multi-channel dash cams capture front, rear, and inside views simultaneously. This comprehensive coverage increases security and provides full accident context from multiple angles.



### Why Is Parking Mode Essential In Dash Cams?



Parking mode records incidents when your car is parked and the engine is off. It detects motion or impacts, offering protection against vandalism or hit-and-run damages while you’re away.



### How Does Gps Functionality Enhance Dash Cam Use?



GPS tracks your car’s location and speed, embedding data into video footage. This helps verify trip details and supports insurance claims or legal evidence with accurate travel information.
