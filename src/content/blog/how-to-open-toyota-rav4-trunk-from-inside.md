## Alternative Methods

### Using The Key Fob

The key fob offers a quick way to open the trunk remotely. Press the trunk button on the fob and hold it for a few seconds. The trunk will pop open without needing to use any interior controls. This method works even if the interior trunk release is broken.

Keep the key fob battery charged to ensure it works well. If the trunk button does not respond, try replacing the battery or check for signal interference.

### Manual Unlock Options

Manual unlock options include using the physical key hidden inside the key fob. Remove the mechanical key and insert it into the trunk lock. Turn the key to open the trunk manually.

Some Toyota RAV4 models have a small lever or button inside the rear seats. Folding down the seats can provide access to this manual trunk release. This option is helpful if the trunk is locked from the outside and other methods fail.
