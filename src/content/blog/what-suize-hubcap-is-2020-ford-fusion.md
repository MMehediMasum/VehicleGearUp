## Hubcap Basics

Understanding the basics of hubcaps can help you make smarter choices for your 2020 Ford Fusion. Hubcaps do more than just cover your wheels—they protect and personalize your ride. Let’s break down what you need to know about these essential car accessories.

### Purpose Of Hubcaps

Hubcaps primarily protect your wheel’s lug nuts from dirt, moisture, and rust. This helps keep your wheels secure and your driving safe. They also improve the look of your car by giving it a clean, polished finish.

Have you ever noticed how a missing or damaged hubcap can make a well-maintained car appear neglected? That’s because hubcaps add visual appeal and a sense of completeness to your vehicle. They also make cleaning easier by shielding the wheel’s inner parts from grime.

### Materials Used

Hubcaps come in different materials, each with its own benefits. Plastic is the most common material because it’s lightweight, affordable, and resistant to rust. However, plastic hubcaps can crack or fade over time.

Metal hubcaps, often made from stainless steel or aluminum, offer greater durability and a shiny finish. They resist dents better but tend to be heavier and pricier. Some drivers choose a mix of both materials to balance cost and longevity.

## Installing Hubcaps

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-hubcaps-on-your-2020-ford-fusion-can-transform-its-look-instantly-its-a-simple-task-that-doesnt-need-professional-help-but-a-little-preparation-makes-all-the-difference&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing hubcaps on your 2020 Ford Fusion can transform its look instantly. It’s a simple task that doesn’t need professional help, but a little preparation makes all the difference. Let’s dive into how you can get this done smoothly and securely.

### Preparation Tips

Start by cleaning the wheel surface where the hubcap will sit. Dirt or rust can prevent the hubcap from fitting properly or cause it to fall off later.

Check the size of your wheels and match the hubcaps accordingly. The 2020 Ford Fusion usually requires 16- or 17-inch hubcaps, so having the right size is crucial.

Gather the tools you might need, like a rubber mallet and gloves. Gloves protect your hands, and a mallet helps tap the hubcap in place without damage.

### Installation Process

Place the hubcap over the wheel, aligning the clips with the rim evenly. Don’t rush this; a misaligned hubcap won’t hold well.

Use gentle but firm pressure to press the hubcap against the wheel. If it doesn’t snap on easily, use a rubber mallet to tap it around the edges carefully.

After installation, give the hubcap a gentle tug to check if it’s secure. You want it tight enough to stay put but not so tight that it could crack.

## Maintaining Hubcaps

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-hubcaps-on-your-2020-ford-fusion-keeps-your-car-looking-sharp-and-protects-the-wheels-regular-care-prevents-dirt-buildup-and-damage&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the hubcaps on your 2020 Ford Fusion keeps your car looking sharp and protects the wheels. Regular care prevents dirt buildup and damage. Clean and repair hubcaps to extend their life and keep your vehicle stylish.

### Cleaning Techniques

Use mild soap and water to clean hubcaps gently. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the finish. A soft cloth or sponge works best for scrubbing off dirt and grime. Rinse thoroughly to remove soap residue. Dry with a clean towel to prevent water spots.

For stubborn stains, use a mixture of baking soda and water. Apply it with a soft brush and scrub lightly. This method removes tough dirt without scratching. Clean hubcaps regularly to maintain their shine and avoid buildup.

### Repair And Replacement

Small scratches and scuffs on hubcaps can be fixed with touch-up paint. Match the paint color carefully for a seamless look. For cracks or broken parts, replacement is the safer choice. Check the hubcap size and style before buying a new one.

Keep spare hubcaps in your car for quick replacements. Loose or missing hubcaps affect the vehicle's appearance and wheel protection. Secure hubcaps properly to avoid losing them while driving. Regular inspections help catch issues early and save money.
