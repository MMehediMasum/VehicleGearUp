## Use A Slim Jim Tool

The slim jim tool is a classic choice for opening car doors when keys get locked inside. It’s a thin, flat piece of metal designed to slide between the window and weather stripping to manipulate the door’s lock mechanism directly. Using it requires patience and a gentle touch to avoid damaging your Ford Explorer’s door components.

### Locate The Door Lock Mechanism

Before inserting the slim jim, you need to find where the lock mechanism is inside the door panel. On most Ford Explorers, the lock rods run vertically near the front edge of the door. You can usually spot them by looking through the window or by feeling around the inner door frame carefully.

Knowing the exact position of the lock rod is crucial. If you insert the slim jim too far or in the wrong spot, you might bend or break the tool or damage the door’s internal parts. Take a moment to study your door’s edge and visualize where the lock mechanism moves when you lock or unlock your door manually.

### Insert And Maneuver The Slim Jim

Once you’ve located the lock rod, slide the slim jim slowly between the window glass and weather stripping. Insert it carefully to avoid scratching your window or bending the tool. Your goal is to hook the slim jim around the lock rod or the control arm connected to the lock.

After hooking the mechanism, gently lift or slide the slim jim to move the lock into the unlocked position. This step often requires a bit of trial and error, so be patient and don’t force the tool. If you feel resistance, adjust the angle of the slim jim slightly and try again.

## Use A Coat Hanger Method

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=if-youve-accidentally-locked-your-keys-inside-your-ford-explorer-a-simple-coat-hanger-can-sometimes-come-to-the-rescue-this-old-school-trick-requires-patience-and-a-bit-of-finesse-but-it-can-save-you-from-waiting-for-roadside-assistance&w=424&h=324&c=7)

If you’ve accidentally locked your keys inside your Ford Explorer, a simple coat hanger can sometimes come to the rescue. This old-school trick requires patience and a bit of finesse, but it can save you from waiting for roadside assistance. The key is to carefully manipulate the hanger to press the unlock button or pull the lock mechanism inside the door.

### Straighten The Hanger

Start by finding a wire coat hanger and straightening it out as much as possible. You want a long, thin, and sturdy tool that can easily slide between the window and the weather stripping. Make sure one end is bent into a small hook shape—this will help you grab the lock or button inside the door.

Keep in mind, the hanger needs to be flexible enough to navigate the narrow gap but rigid enough to apply some force. Have you ever noticed how frustrating it is when tools are either too floppy or too stiff? This balance is crucial here.

### Reach And Unlock The Door

Carefully slide the hooked end of the hanger into the gap at the top corner of the driver's side door. Gently maneuver it toward the lock mechanism. You might need to wiggle the hanger to find the right angle to pull or push the lock.

Patience is key—don’t rush. Press lightly against the lock button or lift the lock tab with the hook. If you feel resistance, adjust your angle instead of forcing it. Have you ever tried unlocking a door blindfolded? This method is quite similar and requires a steady hand.

Remember, this technique works best with older Explorer models that have traditional lock mechanisms. Newer models with electronic locks might not respond to this method. Always consider your vehicle’s specific design before trying.

## Use Ford's Remote Unlock Service

Ford offers a convenient way to open your Explorer if the keys get locked inside. Their Remote Unlock Service lets you unlock the doors from a distance. This service is quick and safe. It requires the FordPass app and an active internet connection. Using this tool saves time and avoids costly locksmith fees.

### Activate Fordpass App

Start by downloading the FordPass app from your phone’s app store. Create an account if you do not have one. Link your Ford Explorer to the app using your vehicle identification number (VIN). Make sure your vehicle has an active FordPass Connect modem. This modem enables communication between your phone and the car. Verify your account by following the app’s instructions. Activation usually takes just a few minutes.

### Remote Unlock Steps

* Open the FordPass app on your smartphone.
* Sign in with your FordPass credentials.
* Select your Ford Explorer from the list of vehicles.
* Tap the “Remote Unlock” button on the app’s main screen.
* Confirm your identity by entering your PIN or using biometric verification.
* Wait for the app to send the unlock command to your vehicle.
* Your Ford Explorer will unlock within seconds.
* Check the doors before entering to ensure they are unlocked.
