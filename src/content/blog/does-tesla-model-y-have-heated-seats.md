## Activating And Adjusting Heated Seats

Activating and adjusting heated seats in the Tesla Model Y is simple and enhances driving comfort. The system warms the seats quickly, making cold rides cozy. Understanding how to control the settings helps tailor warmth to your needs.

### Step-by-step Guide

* Tap the car icon on the touchscreen to open the controls menu.
* Select the “Climate” tab to access seat heating options.
* Choose the seat you want to heat: driver, front passenger, or rear seats.
* Press the heated seat icon to turn it on.
* Adjust the heat level by tapping the icon multiple times to cycle through low, medium, and high settings.
* The icon lights up to show the current heat level.
* To turn off, tap the heated seat icon until the light goes off.

### Troubleshooting Tips

* Check if the car’s battery is low; heated seats may not work properly.
* Restart the vehicle’s touchscreen if seat controls do not respond.
* Make sure software is up to date for the latest features and fixes.
* If heating does not activate, inspect the fuse related to seat heating.
* Consult Tesla support if the problem persists after basic checks.
