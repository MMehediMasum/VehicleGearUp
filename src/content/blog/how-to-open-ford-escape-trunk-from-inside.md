Take a moment to scan the area around your left foot or the lower dashboard; it’s usually placed there for quick access.

The trunk release inside the Ford Escape could be either a handle that you pull or a button that you press. ## Activate The Trunk From The Rear Seat

Activating the trunk from the rear seat of a Ford Escape is simple. This option helps when you cannot open the trunk from outside. It also comes in handy if the trunk release button inside the car is not working.

This process involves folding down the rear seats and reaching into the trunk area. You can then manually pull the trunk release lever or access the trunk space easily. Let’s explore how to do this safely and quickly.

### Fold Down The Rear Seats

Locate the release handles at the top of the rear seats. Pull these handles to unlock the seatbacks. Push the seats forward until they lay flat. This creates a clear path from the rear seat to the trunk.

Make sure the area behind the seats is free of objects. Folding the seats fully helps avoid damage and gives better access to the trunk.

### Access The Trunk Interior

Reach through the opening created by the folded seats. Look for the manual trunk release lever inside the trunk. It is usually a small handle with a glow-in-the-dark feature for visibility.

Pull this lever firmly to open the trunk from inside. If the lever is missing or broken, reach inside the trunk to lift the latch manually. This method allows emergency exit or access if the trunk button fails.

## Troubleshoot Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-the-ford-escape-trunk-can-save-time-and-frustration-problems-like-a-stuck-trunk-or-a-non-responsive-release-button-often-have-simple-fixes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with the Ford Escape trunk can save time and frustration. Problems like a stuck trunk or a non-responsive release button often have simple fixes. Understanding these issues helps to identify the cause quickly and find the right solution.

Sometimes, the trunk may not open from inside due to electrical or mechanical malfunctions. Checking these components first can avoid unnecessary repairs. A few quick checks can reveal if the problem is with power or lock systems.

### Check For Power Or Lock Malfunctions

Start by inspecting the power supply to the trunk release. A dead battery or blown fuse can stop the release from working. Look for any blown fuses in the fuse box linked to the trunk. Test the trunk release button to see if it lights up or responds.

Check if the trunk lock mechanism is jammed or stuck. Dirt, rust, or debris can block the lock from moving. Try gently pushing or tapping the lock to free it. Listen for any unusual sounds when pressing the release button inside the car. If the lock is frozen, warming it can help.

Verify the wiring connections behind the release button and trunk latch. Loose or damaged wires can interrupt the signal. Use a flashlight to see if any wires are broken or disconnected.

### Consult The Owner's Manual

The owner's manual holds specific information for your Ford Escape model. It explains the location of trunk release controls and fuse box. The manual also describes warning lights or error codes related to trunk issues.

Follow the manual’s troubleshooting guide for more detailed steps. It can provide tips on resetting electronic systems or manually opening the trunk. Keep the manual handy for quick reference during problems.
