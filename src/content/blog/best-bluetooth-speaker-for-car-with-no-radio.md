## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Bluetooth Adapters Work Without A Car Radio?



Bluetooth adapters connect your phone to your car’s AUX or FM input. They wirelessly stream audio to your car speakers. This allows music and calls without needing a built-in radio or CD player.



### Can I Charge My Devices While Using Bluetooth Adapters?



Yes, many Bluetooth adapters like LIHAN and Nulaxy offer USB charging ports. They support fast charging standards such as QC3. 0 and PD for quick power delivery.



### Are Hands-free Calling Features Reliable In Car Bluetooth Speakers?



Most Bluetooth car adapters include built-in microphones for hands-free calls. Models like UNBREAKcable and Syncwire provide clear voice quality and noise reduction for safer driving.



### Which Device Supports Both Android Auto And Apple Carplay?



The PLZ 7" Wireless Double Din Car Stereo supports both Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. It offers touchscreen controls and seamless Bluetooth connectivity for both platforms.



### How Do Fm Transmitters Improve Car Audio Quality?



FM transmitters like Nulaxy and Avantree broadcast audio over unused FM frequencies. They provide deep bass and HiFi sound, enhancing music quality through your car’s stereo system.



### Are Wireless Bluetooth Speakers Suitable For Long Drives?



Yes, models like Avantree Roadtrip offer long battery life up to 22 hours. They combine wireless convenience with hands-free calling and quality sound for extended trips.
