## Inspection Due Light Explained

The Inspection Due light on an Audi Q5 signals when maintenance is needed. Resetting it clears the alert after service. This helps keep your vehicle’s system up to date and avoids unnecessary warnings.

### What Does The Inspection Due Light Mean?

The Inspection Due light on your Audi Q5 signals that a scheduled service is needed. It turns on after driving a set number of miles or months. This reminder helps keep your vehicle in good condition.

Ignoring this light can lead to bigger problems and costly repairs. The light alerts you to check essential parts like brakes, oil, and filters. Timely maintenance ensures safety and performance.

### When Does The Inspection Due Light Activate?

The light usually appears after 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. Audi's system tracks your driving and time between services. It calculates when the next inspection should happen.

Driving habits and road conditions can affect this timing. Frequent short trips or rough roads may require more frequent checks. The light helps remind you to stay on schedule.

### What Should You Do When The Light Comes On?

Schedule a service appointment with an authorized Audi dealer or mechanic. The technician will perform a full inspection and replace worn parts. This includes oil change, brake check, and fluid levels.

Resetting the light is part of the service. It confirms that the inspection is complete and the system is updated. After resetting, the light will turn off until the next service is due.

## Resetting Inspection Light Using The Mmi

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=reset-the-inspection-light-on-your-audi-q5-using-the-mmi-system-quickly-and-easily-access-the-service-menu-select-the-inspection-option-and-confirm-the-reset-to-clear-the-reminder&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Reset the inspection light on your Audi Q5 using the MMI system quickly and easily. Access the service menu, select the inspection option, and confirm the reset to clear the reminder. This simple process keeps your car’s maintenance alerts accurate.

Resetting the inspection light on your Audi Q5 is simple. The Multi Media Interface (MMI) helps in this process. It ensures your vehicle's maintenance schedule stays on track. Follow these steps to reset the inspection light.

### Accessing The Service Menu

Begin by turning on the ignition. Do not start the engine. Locate the "Menu" button on your MMI console. Press it to access the main menu. Use the control knob to select "Car" from the options. Press the knob to enter the car menu.

### Navigating To Inspection Reset

Scroll through the car menu options. Look for "Service & Checks." Select this option by pressing the control knob. Find "Service Intervals." Press the knob to enter this section. The "Inspection Reset" option will appear. Select it to proceed.

### Confirming The Reset

The system will ask for confirmation. Select "Yes" to confirm the reset. The inspection light will reset successfully. Turn off the ignition to complete the process. Your Audi Q5 is ready for its next journey.



## Resetting Inspection Light Manually

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-inspection-light-on-an-audi-q5-can-be-done-manually-without-special-tools-this-process-clears-the-maintenance-reminder-after-an-oil-change-or-service&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the inspection light on an Audi Q5 can be done manually without special tools. This process clears the maintenance reminder after an oil change or service. Follow simple steps to turn off the inspection light and keep your dashboard clean.

### Turning The Ignition On

Start by inserting the key into the ignition slot. Turn the key to the position where the dashboard lights up, but do not start the engine. This mode powers the electronic systems and allows access to the reset function.

### Using The Trip Button

Locate the trip button on the instrument cluster or steering wheel. Press and hold the trip button firmly. Keep holding it until the inspection light or service symbol starts flashing or turns off.

### Completing The Reset Process

Release the trip button once the light changes. Turn off the ignition completely. Start the engine to check if the inspection light is gone. The reset is complete when the light stays off during driving.

### Common Problems

* Reset procedure does not start or complete.
* Inspection light returns immediately after reset.
* Dashboard warning messages remain after reset attempt.
* Buttons or controls needed for reset do not respond.
* Battery disconnection does not clear the inspection light.
* Reset fails due to software glitches or outdated system.

Many problems happen because the reset steps are missed or done too fast. The car’s system might need a full restart or update. Simple fixes like checking the ignition mode or holding buttons longer often help. Always follow the exact reset process for your Audi Q5 model and year.

### When To Visit A Dealer

Persistent reset failures mean the car might have deeper issues. Faulty sensors or software errors need professional diagnostics. Dealers have specialized tools to access and fix the system properly. They can also update the car’s software to the latest version.

Visit a dealer if:

* Inspection light stays on despite multiple reset attempts.
* Other warning lights appear along with the inspection due light.
* Reset instructions from the manual do not work.
* Car shows unusual performance or error codes.

Professional help ensures the inspection system works correctly. It keeps your Audi Q5 safe and reliable for every drive.




## Maintenance Tips To Avoid Frequent Resets

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-audi-q5s-inspection-due-light-from-flashing-frequently-saves-you-time-and-stress-frequent-resets-often-signal-overlooked-maintenance-or-minor-issues-that-pile-up&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Audi Q5’s inspection due light from flashing frequently saves you time and stress. Frequent resets often signal overlooked maintenance or minor issues that pile up. You can avoid this hassle by paying attention to key maintenance habits that keep your vehicle’s systems happy and healthy.

### Regular Oil Changes And Fluid Checks

Oil quality directly affects your car’s sensor readings. Skipping oil changes or using the wrong type can trigger early inspection alerts. Check your oil and other fluids like brake and transmission fluid regularly, ideally every 5,000 miles or as recommended in your owner’s manual.

Have you noticed that a simple oil top-up sometimes stops the inspection light from coming back on? That’s a clear sign your Q5 depends heavily on clean fluids for accurate system monitoring.

### Keep Your Tire Pressure And Condition In Check

Under-inflated or worn tires can cause sensor errors, leading to inspection reminders. Make it a habit to check your tire pressure monthly and replace tires before they get too worn. Proper tire care not only extends tire life but also prevents unnecessary dashboard warnings.

### Address Minor Repairs Promptly

Ignoring small issues can cause your car’s computer to flag inspection warnings prematurely. Fix squeaky brakes, replace worn belts, or repair faulty sensors as soon as you notice them. Tackling these problems early keeps your car running smoothly and reduces the need to reset inspection alerts repeatedly.

### Use Quality Parts And Follow Manufacturer Guidelines

Cheap or incompatible parts might save money upfront but often cause sensor conflicts. Stick to OEM (original equipment manufacturer) parts and recommended service intervals. This approach reduces the chance of false warnings and frequent resets.

### Perform A Diagnostic Scan Before Resetting

Resetting the inspection light without understanding the root cause is like putting a bandage on a wound. Use an OBD-II scanner or visit a trusted mechanic to diagnose any underlying issues before resetting. This step ensures the inspection due reminder won’t pop up again shortly after.
