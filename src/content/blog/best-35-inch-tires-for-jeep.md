### Are 10-ply Rated 35 Inch Tires Better For Heavy Loads?



Yes, 10-ply tires like Forceum M/T 08 Plus and Accelera M/T-01 provide superior load capacity and durability. They handle heavy Jeep setups and rough trails with enhanced strength and reliability.



### How Do Mud Terrain Tires Compare To All-terrain Tires For Jeeps?



Mud terrain tires like Cooper Discoverer STT Pro offer aggressive tread for mud and rocks. All-terrain tires provide smoother rides with better highway traction, suitable for mixed driving conditions.



### Can 35 Inch Tires Fit Standard Jeep Wheels Without Modifications?



Most 35 inch tires require Jeep lift kits or wheel spacers for proper clearance. Always check fitment guides and vehicle specs before upgrading to avoid rubbing and handling issues.
