### How Do Wheel Chocks Improve Safety During Tire Changes?



Wheel chocks like Checkers Heavy Duty models prevent vehicle movement on slopes. They stabilize trucks, especially heavy 1 ton models, during tire changes. Using chocks adds a critical safety layer by securing tires firmly against rolling.



### What Tire Lift Range Suits 1 Ton Trucks Best?



A lifting range from about 6. 1 to 17. 7 inches covers most 1 ton truck needs. Electric and hydraulic jacks like FEANISIFEI and ZAISHANG offer this range. This ensures the truck is lifted safely and stably for tire repairs or replacements.
