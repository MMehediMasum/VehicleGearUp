## Locate The Wiper Control

Locating the wiper control on your Ford Escape is the first step to turning off the back wiper. Knowing exactly where the controls are can save you time and frustration, especially when you’re on the road during unexpected weather changes. Let’s look closely at where to find the control stalk and how to check your dashboard settings.

### Identify The Control Stalk

The control stalk for the wipers is usually found on the right side of the steering wheel. It looks like a small lever that you can twist or push to adjust the wiper settings.

In my experience, many drivers miss this because they expect the rear wiper control to be separate from the front wiper controls. But on the Ford Escape, they are combined into this single stalk. You will often see symbols for both front and rear wipers on it.

Try gently twisting the end of the stalk or pressing a button on it to change the rear wiper settings. Have you noticed how simple it is once you locate this control?

### Check The Dashboard Settings

Sometimes, the dashboard screen offers additional control options for your wipers. Look at your instrument panel or infotainment screen while the vehicle is on.

Some models allow you to toggle the rear wiper on or off via a menu. This feature can be handy if you prefer adjusting settings digitally rather than using the stalk.

## Turn Off Rear Wiper Manually

Turning off the rear wiper on your Ford Escape manually is straightforward once you know where to look. Whether you're trying to stop it during light rain or just want to save the wiper blade from unnecessary wear, controlling it yourself puts you in charge. Let's walk through how you can quickly stop that rear wiper with simple actions.

### Use The Control Stalk Position

The rear wiper control is usually found on the same stalk that operates your front wipers, located on the right side of the steering wheel. You’ll notice a small dial or switch dedicated to the rear wiper settings.

To turn off the rear wiper, simply rotate the dial or move the switch to the “Off” position. This action cuts power to the rear wiper motor, stopping its movement immediately.

Have you ever accidentally left your rear wiper running and wondered why the battery drained faster? Using this simple control can prevent that kind of battery drain and unnecessary wear on your wiper blade.

### Stop The Wiper Motion

If your rear wiper is mid-swipe and you want it to stop in the resting position, you can do this manually by toggling the rear wiper control.

* Turn the rear wiper switch off and then back on quickly.
* Repeat this toggle action until the wiper blade returns to the bottom resting position.

This trick works because the wiper motor is designed to park the blade in a specific spot when power cycles. It’s a handy tip if your wiper seems stuck or stops in the middle of the window.

Have you tried this before? Many overlook it, but it’s a quick fix that saves a trip to the mechanic or fiddling under the rear hatch.

## Troubleshoot Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-off-the-back-wiper-on-a-ford-escape-is-simple-locate-the-wiper-control-stalk-and-move-it-to-the-off-position&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning off the back wiper on a Ford Escape is simple. Locate the wiper control stalk and move it to the off position. This stops the rear wiper from running and helps avoid unnecessary wear.

Turning off the back wiper on a Ford Escape can sometimes be tricky. At times, you might face common issues while trying to stop the wiper. Understanding these problems can help you find quick solutions. Let's explore some frequent challenges Ford Escape owners encounter.

### Wiper Still Running

Sometimes, the wiper doesn't stop even after switching it off. This might occur due to a malfunctioning switch. Check if the switch is correctly positioned. If it seems fine, inspect the wiper relay. A faulty relay might keep the wiper running. Replacing it often resolves the issue. Another aspect to consider is the wiring. Loose or damaged wires can cause the wiper to remain active. Secure and fix any identified wiring issues to stop the wiper.

### Electrical Or Fuse Problems

Electrical issues can also affect the back wiper's operation. First, inspect the vehicle's fuses. A blown fuse might cause the wiper to malfunction. Locate the correct fuse using the car's manual. Replace it if necessary. Ensure the new fuse has the right amperage. Besides the fuse, check the electrical connections. Corroded or loose connections can disrupt power flow. Clean or tighten these connections to restore proper function. If issues persist, consulting a professional might be beneficial.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Turn Off The Back Wiper On Ford Escape?

To turn off the back wiper, locate the rear wiper control on the gear lever or dashboard. Rotate or press the control to the “Off” position. This stops the rear wiper from moving and keeps the glass clear without continuous wiping.

### Where Is The Back Wiper Control On Ford Escape?

The back wiper control on a Ford Escape is usually on the right side of the steering column. It may be a rotating switch or a button on the gear shift lever. Check your owner’s manual for exact location and operation instructions.

### Can I Disable The Back Wiper Permanently On Ford Escape?

Ford Escape does not support permanent disabling of the back wiper via standard controls. The wiper only operates when activated manually or in reverse gear. For permanent disablement, professional modification is required, which is not recommended.

### Why Won’t My Ford Escape Back Wiper Turn Off?

If the back wiper won’t turn off, check if the control is stuck or damaged. Electrical issues or blown fuses can cause malfunction. Inspect the fuse box and consult a mechanic if necessary to fix the problem.
