## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Dash Cam Ideal For Commercial Trucks?



An ideal commercial truck dash cam offers 4K resolution, wide-angle lenses, night vision, GPS tracking, and 24-hour parking mode. It should support front and rear recording with durable build quality. Features like Wi-Fi and emergency lock enhance usability and safety on the road.



### How Does 4k Resolution Improve Dash Cam Footage Clarity?



4K resolution captures four times more detail than 1080p. This ensures sharper images and clearer license plate recognition. It is especially helpful for commercial trucks to record incidents accurately. High resolution aids in better evidence collection during accidents or disputes.



### Why Is 24-hour Parking Mode Important For Truck Dash Cams?



24-hour parking mode records any movement or impact while parked. It protects trucks from theft, vandalism, and hit-and-runs. This feature is crucial for commercial trucks left unattended at loading docks or rest stops. It provides peace of mind and continuous security.



### Can Gps Integration Enhance Truck Dash Cam Functionality?



Yes, GPS integration logs the truck’s location, speed, and route. It helps in fleet management and tracking driver behavior. GPS data supports incident investigations by providing exact location details. Many commercial dash cams come with built-in GPS for added efficiency.



### What Benefits Do Dual Front And Rear Dash Cams Provide?



Dual front and rear cameras capture complete road coverage, reducing blind spots. This setup records events both ahead and behind the truck. It increases evidence reliability during collisions or road disputes. Rear cameras also assist in safer reversing and parking.



### How Important Is Wi-fi Connectivity In Truck Dash Cams?



Wi-Fi allows quick video transfer to smartphones or cloud storage. It enables live streaming and remote access to footage. This feature simplifies evidence sharing with insurance or law enforcement. Fast Wi-Fi speeds enhance user experience and convenience for truck drivers.
