## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is A Deionized Water System For Car Washing?



A deionized water system removes minerals from water to prevent spots on vehicles. It ensures a spotless, streak-free rinse. This system is ideal for cars, RVs, boats, and motorcycles, eliminating the need for towel drying after washing.



### How Does The Cr Spotless Water System Work?



The CR Spotless Water System uses resin to filter water, removing impurities. It provides spot-free, deionized water for rinsing all vehicle types. Its simplicity makes it perfect for RVs and cars, preventing water spots and reducing drying time.



### Can Deionized Water Systems Eliminate Towel Drying?



Yes, these systems produce spot-free water that dries naturally without leaving marks. Using deionized water means no mineral deposits remain, so towels aren't needed. This saves time and effort, especially for busy car wash routines.



### Are These Systems Suitable For All Vehicle Types?



Absolutely, deionized water systems work effectively on cars, RVs, motorcycles, boats, planes, and yachts. They provide a spotless rinse that protects delicate surfaces and finishes, ensuring all vehicles look clean and polished without water spots.



### What Features Should I Look For In A Deionized Water System?



Look for built-in TDS meters, pre-filters, resin capacity, and portability. Systems with bypass modes and quick connectors enhance usability. These features ensure efficient water purification and easy maintenance for long-lasting, spot-free car washing results.
