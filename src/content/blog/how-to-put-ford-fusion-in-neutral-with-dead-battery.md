## Locate The Shift Lock Release

Locating the shift lock release is a crucial step when you need to put your Ford Fusion in neutral with a dead battery. This small but important feature allows you to manually override the gear shift lock, giving you control even without power. Knowing exactly where and how to find it can save you time and frustration in an emergency.

### Find The Shift Lock Slot

The shift lock slot is usually a small, hidden opening near the gear shifter. In most Ford Fusion models, you'll find it on the center console, close to the base of the shifter. It might be covered by a plastic cap that blends with the interior, so look carefully.

Using your fingers or a small flat tool, gently remove the cover. Once exposed, this slot allows you to insert a key, screwdriver, or similar object to release the shift lock mechanism manually. Have you ever tried to shift gears without power? This slot is your answer.

### Tools Needed For Access

Accessing the shift lock release requires only simple tools that you probably already have. A flathead screwdriver, a car key, or even a small metal object can work perfectly.

* **Flathead screwdriver:**Ideal for prying off the plastic cover without damage.
* **Car key:**Useful for inserting into the slot to press the release.
* **Small metal object:**A paperclip or similar item can also do the job in a pinch.

Having these tools ready in your glove box can make a difference the moment your battery dies. Don’t wait until you’re stuck—prepare now and stay in control.

### Move The Gear Selector

Locate the shift lock release slot near the gear selector. Use the car key or a flat tool to press into the slot. Hold the brake pedal down at the same time. This unlocks the gear shift, allowing you to move it out of park. Slowly slide the gear selector into neutral.

### Confirm The Gear Change

Jump-starting the battery is a quick way to restore power. It uses another car’s battery or a portable jump starter. Connect the jumper cables carefully to avoid sparks or damage.

* Attach the positive cable to the dead battery’s positive terminal.
* Connect the other end to the good battery’s positive terminal.
* Attach the negative cable to the good battery’s negative terminal.
* Connect the other end to a metal surface on the dead car.
* Start the working car and let it run for a few minutes.
* Try shifting your Ford Fusion into neutral.

This method provides enough power to unlock the gear shifter. Always follow safety steps to prevent accidents.

### Manual Transmission Override

The Ford Fusion has a manual override to shift into neutral without power. This bypasses the electronic shift lock system. Check your owner’s manual for the exact location of the override slot.

* Turn off the ignition and set the parking brake.

* Use a small flat tool or key to press into the slot.
* Hold the tool down while moving the gear selector to neutral.

This override lets you move the car without battery power. Use it carefully to avoid damaging the shift mechanism.

The shift lock release on a Ford Fusion is usually beside the gear shifter. It’s a small slot covered by a plastic cap. Removing the cap reveals the slot where you can insert a tool to override the lock and shift to neutral.

### Can I Tow Ford Fusion With Dead Battery In Neutral?

Yes, you can tow a Ford Fusion with a dead battery if it’s in neutral. Use the shift lock release to move the gear to neutral. Ensure the vehicle’s parking brake is off and follow towing safety guidelines to avoid damage.

### Why Won’t Ford Fusion Shift Out Of Park With Dead Battery?

The Ford Fusion uses an electronic shift lock system. With a dead battery, the system disables the gear shift. The shift lock release must be manually engaged to move the gear lever out of park when the battery is dead.
