## Tips For Safe Heavy Hauling

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=hauling-heavy-loads-with-your-toyota-rav4-requires-care-and-attention-safety-is-key-to-avoid-accidents-and-damage&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Hauling heavy loads with your Toyota RAV4 requires care and attention. Safety is key to avoid accidents and damage. Follow these simple tips to handle heavy towing confidently and securely. Proper techniques help keep control and protect your vehicle.

### Weight Distribution

Balance the load evenly across the trailer. Place heavier items low and near the trailer’s axle. Avoid putting too much weight on the hitch. Uneven weight causes swaying and poor handling. Use weight distribution hitches if possible. Check the tongue weight matches the RAV4’s towing specs.

### Braking Techniques

Start braking early and gently. Heavy loads increase stopping distance. Avoid sudden stops or sharp turns. Use engine braking by downshifting on hills. Maintain a safe following distance behind other vehicles. This gives more time to react and stop safely.

### Regular Maintenance Checks

Check tires for proper pressure and wear. Inspect brakes frequently and replace worn pads. Ensure lights and signals on the trailer work well. Check fluid levels, especially transmission and brake fluids. Keep your RAV4 in good condition for reliable towing.
