## Step-by-step Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-maintenance-light-in-a-honda-odyssey-helps-you-keep-track-of-service-intervals-resetting-this-light-is-essential-after-completing-maintenance-tasks&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The maintenance light in a Honda Odyssey helps you keep track of service intervals. Resetting this light is essential after completing maintenance tasks. Follow these steps to ensure your Odyssey is ready for the road.

### For 2011-2017 Models

Resetting the maintenance light for 2011-2017 models is simple. Follow these steps:

* Turn the ignition to the "On" position. Do not start the engine.
* Press the "Trip" button until the oil life indicator appears.
* Hold the "Trip" button for about 10 seconds. The oil life will start to blink.
* Release the button, then press and hold it again for 5 seconds. The oil life will reset to 100%.
* Turn off the ignition. The maintenance light should now be reset.

### For 2018 And Newer Models

The process is slightly different for newer models. Here's how to reset it:

* Press the "Start" button twice. Ensure the car is in the "On" position, but the engine is off.
* Use the steering wheel buttons to navigate the menu. Find the "Maintenance" section.
* Select "Oil Life" and press "Enter".
* Hold the "Enter" button for a few seconds. The oil life will begin to blink.
* Release, then press and hold "Enter" again until the oil life resets to 100%.
* Turn off the car. The maintenance light should be cleared.




## Troubleshooting Reset Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-maintenance-light-on-your-honda-odyssey-usually-goes-smoothly-but-sometimes-the-light-just-wont-turn-off-this-can-be-frustrating-especially-after-youve-done-everything-right&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the maintenance light on your Honda Odyssey usually goes smoothly, but sometimes the light just won’t turn off. This can be frustrating, especially after you’ve done everything right. Understanding what causes these reset issues can save you time and help you avoid unnecessary trips to the mechanic.

### Light Won't Turn Off

If the maintenance light stays on after you’ve reset it, first double-check that the reset process was done correctly. Small mistakes like not fully pressing the odometer button or turning the key off too soon can cause the light to remain.

Another common reason is that the vehicle’s computer still detects an issue. It might be sensing a problem with the oil life monitor or another system that needs attention. Make sure your oil change or maintenance task was fully completed and that the oil sensor is working properly.

Sometimes, disconnecting the battery for a few minutes can help clear any stubborn electronic glitches. However, be cautious—this might reset other systems in your vehicle, such as radio presets and clock settings.

### When To Visit A Mechanic

If you’ve tried resetting the light multiple times and it still won’t go off, it’s a good idea to seek professional help. Persistent warning lights may indicate deeper issues that require diagnostic tools only a mechanic has.

Mechanics can check for error codes that aren’t visible to you, pinpointing exactly why the maintenance light remains lit. Ignoring this could lead to more serious problems down the road, costing you more in repairs.

Remember, your maintenance light is there to protect your engine and keep your Odyssey running smoothly. Don’t ignore it just because the reset seems tricky—sometimes the best fix is knowing when to ask for expert help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Maintenance Light On Honda Odyssey?

To reset the maintenance light, turn the ignition on without starting. Press and hold the trip reset button until the light blinks and turns off. This process clears the maintenance reminder on your Honda Odyssey dashboard.

### When Should I Reset My Honda Odyssey Maintenance Light?

Reset the maintenance light after completing scheduled maintenance, such as oil changes. This ensures accurate reminders for future service and helps maintain your vehicle’s performance and longevity.

### Can I Reset The Maintenance Light Without Tools?

Yes, resetting the maintenance light on a Honda Odyssey does not require any tools. Use the trip reset button on the dashboard and follow the manual reset procedure.

### Why Won’t My Honda Odyssey Maintenance Light Reset?

If the maintenance light won’t reset, double-check the reset steps. Ensure the ignition is in the correct position and hold the reset button long enough. A persistent light may indicate an unresolved issue needing professional service.
