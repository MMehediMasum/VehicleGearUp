## What Is Valet Mode

Valet Mode in the Tesla Model Y is a special feature designed to protect your car and privacy. It limits access to some functions and hides personal information. This mode is useful when handing over your vehicle to a valet or a stranger.

It helps keep your car safe by restricting speed and acceleration. It also prevents access to the glove box, front trunk, and certain settings. Valet Mode ensures the driver cannot change your saved preferences or access private data.

### How Valet Mode Protects Your Tesla Model Y

* Limits top speed and acceleration for safety
* Prevents access to personal information on the screen
* Locks glove box and front trunk to secure belongings
* Disables driver profiles and certain car settings

### When To Use Valet Mode

* Handing your car to a valet at a restaurant or hotel
* Letting a friend or stranger drive your Tesla
* Parking in public areas where you want extra security

### What Happens After Valet Mode Is Activated

* Car performance is limited to prevent fast driving
* Personal data and locations are hidden on the display
* Access to storage compartments is locked
* Alerts notify you if the car is driven outside limits

## Steps To Activate Valet Mode

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=activating-valet-mode-in-your-tesla-model-y-helps-protect-your-personal-information-and-limits-certain-features-this-mode-is-useful-when-handing-over-your-car-to-a-valet-or-someone-else&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Activating Valet Mode in your Tesla Model Y helps protect your personal information and limits certain features. This mode is useful when handing over your car to a valet or someone else. The process is simple and can be done either through the car’s touchscreen or the Tesla mobile app.

Both ways secure your vehicle by restricting access to sensitive data and controlling speed and power. Follow the steps below to quickly enable Valet Mode and keep your Tesla safe.

### Using The Touchscreen

* Tap the car icon at the bottom left of the screen.
* Select “Safety & Security” from the menu.
* Find and tap “Valet Mode.”
* Enter a 4-digit PIN to activate the mode.
* Confirm the PIN by entering it again.
* Valet Mode will now restrict access and limit performance.

### Using The Tesla Mobile App

* Open the Tesla app on your smartphone.
* Tap on “Controls” to access vehicle settings.
* Select “Valet Mode” from the list.
* Create and enter a 4-digit PIN code.
* Confirm the PIN to enable Valet Mode.
* The app will show a confirmation once activated.

## Deactivating Valet Mode

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=deactivating-valet-mode-in-your-tesla-model-y-is-simple-and-quick-this-mode-limits-access-to-certain-features-to-protect-your-personal-information-and-vehicle-settings&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Deactivating Valet Mode in your Tesla Model Y is simple and quick. This mode limits access to certain features to protect your personal information and vehicle settings. Turning it off restores full control to you.

Choose between the touchscreen inside the car or the Tesla mobile app. Both options provide secure ways to disable Valet Mode whenever needed.

### Via Touchscreen

Start by tapping the car icon at the bottom of the screen. Select “Safety & Security” from the menu. Find “Valet Mode” and tap to open it.

Enter your four-digit PIN to confirm your identity. The system will then disable Valet Mode immediately. You regain full access to all vehicle features.

### Via Mobile App

Open the Tesla app on your smartphone. Tap the “Controls” section to access vehicle settings. Locate the “Valet Mode” option in the list.

Tap to open Valet Mode settings and enter your PIN. The app will confirm when Valet Mode is turned off. You can now use your Model Y without restrictions.

  


## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Troubleshooting common issues with Valet Mode in your Tesla Model Y can save you time and frustration. Sometimes, the feature may not activate as expected or you might face password-related hiccups. Understanding these problems helps you maintain control over your vehicle’s security and usability.

### Valet Mode Not Activating

If Valet Mode refuses to turn on, first check if your Tesla software is up to date. Outdated software can cause features to behave unpredictably.

Next, ensure the car is in Park and all doors are closed. Valet Mode won’t engage if the car is still in gear or a door is open.

Sometimes, a simple reboot of the touchscreen can fix activation issues. Hold down both scroll wheels on the steering wheel until the screen goes black, then wait for it to restart.

Are you using Tesla’s mobile app to activate Valet Mode? Verify that your phone has a strong internet connection and that the app is logged in properly.

### Password Problems

Setting and remembering your Valet Mode password can be tricky. If you forget it, you won’t be able to disable Valet Mode without contacting Tesla support.

To avoid this, choose a password you can recall easily but isn’t obvious to others. Writing it down temporarily in a secure place can help during the learning phase.

If your password isn’t accepted, double-check for typos or accidental use of uppercase letters. The password is case-sensitive and must be entered exactly as set.

Have you tried resetting your password through the Tesla app? Sometimes, re-entering a new password clears glitches causing the system to reject your input.
