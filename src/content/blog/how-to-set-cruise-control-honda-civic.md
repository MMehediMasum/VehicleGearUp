## Cruise Control Basics

Cruise control is a useful feature in many Honda Civic models. It helps maintain a steady speed without keeping your foot on the gas. Understanding how cruise control works can improve your driving comfort and safety. The following sections explain what cruise control does and its benefits.

### What Cruise Control Does

Cruise control keeps your car moving at a set speed. It works on highways or open roads. Once activated, you do not need to press the accelerator. The system automatically adjusts the throttle to maintain speed. It helps avoid speeding and reduces fatigue on long drives.

### Benefits Of Using Cruise Control

* Reduces driver fatigue by keeping a steady speed
* Improves fuel efficiency by avoiding sudden acceleration
* Maintains consistent speed, helping to follow speed limits
* Allows better focus on steering and road conditions
* Helps prevent accidental speeding tickets

## Activating Cruise Control

Activating cruise control in your Honda Civic makes long drives easier and more relaxing. This feature keeps your car moving at a steady speed without pressing the gas pedal. Understanding how to activate it helps you enjoy smoother rides and better fuel efficiency.

### Turning On The System

Locate the cruise control buttons on the steering wheel or the stalk near the steering column. Press the "ON" button to activate the system. A small icon will appear on the dashboard, showing that cruise control is ready. Make sure you are driving at a speed above 25 mph, as the system will not engage below this speed.

### Setting Desired Speed

Once the system is on and your speed is steady, press the "SET" button. This action locks your current speed into the cruise control system. You can adjust the speed later using the "+" or "−" buttons on the steering wheel. To cancel the cruise control, tap the brake or press the "CANCEL" button.

Adjusting your Honda Civic’s cruise control speed while driving is simple and helps maintain a smooth ride without constantly using the gas pedal. Knowing how to increase or decrease speed safely can make your journey more comfortable and efficient. Let’s look at how to make these adjustments on the go.

### Increasing Speed

To increase your cruise control speed, gently press the "RES/ACCEL" button on your steering wheel. Each press typically raises the speed by 1 mph, but holding the button can speed up the process for quicker adjustments.

Imagine you're approaching a highway with a slightly higher speed limit. Instead of canceling cruise control, you can smoothly increase your set speed without disrupting your flow. This keeps your hands on the wheel and your focus on the road.

### Decreasing Speed

To slow down while cruise control is active, press the "SET/COAST" button. Like increasing speed, one press lowers your speed by 1 mph, or you can hold it down for a gradual reduction.

Have you ever needed to slow down for traffic but didn’t want to fully disengage cruise control? This feature lets you adjust safely without braking abruptly, helping you maintain control and comfort throughout your drive.

### Using The Cancel Button

The cancel button pauses cruise control without erasing the set speed. Press it to slow down or stop for traffic or obstacles. The system stays on standby, ready for you to resume. You can also cancel by pressing the brake or clutch pedal. This gives full control back to you safely.

### Resuming Previous Speed

Press the resume button to return to the last set speed. The car accelerates smoothly to the stored speed. This helps maintain consistent driving without manual adjustments. Resume works only if cruise control is paused, not off. Use this feature to keep your pace without extra effort.




## Turning Off Cruise Control

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=turning-off-cruise-control-in-a-honda-civic-is-simple-and-quick-knowing-how-to-deactivate-it-properly-ensures-safe-driving-and-full-control-of-your-vehicle&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Turning off cruise control in a Honda Civic is simple and quick. Knowing how to deactivate it properly ensures safe driving and full control of your vehicle. There are two main ways to turn off cruise control: manually and automatically. Each way works in different situations to stop the car from maintaining speed.

### Manual Deactivation

Manual deactivation lets you switch off cruise control by using controls on the steering wheel or dashboard. This gives you direct control over the system.

* Press the “Cancel” button on the steering wheel to pause cruise control.
* Press the “Off” button to completely turn off the system.
* Pressing the brake pedal also manually deactivates cruise control.

These actions stop the car from keeping a set speed immediately. You regain full control over acceleration and braking.

### Automatic Deactivation

Automatic deactivation occurs without pressing any buttons. The system senses certain actions and turns off cruise control for safety.

* Pressing the brake pedal automatically cancels cruise control.
* Pressing the clutch pedal in manual transmission models also turns it off.
* Turning off the ignition or shifting into neutral stops cruise control.

This feature helps prevent accidents by stopping cruise control when you need to slow down or stop quickly.

  


## Safety Tips For Cruise Control Use

Using cruise control in your Honda Civic can make long drives more comfortable and reduce fatigue. However, safety should always come first when engaging this feature. Knowing when and how to use cruise control responsibly helps you maintain control and react quickly to road conditions.

### When To Avoid Using Cruise Control

Some situations are simply not safe for cruise control. Avoid using it on wet or icy roads where traction is unpredictable. Slippery surfaces demand constant speed adjustments that cruise control can’t manage effectively.

Heavy traffic is another scenario where manual control is better. You need to respond quickly to sudden stops or changes in speed, and cruise control can delay your reaction time. Also, steer clear of cruise control on winding roads where frequent speed and steering adjustments are necessary.

### Staying Alert On The Road

Using cruise control doesn’t mean you can zone out behind the wheel. Keep your hands on the steering wheel and your eyes on the road at all times. Your brain must stay active and ready to take over instantly if something unexpected happens.

Think of cruise control as a helper, not a replacement for your attention. Regularly scan mirrors and monitor traffic flow. Have you ever noticed how quickly conditions change? Staying alert ensures you’re prepared for those moments.




## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Dealing with cruise control issues on your Honda Civic can be frustrating, especially when you expect smooth, hands-free driving. Understanding common problems helps you fix them quickly and get back on the road with confidence. Here are some typical issues and how you can troubleshoot them effectively.

### Cruise Control Not Engaging

If your cruise control refuses to turn on, the problem might be simpler than you think. First, check if your vehicle speed is high enough—most systems won't activate below a certain speed, usually around 25 mph.

Also, inspect the brake lights. A faulty brake light switch can trick the system into thinking you’re braking, disabling cruise control. You can test this by seeing if your brake lights illuminate when you press the pedal.

Sometimes, the issue lies in the cruise control fuse. Locate your fuse box and check for a blown fuse related to cruise control. Replacing a blown fuse often restores functionality without a visit to the mechanic.

### Unintended Speed Changes

Unexpected acceleration or deceleration while using cruise control can feel unsafe. This might happen if the throttle cable or sensor is dirty or misaligned. Cleaning or adjusting these parts can stabilize your speed.

Another common cause is an uneven road surface or steep hills, which can confuse your Civic's system. Have you noticed your car speeding up downhill? Using manual control in these situations might be safer.

Electrical issues in the cruise control module can also cause erratic speed changes. If you’ve ruled out mechanical causes, a diagnostic scan tool can identify sensor errors or software glitches.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Activate Cruise Control On A Honda Civic?

To activate cruise control, first press the "CRUISE" button on the steering wheel. Accelerate to your desired speed, then press the "SET" button to maintain it.

### Can I Adjust Speed While Using Cruise Control?

Yes, you can increase or decrease speed by pressing the “RES/ACCEL” or “DECEL/SET” buttons on the steering wheel.

### How Do I Cancel Cruise Control In A Honda Civic?

You can cancel cruise control by pressing the brake pedal or the “CANCEL” button on the steering wheel.

### Is Cruise Control Safe For City Driving?

Cruise control is best used on highways and open roads, not in heavy city traffic or stop-and-go conditions.
