## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Spark Plug Wires Fit Chevy 350 With Headers?



JDMSPEED, Moroso Ultra Series, and JEGS Red Hot Pow’r wires fit Chevy 350 with headers. They offer proper length and heat resistance for under-header routing. These wires ensure optimal performance and durability for engines with headers.



### Why Choose 8mm Or 10.5mm Spark Plug Wires?



8mm wires like JEGS Red Hot Pow’r offer flexibility and heat resistance. 10. 5mm wires like JDMSPEED provide better insulation and durability. Both sizes fit Chevy 350 engines but choice depends on header clearance and performance needs.



### How Do Heat Shield Sleeves Protect Spark Plug Wires?



Heat shield sleeves, such as SHINEHOME covers, protect wires from header heat up to 2500°F. They prevent wire melting and improve wire lifespan. These sleeves are essential for high-performance Chevy 350 engines with headers.



### Are Angled Boots Better For Under-header Spark Plug Wires?



Yes, 90 or 135-degree angled boots fit better under headers. They reduce wire stress and improve clearance. Products like Moroso Ultra Series and JDMSPEED use angled boots for better routing and protection.



### Which Spark Plug Wires Are Best For Hei Distributors?



Wires designed for HEI distributors, like JDMSPEED and Moroso 51045 Ultra Series, ensure proper connection and performance. They come with compatible boots and length, enhancing ignition efficiency on Chevy 350 engines.



### How To Choose Spark Plug Wires For Sbc And Bbc Chevy Engines?



Select wires based on engine size, heat exposure, and routing needs. JDMSPEED and Spocoro offer sets compatible with SBC and BBC 350, 383, 454 engines. Focus on wire diameter, boot angle, and heat resistance for best results.
