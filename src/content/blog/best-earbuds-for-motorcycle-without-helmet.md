## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Earbuds Are Best For Motorcycles Without Helmets?



PocBuds and TOZO Hybrid earbuds are ideal for motorcycles without helmets. They offer secure fit, waterproof design, and long battery life. Earhooks and noise cancellation enhance comfort and sound clarity during rides.



### How Does Noise Cancellation Improve Motorcycle Earbuds?



Noise cancellation reduces wind and road noise, ensuring clearer calls and music. Models like TOZO Hybrid and MIPEACE offer ENC mics for effective noise reduction, improving rider safety and audio experience.



### Are Waterproof Earbuds Necessary For Motorcyclists?



Yes, waterproof earbuds like PocBuds (IPX7) and TOZO Hybrid (IPX8) protect against rain and sweat. This durability ensures reliable performance in all weather conditions during rides.



### How Long Is The Battery Life On These Motorcycle Earbuds?



Most earbuds offer between 14 to 80 hours of playtime. PocBuds provide 80 hours, while TOZO and JBL models offer 32 to 59 hours, supporting long rides without frequent charging.



### Do Earbuds With Earhooks Provide Better Stability For Riders?



Yes, earbuds with earhooks like PocBuds and JLab Go Sport+ offer improved stability. They prevent earbuds from falling during bumpy rides or high-speed movements, making them perfect for motorcyclists.



### Can Motorcycle Earbuds Support Phone Calls And Voice Assistants?



Absolutely. Many models, including Elgin Discord Gen 3 and ELIKIDSTO, support clear calls and voice assistant commands. This allows riders to stay connected without distractions.
