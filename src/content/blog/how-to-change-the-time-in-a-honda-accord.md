## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls in a Honda Accord is the first step to changing the time. The controls vary slightly depending on the model year and trim. Knowing where to find these controls saves time and avoids confusion.

Most Honda Accords have the clock controls integrated into the dashboard or infotainment system. The buttons or touchscreen options allow easy adjustment of hours and minutes. Identifying the correct controls is crucial before making any changes.

### Check The Dashboard Panel

Look closely at the dashboard near the radio or infotainment screen. Small buttons labeled “Clock,” “H,” or “M” often appear here. These buttons let you adjust the hour and minute settings manually.

### Use The Infotainment Touchscreen

Newer Honda Accord models feature a touchscreen interface. Access the settings menu by tapping the gear icon or “Setup.” Find the “Clock” or “Time” option to adjust the time digitally.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual

The owner’s manual provides detailed instructions for locating clock controls. It shows exact button locations and steps for each model year. Keep the manual handy for reference during the process.

### Locate Steering Wheel Controls

Some models include clock settings accessible via steering wheel buttons. These controls may appear as part of the audio or menu buttons. Use them to navigate to the clock settings without reaching the dashboard.

## Change Time Via Infotainment System

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-time-on-your-honda-accord-using-the-infotainment-system-is-quick-and-simple-this-system-lets-you-adjust-the-clock-without-any-tools-or-extra-devices&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the time on your Honda Accord using the infotainment system is quick and simple. This system lets you adjust the clock without any tools or extra devices. You can update the time to stay accurate for appointments and travel.

The touchscreen interface makes the process clear and direct. Follow the steps below to set the correct time easily.

### Access The Settings Menu

Start by turning on your car's infotainment system. Tap the home button on the screen to open the main menu. Look for the “Settings” icon, usually represented by a gear symbol. Select it to enter the settings area where you can customize many features of your vehicle.

### Navigate To Clock Settings

Inside the settings menu, find the option labeled “Clock” or “Time.” This section is dedicated to managing the date and time settings. Tap on it to open the clock adjustment screen. Here, you can see the current time displayed clearly.

### Set The Correct Time

Use the on-screen arrows or input fields to change the hours and minutes. Make sure to choose AM or PM if your system uses a 12-hour format. Double-check the time before confirming. Press “Save” or “OK” to update the clock. The new time will appear on your dashboard and infotainment display immediately.

Changing the time on your Honda Accord can vary depending on the model year. Understanding these differences helps you adjust the clock quickly without frustration. Let’s look at tips tailored for older and newer Honda Accord models to make the process smooth for you.

### Older Honda Accord Models

In older Honda Accords, the clock settings are often controlled by physical buttons located near the radio or on the dashboard. You might find buttons labeled “H” for hour and “M” for minute, which makes it straightforward to adjust the time.

Press and hold the “Clock” or “Time” button until the display starts blinking. Then, use the “H” and “M” buttons to set the correct hour and minute. This hands-on method is simple but requires patience if you need to scroll through many hours or minutes.

Have you noticed how some older models might reset the clock after disconnecting the battery? If so, keep a note of your current time before any battery work to save yourself from surprises later.

### Newer Honda Accord Models

Newer models usually integrate the clock settings into the infotainment system, which means you adjust time through the touchscreen or digital menus. This setup can feel complex at first, but it offers more control and synchronization options, like syncing with your phone or GPS.

Look for the “Settings” menu, then select “Clock” or “Time & Date.” From here, you can manually set the time or choose to update it automatically via GPS. Automatic updates are handy if you travel across time zones frequently.

Sometimes, the touchscreen can be slow or unresponsive, making time adjustments frustrating. Have you tried restarting the system or updating your car’s software? These small actions often resolve glitches and improve your experience.




## Common Problems And Fixes

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=changing-the-time-on-a-honda-accord-can-sometimes-lead-to-common-issues-these-problems-usually-have-simple-fixes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Changing the time on a Honda Accord can sometimes lead to common issues. These problems usually have simple fixes. Understanding these helps keep the clock accurate and the display functioning well.

### Clock Not Holding Time

The clock might reset after turning off the car. This often happens if the battery is weak or disconnected. A low battery can cause the clock to lose power and reset.

* Check the car battery and replace it if needed.
* Ensure the fuse related to the clock is not blown.
* If the problem continues, the clock module might need repair.

### Display Not Responding

Sometimes, the clock display does not respond to button presses. This makes setting the time difficult or impossible. Dirt or worn-out buttons can cause this issue.

* Clean the buttons gently with a soft cloth.
* Try pressing buttons firmly but carefully to reset the display.
* If still unresponsive, the control panel may require professional service.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Clock On A Honda Accord?

To change the clock, turn on your Honda Accord’s ignition. Press and hold the “Clock” button until the time blinks. Use the “Hour” and “Minute” buttons to set the correct time. Press “Clock” again to save your settings.

### Can I Change The Time Without The Engine Running?

Yes, you can change the time with the ignition in the “Accessory” mode. This allows you to adjust the clock without starting the engine. Just follow the same steps as when the engine is on.

### Why Won’t My Honda Accord Clock Update Automatically?

Some Honda Accord models don’t have an automatic time update feature. You must manually set the time after power loss or daylight saving changes. Check your owner’s manual for model-specific instructions on clock settings.

### What If My Honda Accord Clock Is Flashing?

A flashing clock usually means the battery was disconnected or power was lost. Reset the clock by pressing and holding the “Clock” button, then adjust the time using the “Hour” and “Minute” buttons.
