## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Tire For A 3/4 Ton Truck?



The best tire depends on your truck’s use and terrain. Look for durable tires with strong traction for heavy loads and off-road conditions. Brands like Michelin, BFGoodrich, and Goodyear offer excellent options for 3/4 ton trucks.



### How Do Traction Boards Help 3/4 Ton Trucks?



Traction boards provide extra grip on sand, mud, or snow. They prevent your truck from getting stuck by improving tire traction. They are essential for off-road recovery and emergency situations, especially for 4WD trucks like 3/4 ton models.



### Can I Use A 5-ton Electric Car Jack On A 3/4 Ton Truck?



Yes, a 5-ton electric car jack like Simtamy’s kit safely lifts 3/4 ton trucks. It offers a wide lifting range and includes useful tools like impact wrenches and tire inflators for quick tire changes.



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Traction Bars On 3/4 Ton Trucks?



Traction bars reduce wheel hop and improve stability during acceleration. They enhance tire contact with the road, increasing control and safety. Tuff Country traction bars are popular for Chevy 3/4 ton trucks from 1973-1987.



### Are Dual Shock Kits Recommended For 3/4 Ton Trucks?



Yes, dual shock kits improve suspension performance and ride comfort. They help handle heavy loads and rough terrains by better absorbing shocks. Tuff Country’s front dual shock kits fit Chevy 3/4 ton trucks from 1969-1987.



### How Do Wheel Dollies Assist In Truck Maintenance?



Wheel dollies, like the Omega 47053, help move heavy trucks easily in tight spaces. They support up to 3/4 ton capacity and simplify garage repairs by allowing effortless truck repositioning without engine use.
