## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Aroma Beads For Car Freshies?



Aroma beads are small scented beads used to freshen car interiors. They slowly release fragrance and absorb odors. They are perfect for DIY car fresheners and come in scented or unscented varieties. They provide a long-lasting and customizable scent experience for vehicles.



### How Do I Use Unscented Aroma Beads?



Unscented aroma beads can be mixed with essential oils or fragrance oils. This lets you create custom car freshies with your preferred scents. Simply add drops of fragrance to the beads, let them absorb, and place them in silicone molds or sachets for use in your car.

Yes, premium scented beads like MUBYOK Love Spell offer stronger, longer-lasting fragrances. They are pre-scented with quality oils that maintain scent for weeks. These beads provide a consistent aroma and better odor control compared to standard or unscented beads.



### Can Aroma Beads Be Used For Crafts Besides Car Freshies?



Absolutely. Aroma beads are versatile for crafts such as sachets, candles, and potpourri. Their gel-like texture and fragrance retention make them ideal for various DIY projects. Many crafters use silicone molds to shape freshies or decorative scented items.



### What Scent Options Are Available For Car Freshie Aroma Beads?



Popular scent options include Love Spell, Icy Black, and earthy masculine blends. Brands like Lone Star Candles and MUBYOK offer unique fragrances made in the USA. You can also create personalized scents by adding essential oils to unscented beads.
