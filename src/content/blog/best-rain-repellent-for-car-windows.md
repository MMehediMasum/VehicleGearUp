### Are Ceramic Rain Repellents Worth It?



Yes, ceramic rain repellents like Cerakote provide durable, long-lasting protection. They form a hydrophobic ceramic coating that repels water better than traditional treatments. ### Can Rain Repellents Prevent Fogging Inside Windows?

Most rain repellents last from a few weeks to a couple of months. For best results, reapply every 4 to 6 weeks or after heavy washing. Regular application ensures consistent water beading and clear visibility during wet weather.

Rain repellents generally improve wiper performance by reducing water buildup. Water beads off quickly, allowing wipers to clear windows more easily. However, always use good quality wiper blades for optimal results and avoid streaks or smearing on treated glass.
