Locking your Honda Accord while it’s running keeps the doors secured against unexpected entry. It prevents anyone from opening your doors suddenly, which is especially helpful when you’re stopped at a red light or waiting in traffic.

### Using Key Fob

The key fob is a remote control device that locks your car doors instantly. Press the lock button once to secure all doors while the engine runs. It works from a short distance, letting you lock the car before walking away. Some models provide a beep sound or light flash as confirmation.

### Manual Locking From Inside

You can lock your Honda Accord manually using the door lock lever. Push the lock button or slide the lock switch on the door panel. This action secures the door without turning off the engine. It is useful when you stay inside the car but want the doors locked.

### Using Door Lock Button

Each door has a lock button near the handle or on the armrest. Press it to lock the individual door while the car is running. This method is quick and simple, especially if you are inside the vehicle. It ensures safety without needing the key fob or reaching the manual lock.
