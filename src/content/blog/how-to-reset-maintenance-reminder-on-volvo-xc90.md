## Step-by-step Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-maintenance-reminder-on-your-volvo-xc90-keeps-your-cars-service-alerts-accurate-this-process-helps-avoid-unnecessary-visits-to-the-service-center&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the maintenance reminder on your Volvo XC90 keeps your car's service alerts accurate. This process helps avoid unnecessary visits to the service center. Follow these clear steps to reset the reminder easily. Each step is simple and quick to perform.

### Accessing The Menu

Start by turning on the ignition without starting the engine. Locate the center display screen in your Volvo XC90. Use the control knob or touchscreen to enter the main menu. Look for the settings icon. Press it to open the car’s settings options.

### Navigating To Maintenance Settings

Within the settings menu, find the section labeled "Vehicle" or "Car Settings." Scroll through the options until you see "Maintenance" or "Service." Select this option to view maintenance details. This area shows your current service reminders and intervals.

### Confirming The Reset

Choose the option that says "Reset Maintenance Reminder" or similar. The screen will ask for confirmation to reset the alert. Confirm your choice by selecting "Yes" or "OK." Wait a moment while the system updates. After confirmation, the maintenance reminder will clear.

### Reminder Not Resetting

If your maintenance reminder refuses to reset, it could be due to a few simple reasons. One common cause is not following the exact reset sequence required by your specific model year. Make sure you hold the reset button long enough and perform the steps precisely as outlined in your owner's manual.

Another factor might be a low battery or electrical glitch interfering with the system's ability to register the reset. Try turning off the engine completely and then attempting the reset again after a few minutes. Sometimes, disconnecting the car battery for a short time can help clear any glitches.

### Dashboard Display Errors

Errors on the dashboard after a reset attempt can be confusing. You might see warning lights staying on or strange messages appearing. This often signals a communication issue between the car’s computer and the display system.

Check if all software updates are current, as outdated software can cause display malfunctions. Also, inspect the dashboard for any signs of damage or loose connections. If problems persist, a professional diagnostic scan might be necessary to pinpoint the exact cause.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset The Maintenance Reminder On Volvo Xc90?

To reset the maintenance reminder, turn on the ignition without starting the engine. Press and hold the trip meter button until the service light blinks and resets. Release the button, and the reminder will be cleared.

### When Should I Reset The Maintenance Reminder On Volvo Xc90?

Reset the maintenance reminder after every scheduled service or oil change. This ensures the system accurately tracks the next maintenance interval and keeps your vehicle running smoothly.

### Can I Reset The Maintenance Reminder Without Tools?

Yes, you can reset the reminder using the trip meter button on the dashboard. No special tools or diagnostic devices are required for this process.

### What If The Maintenance Reminder Doesn’t Reset On Volvo Xc90?

If the reminder doesn’t reset, try repeating the reset steps carefully. If it still fails, consult a Volvo dealer or a qualified mechanic for assistance.
