## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Ideal Battery Range For Senior Electric Bikes?



Seniors should look for electric bikes with a battery range of at least 60 km. This ensures longer rides without frequent charging. Models like MIOOX and ULTRABIKES offer ranges up to 140 km, perfect for extended comfort and convenience.



### Are Three-wheel Electric Bikes Safer For Seniors?



Yes, three-wheel electric bikes provide better stability and balance. This reduces the risk of falls, making them safer for seniors. The wider foot decks and sturdy frames, like those in GODAUP and Gigaglitz models, enhance safety during rides.



### How Fast Can Senior Electric Scooters Go?



Senior electric scooters typically reach speeds between 45 km/h and 55 km/h. These speeds provide a balance between safety and efficient travel. For instance, ULTRABIKES Zeal Plus offers a top speed of 55 km/h, suitable for seniors.



### Do These Electric Bikes Require A License Or Registration?



Most models listed, such as MIOOX and GODAUP, are non-RTO and require no license. This makes them convenient for seniors who want hassle-free riding. Always check local regulations before purchase to ensure compliance.



### What Braking Systems Are Common In Senior Electric Bikes?



Disc and drum brakes are commonly used in these electric bikes. They provide reliable stopping power and safety. For example, MIOOX scooters feature both disc and drum brakes, ensuring secure and smooth braking for seniors.



### How Long Is The Battery Warranty For These Scooters?



Battery warranties typically last between 2 to 3 years. MIOOX offers a 3-year battery warranty, while ULTRABIKES provides 2 years. This coverage supports long-term use and peace of mind for senior riders.
