## Locate The Manual Trunk Release

Locating the manual trunk release is essential when the Ford Fusion’s battery is dead. The electronic trunk opener won’t work without power. Knowing the exact spot of the manual release lets you open the trunk safely. This process is simple with clear steps and a bit of guidance.

### Check The Owner’s Manual

The owner’s manual holds important details about the manual trunk release. Find the section on the trunk or emergency release. It shows the exact location and how to use the release lever. The manual often includes diagrams to make it easier. Keep the manual in the car for quick reference.

### Find The Release Lever Inside The Car

In most Ford Fusion models, the manual trunk release is inside the cabin. Check near the driver’s seat, usually on the floor or side panel. The lever may have a trunk icon or label. Pulling this lever will open the trunk even without battery power. It’s a simple tool to have in an emergency.




## Access The Trunk Through The Rear Seats

Accessing the trunk of your Ford Fusion through the rear seats can be a lifesaver, especially when the battery is dead and the usual electronic trunk release won’t work. This method gives you a manual way to get inside without relying on power or external tools. It’s straightforward once you know where to look and how to move the seats out of the way.

### Fold Down Rear Seats

Start by locating the levers or buttons that release the rear seatbacks. These are usually found at the top or side of the seat, near the door or in the trunk area if accessible. Pulling the lever will allow the seat to fold forward, creating a clear path to the trunk.

If your model has split-fold seats, fold down the section closest to the trunk release mechanism for easier access. This simple step can save you time and effort, especially if you’re dealing with a dead battery and limited light.

### Reach The Trunk Release Mechanism

Once the seat is folded down, look for a manual trunk release lever or cable inside the trunk area. This is often a small handle or cord designed for emergency use. Reach through the opening you created by folding the seats and pull this lever to unlock the trunk.

Don’t rush this part. Feel around carefully as the release might be tucked away or covered by upholstery. Have you ever noticed how many cars hide their emergency releases in plain sight? This hands-on approach can quickly get your trunk open without needing jumper cables or a locksmith.

## Try The Key Fob Backup Method

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=when-your-ford-fusions-battery-dies-the-trunk-wont-open-with-the-usual-remote-commands-but-dont-panic-just-yet&w=424&h=324&c=7)

When your Ford Fusion’s battery dies, the trunk won’t open with the usual remote commands. But don’t panic just yet. The key fob backup method offers a simple, reliable way to access the trunk without power.

This method requires using the physical key hidden inside your key fob. It’s a feature many overlook until they’re stuck, but it can save you time and stress.

### Use Physical Key From Fob

Your Ford Fusion’s key fob contains a hidden mechanical key. To access it, look for a small release button or slider on the fob’s side. Press or slide it to pull out the physical key.

This key doesn’t rely on the battery, so it’s perfect for emergencies. Keep it handy in your wallet or glove box just in case you ever need it.

### Insert Key In Trunk Lock

Locate the trunk lock on your Ford Fusion, usually found just above the license plate or integrated near the trunk handle. Insert the physical key into the lock and turn it gently.

Be careful not to force the key, as this could damage the lock. Turning the key should manually unlock the trunk, giving you access even with a dead battery.

Have you ever found yourself locked out with a dead battery and no backup plan? This small step can make a big difference and keep you moving without calling for help.
