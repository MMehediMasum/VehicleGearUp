## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Electric Da Sander For Auto Body Work?



The Inditrust 700W Dual-Action Orbital Sander is ideal. It offers variable speed from 600 to 3700 RPM. It’s perfect for cars, bikes, and furniture detailing with smooth finishing results.



### How To Choose The Right Electric Car Polisher?



Look for adjustable speed, motor power, and pad size. A 1400W motor with 7-inch pads like Mass Pro models suits auto body work well. Variable speed helps control polishing precision and finish quality.



### Are Dual-action Sanders Better For Auto Body Polishing?



Yes, dual-action sanders reduce swirl marks and overheating. They combine rotary and orbital motions. This makes them safer for paintwork and gives a smoother, professional finish.



### What Features Improve Electric Polishers For Car Detailing?



Key features include variable speed control, ergonomic design, and spindle lock for easy pad changes. Models like Sulfar 450W and Digital Craft 180mm offer these, enhancing control and comfort during use.



### Can Electric Polishers Be Used For Sanding And Waxing?



Absolutely. Most electric polishers, like Mass Pro and Artnery models, handle sanding, polishing, and waxing. Adjustable speed settings allow switching between these tasks easily for versatile auto body work.
