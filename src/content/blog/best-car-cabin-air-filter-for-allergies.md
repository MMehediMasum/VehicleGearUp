## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Cabin Air Filter Good For Allergies?



A good allergy cabin air filter traps pollen, dust, and allergens effectively. HEPA and activated carbon filters are best for allergy relief. They ensure clean air inside your car, reducing allergy symptoms while driving.



### How Often Should I Replace My Car Cabin Air Filter?



Replace your cabin air filter every 12,000 to 15,000 miles or once a year. Frequent replacement ensures optimal filtration performance and reduces allergens inside your vehicle. Check your car manual for specific recommendations.



### Are Hepa Cabin Air Filters Better For Allergy Sufferers?



Yes, HEPA filters capture 99. 97% of particles as small as 0. 3 microns. This includes pollen, dust, and other allergens. Using a HEPA cabin filter significantly improves air quality for allergy sufferers.



### Can Activated Carbon Filters Eliminate Odors And Allergens?



Yes, activated carbon filters absorb odors, harmful gases, and allergens. They improve air freshness and reduce irritants inside your car cabin. Many premium filters combine HEPA and activated carbon for better protection.



### Which Cabin Air Filters Fit Most Cars And Trucks?



Filters like Pureflow and Puroma fit most cars, trucks, and SUVs. Always check compatibility with your vehicle model before purchase. Most brands list compatible makes and models for easy selection.



### Do Cabin Air Filters Affect Airflow Or Car Ventilation?



High-quality cabin air filters like Spearhead provide excellent filtration without reducing airflow. They maintain good ventilation while trapping allergens. Poor-quality filters may restrict airflow, so choose reputable brands for best results.
