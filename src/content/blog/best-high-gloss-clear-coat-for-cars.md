## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best High Gloss Clear Coat For Cars?



The best high gloss clear coat offers durability, shine, and easy application. SprayMax USC 2K and LiME LiNE kits deliver professional, long-lasting finishes for cars, trucks, and motorcycles.



### How Do 2k Clear Coats Differ From Acrylic Enamels?



2K clear coats use a hardener for superior durability and gloss. Acrylic enamels provide a clear finish but are less resistant to chemicals and scratches.



### Can I Use Aerosol Clear Coats For Automotive Touch-ups?



Yes, aerosol clear coats like SprayMax USC 2K and Dupli-Color offer easy, precise touch-ups with a smooth high gloss finish.



### What Hardener Ratio Is Ideal For 2k Automotive Clear Coats?



A common hardener mix ratio is 4:1 (clear coat to hardener). This ensures optimal curing and glossy, durable results in products like LiME LiNE kits.



### Are Usa-made Clear Coats Better For Car Refinishing?



USA-made clear coats, such as Auto Paint Depot’s, often meet high quality and safety standards. They provide reliable coverage and lasting gloss.



### How Long Does It Take For 2k Clear Coats To Dry?



2K clear coats dry to the touch within 20-30 minutes and fully cure in 24-48 hours, depending on temperature and humidity.
