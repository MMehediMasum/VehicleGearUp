## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Welder For Automotive Body Work?



The best welder for automotive body work is a spot welder with versatile modes. It ensures precise dent repair and metal bonding. Models like VEVOR Stud Welder Kits offer multiple welding modes and complete accessories for efficient auto body repairs.



### How Does A Stud Welder Dent Repair Kit Work?



A stud welder dent repair kit uses electrical current to weld studs onto dents. These studs allow pulling out dents without damaging the paint. This method is quick, effective, and ideal for automotive body work, preserving the vehicle’s original finish.



### Which Welding Mode Suits Car Dent Repair Best?



Auto and manual welding modes are ideal for car dent repair. Auto mode offers ease and consistency, while manual mode provides control for detailed work. Kits with multiple modes, like the VEVOR 7-mode spot welder, cater to various dent sizes and metal thicknesses.



### Can I Use A Spot Welder On All Vehicle Metals?



Spot welders work best on common automotive metals like steel and aluminum. However, some metals require specific settings or accessories. Always check the welder’s specifications to ensure compatibility with your vehicle’s body material for safe and effective repairs.



### What Accessories Come With A Stud Welder Dent Puller Kit?



Stud welder dent puller kits typically include studs, puller guns, welding cables, and hammers. Some kits offer additional tools like plastic welders or staplers for bumper repair. Complete accessory sets enhance the ease and precision of automotive dent removal tasks.



### Is A 110v Or 220v Welder Better For Auto Body Repairs?



110V welders are suitable for light to medium auto body work and are more portable. 220V welders provide higher power for heavy-duty repairs and thicker metals. Choose based on your repair needs and available power supply for optimal performance.
