## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Touch Up Paint For Car Chips?



The best touch up paint matches your car’s color exactly. Choose quick-drying, easy-to-use pens like FIXEZPRO or SYOAUTO for effective chip repair. These products provide lasting coverage and restore your vehicle’s appearance without professional help.



### How Do I Choose The Right Color For Touch Up Paint?



Select touch up paint by matching your car’s exact color code. Brands like Dupli-Color and FIXEZPRO offer color-accurate options. Checking your vehicle’s paint code ensures a seamless repair that blends well with the original paint.



### Can Touch Up Paint Fix Deep Scratches On Cars?



Touch up paint works best for small chips and light scratches. For deep scratches, use a 2-in-1 paint pen with filler, like SYOAUTO or Brohood. These products provide better coverage and a smoother finish for deeper damage.



### Are Touch Up Paint Pens Safe And Non-toxic?



Yes, most automotive touch up paint pens, such as FIXEZPRO and SYOAUTO, are safe and non-toxic. They use automotive-grade formulas designed for easy, safe application without harmful fumes or chemicals.



### How Long Does Touch Up Paint Take To Dry?



Most touch up paints dry quickly, usually within 10 to 30 minutes. Quick-drying formulas like Brohood and UBOG let you apply multiple coats faster, speeding up the repair process and minimizing downtime.



### Do Touch Up Paint Kits Include Applicators?



Many kits include applicators, but disposable micro applicators like the 1. 5mm brushes improve precision. These tools help apply paint smoothly, especially for small chips and scratches, ensuring a neat and professional-looking finish.
