**If you own a 2013 Honda Odyssey, knowing how to reset the oil life indicator is key to keeping your vehicle running smoothly. Ignoring this step can lead to confusion about when your next oil change is due and might even affect your engine’s health.**

In this guide, you’ll learn the simple, step-by-step process to reset your oil life quickly and easily. By the end, you’ll feel confident maintaining your Odyssey like a pro and avoid unnecessary trips to the mechanic. Let’s dive in and make sure your van stays in top shape.




## Oil Life Indicator On Honda Odyssey

The oil life indicator on a 2013 Honda Odyssey helps drivers maintain engine health. It tracks the condition of the engine oil and alerts you when it needs changing. This feature saves time and prevents engine damage by reminding you to service the oil at the right moment.

The indicator uses sensors and software to estimate oil quality based on driving habits and engine conditions. It shows a percentage that decreases as the oil gets older. Once it reaches zero, it's time to change the oil and reset the indicator.

### Purpose Of The Oil Life Monitor

The oil life monitor tracks oil performance and engine wear. It helps avoid unnecessary oil changes, saving money and resources. It also prevents engine problems by signaling when oil loses its effectiveness. This system ensures the engine runs smoothly and lasts longer.

### When To Reset The Indicator

Reset the oil life indicator after every oil change. This tells the system that fresh oil is in use. Resetting keeps the monitor accurate and reliable. Ignoring the reset may cause false alerts or missed warnings.

Always reset the indicator before driving. This guarantees proper tracking of the new oil's lifespan. Resetting is simple and quick, requiring no special tools. Follow the owner’s manual steps to complete the reset properly.

Before resetting the oil life on your 2013 Honda Odyssey, proper preparation is important. This helps ensure the reset works correctly and the vehicle's system updates as needed. Taking a moment to follow these steps prepares your car for the reset process.

Make sure the engine is completely off. The ignition should be in the "off" position before starting any reset steps. This prevents errors and allows the system to reset properly.

### Turn The Ignition To On

Without starting the engine, turn the ignition key to the "on" position. This powers the dashboard and electronic systems. You will see the warning lights light up, but do not start the engine yet. This step activates the reset mode.

  


## Step-by-step Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-life-on-your-honda-odyssey-2013-is-a-straightforward-process-that-you-can-handle-yourself-with-just-a-few-simple-steps-this-task-is-crucial-to-maintaining-the-health-of-your-vehicle-and-ensuring-youre-keeping-up-with-regular-oil-changes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil life on your Honda Odyssey 2013 is a straightforward process that you can handle yourself with just a few simple steps. This task is crucial to maintaining the health of your vehicle and ensuring you’re keeping up with regular oil changes. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you navigate through the reset process with ease.

### Access The Dashboard Menu

Start by sitting comfortably in the driver’s seat and turning on the ignition without starting the engine. Locate the “Select/Reset” knob on your dashboard. This knob is your key to accessing the menu you need. By turning and pressing it, you can interact with the display screen.

Do you remember the last time you explored your car’s dashboard features? It’s like rediscovering hidden treasures that you didn’t know were there. This simple action will open up a menu full of options, allowing you to check various settings and statuses, including the oil life.

### Navigate To The Oil Life Screen

Once you have accessed the dashboard menu, use the “Select/Reset” knob to scroll through the available options. Look for the “Vehicle Settings” option. This is where you’ll find the oil life indicator.

Doesn’t it feel empowering to take control of your vehicle’s maintenance? As you scroll, pay attention to the oil life percentage displayed on the screen. This number tells you how much life is left before your next oil change is due.

### Perform The Reset Action

When you arrive at the oil life screen, press and hold the “Select/Reset” knob for about 10 seconds. You’ll notice the screen starts flashing, indicating that the reset process has begun. Release the knob, and then press it again to confirm the reset.

Isn’t it satisfying to know that such a small action can have a significant impact on your car’s performance? The oil life percentage should now reset to 100%, signaling that your Odyssey is ready for the road with a fresh start. Always remember to check your oil level and quality regularly, as this is key to keeping your vehicle running smoothly.

## Confirming The Reset

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-resetting-the-oil-life-on-your-2013-honda-odyssey-confirming-the-reset-is-important-this-ensures-the-vehicles-system-properly-recognizes-the-new-oil-status&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After resetting the oil life on your 2013 Honda Odyssey, confirming the reset is important. This ensures the vehicle's system properly recognizes the new oil status. A quick check helps avoid confusion during your next service.

### Check The Oil Life Percentage

Look at the dashboard display to find the oil life percentage. It should show 100% or a full reset value. If the number is lower, the reset did not complete correctly. Confirm that the oil life indicator is clear and accurate.

### Restart The Vehicle For Verification

Turn off the engine and wait a few seconds. Start the vehicle again and observe the oil life display. The percentage should stay at 100% after restarting. This confirms the reset is saved in the system. If it changes, repeat the reset process.

## Common Issues And Fixes

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-oil-life-on-a-2013-honda-odyssey-is-usually-simple-still-some-common-problems-can-stop-the-reset-process&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the oil life on a 2013 Honda Odyssey is usually simple. Still, some common problems can stop the reset process. Understanding these issues helps fix them quickly. This section covers frequent problems and their fixes for oil life reset.

### Indicator Not Resetting

The oil life indicator sometimes refuses to reset after oil change. This issue often happens due to incorrect reset steps or electrical glitches. Check if the ignition is in the "ON" position but the engine is off. Follow the reset steps carefully. If the indicator stays the same, try this:

* Turn the ignition off and wait a few minutes.
* Turn the ignition on again and repeat the reset process.
* Disconnect the car battery for a few minutes, then reconnect it.

These actions can clear minor system errors. If the problem continues, the vehicle may need a professional scan for deeper electrical faults.

### Error Messages On Dashboard

Error messages can appear when the oil life reset does not complete properly. These messages might warn of engine or sensor problems. Common errors include low oil pressure or maintenance required alerts.

* Check the oil level and quality to rule out real issues.
* Ensure the oil life reset was done correctly.
* Clear error codes with an OBD-II scanner if available.
* Visit a mechanic if errors persist after resets.

Ignoring these warnings can cause engine damage. Always address dashboard alerts promptly for safety and car health.




## Tips For Maintaining Oil Life

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-the-oil-life-of-your-honda-odyssey-2013-helps-keep-the-engine-healthy-proper-care-extends-the-time-between-oil-changes&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining the oil life of your Honda Odyssey 2013 helps keep the engine healthy. Proper care extends the time between oil changes. It also improves fuel efficiency and reduces engine wear. Understanding simple tips can make a big difference in oil performance and vehicle longevity.

### Regular Oil Changes

Change the oil at the intervals recommended by Honda. Use the correct oil type and quality for your engine. Regular changes prevent sludge buildup and keep engine parts lubricated. This protects the engine and ensures smooth operation.

* Check oil level monthly.
* Replace oil filter during every oil change.
* Use genuine or high-quality oil products.

### Monitoring Driving Conditions

Driving style affects oil life significantly. Frequent short trips and stop-and-go traffic can reduce oil effectiveness. Extreme temperatures and heavy loads also impact oil quality. Adjust maintenance schedules based on your driving habits and environment.

* Drive gently to reduce engine stress.
* Avoid excessive idling and rapid acceleration.
* Consider more frequent oil checks in harsh conditions.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Reset Oil Life On Honda Odyssey 2013?

To reset oil life, turn ignition to ON without starting. Press and hold the Select/Reset button until the oil life indicator blinks and resets to 100%.

### When Should I Reset The Oil Life Indicator?

Reset the oil life indicator after every oil change. This keeps your vehicle's maintenance schedule accurate and the engine running efficiently.

### What If Oil Life Reset Doesn’t Work?

If reset fails, check the owner's manual for steps. Ensure you hold the button long enough and the ignition is in the ON position.

### Can I Reset Oil Life Without Tools?

Yes, resetting requires no tools. Use only the vehicle’s dashboard buttons to complete the oil life reset process easily.
