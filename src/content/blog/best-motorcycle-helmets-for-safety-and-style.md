## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Motorcycle Helmet Safe And Stylish?



A safe helmet meets ISI or DOT certifications for impact resistance. Style comes from design, color, and visor options. Models like Sage Square and TVS Racing combine both for optimal protection and modern looks.



### How Do Motorcycle Goggles Improve Riding Safety?



Goggles protect eyes from UV rays, dust, and wind. Anti-scratch and anti-UV lenses enhance visibility. Products like OTOROYS and SARTE provide comfort and clear vision for safer rides.



### Are Full-face Helmets Better Than Open-face Helmets?



Yes, full-face helmets offer more protection to the chin and face. They reduce wind noise and shield from debris. Models like Sage Square and TVS Racing are great full-face options for safety and comfort.



### Can Helmet Accessories Enhance Safety Or Style?



Yes, accessories like helmet locks and decorative horns add value. Locks prevent theft, while stylish horns personalize your helmet. For example, EXOFLY locks secure your helmet, and THE STYLE SUTRA horns add unique flair.



### What Features Should I Look For In Motorcycle Helmet Goggles?



Look for UV protection, anti-scratch coating, and windproof design. Adjustable straps and foam padding improve comfort. OTOROYS and QONETIC goggles offer these features for better eye protection during rides.
