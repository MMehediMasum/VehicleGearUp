## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Loudest Train Horn For Trucks?



The AOLIHAN Super Loud Train Horn Kit produces up to 180dB. It features four trumpets and a 150 PSI air tank. This makes it the loudest option for trucks, ensuring maximum sound impact and safety on the road.



### How Do Train Horn Kits Install On Trucks?



Most kits like Animgrth and VEVOR come with easy-to-follow instructions. They include compressors, tanks, and trumpets. Installation usually requires mounting the compressor and trumpet, then connecting them to the 12V vehicle power supply.



### Are 12v Train Horns Compatible With Suvs And Pickups?



Yes, models like Animgrth, GAMPRO, and HONSDUKE are designed for any 12V vehicle. This includes trucks, SUVs, pickups, boats, and motorcycles. They deliver powerful sound while fitting standard 12V electrical systems.



### What Air Compressor Size Is Ideal For Truck Train Horns?



A 1 to 3-liter air tank with 120-150 PSI pressure is ideal. Brands like VEVOR and HornBlasters offer compressors with 0. 8 to 3 liters capacity. Larger tanks ensure longer horn blasts and consistent performance.



### Can Train Horns Be Used Safely On Public Roads?



Train horns are extremely loud, often over 150dB, and may violate local noise laws. Use them responsibly and check local regulations before installation. They are best for emergencies or off-road use to avoid legal issues.



### What Materials Ensure Durability In Train Horn Kits?



Chrome and brass trumpets, like in GG Grand General and United Pacific, offer superior durability. These materials resist rust and weather damage, ensuring long-lasting performance for trucks and heavy-duty vehicles.
