Spark plugs should be replaced every 30,000 to 60,000 miles. Iridium plugs can last longer, up to 100,000 miles, ensuring better performance and fuel economy.

Yes, ignition coil packs paired with quality spark plugs improve ignition efficiency. This combo boosts engine power, smooths starts, and reduces misfires in your Ram 1500.

Iridium plugs provide stronger sparks, longer life, and better fuel efficiency. They are ideal for Ram 1500 5. 7 Hemi engines needing reliable performance and longevity.



### Which Spark Plugs Fit Dodge Ram 1500 5.7l V8 From 2003-2024?

### What Tools Are Needed To Replace Spark Plugs On Ram 1500 5.7 Hemi?
