### What Is The Difference Between Studded And Non-studded Winter Tires?



Studded tires have metal studs for ice grip; non-studded rely on rubber tread. Studded tires excel on ice but can be noisier and wear faster on dry roads. Non-studded tires perform better on snow and slush and offer quieter rides.



### Are Tire Studs Like Marrkey Spikes Compatible With All Winter Tires?



Marrkey studs fit many tires but require proper installation. They enhance traction on icy surfaces when added to compatible tires. However, not all tires or local laws permit stud installation. Always check tire specifications and regulations before using studs.
