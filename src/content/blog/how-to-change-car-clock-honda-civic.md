## Locate The Clock Controls

Locating the clock controls in your Honda Civic is the first step to changing the time. These controls are usually easy to find but can vary by model year. Knowing where to look saves time and makes the process simple.

Most Honda Civics place the clock controls near the dashboard or the audio system. Some models use buttons directly on the clock display. Others combine the clock settings with the infotainment system.

### Check The Dashboard Area

Look around the main dashboard area. The clock might have small buttons labeled "H" for hour and "M" for minute. These buttons can be right next to the digital clock display.

### Inspect The Infotainment Screen

Newer Honda Civics may use the touchscreen to set the clock. Look for a "Settings" or "Clock" option in the menu. Use the touchscreen to adjust the hours and minutes.

### Examine The Audio System Buttons

Some Honda Civics have clock controls integrated into the audio system. Buttons like "Clock," "Setup," or "Menu" could lead to clock settings. Use these to find and change the time.

### Consult The Owner’s Manual

The owner’s manual provides exact locations for clock controls. It includes images and instructions specific to your Honda Civic model. Keep it handy for quick reference.

## Adjusting The Hours

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adjusting-the-hours-on-your-honda-civics-clock-is-a-straightforward-task-but-it-can-feel-tricky-if-youre-not-familiar-with-the-controls-getting-the-time-right-is-important-especially-if-you-rely-on-your-cars-clock-for-daily-schedules&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adjusting the hours on your Honda Civic’s clock is a straightforward task, but it can feel tricky if you're not familiar with the controls. Getting the time right is important, especially if you rely on your car’s clock for daily schedules. Let’s look at how you can easily set the correct hour.

### Locate The Clock Adjustment Buttons

First, find the buttons or knobs specifically meant for clock settings. These are usually near the car radio or on the dashboard. In many Honda Civic models, look for buttons labeled “H” for hours and “M” for minutes.

Some models might have a single “Clock” or “Clock Adjust” button instead. Pressing this may activate the clock setting mode, allowing you to change the hour.

### Press And Hold To Enter Setting Mode

Try pressing and holding the “H” button or the “Clock” button for a few seconds. This often switches the display into a mode where you can adjust the hour. If the clock doesn’t respond, check your owner’s manual for specific instructions.

When the hour digits start blinking, it means you’re ready to adjust. If they don’t blink, you might need to press another button to enter the correct mode.

### Adjust The Hour Value

Once in hour adjustment mode, press the “H” button repeatedly to increase the hour. Each press usually advances the hour by one. Watch closely as the numbers change to avoid overshooting your desired time.

Remember, some models use a 12-hour clock, so be mindful of AM and PM. If your car doesn’t show AM/PM, set the hour as you see it and cross-check with your watch or phone.

### Confirm The New Time

After setting the correct hour, press the “Clock” or “Set” button to save the changes. If your car doesn’t have a confirm button, simply waiting a few seconds may automatically save the new time.

Check if the clock stops blinking—that usually means the time is set. If it resets or doesn’t change, try the process again or verify you are in the right mode.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Changing the clock on a Honda Civic is usually simple, but some common issues can cause trouble. These problems often stop the clock from updating or keep the time from saving properly. Knowing how to fix these helps keep your car’s clock accurate and easy to set.

### Clock Not Responding

The clock may not respond if the buttons seem stuck or nothing changes when pressed. Check if the buttons are clean and free from dirt or debris.

* Try pressing and holding the clock button for a few seconds.
* Ensure the car is in the right mode, like ACC or ON, for the clock settings to work.
* Reset the system by turning off the car and restarting it.
* Inspect the fuse related to the clock in the fuse box; replace if blown.

If the clock still doesn’t respond, the internal clock module might need professional attention.

### Time Reset After Battery Change

The clock often resets to 12:00 after the car battery is disconnected or replaced. This happens because the clock loses power and resets.

* Reset the clock manually using the hour and minute buttons.
* Some models keep time with a backup battery; if this is dead, the clock resets.
* Check the car’s manual for specific reset instructions after battery changes.

Keeping a small note of your clock settings helps after battery replacements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Change The Clock On My Honda Civic?

To change the clock, press the "Clock" button on the dashboard. Use the "H" and "M" buttons to set hours and minutes. Confirm the time by pressing the "Clock" button again. Your Honda Civic clock will now display the correct time.

### Can I Set The Honda Civic Clock Without The Manual?

Yes, you can set the clock without the manual. Locate the "Clock," "H," and "M" buttons near the radio. Press and hold the "Clock" button until the time blinks. Use "H" and "M" to adjust time, then press "Clock" to save.

### Why Won’t My Honda Civic Clock Change?

If the clock won’t change, check if the ignition is on. Some models require the car to be in accessory mode. Also, ensure you are holding the "Clock" button long enough before adjusting time. If issues persist, consult your vehicle manual.

### Does The Honda Civic Clock Reset After Battery Change?

Yes, the Honda Civic clock resets after a battery change. You need to manually reset the time using the "Clock," "H," and "M" buttons. Always set the correct time to avoid confusion while driving.
