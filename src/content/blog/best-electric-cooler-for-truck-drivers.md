## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Voltage Do Electric Coolers For Trucks Use?



Most electric coolers for trucks operate on 12V or 24V DC power. This allows easy connection to truck batteries. They also support 110-240V AC for home or campsite use, providing versatile power options for various environments.



### How Long Can A 12v Electric Cooler Keep Items Cold?



A 12V electric cooler can maintain cold temperatures for up to 50 hours. This depends on the model, insulation, and outside temperature. Compressors ensure consistent cooling, making them ideal for long truck trips and camping.



### Are Electric Coolers Suitable For Both Cooling And Warming?



Yes, some electric coolers offer both cooling and warming functions. Models like the TREFAN and Ivation provide temperature ranges from cold to warm. This feature is convenient for storing beverages or keeping food warm during travel.



### Can I Control My Electric Cooler Remotely Via An App?



Certain models, such as EUHOMY and JicSuXi, support app control. This feature allows temperature adjustments and monitoring from your smartphone. It enhances convenience and helps maintain optimal cooling while on the road.



### What Size Electric Cooler Is Best For Truck Drivers?



Truck drivers usually prefer coolers between 15L to 50L capacity. Smaller sizes fit limited space, while larger ones hold more supplies. The choice depends on travel duration and storage needs during trips.



### Are Electric Coolers Portable And Easy To Move?



Most electric coolers for trucks are designed to be portable. Features like wheels and handles, seen in EUHOMY 53QT, make transportation easy. Portability is essential for camping, road trips, and frequent use in different vehicles.
