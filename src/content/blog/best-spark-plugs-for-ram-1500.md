For optimal performance, replace spark plugs every 30,000 to 60,000 miles. Iridium plugs last longer, often up to 100,000 miles. Regular checks help maintain fuel efficiency and engine power. Always follow manufacturer recommendations for your specific Ram 1500 model.



### Are Iridium Spark Plugs Better Than Copper For Ram 1500?



Yes, iridium spark plugs last longer and provide more efficient ignition. They improve fuel economy and reduce misfires. Copper plugs are cheaper but wear out faster. For Ram 1500 with 5. 7L V8 engines, iridium plugs are a better long-term investment.



### Can I Use Ngk Spark Plugs On Ram 1500 Trucks?



Absolutely. NGK offers several compatible spark plugs like Iridium IX and Laser Iridium for Ram 1500 5. 7L V8 engines. They are known for high quality and reliable performance, making them a popular choice among Ram truck owners.



### Do Champion Copper Plus Plugs Fit Older Ram 1500 Models?



Yes, Champion Copper Plus spark plugs fit Dodge Ram 1500 models from 1997 to 2008 with 5. 2L, 5. 7L, and 5. 9L V8 engines. They provide reliable ignition and are a cost-effective replacement option for older trucks.



### What Spark Plug Type Is Best For Fuel Efficiency In Ram 1500?



Iridium spark plugs are the best for fuel efficiency in Ram 1500 trucks. They ignite fuel more effectively, leading to better combustion and improved mileage. NGK Iridium IX and Laser Iridium plugs are highly recommended for this purpose.
